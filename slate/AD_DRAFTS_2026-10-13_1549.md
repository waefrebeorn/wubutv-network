  on the door.
- Cast: the man — heavyset Black man, large rounded afro, mustache and
  goatee, bare eyes, no glasses; plain BLACK hoodie with exact yellow/gold
  `~ WuBu ~` — exactly one tilde before the W and one tilde after the u,
  the two u letters plain u with no dots, no diaeresis, no umlaut, never ü;
  the wordmark must be legible on the bright chest panel in the 0.5s
  opening frame, the 5.0s beat frame, and the 9.5s verdict frame.

  the camera, chest square to the lens, well lit, body still, hands at his
  sides. At ~2s an unseen lobby PA speaker crackles once, off-camera: "The
  half." His mouth stays closed the whole take; he never speaks.
- Beat 4-6s: with an arm-only motion, body still, chest staying square to
  the camera, the reach arc staying to his right side below shoulder height

  T-shaped; no VHS caption, no third line under the bug.
- TEXT allowlist: exactly "CLOSED" and "OPEN" on the hanging sign — nothing

  no plates, no posters, no papers, no boards, no furniture text anywhere.
