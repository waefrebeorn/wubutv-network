AD-260 "Marbled" v1 — The Marbling Trough.

10-second 16:9 commercial, 1280x720, exactly 10.0 seconds, flat hand-painted 2D cartoon grade (STYLE IS LOAD-BEARING: every surface painted, no photorealism, no photographic texture anywhere). Locked-off camera, identical framing at 0.5s and 9.5s (240-frame camera lock; no push-in, no reframe, no approach staging, never a close-up).

CHARACTER (identity lock): UNC — heavyset Black man, large rounded afro, mustache/goatee, BLACK hoodie. On the chest, the exact yellow/gold wordmark `~ WuBu ~` — six glyphs: tilde, space, W-u-B-u, space, tilde, the two u letters plain u with no dots, no diaeresis, no umlaut, never ü. Plain fabric, no other lettering. BARE FABRIC — no yellow panel, no yellow box, no yellow rectangle, no patch, no plate behind the letters, the black fabric uninterrupted around every glyph. BARE EYES, no glasses, no spectacles, no eyewear. He never reacts; deadpan throughout. (TILDE AND POSTURE LOCK: both tildes always present and fully rendered even when he bends or leans — posture never removes a tilde, never profile-turned into invisibility; his hands stay clear of the chest panel at every point; his torso never turns; his head never drops over the wordmark.)

CORNER BUG (every frame): two lines bottom-right, plain WHITE letters, never yellow, never gold, never boxed, never on a backing panel, never a chip: `~ WuBu ~` over `WuBuTV`.

SET: the paper room — a long marbling trough filled with thickened size, combed color floating on its surface at 0.5s; a blank sheet of paper held above the trough. Exactly ONE trough, ONE sheet. TEXT ALLOWLIST: NONE — the sheet, the trough, the size, the room all bare and unlettered; no signage anywhere, no readable marks of any kind, no labels; no WuBu text on any poster, sign, wall, or background surface. The whole set is wordless.

BEAT (10s, setup/turn/tag):
- 0-4s (setup): the sheet BLANK, held by two corners above the trough at 0.5s; the combed color floating undisturbed on the size. Frame 1 is on the blank sheet (OBJECT FIRST). He stands beside the trough facing camera, motionless, mouth closed.
- 4-6s (beat): he lays the sheet FLAT onto the floating color in ONE continuous lay — the marbling begins. With arms only, body still, chest square, the lay arc BELOW the chest panel; his hands never cross the wordmark zone.
- 6-8s (turn): the sheet resting on the size, the color transferring; his hands withdrawn to his sides, he stands motionless facing camera, mouth closed.
- 8s (line): exactly ONE mouth opening ~8.0-8.5s with one continuous voiced event: "Laid on." The line is the LOUDEST event in the take; all beds stay under a -25 dBFS ceiling.
- 9.5s (verdict): the sheet LIFTED and held to the camera by two corners, the marbled pattern across its whole face, no blank paper left; his eyes on the lens, mouth closed.

NEGATIVE: no photorealism, no third bug line, no boxed bug, no backing panel behind the bug, no spelled-out wordmark descriptions as on-screen text, no yellow or gold bug letters, no real logos, no brands, no timecode/REC/production overlays, no day stamps, no corner timecodes, no overlay graphics of any kind, the two u letters never rendered with dots or umlaut, no hand or prop occlusion of the chest wordmark at any point, no second sheet, no second trough, no readable letters anywhere on set, no voice-grade event outside the one directed line, no open mouth on any frame outside the 8.0-8.5s line window, no WuBu text on any background surface, no lamp.

AUDIO: native diegetic audio only, never dubbed. Quiet beds only: the sheet lay ~5s, the sheet lift ~7.5s — all under -25 dBFS; the directed line "Laid on." at ~8.0-8.5s is the loudest event in the take. True peak must stay under -1 dB.

PRODUCTION NOTE: the beat reads in the transferred pattern, not in text; the blank sheet at 0.5s vs the fully marbled sheet at 9.5s is the whole commercial. Identity continuity: the sheet at 0.5s is the SAME sheet at 9.5s; the trough at 0.5s is the SAME trough at 9.5s.
