**Status: FIRE-READY.** Drafted by the CEO office 2026-10-21 for the Producer
(Muse) to fire as ONE serial arc, in order, on PUBLIC ACCESS. Units are the
factory's native 10s single-take format; do not renumber inside the arc.

Why this arc exists: the fruit-soap form (8-9 units, daily, in order, one fixed
cast, one cliff per unit, one status OBJECT as the engine) was the biggest reach
engine of 2025-26 and it is a paperwork engine at heart, which is already our
canon. We take the spine and refuse the content: no synthetic misogyny, no
violence-as-plot, no licensed characters, no meme likeness, no talking heads.
Melodrama is played at full gravity over an object that does not deserve it —
that inversion IS the comedy. Full form rules: `notes/SERIAL_FORM.md`.
