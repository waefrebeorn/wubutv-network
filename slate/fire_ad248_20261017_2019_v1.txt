Locked-off camera, identical framing at 0.5s and 9.5s (240-frame camera lock; no push-in, no reframe, no approach staging, never a close-up).

CHARACTER (identity lock): UNC — heavyset Black man, large rounded afro, mustache/goatee, BLACK hoodie. (TILDE AND POSTURE LOCK: both tildes always present, never profile-turned into invisibility; his hands stay clear of the chest panel at every point; his torso never turns; his head never drops over the wordmark.)

CORNER BUG (every frame): two lines bottom-right, plain WHITE letters, never yellow, never gold, never boxed, never on a backing panel, never a chip: `~ WuBu ~` over `WuBuTV`.

SET: the tinsmith's bench — one flat sheet-metal bracket with a single upright tab at one end, one steel base plate with a punched slot in its center, one small ball-peen hammer resting on the bench. Exactly ONE bracket, ONE base plate, ONE hammer. TEXT ALLOWLIST: NONE — the bracket, the tab, the base plate, the hammer, the bench all bare and unlettered; no signage anywhere, no readable marks of any kind, no labels. The whole set is wordless.

BEAT (10s, setup/turn/tag):
- 0-4s (setup): the bracket standing beside the base plate at 0.5s, its single tab UPRIGHT. Frame 1 is on the upright tab (OBJECT FIRST). He stands beside the bench facing camera, motionless, mouth closed, both hands empty at his sides.
- 4-6s (beat): he slips the tab through the slot, lays the bracket flat on the base plate, and hammers the tab folded flat — ONE continuous slip-lay-hammer motion across the whole 4-6s window, never a frozen take (MOTION IS LOAD-BEARING: a take where the 0.5s and 9.5s tab states match is rejected). During the beat his hands stay below shoulder height, his hands never cross the wordmark zone, his torso never turns, his head never drops over the wordmark.
- 6-8s (turn): the joint made — the tab folded FLUSH through the slot, the bracket seated flat on the base plate; he stands motionless facing camera, hands at his sides, mouth closed.
- 9.5s (verdict): the bracket seated on the base plate, the tab folded flat and flush through the slot; the hammer resting on the bench; his hands empty at his sides, eyes on the lens, mouth closed.
