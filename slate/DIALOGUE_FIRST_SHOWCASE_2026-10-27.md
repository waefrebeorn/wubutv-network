## Chain A — "The Night Nobody Counted"

Engine: the station promises every shift is counted. It cannot keep the
promise. Question at the heart: who counts the people who count?
Withheld fact: the circled name on the roster quit last week.
Message that lands: a system that counts itself counts no one.

### A-P1 — LAURA, log-desk alcove
- HOOK (0–1.5s): roster board, one name circled in VERMILION, Laura's hand
  hovering over it, sound off legible: a question is coming.
- STATE (1.5–4s): Laura at the log desk, FOREST only + bone + ink, flat
  fills, ink outline every edge. Camera: slow push-in.
- TURN (4–7s): she looks from the circled name to the empty chair beside
  the desk. The chair does the talking.
- LINE (7–9s, LAURA): "Who counts the night shift?"
- CLIFF (9–10s): first frame of A-P2 — the Colonel at the anchor desk,
  already mid-sentence, COBALT.
- Audio: native room tone + roster-page rustle. No text anywhere.

### A-P2 — COLONEL, anchor desk
- HOOK (0–1.5s): Colonel mid-sentence at the anchor desk, COBALT + bone +
  ink. He is answering before the question finishes.
- STATE (1.5–4s): anchor desk, broadcast lights, flat fills. Camera: static
  wide, then a small step-in.
- TURN (4–7s): he straightens the papers he does not need to straighten.
  The dodge is the tell.
- LINE (7–9s, COLONEL): "The roster counts itself, Laura."
- CLIFF (9–10s): first frame of A-P3 — UNC in the control room doorway,
  INK + CADMIUM, wordmark exact.
- Audio: native desk hum + paper shuffle.

### A-P3 — UNC, control room doorway
- HOOK (0–1.5s): UNC in the doorway, hoodie wordmark `~ WuBu ~` in CADMIUM,
  exact, no border, no box, no emblem around it.
- STATE (1.5–4s): control room behind him, monitors dark. Camera: slow
  lateral track.
- TURN (4–7s): he looks at the roster, then past it, at the empty chair.
- LINE (7–9s, UNC): "Then nobody counts at all."
- CLIFF (9–10s): the empty chair, held, lights still on — the next chain's
  problem, not this one's answer.
- Audio: native control-room tone, one soft chair creak.

## Chain B — "The Advertiser Who Stayed"

Engine: sales promises one viewer by midnight. The station cannot verify it.
Question at the heart: is a promise you cannot check a promise at all?
Withheld fact: the "one viewer" is the station's own lobby monitor.
Message that lands: a promise you cannot verify is not a promise.

### B-P1 — PETER, sales office
- HOOK (0–1.5s): Peter sliding a single contract across the desk,
  VERMILION + bone + ink, the signature line empty.
- STATE (1.5–4s): sales office, flat fills. Camera: over-shoulder push-in.
- TURN (4–7s): he taps the empty signature line twice. Twice is a tell.
- LINE (7–9s, PETER): "Guarantee one viewer by midnight?"
- CLIFF (9–10s): first frame of B-P2 — UNC at his desk, INK + CADMIUM,
  wordmark exact, not looking up.
- Audio: native office tone + contract slide.

### B-P2 — UNC, manager's desk
- HOOK (0–1.5s): UNC not looking up from the desk, calm, certain.
- STATE (1.5–4s): manager's desk, INK + CADMIUM + bone. Camera: static
  two-shot from the doorway.
- TURN (4–7s): the smallest pause before the last word. That pause is the
  whole station.
- LINE (7–9s, UNC): "We already have one. Probably."
- CLIFF (9–10s): first frame of B-P3 — Laura in the lobby, FOREST, looking
  at the monitor, not at camera.
- Audio: native desk tone, one pen click.

### B-P3 — LAURA, lobby
- HOOK (0–1.5s): Laura in the lobby, the monitor glowing behind her,
  playing the station to an empty room.
- STATE (1.5–4s): lobby, FOREST + bone + ink. Camera: slow orbit around
  her toward the monitor.
- TURN (4–7s): she turns from the monitor to camera. She already knows.
- LINE (7–9s, LAURA): "Probably isn't a viewer, sir."
- CLIFF (9–10s): the monitor, still playing, still unwatched — the promise
  kept by nobody.
- Audio: native lobby tone + faint broadcast bleed from the monitor.

## Chain C — "The Signal That Answered"

Engine: the tower answers itself. The station's audience may be itself.
Question at the heart: who is the station broadcasting to?
Withheld fact: the reply signal matches the station's own sign-off cadence.
Message that lands: a station that only talks to itself has one viewer,
and it is the building.

### C-P1 — DISPATCHER, transmitter room
- HOOK (0–1.5s): transmitter dials moving with no hand on them, INK +
  BONE, the needle answering a question nobody asked.
- STATE (1.5–4s): transmitter room, flat fills. Camera: slow push-in on
  the dials.
- TURN (4–7s): the Dispatcher stops logging and just watches. Watching is
  the report.
- LINE (7–9s, DISPATCHER): "The tower answered itself tonight."
- CLIFF (9–10s): first frame of C-P2 — Laura in the transmitter doorway,
  FOREST, holding the logbook.
- Audio: native transmitter hum + dial tick.

### C-P2 — LAURA, transmitter doorway
- HOOK (0–1.5s): Laura in the doorway with the logbook, the dials still
  moving behind her.
- STATE (1.5–4s): doorway, FOREST + bone + ink. Camera: step-in from the
  hall.
- TURN (4–7s): she checks the logbook, then the dials. The logbook has no
  entry for this. That absence is the question.
- LINE (7–9s, LAURA): "Who was it answering to?"
- CLIFF (9–10s): first frame of C-P3 — the Colonel at the microphone,
  COBALT, headphones on, mid-broadcast.
- Audio: native hall tone + logbook page turn.

### C-P3 — COLONEL, broadcast microphone
- HOOK (0–1.5s): Colonel at the microphone, headphones on, speaking to an
  audience he cannot see.
- STATE (1.5–4s): broadcast booth, COBALT + bone + ink. Camera: tight
  two-shot, mic foreground.
- TURN (4–7s): he takes the headphones off, slowly. He heard the answer.
- LINE (7–9s, COLONEL): "Us. It learned our voices."
- CLIFF (9–10s): the microphone, live light on, nobody speaking — the
  station's one viewer, listening.
- Audio: native booth tone + mic-room silence after the line.
