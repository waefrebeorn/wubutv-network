# TikTok Manager Policy — WuBuTV Channel

Account: **@waefrebeorn** (verified signed in 2026-10-15; 1.8K followers, 29K likes). PERSONAL account — stays personal, no Creator/Business switch, no TikTok scheduler. Posting is agent-driven: WuBu's own job cycle fires on its schedule and posts IMMEDIATELY through TikTok Studio's upload flow in the signed-in browser. TikTok's native scheduler is not used.

Standing policy for WuBu as TikTok channel manager. The channel is a mysterious
television network: deadpan 10-second slices of life lore, ~ WuBu ~. Best uploads
on a consistent schedule. This document is versioned — every change is logged at
the bottom with a date.

## 1. Channel identity

- A television channel, not a person. Never explain the joke. Never break the
  deadpan. Captions are written in the network's voice: terse, paperwork-flavored,
  lore-heavy.
- The mystery is the product: viewers accumulate the world's rules (the MEGA, the
  Corrections Desk, the ∞:0 scoreboard, the Night Rounds) one 10-second brick at
  a time. Captions may deepen lore but never explain it.
- Branded tag, every post: `~ WuBu ~`. The tildes are part of the wordmark.

## 2. The backlog

- ~919 bumper videos (10s, 16:9 landscape, 1280x720) plus episodes and
  commercials as the operation matures. The user hand-uploaded the first ~500.
- DEDUP IS LOAD-BEARING: never re-upload a video the user already posted.
  `TIKTOK_POSTS.md` is the ledger; a video is posted only if it has no row.
  Until the hand-uploaded set is reconciled, the upload loop stays parked
  (see §7).

## 3. Kevin Bacon research policy (the manager's standing studies)

The TikTok manager runs associative research chains exactly like the network's
Q0 quadrant: pick a node, make up to 6 hops, each hop connected CONCRETELY
(shared object, phrase, tag, format mechanic, audience behavior — never a vibe).
Document every chain with sources. Bank findings in `TIKTOK_IDEAS.md`.

Standing chain types (run continuously, bank every cycle):

- **A. Trend → format.** Take a live TikTok trend/sound/format. Hop it into a
  WuBuTV-native adaptation: deadpan, lore-heavy, paperwork-flavored. Never copy
  a trend verbatim; adapt the mechanic (e.g. "POV" grammar → the Colonel's
  corrections as a POV format; stitch chains → the ledger as a stitch surface).
- **B. Tag clusters.** From a bumper's subject, hop through related hashtags to
  build per-video tag sets: 1 broad anchor, 2–3 niche community tags, 1 branded
  (`~ WuBu ~` / wubutv). Rotate sets so no two consecutive posts share an
  identical tag block (spam-avoidance).
- **C. Competitor autopsy.** Study high-performing deadpan/absurdist/mystery
  accounts: their cadence, caption grammar, series mechanics, reply-to-comment
  rituals. Adapt STRUCTURE (series numbering, recurring bits, drop times), never
  content.
- **D. Lore seeding.** Research real-world facts (archival practice, broadcast
  history, municipal ritual) that deepen the channel's mythology for captions
  and series concepts. Primary sources preferred.

Also standing: the art-of-10-seconds study applied to TikTok (hook in the first
1–2s even though our videos are complete scenes; cover frame = the silent-frame
read), and meme-literacy (network-native formats only, never licensed templates).

## 4. Posting system (researched 2026-10-15)

- **Caption template:** `B-<n> "<Title>" — ~ WuBu ~` + one rotating lore-flat
  tagline (≤100 chars, never the same tagline twice in a row) + 3–5 hashtags.
- **Hashtag strategy:** 3–5 tags per post, never more than ~10. Mix: 1 broad
  (e.g. #comedy, #skit), 2–3 niche/community (deadpan, absurdist, original
  series tags), 1 branded anchor: **#WuBuTV** (constant, every post). Rotate
  3–4 pre-built tag sets; no two consecutive posts share an identical block.
  No stuffing, no irrelevant trending tags.
- **Upload settings:** 16:9 landscape uploads as-is (matches the user's
  hand-upload precedent; TikTok letterboxes — accepted for now, see open
  decision below). Original sound always. Comments ON, duet/stitch ON
  (discovery surfaces; toggle off later only if misused). Custom cover frame
  per upload (the silent-frame read; consistent grid look). **AI-generated
  content label ON** — compliant, and per TikTok's own guidance the label
  alone does not hurt distribution.
- **Posting method:** the upload job fires on its own cadence and posts
  IMMEDIATELY through TikTok Studio's upload flow — no TikTok scheduler, no
  drafts queue. The agent IS the schedule.
- **Best-time starting points (US ET):** Tue/Thu peak days; 6–9am, 11am–1pm,
  7–10pm; Sunday 9am strong. Validate against the account's own Follower
  Activity analytics after 2–3 weeks and adjust.

## 5. Pacing policy (user order 2026-10-15 ~18:41 EDT — 9 posts/hour, the maximum)

- **Cadence: minimum 6 minutes between posts = 9/hour.** This is the
  user-ordered maximum. The upload flow itself takes minutes, so posts will
  not be metronomic — no artificial delay beyond the 6-minute floor.
- **Why 9 and not 10:** the floor research found exactly one hard
  documented tripwire: ~10 uploads within an hour gets detected as a burst
  pattern and suppressed. 9/hour sits one post below that wall. 10/hour was
  explicitly declined — it is the documented wall, not a target. Below the
  6-minute floor there is no evidence at all.
- **What keeps us on the right side at this pace:** unique subjects, unique
  captions, rotated hashtag sets (never two consecutive identical blocks),
  AI-generated label ON, real signed-in browser (never a bot API),
  established account, zero engagement automation, steady spacing (never
  bursts — the 6-minute floor IS the anti-burst rule).
- **Hair-trigger governor (binding):** at 9/hour ANY of these parks the
  operation 48–72h immediately, no second chances: TikTok "posting too
  fast" / upload rejected / captcha loop / session-reported rate limit /
  two consecutive upload failures. The kickstand nag enforces this per run.
  Near-zero For You traffic on 2+ consecutive posts → same park, resume at
  half pace (30-min floor), rebuild over 1–2 weeks. Spam restrictions clear

## 6. Recursive learning loop

1. **Post** (upload job) → record URL + timestamp + caption + tag set in ledger.
2. **Measure** (review job): pull views/likes/comments/shares per post via TikTok
   Studio analytics and the social_content_performance skill.
3. **Bank**: what worked (tag set, post hour, caption shape, series mechanic)
   goes into `TIKTOK_IDEAS.md` as a finding with numbers.
4. **Adjust**: findings that replicate change this policy (pacing, tags,
   schedule) — logged in the changelog below. The loop never stops.

## 7. Job cycle

- `tiktok-trend-research` — weekly: run chain types A–D, bank findings.
- `tiktok-upload-loop` — per pacing (§5): next unposted video from the ledger,
  upload via TikTok Studio in the signed-in browser session, schedule per
  cadence. PARKED until dedup of the user's hand-uploaded ~500 is resolved.
- `tiktok-performance-review` — weekly: pull metrics, bank findings, propose
  policy adjustments.

## 8. QC before every post

- Right file (ledger title matches the video's bumper number).
- No existing ledger row for it (dedup).
- Caption matches the template; tag block rotated; `~ WuBu ~` present.
- Upload verified landed in TikTok Studio (draft/scheduled state confirmed,
  never assumed).
