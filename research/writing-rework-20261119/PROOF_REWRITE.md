## U1 — HOOK + SETUP · LAURA · proof object: the tin

Facts carried (identical to v6): the tin held nine coins and is
empty now; Laura will not chalk 'thief' on a say-so; the Courier
is ordered to hold her hands up; (picture) the Peach presses her
bouquet shut over a glint of coins.

| # | v6 (locked) | Rewrite | Words |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | "Nine went in, Ansel. Count what's left." | "Nine went in. Count what's left." | 7 → 6 |
| L2 | "Empty. Not chalking 'thief' off that." | "Empty. No chalking 'thief' off drama, Ansel." | 6 → 7 |
| L3 | "Hands up, Courier — show me empty." | "Hands up, Courier — show me empty." | 6 → 6 |

**Total: 19 words (at cap, as v6).**

- **L1 — name moved, count kept.** The line was already the
  strongest in the unit: an accusation-with-proof in her
  catchphrase shape. "Ansel" moves to L2 (names rationed; the
  count addresses itself — he is the only man who brought a tin).
  What it buys: nothing new, deliberately. The standard does not
  churn working lines for novelty; it protects them.
- **L2 — the refusal gets a person in it.** v6's "off that" grounds
  the refusal in procedure: the tin is empty, therefore no
  chalking — a clerk citing evidence standards. The rewrite keeps
  the evidence ("Empty." — the count answered in one word) and
  prices what Ansel actually did: he *slammed* the tin to stampede
  the wall, and she names it — drama. What it buys: (a) attitude —
  she cannot be stampeded, and says so in the street's own word;
  (b) the brag surfaces — her wall's standards are her pride, not
  her paperwork; (c) contact — the line answers his *act* (the
  slam the audience just saw), not only his claim. The story fact
  is unchanged: no verdict on a say-so; the test is set in L3
  instead, exactly as v6.
- **L3 — kept verbatim.** The order echoes L1's count ("show me
  empty" — the hands must now do what the tin did), payload last,
  the chair-turn named. It passed every test including this one;
  the rewrite's discipline is to spend its changes where the
  flavor is missing, not to redecorate what already sounds like
  her.

## U2 — PROOF + FIRST LIE · THE PEACH · proof object: the bouquet

Facts carried (identical to v6): (picture) the Courier tips the
tin over her palm — nothing falls; Ansel's fee coin is pocketed
by the Watcher; the Peach demands to be written next; her sworn
claim — she and the Lime have held the bouquet since dawn, both
hands, all morning — is the lie U5's spill must convict, in both

| # | v6 (locked) | Rewrite | Words |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | "Us next, Watcher." | "Us next, Watcher." | 3 → 3 |
| L2 | "Us two have held this bouquet since dawn." | "Us two. This bouquet. Since dawn." | 8 → 6 |
| L3 | "Both hands on it. All morning." | "Both hands, all morning — ask the street." | 6 → 7 |

**Total: 16 words (cap 17; the spare word is reaction headroom,
per the B-12 budgeting rule).**

- **L1 — kept verbatim.** A demand, name-spammed at the keeper,
  in the show-off's carrier habit. Already hers.
- **L2 — the claim becomes a stack.** v6's full sentence is a
  claim *filed* — grammatical, complete, sworn in the shape of a
  form. The rewrite delivers it the way she delivered it in the
  banter draft (TU2-04: "Peach and Lime. A couple. Holding this
  bouquet since dawn"): in fragments, stacking — *Us two. This
  bouquet. Since dawn.* What it buys: (a) an audible voiceprint —
  Laura speaks in declaratives; the Peach speaks in piles, and a
  cold listener can tell them apart with the names removed;
  (b) the performance of swearing — she is *building* the lie in
  front of us, piece by piece, which is funnier and more damning
  than reporting it; (c) every convictable element survives
  verbatim in substance — the couple, the bouquet, *since dawn* —
  so U5's conviction is untouched.
- **L3 — the tell restored.** This is the banter line the harvest
  cut (TU2-06: "Both hands! His hands, my hands, all the hands,
  all morning. Ask the street, the street saw."), restored at the
  shortest length at which the tell is still audible: the sworn
  detail ("Both hands, all morning" — convictable form two,
  intact) plus **one volunteered witness nobody asked for** —
  "ask the street." What it buys: (a) the audience *hears* her
  lying — over-eagerness is the sound of it, per the
  too-specific-denial mechanic; v6's audience had to be told
  later that she lied, this audience knows at the line; (b) the
  episode's QUOTABLE — three words built to be repeated, and the
  hanger's voice: in U6 the name "goes to the street tomorrow,"
  so the witness she invented returns as the court that decides
  her name. Her own flavor becomes her consequence.

## What the proof demonstrates

1. **The facts did not move.** Both units carry v6's fact spend
   exactly — the count and its answer, the refusal and the test,
   the demand and both convictable claims. Flavor was added by
   *restoring and protecting* material the old pipeline removed,
   not by decorating the plot.
2. **The word budget did not move.** 19 and 16 against caps of 19
   and 17. Taste is not wordier; it is differently spent — on a
   tell, an attitude, a repeatable phrase — instead of on
   grammatical completeness.
3. **The two voices are now distinguishable blind.** Declaratives
   vs. stacks; a count vs. a stamp; dry vs. glossy. The old
   method's swap test — any clerk could say v6's lines — fails
   on the rewrite: "No chalking 'thief' off drama" is Laura's
   pride speaking, and "ask the street" could only be said by
   someone lying beautifully.
4. **The episode now contains a designed repeatable unit** —
   "ask the street" — with a built-in Part 2 payoff. That is the
   meme mechanic as a construction, not a hope.
5. **Run the QUOTE-BACK TEST mentally:** a cold reader leaves
   with "ask the street" (quotable), names the Peach and her
   stacking, witness-invoking signature (show-off identified),
   and retells with attitude — *a cook got robbed, the clerk
   won't be stampeded, and this princess is lying through her
   bouquet and dares the whole street to back her up.* v6's
   retell, by the same reader, is a summary of a procedure.
