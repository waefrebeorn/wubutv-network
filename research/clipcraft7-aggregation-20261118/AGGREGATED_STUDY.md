## 3. Format mechanics — the frutinovela recipe as evidenced

**[BANK 2026-11-18] — VERIFIED where marked, INFERRED from the artifact
fingerprint where marked. The workflow itself is disclosed nowhere (§7);
what follows is what the artifacts prove or strongly indicate.**

1. **Still-first, image-to-video pipeline (INFERRED, high confidence).**
   Poster frames are highly composed, painterly stills with clean
   in-image text ("IM TRANS FRUIT" shirt; the LED countdowns) — the
   signature of a current image model generating a finished key still
   per scene, then an image-to-video pass animating it. This matches
   the documented genre-standard pipeline (character still → I2V with
   dialogue → assemble).
2. **Per-scene generation, loose assembly (INFERRED, high confidence).**
   X encodes vary in resolution and aspect (576×1024 → 720×1310) across
   posts of the same series — scenes are generated at model-native
   sizes and concatenated without a strict normalization/mastering
   step. Speed over polish.
3. **Dialogue/narration lives inside the clip, never in posted text
   (VERIFIED as to placement; mechanism undetermined).** Captions carry
   no story text; the genre's format is voiced scenes. Whether lines
   are model-native audio or TTS-plus-lip-sync cannot be determined
   from fetched metadata — flagged, not guessed (§7).
4. **One value change + one open rope per part (genre mechanics,
   [BANK 2026-11-18] §2c + [MINING]).** Plots are moralized telenovela
   machinery — infidelity exposed and punished, sacrifice,
   rags-to-riches, hidden crowns — executed with recurring fruit-family
   archetypes (peach mother, pineapple father, strawberry mother).
   Each part moves one value and leaves one rope open; the quote-chain
   (§2a) carries the rope forward.
5. **THE RIPENING CLOCK — the worn stake (VERIFIED — poster frame
   inspected).** The Oct 21 post (1.31M views): a pineapple father,
   strawberry mother and pineapple child in a torn bed, each wearing an
   **LED headband counting down remaining life — "5 YEARS", "3 YEARS",
   "6 MONTHS"**. A lifespan-clock premise worn on the body; the

   is not carried in a prop or a line; it is worn, readable in every
   frame, and bargained against on screen.
6. **Contrast staging: squalor vs. mansion (VERIFIED — poster frames

   evening gown (Oct 1, 1.38M). One location pair, reused, carries the
   whole class story. [MINING]: "Mansion wealth vs. the squalor of
   frame 1 is the genre's core contrast."
7. **Triple monetization of every scene (INFERRED, high confidence).**
   The expensive step (key stills) is spent once per scene; the same
   scenes are then monetized three times: serialized X parts, YouTube
   Shorts funnels, and the 30–45-min FULL STORY compilation. The
   compilation is an assembly job, not a new generation job.
8. **Farm cadence with a formula title bank (INFERRED, medium-high).**

   STORY every 2–3 days imply a premise/title rotation (secret /

## 4. Packaging law

**[BANK 2026-11-18, VERIFIED] + [MINING] — this is the layer where
clipcraft7's "writing" is fully visible, and the only layer the msg-677
mining could mine (§6).**

- **THE EMPTY CAPTION.** X captions are vestigial: either "Part N:" or
  *nothing*. The two biggest posts of the measured sample (6.5M+ views
  each) carry **empty text**. The video's poster frame is the entire
  pitch — if the frame can't pitch it alone with zero copy, it isn't
  ready.
- **Poster-frame logic.** The frame does all the work the caption
  refuses: one legible emotional extreme (misery, provocation,
  countdown dread), readable at thumbnail size, carrying the story's
  stake visibly on the body (§3.5, §3.6).
- **Title-as-funnel grammar (YouTube).** ALL-CAPS tabloid constructions
  — possessive secrets (*GRANNY'S DIRTY LITTLE SECRET*, *THE
  MILLIONAIRE'S LAST SECRET*), commodified family (*FROZEN WIFE FOR
  PROFIT*), moral abstracts (*THE BOY WITH AN HONEST HEART*),
  transformation boasts (*SHE MADE ME CEMENT, I TURNED GOLD*). The
  title is a spoiler + a moral hook; paired with literally empty
  descriptions, **the title is the entire metadata funnel**.
- **Part-numbering as the only copy.** On X, the complete caption
  vocabulary is "Part 1:", "Part 2:", and silence. Numbering is not
  decoration — combined with quote-chaining it is the series'
  navigation system (§2a).
- **Funnel shorts.** Shorts titles literally carry the destination
  ("Full Story link | https://youtu.be/…", "Go to profile for the Full
  Story🤭") — shorts convert scrollers instead of entertaining them.
