# PROPOSED AMENDMENTS — Writers Room Method (from talking-story-creators research, 2026-11-15)

Parent integrates; this file only proposes. Each amendment names the
method section it touches and the evidence behind it (full report:
`report.md` in this directory). Driver: user msg 470 — wall-to-wall
talk microwaves the viewer; story must read on the surface;
characters get smarter and question their environment.

## A1. Revised word budgets — REPLACES §3 Pass 3, item 2

The old budget (22–25 full-talk / 15–18 silence-beat / ceiling 28)
sums to 132–150 words per episode = 88–100% of runtime spent
speaking at 150 wpm. Replace with an **episode budget + unit
classes**:

- **Episode total: target 80–90 spoken words; hard cap 95.**
  (Speech fraction target 50–57% of runtime, ceiling 60%. Evidence:
  Follows 61,517-film dialogue share ≈50%; Pavlik ≈82–100
  words/runtime-minute; DCMP/BBC absorption standards.)
- **FULL-TALK unit: 17–19 words.** Max 2 per episode, never adjacent.
- **MIXED unit (the new default): 12–15 words.** Talk + action/
  reaction sharing the unit.
- **QUIET unit: 5–8 words, or 0.** At least 1 per episode.
- **SILENCE-BEAT unit: 10–13 words**, with its 2–3s gap counted
  *inside* the budget, not added on top of a full talk load.
- **Per-unit hard ceiling: 20 words** (was 28). **Per line: ≤12
  words. Per episode: ≤14 lines.**
- Method B's per-turn shape caps still bind inside these totals.
- Worked template (86 words): U1 full-talk 18 · U2 mixed 15 ·
  U3 quiet 8 · U4 full-talk 19 · U5 mixed 14 · U6 cliff 12 + hold.

## A2. Digestion-gap rule — REPLACES Pass 2's "at most one silence beat"

- **Three scripted digestion gaps per episode** (was: one), each
  **2–3s of no dialogue** — picture, face, object or room sound
  continues; never black/frozen.
- **Placement is fixed by the turns:** (i) immediately after U2's
  turn (~the 20–25s reversal), (ii) immediately after U4's peak
  turn, (iii) the **final 2–3s of U6**, holding on face/door/object
  after the cliff line — the cliff lands, then the episode breathes.
- **Plus, uncounted micro-structure:** a ~1s reaction hold after any
  punchline or reveal (production note on the unit); natural turn
  gaps left unfilled.
- Pass 2 declares each gap's placement and carrier (face / object /
  room); **Pass 4 verifies gap seconds exactly as it verifies word
  counts.** A gap with no declared carrier does not count.
- Rationale: film's normal inter-line gap is 1.9–3.7s (Follows); a
  2–3s gap is 10–15× the natural 200 ms turn gap (Stivers, PNAS), so
  it reads as meaning — spend it only on the two turns and the cliff.

## A3. NEW Gate E — story legibility (cold-reader test)

Distinct from the existing Critic *legibility test* (which checks
subtext — the unsaid sentences). Gate E checks the **surface story**.
Run twice: on the six unit summaries at Pass 1, and on the locked
draft in the Critic's cold read.

A reader who has never seen the show must state, in plain words:
1. who wanted what;
2. what blocked them;
3. what changed by U6;
4. **why it matters to someone who knows nothing about the world's
   lore** (the relatability clause — one universal stake: a first
   job, a debt, an accusation, waiting for someone, a kept object).

- Any answer that requires a lore term (an in-world mechanic, a
  name-chain, a number system) **fails the gate**.
- **Fix order on failure:** cut or replace lore lines → add a
  visible action or shared task → re-test. **Never** fix by adding
  explanation lines.
- Companion rule (Pass 1): **title-as-contract** — U1's summary must
  state place + person + want/danger in plain words, and U1's first
  line must name a visible object + action, not a lore term.
- Evidence: Domer's done-test ("watch it start to finish without
  wanting to explain anything"); every 2026 Runway winner "an idea
  you can say in a sentence"; Clark's one-sentence true stake; POV
  template's title-as-contract.

## A4. Character-intelligence / awareness beats — NEW Pass 2 requirement

Declared on the board for every episode, verified by the Critic:

1. **The questioning job.** One named character is assigned it:
   *notices* the environment is wrong by U2, *names it* in one short
   line by U4, *acts on it* in U6. (The Well's investigating hare;
   Tairell; Neural Viz's experts.) Awareness is a plot job held by
   one character, not extra chatter from everyone.
2. **Character cards.** Before Pass 3, each speaking character gets
   a 3-line card — **history** (what happened to them last episode)
   / **goal** (what they want this episode) / **entering emotion** —
   pasted verbatim into the dialogue pass context. No card, no
   lines. (SHOW-1's simulation-before-dialogue design.)
3. **One shared wrong belief.** The board declares one belief the
   cast holds that the viewer can see is wrong (dramatic irony).
   "Smart" on screen = the viewer watching intelligence operate
   inside a visible error. (Neural Viz's glurons.)
4. Awareness beats are **lines inside the A1 budgets**, not additions
   on top of them. One production glitch per episode may be written
   in-world as a character trait instead of hidden (Neural Viz).

## A5. Supporting amendments

- **Plot-pattern letters (Pass 1).** The board assigns each unit a
  letter (cast/location/storyline), e.g. A B A C A B, and the room
  alternates by letter. Six consecutive same-cast/same-room units
  fail the board review. (SHOW-1 alternation; Nothing, Forever
  segment rotation; also serves the msg-467 subject-fatigue
  calibration.)
- **One talker by default (Pass 2 fill classes).** Units default to
  a single speaking character; two-handers are declared exceptions
  and belong on the episode's tensest unit. (Big Yowie production
  economics: multi-character scenes are the failure-prone ones.)
- **Shared task (Pass 1, where format allows).** Prefer premises
  built on one visible task with a finish line (rank, count, build,
  choose, repair); the task's completion is the episode's ending,
  and lines must point at the task. (AI Presidents.)
- **Scratch-read timing (Pass 4, addition).** Alongside the counts,
  the Dialogue Writer's units are read aloud once with pauses; any
  line with no natural breath in performance loops back to Pass 3.
  Performed timing is a check, not a vibe. (Neural Viz; Dave Clark.)
- **Clippable line (Pass 3 punch-up, addition).** The punch-up list
  must include at least one line designed to stand alone out of
  context — complete, strange, quotable. (ai_sponge; Bigfoot.)
- **Repetition audit (Critic checklist, addition).** Any phrase or
  information repeated across units *without a change of meaning*
  is a located objection. Repetition with a difference is exempt —
  it is a story device. (Sunspring's 4× loop; RUPU-KK's loop-with-
  change; SHOW-1's "10,000 bowls of oatmeal".)
- **Coverage note (production handoff, addition).** Unit plans
  should name the insert/reaction coverage (speaker → object/hand
  insert → silent listener reaction) rather than assuming a 10s
  two-person dialogue master. (Graver; The Frost.)

## Explicitly NOT proposed

- Micro-drama dialogue density (180–360 words/episode, "no dead
  air") — a paywall-retention technology that reproduces the exact
  complaint; 0.16% hit rate in its own market. Its *event* cadence
  (hook ≤3s, one event, turn ~20–25s, cliff at the end) is already
  compatible with our board practice and needs no amendment.
- Any "pattern interrupt every X seconds" rule — no citable
  measured basis exists.
