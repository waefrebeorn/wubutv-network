## Strongest devices

1. **The TV anchor is a third character.** The news broadcast runs inside the
   scene the whole time — the world's verdict on the plea is visible on a
   screen behind the actors, no narrator needed.
2. **Status is posture.** He is kneeling in nearly every frame; she stands,
   arms crossed. You can mute it and still read who is losing.
3. **Karaoke word captions as a metronome.** One word at a time forces the
   eye to the speaker's mouth and makes even nonsense lines feel like moves.
