## The diagnosis — why the lines aren't dialogue

The current lines fall into two narration classes. Both fail the same test: a narrator could say them over the picture.

**INVENTORY — describes what's in frame:**
- "One set. Taller than its tower." / "The set raised its tower." / "The tower matched its set." (CHAIN-290, MEGA)
- "Thirteen fruits. Twelve numbers. One loose." (CHAIN-294, fruit)

**APHORISM — states a station truth to nobody:**
- "The roster keeps everyone." (CHAIN-292 / p373)
- "Some counts stay true unfinished." (CHAIN-294)
- "The aisle kept its fruit." (CHAIN-288)

In both classes nobody wants anything from anybody. Real dialogue, from the soap research, works the opposite way: characters talk past each other, presume shared gossip, fear exposure, answer a question with a question, manipulate by planting suspicion, and dodge the real issue by bickering about the safe surface. The line's job is not to inform the viewer — the hook already did that. The line's job is to DO something to the other character.

## The narrator test (binding for Q0 + Q1)

If a narrator could say the line over the frame, it is not dialogue. Rewrite until the line only works spoken TO someone who can answer back. Every drafted line must name, in the draft, its addressee and what the speaker wants from them. The hook shows the fact; the line never restates it.

## The three pool moves

**SOAP move — talk past each other about the safe thing.** The real thing (an erasure, a sale, an exposure) sits underneath and is never named directly. Answer a question with a demand; answer a demand with a counter-question. The withheld fact does the work. Soap scenes run on the threat of a slip: someone almost says the thing, then doesn't.

**FRUIT move — talk about the fruit to avoid talking about the count.** The count is the sale (Peter sold it as an audience); the fruit is the excuse. The dodge IS the dialogue. A character who answers straight has nothing to hide and no scene. Every fruit line should be a swerve away from the number.

**MEGA move — scale as status.** Argue about who built what first, who outranks whom, who takes credit. The set/tower is the excuse; the credit is the fight. A MEGA line is a boast, a claim, or a command about rank — never a measurement.
