# "THE STATION" — PIECE 01, three parts (30 s) — proof of the new grammar

Written to the grammar in `findings/04_PRODUCTION_GRAMMAR.md` and the retention
numbers in `findings/03_ALGO_MAX_10S_STORY.md`. No lore required, no on-screen
text, dialogue in every part, one withheld fact per part, and the cliff frame of
each part is the first frame of the next.

**Piece logline:** the station has one viewer and Peter has sold an advertiser ten
thousand. **Cast hues (locked):** UNC = INK + CADMIUM · LAURA = FOREST · PETER =
VERMILION · THE COLONEL = COBALT.

---

## PART 1 — "ONE" (the sell)

```
SHOT & CAMERA: medium shot, low angle, ONE snap-zoom in on the board at 1.5 s.
SUBJECTS: UNC (INK hoodie, CADMIUM ~ WuBu ~ wordmark, station manager) standing
          under a large ratings board; PETER (VERMILION blazer, clipboard) entering
          frame right; the board itself (BONE face, INK digits).
ACTION:
  (0-1.5s)  HOOK FRAME - the ratings board fills the top of frame with a single
            CADMIUM-lit digit 1; UNC below it, small, looking up.
  (1.5-4s)  STATE      - UNC points one finger at the 1; PETER walks in and slaps
            a clipboard onto the desk.
  (4-7s)    TURN       - PETER taps the contract twice; a printed bar reading
            10,000 in VERMILION ink.
  (7-9s)    LINE       - PETER: "I sold ten thousand."
  (9-10s)   CLIFF FRAME - the board's 1 flickers and drops to 0. Hold.
LIGHT: one hard CADMIUM source from above the board, everything else flat shadow.
AUDIO: one clipboard slap at 4 s; native dialogue as written above.
STYLE: BIG PRIMARY - CADMIUM + VERMILION, BONE board, INK everything else; flat
       fills, ink outline on every edge, 1970s broadcast texture.
TEXT: NONE on screen except the board's digits (diegetic, one digit and one number).
NEGATIVE: NO fisheye, NO plastic skin, NO commercial gloss, NO titles, NO subtitles,
          NO watermark, NO third primary, NO gradients, NO crowd, NO second voice,
          NO lip movement outside 7-9 s.
```

## PART 2 — "TWO" (the fix)

```
SHOT & CAMERA: over-the-shoulder medium, ONE push-in during the pull.
SUBJECTS: LAURA (FOREST hoodie, new hire) at a big VERMILION knife-switch lever;
          the CADMIUM ratings board above; UNC behind her, arms folded.
ACTION:
  (0-1.5s)  HOOK FRAME - LAURA's hand gripping a big red lever against a CADMIUM
            backlit wall: the question is what happens when she pulls it.
  (1.5-4s)  STATE      - she looks up at the board (still 0), then at UNC; he
            jerks his chin at the lever once.
  (4-7s)    TURN       - she hauls the lever down; every light in frame dies for
            one beat, then the board's 0 flickers to 2.
  (7-9s)    LINE       - LAURA: "That's two."
  (9-10s)   CLIFF FRAME - tight on the board's 2: the second digit is PETER's
            reflection in the glass. Hold.
LIGHT: one hard CADMIUM from the board; the lever's VERMILION the only warm object.
AUDIO: one heavy breaker CLUNK at 4 s; native dialogue as written.
STYLE: BIG PRIMARY - CADMIUM + FOREST with the VERMILION lever; flat fills, ink
       outlines, broadcast texture.
TEXT: NONE on screen except the digital board.
NEGATIVE: (same as part 1)
```

## PART 3 — "THE AUDIENCE" (the reveal)

```
SHOT & CAMERA: wide, ONE slow dolly left; no cut, no zoom.
SUBJECTS: the studio door (VERMILION), a row of empty BONE chairs, UNC holding the
          door open, THE COLONEL (COBALT uniform) seated at the anchor desk beyond.
ACTION:
  (0-1.5s)  HOOK FRAME - one empty chair in a dark studio swivels slowly to face
            the lens. The question: who turned it?
  (1.5-4s)  STATE      - UNC pushes the studio door open, filling frame left with
            a slab of CADMIUM light; the chairs are all empty.
  (4-7s)    TURN       - a man in a grey suit walks past UNC and sits in the
            swivelled chair - he is the advertiser; the studio audience is him.
  (7-9s)    LINE       - THE COLONEL, off-frame: "We're on."
  (9-10s)   CLIFF FRAME - the ON AIR lamp above the door snaps from dark to
            VERMILION; everyone in frame turns toward the lens. Hold.
LIGHT: CADMIUM door-light, one VERMILION practical (the ON AIR lamp), COBALT desk.
AUDIO: one chair squeak at 0 s; one ON AIR relay thunk at 9 s; the line at 7-9 s.
STYLE: BIG PRIMARY - VERMILION + CADMIUM with COBALT; flat fills, ink outlines.
TEXT: NONE on screen (the ON AIR lamp reads as a lamp, not as a caption).
NEGATIVE: (same as part 1) plus NO audience except the one seated man.
```

## Why this shape works (check it against the data, not against taste)

- **Hook frames with a question in them**: a lone digit 1; a hand on a lever; a
  chair turning by itself. Each is legible in 1.5 s, sound off, no prior lore.
- **A visible change every 1.5 s**: digit flicker, clipboard slap, tap, lever pull,
  lights cut, door open, man sits, lamp changes — five-plus events per part.
- **One line per part**, 2–3 words, spoken by a named character inside 7–9 s.
- **The chain**: part 1 ends on the 0 that part 2 opens on; part 2 ends on Peter's
  reflection that part 3 explains; part 3 ends on the ON AIR lamp that piece 02
  opens on.
- **30 s shipping unit** = three parts = inside the Shorts/Reels watch-time window.
