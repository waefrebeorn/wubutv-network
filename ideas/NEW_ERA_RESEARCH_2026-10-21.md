# NEW ERA RESEARCH — fruit channels, Tung Tung Sahur, and the coherent-media lane

**Run:** 2026-10-21 by the CEO office. Seven Kevin Bacon chains, six-degree style:
each starts at something the network already owns, hops through primary or
academic sources, and ends on a production decision. Nothing here is a mood
board — every chain terminates in an instruction the Producer can execute.

**Why this run.** Era I built a factory: nine channels, 1,300+ ten-second
masters, a wheel that never stops. The question put to the CEO office is what
the **new era** is. The answer is in the two lanes that ate 2025–2026 — the fruit
dramas and the brainrot bestiary — because both got enormous reach on signal we
are not yet using: **a music bed, a serial spine, and a nameable sound.**

---

## Chain 1 — THE FRUIT DRAMAS: what the format actually is

**Start:** the network's own serial instinct (numbered episodes, callback canon).

1. **The academic record.** Riccio, *"I Was Scrolling and Then I Saw a Pregnant
   Strawberry"* (arXiv:2606.09589, 08 Jun 2026, Univ. of Amsterdam) names the
   form **AI minidramas / fruit dramas**: short, algorithmically distributed
   generative-AI video series with anthropomorphised characters.
2. **The anatomy, measured.** Her observed series ran **8–9 episodes**, new
   episode **daily from the same account**, then the account vanished from her
   feed. Plot vocabulary is standardised: love, betrayal, cheating, paternity.
3. **The craft under the slop.** The "aesthetic laundering" thesis: softness,
   roundness and cuteness neutralise ideological weight and slip past moderation
   — the aesthetic is a *delivery mechanism*, not an accident.
4. **The political proof of the format.** During her observation window a fruit
   drama was posted on the **official TikTok account of Lega** (Salvini's party).
   When a party press office adopts a format, the format is furniture, not a fad.
5. **The criticism.** Clarke, *"Rotten on Arrival: AI Fruit Dramas and the Death
   of Taste"* (Savage Minds, 06 Apr 2026) catalogues the content: affairs,
   eugenic "perfect genes" talk, women thrown out of windows as plot device,
   every clip scored by the same melancholic track, and the moral collapse of
   treating synthetic misogyny as a twist.
6. **The tell.** Both the academic and the polemic describe the *same* engine:
   a tiny fixed cast, one bed, daily drops, and a cliff per episode.

**Production decision (the network).** Take the serial spine, refuse the
content. Era II ships **SERIAL-<n>**: 8–9 units, one channel, one arc, one
recurring prop, dropped daily in order. The network already has the cast (UNC,
the Colonel, Laura, Peter), the props (penny, token, pallet, blank card) and the
callback grammar. What it does not have is **order** — a viewer who must come
back tomorrow.

**Music decision.** One bed per serial, never per clip. The bed is the serial's
signature; a returning viewer should recognise the arc from bar one, exactly the
way the fruit lane's single reused track became its identity.

## Chain 2 — THE BED IS THE BRAND: the sound did the work the picture couldn't

**Start:** Chain 1's repeated observation — "the background music is almost
always the same".

1. **Riccio footnote 11** points at a single recurring YouTube track used across
   unrelated accounts; her corpus note is that the bed is "almost always the
   same."
2. **Clarke identifies it** as the same melancholic song across the whole feed —
   in his account the fruit dramas are backed by one track, *Kamin*, and he
   reads the mismatch between that earnest bed and the absurd plot as the
   unintentional joke.
3. **The consequence.** Those channels had **no visual identity** (same rounded
   AI look as everyone else) yet were legible as a genre in under a second.
   The only distinguishing asset they owned was **audio**.
4. **The inverse lesson for us.** A 10-second clip with native audio carries at
   most one sound event. A network carries a *key*. Nine channels × one bed each
   = nine recognisable rooms, at zero generation cost, forever.

**Production decision.** `MUSIC_TAG` becomes a required field in the manifest
alongside title/tagline/channel: every shipped master names its bed and its
sting. A clip without a music identity is a clip from the factory era.

**Music decision.** Build the **nine channel stings** (below, in the doctrine)
as the first hard deliverable of Era II — 3–6 s each, one per channel, derived
from one shared interval so they are siblings.

## Chain 3 — TUNG TUNG TUNG SAHUR: the character is a sound

**Start:** the network's own bells-and-pips grammar (B-178 pips, the mast bells,
the count rhythm).

1. **Provenance (primary).** Wikipedia, *Tung Tung Tung Sahur*: created
   **28 February 2025** — the day before Ramadan began in Indonesia — by TikTok
   user **Fernanda Bagas Indrastata ("Noxa")**, using **DALL-E**, from prompts
   including "draw a *pentungan sahur* with a face and legs", "neighborhood watch
   post *kentungan*", "a patrol *pentungan* with a single human leg". Seven
   prompts produced the canonical image (NPR, 21 Aug 2026).
2. **What the object is.** An anthropomorphic **kentongan** — an Indonesian
   wooden slit drum struck during Ramadan to wake the village for **sahur**, the
   pre-dawn meal. It carries a drumstick (sometimes rendered as a baseball bat).
3. **The name is onomatopoeia.** *Tung tung tung* is the sound the kentongan
   makes. Monteanni (Algorithmic Folklore / ALGOFOLK, Univ. of Bergen, 15 Apr
   2026) reads the name as indexing "mosque-centred timekeeping, communal
   patrols, and the sacred temporality of Ramadan" — with an online instance of
   the chant dating to a **2013 tweet**, a decade before the character.
4. **The genre detach.** It was filed under *Italian brainrot* though it is
   Indonesian; Monteanni's point is that "Italianness" is a **phonetic costume**
   (open vowels, `-ini`/`-ello` suffixes), cheap to reproduce at scale. The
   character then outlived the genre once the costume stopped mattering.
5. **The proof of durability.** Wikipedia: as Italian brainrot subsided, Sahur
   persisted through shitposting, Fortnite, and the Roblox game *Steal a
   Brainrot*. It crossed from meme to furniture.
6. **Six degrees back to us.** Our pips already *are* a religious-temporal
   counting device turned broadcast furniture (the Greenwich six-pip chain is in
   `ideas/IDEAS_BANK.md`). We have been staging the Sahur mechanism for months
   without naming a character after it.

**Production decision.** Cast **THE PIPS**: a character whose *name is a sound
and whose job is time*. Not a meme likeness — no bat, no stick-man, no template.
A network-native object (the mast bell / the seven-pip count) that appears when a
count is wrong. Gaps never explained, per the LORE rule.

**Music decision.** The Pips character's entrance *is* music: three struck notes
and a held fourth, sourced from the same interval family as the channel stings.
The anthem's count section ("You count it out and chalk the whole count on the
line") is the first appearance of that grammar in song.

## Chain 4 — SENSOnarrative POWER: circulation comes from the senses

**Start:** the network's deadpan, anti-hype voice.

1. **The concept.** Monteanni proposes **sensonarrative power**: "the force
   resulting from sensorial encounters that generate affection, interest, and
   narrative practices." Circulation and replication do **not** derive from the
   genre's formal qualities but from what its material instantiations *do to us
   sensorially* when met in the wild.
2. **What that means concretely.** A resonant *tung*, a rolling open-vowel name,
   a glossy mid-tier render — the encounter is the engine, not the story.
3. **The folkloric layer.** Wikipedia notes fans built their own stories,
   described as internet folklore with "overly dramatic vocal delivery and
   increasingly exaggerated versions" — i.e. the audience completes the mythos.
4. **The Korean media-studies read** (KCI, *Simulacral Operations of AI Memes*)
   analyses Tralalero Tralala, Bombardiro Crocodilo and Sahur through Baudrillard:
   production/distribution, originality/reproduction, contextuality — the meme
   works as a copy with no original.
5. **The trap for us.** Sensorially-empty work cannot borrow this power. Our
   house style is intentional deadpan: a held still, a filing line, no reaction.
   That is anti-sensorial by design, which is why it reads as *paperwork* and not
   as *folklore*.

**Production decision.** Keep the deadpan as the **face**, add the sensorial as
the **body**: one hard, deliberate sound per piece (a strike, a latch, a clunk)
and one texture (teal-orb pulse light, the Kept Gap breathing). "The room was
found" is the line; the sound of the room is the hook.

**Music decision.** Native sound design is not "music added later" — Era II
mixes the *diegetic* strike into the bed's rhythm so the picture's own sound is
on beat. That is the one place a dub is legal: we never replace native dialogue,
but a struck object landing on the bar is the network's sonic signature.

## Chain 5 — PUBLIC DOMAIN BY CONSTRUCTION: nobody owns the character, so own the system

**Start:** the network's ledger — "a number with a finished clip behind it is a
fact."

1. **The lawsuit (NPR, 21 Aug 2026, *All Things Considered*).** A legal battle
   in a California courtroom: DoBig Studios (the *Steal a Brainrot* maker) vs
   **Mementum Lab**, a French startup representing young creators of brainrot
   characters. At stake: whether the characters can be used in games "without
   paying or acknowledging their creators" — potentially many millions.
2. **The legal status.** Wikipedia (both articles): because Sahur originates from
   an AI-generated image, it is **ineligible for copyright protection** and is
   therefore a **public domain** character; Mementum's claim led to his removal
   from *Steal a Brainrot*, and proceedings continue.
3. **The strategic inversion.** The creators cannot own the character. So the
   value cannot sit in the character. It sits in the **container** — the game,
   the series, the schedule, the canon, the archive.
4. **Our asset is the archive.** 1,300 masters with a manifest, a canon file,
   a learning log, an episode ledger, nine channel specs, a numbering system,
   and a media dataset under the Umbrella License v3.0. That is ownable,
   licensable, and provable. A single AI-designed mascot is not.
5. **The kid-feed harm evidence** (The Cool Down, summarising 2026 research)
   warns AI slop on children's feeds may hide violence and fuel cognitive
   decline. Being *not that* is a market position, and it must be stated, not
   assumed.

**Production decision.** Write and publish the **COHERENCE CONTRACT** — a short,
public, checkable page: native audio only / never dub / one callback max / cast
lock / wordmark gate / retirement is filing, not deletion / a number with a
finished clip behind it is a fact. Put the ledger on screen at the end of every
outlet block. Own the frame, not the mascot.

**Music decision.** The anthem is the licensable asset. `suno_prompt.txt` is the
prompt; the *delivered* file plus lyric sheet is the thing that can be licensed,
covered, and re-scored. Keep lyric sheets in-repo for every MV.

## Chain 6 — STEAL A BRAINROT: the container captured the value

**Start:** the network's outlet block (30 min = two 12.5-min episode blocks +
two 2.5-min commercial pods).

1. **Scale.** *Steal a Brainrot* (SpyderSammy / DoBig Studios, Roblox, released
   16 May 2025) is an idle multiplayer game about buying and stealing brainrot
   characters. Wikipedia: the **only Roblox game to pass 25 million concurrent
   users**, second to pass 20M after *Grow a Garden*.
2. **The mechanism.** Value is created by **the rule set** (buy, place, steal,
   defend) plus social stakes — viral clips are of *children losing their
   Brainrots*. The characters are interchangeable inventory; the container is
   what prints.
3. **The legal consequence.** That success is exactly what triggered Chain 5's
   suit — the container made the free characters expensive.
4. **The parallel to us.** Bumpers are our characters; the **outlet block** is
   our container. Nobody watches a bumper; a 30-minute block with pods, order and
   an ident is a *thing you put on*. We have the block assembled (`outlet/`) and
   have not been treating it as the product.
5. **Cadence as format.** The fruit lane's daily drop and the idle game's
   always-on server are the same idea: presence, not hits.

**Production decision.** Ship the **container** in Era II: one 30-min outlet
block per week with a cold open, three serial arcs, commercial pods whose
fictional sponsors recur, and a closing ledger card. Sell/position the block and
the schedule, not the individual clip.

**Music decision.** The block needs a **theme-and-variations**: the anthem's
hook appears as the cold open, as a 3-second sting between arcs, and as the
credit bed. One song, four jobs — that is how a network sounds like one thing.

## Chain 7 — THE COHERENT LANE: slop has no definition, so define it against us

**Start:** the network's own NON-SLOP rule.

1. **Slop resists definition.** Kommers et al. (Jan 2026, cited in Wikipedia's
   *AI slop* article) argue a precise boundary between slop and non-slop is
   impossible, while identifying prototype features.
2. **The folk definition is the useful one.** *"Defective, Amplified, Contested:
   Folk Conceptualizations of AI Slop"* (arXiv:2610.14231) derives a user-driven
   definition: slop is "audience-contested AI-generated content characterized by
   **constitutional defects** and enabled by **platform logics**."
3. **Unpack that.** *Constitutional defect* = the work is broken at the root
   (hands, continuity, sound, plot logic). *Platform logic* = it exists to feed
   an algorithm, not an audience. Two tests, both runnable.
4. **Our position.** WuBuTV passes test one by construction (native audio, cast
   lock, numbered ledger, QC gates) and **fails test two by default** — we are
   algorithm-shaped too. So the era's claim must be narrower and honest: *we are
   coherent, we are on the record, and we show our paperwork.* Not "we are not
   slop"; rather "you can check us."
5. **The measurement consequence.** If coherence is the product, it has to be
   countable: continuity tags per master, callback count per piece (max one),
   cast-lock verification per frame, duplicate/static audits. Numbers, not
   adjectives.

**Production decision.** The **COHERENCE CONTRACT** (Chain 5) plus a public
**coherence score** per outlet block: percent of masters with verified cast lock,
percent with a music tag, retirements filed, callbacks per piece. Publish the
number even when it is bad.

**Music decision.** The lyric sheet becomes a checked artifact, not a mood:
`suno_qa` (boxes, tags, bar maths, lexicon, grade), `prosody` (meter), `vowels`
(buried chains). The anthem in `musicvideo/MV-1_ON_FILE/` already passes all of
them — 334 words, 98% monosyllables, 6.9% second person, 48 declared bars
matching the 48-bar 96.000 s grid.
