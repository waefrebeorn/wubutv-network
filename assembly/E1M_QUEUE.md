# E1M Queue — THE RAPID MINUTE

Editorial identity: PUNCHY, HIGH-CONTRAST, COMEDY-FORWARD.
Six units, sixty seconds, no breathing room. Every unit must land a beat
or a turn — the minute should feel like six punches in sequence. Favor
comedy turns, sharp visual gags, quick lore hits. Contrast between adjacent
units is load-bearing: cut hard, never drift. This is the minute for
scrollers — grab, turn, release, repeat.

NOT the same style as the Slow Build (E2M). These queues never share units.
