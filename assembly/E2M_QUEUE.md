# E2M Queue — THE SLOW BUILD

Editorial identity: LORE-HEAVY, ATMOSPHERIC, SUSTAINED MOOD.
Twelve units, two minutes, one unbroken atmosphere. Units should accumulate
like chapters — a place, a rule, a history — rather than punch. Favor
hallway shots, paperwork, tallies, held objects, rooms that imply a world
beyond the frame. Transitions should feel like turning pages in the same
ledger. This is the two minutes for watchers — settle in, the world deepens.

NOT the same style as the Rapid Minute (E1M). These queues never share units.

Reserved B-numbers in assembly order (12 per episode). The assembler consumes
from the top and refills from the freshest unposted bumpers.
