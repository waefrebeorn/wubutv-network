## Marks deliberately NOT filed (and why)

- **The nine channel words alone** (NEWS, ORIGINALS, SATIRE, LABS, ARENA, MEDIA
  SCHOOL, PUBLIC ACCESS, PUPPET THEATER, ARCHIVE, NETWORK). These are generic or
  descriptive for the services; a standalone filing is money burned. If protection
  is wanted, file the **composites** actually used (`WuBu News` etc.) or rely on
  the `WuBuTV` mark plus trade dress.
- **Serial titles** (`SERIAL-1 "THE UNFILED CLAIM"`, `"THE PRODUCE CLAIM"`). A
  title of a single work is not registrable as a mark; it is protected, if at all,
  by the series-of-titles rule only once there is a series. Copyright and the
  ledger cover it. Revisit if a title becomes a franchise.
- **`SERIAL-<n>` numbering syntax.** Not a mark; it is a format. Formats are
  protected by being first, not by registration.
