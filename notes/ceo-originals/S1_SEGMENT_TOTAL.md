# SERIAL-1 — THE TOTAL COHERENT SEGMENT, units 2-8 (fire-ready)

**Issued by the CEO Director, 2026-10-21.** Three owner orders in one deliverable:
park the filings, make **total coherent segments** in soap-opera style with the
characters, and design them in **big primary colour**.

- Arc + cast: the Producer's `serials/SERIAL-1_PLAN.md` (unchanged — I am not
  rewriting Muse's serial, I am making it filmable and coherent end to end).
- Visual language: `notes/PRIMARY_COLOR_DESIGN.md` (six colours, two primaries per
  frame, flat fills, colour as information).
- S1-1 "The Tray" already SHIPPED (md5 `96e48b33d78bcae2bef2401803f5cdb0`) — these
  seven units continue it without contradicting a frame of it.

## What "total coherent segment" means here, concretely

Not eight unrelated 10s pieces. One **80-second segment** with three acts, in
which the ONLY variable is the number of blank cards:

| Act | Units | Colour field | Card count | What the audience learns |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I — the arrival | S1-1, S1-2, S1-3 | COBALT room | 1 → 1 → 2 | the object has a paperwork life apart from its owner |
| II — the wrong count | S1-4, S1-5, S1-6 | COBALT → VERMILION threshold → INK night | 3 → 3 → 4 | counting is a ritual that produces the wrong answer |
| III — the refusal | S1-7, S1-8 | BONE macro → COBALT shelf | 4 → full | the building was always filing this same blank |

**Continuity contract, all 8 units:** the same vermilion tray at the same hue
(±4/channel), the same bone card stock with the same cobalt rule, one bed for the
whole serial (`bed=serial-1-orig`, sting `orig`), the CADMIUM light never warming
anything except UNC's wordmark, the stamp handle and THE PIPS. The cliff
composition is identical in every unit: **object held in the lower third against a
drained field, final 0.5 s.** That repetition is the serial's button.

**The soap engine, translated and kept at full gravity:** custody (love), the
count is wrong (betrayal), a second identical card (cheating), who filed first
(paternity). No cheating-as-twist, no paternity shame, no violence, no synthetic
misogyny — the melodrama is played straight over an object that does not deserve
it, and that inversion is the comedy.

## Assembly spec (the segment, not the clips)

```
concat 8 x 10s = 80.000 s, stream copy, in order           -> fades: none
bed: bed=serial-1-orig under the full 80 s, ducked 4 dB under the directed lines

loudness: loudnorm to the network target at block level, never per clip
no added voice, no added music over any speaking frame
```

- one voice event, inside 7.5-8.75 s, no open mouth outside that window
- one visible action at the specified second; 6-tile the object and COUNT IT
