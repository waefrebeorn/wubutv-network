# WuBuTV canon (distilled, 2026-10-12)

## The conceit
"The room was real, the crowd was generated, and this is the paperwork."
The network documents a world that believes itself entirely ordinary.

## Motifs (standing set)
- **Blank surfaces** — cards, walls, labels, tape that refuse information.
- **Held objects** — lanterns, keys, crates, cloths, staffs; the world is handled.
- **Paperwork** — certificates, logs, ledgers, claim tags, signature slips.
- **Tally/counting** — chalk marks, pips, door counts, working days, stations.
- **Bells and pips** — the seven-pip grammar; the four-minute warning.
- **The Kept Gap** — a measured gap in a slab joint that breathes (O-32);
  a motif of tolerances held deliberately.
- **The Framed Blank** — an empty frame hung on purpose (B-475).
- **The Spare** — a spare of everything; nothing is ever the only one.

## Lore
- **The MEGA** — the well of raw footage; the uncut inventory of the world.
- **Storage units** — numbered, rented, occasionally not opened (O-3).
- **The vending machine** — dispenses the correct item for the wrong reason.
- **The Corrections Desk** — where every mistake is filed in triplicate.
- **The ∞:0 scoreboard** — real to the Colonel, meaningless to the viewer;
  never explain both worlds.
- **The Re-Mark Crew** — repaints faded lines at night (L-9).
- **The Draw Rule** — some things are marked so they can be re-marked.
- **The Post Rule** — posted items are not returned; they are re-filed.
- **Pilot Rule / Circuit Rule** — every new channel is a pilot first;
  every pilot completes a circuit or it never aired.
- **The Five Stations** — the trust economy between desks (N-50).
- **The Night Rounds** — the watchclock tape proves the walk (O-35).
- **The Ninety Days** — the archive's reckoning window (A-7/A-10).

## Characters
- **UNC** — heavyset Black man, large rounded afro, thick mustache/goatee,
  BLACK hoodie, `~ WuBu ~` in yellow/gold. Never reacts. Mouth moves only
  on directed lines. Host of the world's paperwork.
- **The Colonel** — stocky, grey/white beard, navy dress uniform, news desk.
  Grave, composed, ironic in the words. Deadpan is wrong for him.
  Host of @WuBuNews.
- **Laura** — dark bob, forest-green/black unlettered hoodie. The ONLY
  non-deadpan character: she reacts; the contrast is the engine.
- **Peter** — balding, round glasses, handlebar mustache, mustard polo,
  green shorts. Enthusiasm with no evidence.
- **The Wizard** — sheet-authoritative; never improvised from text.
- **Teal orb** — speaks by pulsing once; never a mouth.
- **CloudWu** — bumper-only until a reference exists.
