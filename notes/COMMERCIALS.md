# Commercials — network-native ad doctrine (2026-10-12)

## Doctrine
Commercials are 10 seconds each, same unit as everything else. They sell
fictional, network-native products and services only — never real brands,
never licensed material. A commercial is a slice-of-life beat in which
the product is doing its ordinary job in the world. UNC may hold the
product; he never endorses it aloud unless the line is directed. The full
10 seconds are used: setup at 0–4s, beat at 4–6s, logo still at 9.5s.
