# THE FRUIT-SOAP FORM — what we learned, what we refuse, how it maps to canon

Learned form (from the Era II research, `ideas/NEW_ERA_RESEARCH_2026-10-21.md`
chains 1–4), reduced to rules the factory can actually execute:

1. **A series, not a clip.** 8–9 units. Daily, in order, from one channel. The
   fruit lane's observed series ran 8–9 episodes then the account vanished —
   the arc ENDS. Ours ends on purpose and hands to the next serial.
2. **One fixed cast, never grown mid-arc.** A claimant, an object of dispute, a
   witness, counsel, and the desk. No new character may be introduced to solve a
   plot problem; the arc must pay with what it opened with.
3. **A cliff per unit, paid by the next.** Each unit withholds exactly one fact.
   The next unit's first beat must spend that fact. A unit that withholds two
   facts is a bad unit; a unit that withholds none is a bumper.
4. **A status object, not a secret.** The genre runs on a sticker, a scale
   reading, a card, a signature — paperwork, not confessions. This is the fruit
   lane's actual engine (paternity == provenance) and it is already our canon.
5. **The deadpan inversion is the comedy.** The fruit lane plays melodrama
   straight, which is why critics read it as unintentional horror. We play the
   SAME beats with the SAME gravity over an object that does not deserve it.
   Nobody raises a voice; the claim form is treated like a custody hearing.
6. **One bed, one sting, per serial.** The bed identifies the arc in bar one.
7. **Native audio is inviolable.** The fruit lane's synthetic voiceover is the
   part we do NOT take. Dialogue is the generator's own; the Suno track is the
   THEME (card + sting + end card), never a dub over a speaking frame.

What we refuse (from the criticism, cited in the research file):
- No synthetic misogyny: no cheating-as-twist, no paternity shame, no violence as
  a plot device, no "perfect genes" talk.
- No licensed characters, no meme likeness, no stick-man, no bat, no template.
- No talking heads: every beat is an ACTION on an object (stamp, peel, weigh,
  square, file). A unit where two characters only speak is a failed unit.

  count) + the CORRECTIONS DESK.

- Callbacks: max one per unit. The penny, the clip board, the drawer of blank

- Tone register: PUBLIC ACCESS (cable-access chaos) — the serial is a show
  INSIDE the network, which is why fruit characters are allowed in the building.
