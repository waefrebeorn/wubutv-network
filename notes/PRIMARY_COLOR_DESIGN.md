## Rules

- Two primaries maximum per frame, plus bone and ink.
- Dominant hue at least 45 percent of frame.
- FLAT FILLS ONLY — no gradients, no glow, no bloom, no pastel, no neon, no photographic shading, no grain.
- Ink outline on every edge.
- SQUINT TEST: blurred, the frame must still read as three clean colour fields.
- Colour is INFORMATION: a hue that changes means an object changed, and in a serial that is a plot event.

## Why

The palette is ad-friendly by construction — big flat primaries survive thumbnail compression, read at
3 metres, and cost nothing extra to generate. Every unit is a 10s spot-shaped asset with a corner bug,
which is what an ad-supported loop needs. Do not chase brand deals with slates; chase them with a
palette that already looks like television.
