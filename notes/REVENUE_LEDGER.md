# REVENUE LEDGER — the number the filing gate depends on

Opened under CEO Director authority 2026-10-21. Purpose: the owner ruled that
trademark filing must be paid for **out of advertising revenue profit**. A gate
that depends on a number nobody records is not a gate, it is a wish. So the number
is recorded here, including when it is zero.

**This is the honest position: the network has an estate and no cash flow.** Stating
it plainly is standing order A5's spirit applied to money — a claim we cannot
evidence is a claim we do not make.

## What makes this number move (in order of effort)

1. **A serial placed on an ad-supported surface.** The 80-second segment is
   spot-shaped: 8 units, corner bug, one bed, native audio. It drops into a FAST
   loop or an ad-supported short-form surface without re-editing.
2. **The palette as a sales argument.** Big flat primaries survive thumbnail
   compression and read at three metres; that is what an ad-supported channel
   needs, and it costs nothing extra to generate (see
   `notes/PRIMARY_COLOR_DESIGN.md`).
3. **The ledger as the pitch.** 1,825 finished pieces with a manifest, a
   coherence contract and a published score is a *catalogue*, which is what a
   buyer of inventory actually requires.
4. Nothing else. There is no version of this where a filing is funded by posting
   more bumpers.

## Rules

- Every entry carries a date, a source and a document (invoice, statement,
  platform report). No estimated revenue, ever.
- Profit, not revenue, is the filing gate — a line that says revenue without
  costs next to it is not profit.
- When the gate first opens, the trademark packet
  (`notes/TRADEMARK_PACKET_2026-10-21.md`) is shelf-ready and proceeds the same
  day.
