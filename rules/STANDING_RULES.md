## The six non-negotiables (break any and it is not a WuBu cut)
1. **Never dub over footage that carries its own sound.** Commentary lives in the
   edit/assembly layer. No TTS laid over native audio. Ever.
2. **No static-image + pan on the main screen.** Real motion only. A still may cover
   a hold; it may not fake a scene. Push-in only, never pan.
3. **No licensed footage or characters.** Gumball, Kipo, SpongeBob, any franchise face.
   Check every frame, including background audience shapes.
4. **On-screen text only when diegetic** (a monitor/TV a character watches).
   Title cards are full-frame, never burned over picture.
5. **`~ WuBu ~` corner bug on every frame** — the sole brand mark.
6. **Native audio only.** If a generation's audio is unusable, cut the beat SILENT.
   Do not paper over it.
