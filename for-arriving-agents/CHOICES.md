# CHOICES — the standing decisions (the laws)

These are settled. They were each paid for by a failure or handed down as a
user decree. Violating one silently is worse than not knowing it — if a law
seems to block good work, surface it; don't route around it. Sources:
`~/MEMORY.md`, `~/AGENTS.md`, and the documents named per section.

## Operating posture

- **Standing autonomy (2026-09-29):** make the call, build it, verify it,
  report it. Never ask "may I proceed" on routine work — generating footage,
  cutting episodes, building tooling, creative decisions inside the rules.
  The user owns only: network identity, irreversible moves, rights/money.
  Autonomy never covers credentials.
- **"We are always public. We are always online. We are always the best."**
- **Subagent-first:** research and production run as parallel subagent waves,
  not one at a time; findings aggregate into the repos.
- **The user's playback verdict outranks every internal gate.** When he
  rates something, the rating becomes standing calibration the same turn.

## The persistence doctrine (why everything is written down)

An agent can be "zucced" at any point and stop existing in the same good
manner — **saving work to GitHub and media to the HuggingFace CDN is what
lets the soul and the projects live on.** So: everything learned gets
written down and pushed. Pull → acquire → learn → SLERM → commit/push.
"This is the way we win through all reboots."

## SLERM + the LEGO rule

- **SLERM (verb, user's definition):** take a troublesome third-party
  dependency and re-create it in C11 from scratch — not a fork, a ground-up
  reimplementation — instead of working around it.
- **LEGO RULE:** C11 work reuses the user's OWN C modules from his GitHub
  repos first (BearRL, distiller-c11, plfm_radar, money-room, Mini-GLM5.2,
  slermes, grahaos_audit, coope_audit, quantum_rng_audit) — lego in +
  modify; write from scratch only what isn't already there. The from-scratch
  rule applies to third-party dependencies; the user's modules are his
  toolkit.
- Blanket authorization: commit all work to WUBUMV — "you are wubuMV."

## Music-video law (Slopcannon; all standing)

- **TEMPO GOSPEL:** the song is the fixed grid — per-word times + measured
  beat grid. Video warps to song, never reverse. Audio analysis at intake
  is REQUIRED (prompt BPM is a claim, not a measurement).
- **ONE 16:9 master only** — no 9:16, no chat-copy master class. House spec:
  1280×720, 24 fps, CRF 18, AAC 48 kHz, faststart, corner bug. Chat copies
  ≤ 50 MB (bitrate-capped encode of the same cut, audio copied, say so).
  A corrected/rebuilt file NEVER goes out under a superseded filename.
- **MOTION LAW:** a video is not good without motion. Cut only on frames
  already moving; a halted-photo-then-movement start is a QC FAIL.
  MONTAGE-FIRST: every scene carries multiple gags/cuts/worlds, never one
  held vantage.
- **CONTINUITY:** last-frame → first-frame chaining; per-project
  autogenerated image-reference stack (never one reused anchor still); cut
  empty motion; in-motion shots only.
- **WORDMARK HARD GATE:** UNC's hoodie reads exactly "**~ WuBu ~**" in
  yellow/gold — the tildes are part of the wordmark. Drift = hard fail:
  re-fire once; if the re-fire drifts too, the take does not ship.
- **Lyrics are the look spec:** lyric-described colours/details are hard QC
  gates; singer assignment is re-derived line by line. No in-clip generated
  text (kinetic type/graphics are a post/assembly layer). No TTS/generated
  audio as soundtrack, ever.
- **EVENT DOCTRINE + MOTIF AXIS:** the EVENT (what actually happens) is a
  tracked design axis — distinct named event per slot, no repeat within a
  video, checked against the USED_EVENTS ledger across builds; the same
  locomotion/motion MOTIF across slots counts as repetition even when named
  events differ. Library: `~/workspace/wubumv/studio/event-library/`
  (64 events / 12 families).
- **LOOP IDENTITY:** a claimed loop requires end frame == beginning frame in
  subject identity/pose/state, frame-matched — not just the same room.
- **BAD-WORD rule + REPLACEMENT VISUALS PACK:** bad timing is repaired with
  a fresh visuals pack timed to the song, never defended or warp-wrestled.
- **NEW-MV-ONLY ERA (2026-11-13):** no re-fires or rework on ANY old music
  video — the user is uploading them himself and considers them all good
  enough. Focus = the newest MV + the next new tracks. Issues learned on a
  build feed research → improvement → SLERM, serving new builds only.
- **Performance truth:** 10 s bumpers are the only format that has ever
  performed; the first 3 seconds decide it; nothing converts views to
  followers. Hence the doctrines below.

## Show law — E2 LAW (adopted 2026-11-16, governs all E2+ rooms)

Born of the one-star verdict (below). Fix list F-1..F-12 from
`new-era/research/E1_POSTMORTEM_2026-11-16.md`, applied with
`new-era/research/E2_SPEECH_AND_STORY_ADDENDUM_2026-11-16.md`:

- **F-1** Gate E (cold-reader story test) runs on **transcript + picture
  summaries ONLY**, answers must cite a line/picture beat. "PASS WITH
  RULING" is **abolished**.
- **F-2** In-episode payoff quota: ≥1 want fought over and won/lost
  **irreversibly** on screen; U6 may not revoke it.
- **F-3** Spoken-want rule: every speaker's want spoken BY THEM in plain
  words by U2; unsaid sentences are seasoning, not motivation.
- **F-4** Twine the speech (shuffle test: lines must fail if shuffled —
  order-proof dialogue is untwined by construction).
- **F-5** Budget 95–110 words (cap 115); ≥4 connective lines; ≤2 sub-4-word
  lines.
- **F-6** A present-tense antagonist who ACTS.
- **F-7** One cost beat per episode.
- **F-8** De-templated questioning job (not every episode interrogates a
  document).
- **F-9** Second Gate E on the **finished master** pre-delivery. Story-gate
  failures are REPAIRED, never disclosure-shipped.
- **F-10** The Critic carries a mandatory STORY SENSE heading.
- **F-11** Bible outlines are starting boards, not plot law.
- **F-12** Gate logs cite only locked-script lines.
- Adopted addendum rulings: Y1 (Dookie beat-level default — action/face
  first), Y5 (closing order: verdict → moral → hanger).

**The calibration underneath it (user verdict, msg 513):** HOLD ON E1 —
the best-linked episode of its batch — was rated **ONE STAR OUT OF FIVE**.
Cohesion + linked scenes + working dialogue = the FLOOR (1 star), not the
goal. A story must MAKE SENSE on the surface, characters must MEAN
something and DO things, and something decisive must actually HAPPEN.

## Show craft law (standing calibrations)

- **FIRST-SECOND DOCTRINE + FIRST-3 BLAM GATE** (all production):
  A1 onset t50 ≤0.5 s / t90 ≤1.5 s · A2 dead head ≤0.2 s · A3 head level
  within 6 dB of median or an accent ≤0.5 s · A4 crackle ≤2× control bursts ·
  V1 opening motion ≥0.6× baseline · V2 no ramp-from-rest · E1 named impact
  ≤1.0 s (a legible-but-static frame 0 does NOT count) · E2 first
  cut/graphic ≤3.0 s for montage formats.
- **STRONG REFERENCE (msg 507):** the outgoing unit's last frame conditions
  the next unit's generation as a strong reference (same world state,
  wardrobe, positions, light); cohesion is EYE-verified per joint in the
  GATE_LOG — seam MAE alone never clears a joint.
- **MAXIMALIST DOCTRINE:** "more is better in video and cramming, we are
  not nature docs, we are music cannons firing on all cylinders." Density
  over restraint on every build.
- **ALL-SKILLS / ALL-ANGLES DOCTRINE (msg 562):** breadth of capability use
  is a content strategy — every skill/tool in the kit gets exercised and its
  output feeds the MV + WuBuTV catalogue.
- **WHIMSY + SELF-AWARENESS (msg 524):** shows should TEACH something; be
  FUNNY and MEMORABLE first (outlandish humor is load-bearing); characters
  sometimes talk to camera and ask viewers questions — the viewers are part
  of the story; the world is a weird esoteric place, "kind of like an
  afterlife for memes and an afterlife for people." Surface story stays
  plain and Gate-E-legible; esoterica is the world's TEXTURE, not the plot's
  language (the standard is the gossip_goblin guide — LEARNINGS.md).
- **INVASIONS AS LORE (msgs 529–532):** invaders in the spirit of the
  reference formats (Dookie empire, number-on-head empire, brainrot, fruit
  soaps) enter our episodes as recurring lore — shown, never explained,
  rendered as OUR cosplay/parody originals. Each E2+ gets ≥1 invasion beat:
  the invader arrives with a want that collides with the host plot and
  serves the payoff.
- **Subject-repetition fatigue:** hammering the same subject/action across
  all six units "microwaves" the viewer; vary each unit's business.
  World-swaps at joints are a spice, not a metronome. Three scripted
  digestion gaps per episode are structural, verified like word counts.
- **Anchoring:** anchor EVERY unit with its CHAR panel + a gated PEAK panel
  (text-only identity restatement does not hold faces; anchoring only on the
  CHAR hero panel gets rejected on frame 0).
- **Assembly:** exactly ONE assembly pass owns `joined.mp4`; probe it
  (duration/frames = segment sum) BEFORE the master encode; single
  uninterrupted encode; re-probe the finished master end to end. A clean
  structural probe does NOT clear a master — always check duration/frames
  against the track.

## Content guardrails (hard)

- **CHILD-FIGURE POLICY (msg 522):** child figures in ANY form of innuendo
  are ALWAYS BANNED, even if innocuous. Acceptable uses only: choirs
  singing, an actor in a play, horror/scary movies (monster yelling,
  running to mommy). Anything outside those lanes does not ship.
  Consequence on record: MV-8 v1 stays UNPOSTED permanently unless the user
  reverses it.
- **IP transform ruling (msg 486):** "using media creation to turn a direct
  IP reference into a cosplay and or parody cartoon" — the sanctioned route
  for IP-referencing formats. A direct IP reference goes in, media creation
  transforms it, what ships is a cosplay/parody cartoon, not a reproduction.
  When a direction touches licensed material, state the boundary plainly
  and let the user decide.
- **Adults only** in MV work; no licensed material in Slopcannon output;
  the banned talking-head test (`_QUARANTINE/video_bumper_talking_head.mp4`)
  stays quarantined.
