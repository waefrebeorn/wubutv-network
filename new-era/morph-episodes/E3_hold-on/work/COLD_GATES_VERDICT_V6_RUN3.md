## GATE 1 — HEAR-BEFORE-IT-MOVES

A line is marked a STUMBLE only on a meaning failure — after one hearing, the reader cannot say what was meant or who it was meant for. Contractions, fragments that complete or answer, and lines whose grammar leans on the previous line were not counted as stumbles.
