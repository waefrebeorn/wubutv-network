## GATE 1 — HEAR-BEFORE-IT-MOVES

Method: the spoken lines were read aloud in order, once, as a first hearing. A line is marked a STUMBLE only on a MEANING failure — after one hearing, I cannot say what was meant or who it was meant for. Contractions, fragments that complete or answer, and grammar that leans on the previous line were not counted as stumbles.

Lines heard, in order:

- U1 LAURA: "Nine coins went in, Ansel. Count what's left." — clear: nine of Ansel's coins are missing; he is to count the remainder.
- U1 LAURA: "Not chalking 'thief' on say-so." — clear: Laura will not write 'thief' on the wall on accusation alone.
- U1 LAURA: "Hands up. Bell's watching." — clear: an instruction to raise hands because the bell observes them.
- U2 THE PEACH: "Us next, Watcher." — clear: the Peach (for herself and the Lime) asks to be heard next, addressed to the Watcher.

- U2 THE PEACH: "Both hands on it. All morning." — clear: completes the alibi; both hands were occupied all morning.
- U3 LAURA: "Point while you can, Peach — loose hands lose at the bell." — clear: a warning to the Peach that pointing means a loose hand, and loose hands lose when the bell rings.
- U3 LAURA: "Keep 'em up." — recoverable: keep the raised hands up, continuing the "Hands up" instruction from U1.
- U4 LAURA: "Four hundred bells that tin came home safe." — recoverable: the tin has come home safe over four hundred bells, i.e. it has a long safe record; "bells" reads as the recurring bell cycles already established by "Bell's watching" and "lose at the bell".
- U4 LAURA: "What's in yours, Peach — besides flowers?" — clear: Laura challenges the Peach over what else is in the bouquet.
- U5 LAURA: "There's your nine." — recoverable: the nine coins from the first line ("Nine coins went in, Ansel") are now there in the open.
- U5 LAURA: "Bell counts hands, Peach. Yours pointed." — clear: the bell's hand-count is the test, and the Peach's hands pointed.
- U5 LAURA: "Three in the drain. Gone." — clear: three of the coins went into the drain and are lost.
- U6 LAURA: "Not yet, Peach." — clear: Laura refuses/defers whatever the Peach is waiting for.
- U6 LAURA: "The street hasn't had its say." — clear: the reason for the deferral; the street's judgment is still to come.

### Stumble list

None. Every line's meaning, and who it was meant for, was recoverable on a single hearing.
