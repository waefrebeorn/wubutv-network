## Q4 — The gossip retell

### Retell (exactly two sentences)
Laura was checking a tin that nine of something had gone into, found it empty, and had a courier held with his hands up while Peach pushed to be dealt with next, insisting she and someone else had had both hands on a bouquet all morning so they couldn't have taken anything. Laura didn't buy the bouquet alibi, found the nine in whatever Peach was carrying besides the flowers, said three of them had ended up in a drain, and told Peach her name wasn't going on the board yet because tomorrow's docket — the street — would decide.

### (a) Facts the retell NEEDED that are not actually in the dialogue
- That the "nine" are physical, countable things that can be put in a tin, found, and lost down a drain. The dialogue never says what the nine are.
- That "Empty" (line 2) describes the tin. The tin is not mentioned until line 9; the retell had to connect the two.
- That Ansel and the Courier are two different people, both present. Neither is ever identified or heard.
- That Peach's bouquet story is meant as an alibi against a theft accusation. She never says she is accused, and never says "we couldn't have taken anything" — the retell inferred the purpose of the holding claim from Laura's word "thief" in line 2.
- That Laura is the authority in charge — running the count, the board, and the queue. Peach calls her "Watcher," but her role and powers are never stated.
- That "yours" in line 9 is a container like the first tin. The dialogue never names what Peach's thing is.
- That the board is a public naming/shaming or verdict board. What being on it means is never stated.

### (b) Things I am unsure I got right
- What the nine are, what the tin is, and what the bell signifies or triggers.
- What "Yours pointed" (line 11) means — a hand, the bouquet, a tin? Pointed at what, and why it matters.
- How the nine can be found in line 10 ("There's your nine") and yet three of them be "in the drain. Gone" in line 12 — the same nine, or a different set?
- Who the second person in Peach's "us two" is — the Courier, Ansel, or someone never named.
- Whether Peach is actually the suspected thief, or just next in a queue of people being checked.
- What happens to someone whose name goes on the board, and what tomorrow's docket can decide.
