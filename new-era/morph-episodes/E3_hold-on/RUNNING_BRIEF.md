## Who is lying to whom

- **The Peach → the wall and the street:** "holding this bouquet
  since dawn; both hands on it, all morning" (E3 Part 1, U2).
  False — her hands were in Ansel's tin across the morning bells.
  The lie is convicted by the bell in U5, in picture, not by a
  confession. She has never apologised and never admitted a hand
  of hers is empty (card law).
- **The Peach → the Lime:** by omission — he holds a bouquet he
  knows is heavy and follows her claim anyway. His copying hand
  (one hand off in U3, both hands off in U5) is the mechanism of
  the loss, as it was in E2.
- **The Watcher → no one, in words:** he takes Ansel's fee coin
  in plain sight in U2 and pockets it without looking down — a
  shown flaw, never explained. When the claim proves false he
  slides the fee back (U6). The wall sells order, not verdicts.
- **Laura → no one:** she withholds her U1 sighting until she can
  point at its proof (U4). Withholding is her discipline, not a
  lie; her chalk writes only what she can stand behind.
