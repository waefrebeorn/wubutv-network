# GATE INPUT — HOLD ON E3 Part 1 (cold gates, v5)

## Transcript (spoken order)

[U1]
LAURA: Ansel says the Courier held his tin. His coins are gone.
LAURA: I write one true line today, or none.

[U2]
THE PEACH: Write us next: the Peach and the Lime, holding this bouquet since dawn.
THE PEACH: Both hands stayed on it.

[U3]
LAURA: At the bell, what's held stays held. What's loose changes. The Courier's hands are open.

[U4]
LAURA: The Courier kept that tin safe four hundred bells.
LAURA: And the Peach's bouquet is heavier than flowers.

[U5]
LAURA: No hand held those coins at the bell.
LAURA: Three coins in the drain. Gone.

[U6]
LAURA: I wrote one true line today. The Peach's name stays unwritten.

## Pictures (one line per unit)

[U1] Ansel slams his tin onto the wall's stone counter and it spins; he points at the Courier, whose hands are open and empty; the Watcher, keeper of the wall's word, stands at a tall brick wall with his chalk; down the street, the Peach presses her bouquet closed over a glint of coins hidden inside it; Laura chalks on the wall.
[U2] The Courier tips the tin over her open palm and nothing falls out; Ansel presses a coin into the Watcher's palm to have his line heard first, and the Watcher pockets it without looking down and turns back to the wall; the Peach steps forward holding a bouquet high in both hands, grinning.
[U3] The bell has rung in the reset and one detail of the street has changed: the stall awning's stripe is cobalt now where it was bone; the Courier sets the tin down open on the counter and holds both hands up, open, turned over once, offering them to the bell to clear her name; the Peach points at her and laughs while the Lime copies her, letting go of the bouquet with one hand to point too, and the bouquet sags.
[U4] Ansel jabs a finger at the tin, then at the Courier's open hands, still raised from her offer to the bell; the Peach still holds her bouquet out toward the wall, waiting for her line; Laura steps between Ansel and the Courier and looks at the Peach's bouquet.
[U5] A bell rings; the Peach points at the Courier and laughs, and the Lime copies her with both hands and lets go of the bouquet entirely; it drops, spilling nine coins across the ground, three of them rolling into the storm drain at the counter's base; the Courier's hands are still raised, as they have been since she offered them; the Peach grabs at the coins with both hands.
[U6] The Watcher strikes through a chalk line on the wall — his wall keeps only what's true — slides Ansel's fee coin back across the counter to him, and puts six coins into Ansel's palm; Ansel nods at the Courier; Laura chalks a new line on the wall — HANDS OPEN. COINS LOOSE. — and her chalk hovers over a blank space below it; the Peach and the Lime stand over their dropped bouquet, nothing written for either of them; the picture freezes.
