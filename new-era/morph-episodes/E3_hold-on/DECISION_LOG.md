## Rulings (law tensions, decided in the room)

R-1 · SPEAKER COUNT. Task law and F-14 cap the episode at TWO
designed speakers; the bible (§4) caps at three and gives the
Watcher the reveal. RULED: two speakers — LAURA and THE
PEACH. The Watcher's reveal function is discharged by deed
(the cap set beside the tin, the strike through the false
line) while Laura, the chalk-hand, voices the room's verdicts
— the wall's rulings have always passed through the chalk.
The invader is one of the two speakers: the Peach alone, the
Lime silent. This also repairs E2's named failure (unison
invaders who "said and did nothing"): the Peach gets two real
lines, commits the theft, and takes the loss. No unison
entries anywhere in the episode.

R-2 · BUDGET SHAPE. The bible outline totals 85 words across
two-speaker units; F-5's episode band is 95–110; the bible's
band is 80–90 with a hard cap of 95; template v2's per-unit
caps (target 14–17, hard 20, one speaker) are the newest law
and bind per unit. The episode locks at 94 words: inside the
bible's hard cap, one word under F-5's floor — the two bands
conflict in the seam and cannot both be satisfied under the
per-unit caps; the cold gates' clarity demands (a stated
law, named subjects, the cost spoken) set the final count,
and clarity is the higher law (the MISHMASH verdict was a
clarity failure). Logged as a deviation, not hidden.
U1 (20w) sits AT the hard cap and U4 (18w) above the
per-unit target; both audits are signed with their reasons
(hook unit carries claim + condition; clash unit carries
the stake count + the naming). U3 was reclassed QUIET →
MIXED (12w) when the law line moved into it.

R-3 · SERIAL POSITION. This episode is E3 in continuity and
PART 1 of the retooled serial run. E2's locked hanger —

R-4 · THE INVADER (standing record). THE PEACH (with the
Lime, silent) — Invasion Circuit recurring (series LEDGER
PL-9). Source format: the talking-fruit soap operas of the
reference class; she is OUR cosplay/parody original (a peach
and a lime in the wedding clothes of their refused E2
claim), never a reproduction of any reference character.
Want: her couple written next on the wall — colliding with
the host plot twice over, by jumping the theft docket's
queue and by being the theft herself. Lines: 2 (U2, both
hers alone). Visible action with consequence: palms a coin
into the bouquet (U1, the audience secret); performs the
hold (U2–U4); grabs at the spill (U5). Consequence: the
spill exposes her, the claim is refused a second time,
three of the nine coins are lost down the drain by her
method, and her name hangs on the street's vote. She serves
the payoff (her loss is the episode's irreversible loss) and
the cost beat (the drained coins). Lore function: the invader is
WINNING at the open — she has the coins and demands the
wall — while the home characters are stifled by the form:
the accused may not speak, the chalk-hand may write only
what she can prove. The gentrification is shown in the
docket's own terms, never explained. The one self-awareness
device (F-21 ration) is the freeze question itself: the
viewer is beckoned to decide the name — carried by the
participation mechanic, not by a camera address (bible B7
holds: nobody talks to camera).
