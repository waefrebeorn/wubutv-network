## CANON FACTS (append-only)

- **F-001** (carry-in, SHOW-2): LAURA's name stands finished on
  the chalk wall (SHOW-2 F-018/F-019). Social reading in the
  morph: the town believes the wall *chose* her. This is why the
  town lets the chalk-hand write the docket.
- **F-002** (carry-in, SHOW-2): The Courier's custody record is
  town gospel: "Four hundred bells. Never dropped one."
- **F-003** (E1): The world restarts every ten seconds. Whatever
  a person is holding at the bell persists exactly as it was;
  everything else may change — by one detail, usually. Nobody in
  town marvels at this; they check their hands.
- **F-004** (E1): The chalk wall at the depot corner is the
  town's only record. What the wall says, the town treats as
  true. The Watcher keeps the wall. Laura is his chalk-hand: she
  physically writes the docket.
- **F-005** (E1): Bruno, a lodger in Rosa's rooms above her
  diner, held Rosa's lost ring in his closed fist for four
  hundred bells, intending to return it to her hand in person.
- **F-006** (E1): Bruno's three-month rent debt stood struck
  through on the wall — line nine — from the day Rosa's ring
  vanished. Who struck it, and why, is NOT established on screen
  and is an open fact (see PL-2). The Watcher knew the line was
  struck and knew what Bruno's fist held: "I've watched him hold
  it four hundred bells."
- **F-007** (E1): Laura has written exactly one line on the wall
  herself: "PAID. ASK HIS HAND." (E1 U6). Before E1 she had
  written nothing of her own judgment — in E1 U1 she began the
  demanded line ("BR—") and stopped.
- **F-008** (E1): The ring is back on Rosa's hand (she took it
  from Bruno's open palm, E1 U6). Bruno's room key is back on its
  nail at the wall.

## CHARACTER STATE CARDS

**LAURA** — chalk-hand, the Watcher's assistant. C1 layers:
*Identity* — the hand that writes the town's record; her own name
stands finished on the wall (F-001). *Personality* — Short
response style; questions, mostly; accuses by noticing; her
signature line is "I saw what you were holding." *Backstory* —
the new hire of SHOW-2 who asked the metaphysical question and
stopped asking it; the town's belief that the wall chose her
precedes her everywhere.
- KNOWS: the docket; what she has seen held (E1: the ring, from
  the moment the fist opened — before that, only that Bruno was
  guarding something). Does NOT know who struck line nine.
- WANTS: to write one true thing on the wall. (E1: achieved once,
  F-007 — the want now compounds: to keep the wall true.)
- OWES: the Watcher her position; Bruno an unwritten "thief"
  (she refused the order — the refusal is known to Rosa, not yet
  to the Watcher).
- IS OWED: the truth about line nine.
- STATUS: the chalk-hand whose one line contradicted the
  record's silence — noticed by the town.
- LAST CHANGE: E1 U6 — wrote "PAID. ASK HIS HAND." herself.

**THE WATCHER** — keeper of the wall, the authority (B10). C1:
*Identity* — the man who runs the place and keeps the book; his
role is his license to know. *Personality* — Long response style,
the one rambler; flat, deadpan; catchphrase "The wall says what
the wall says" (plant reassigned to E2 — see DECISION_LOG).
*Backstory* — the wall says what the wall is paid to say, and he
is the one being paid; he holds the show's wrong belief as a
practice, not a creed.
- KNOWS: everything the wall holds, including line nine's strike
  and what Bruno's fist held for four hundred bells (F-006). Who
  struck line nine: not established (withheld — even from this
  card; the E4 room settles it on record).
- WANTS: the wall's word to stay final — his authority is the
  wall's authority.
- OWES: Laura an answer he has not been asked for yet.
- IS OWED: the town's continued belief.
- STATUS: authority intact on the surface; his fallibility has
  been witnessed by exactly one person (Laura).
- LAST CHANGE: E1 U5 — revealed the strike himself, in person,
  with his hand on the line.

**THE COURIER** — the Holder for hire (co-lead, even episodes;
