## GATE E — COLD-READER ANSWERS (lock gate, no lore terms)

- **Who wants what:** Rosa, the landlady, wants the wall to
  write that her lodger Bruno owes her three months' rent.
  Laura, who holds the chalk, wants to write only what she has
  seen to be true. The Watcher, who keeps the wall, wants the
  wall's word to stand.
- **What blocked them:** Laura would not finish the line — her
  chalk stopped — because Bruno swore he had paid; and Bruno
  would not open his fist, which was the only thing that could
  settle it.
- **What changed by U6:** The wall's own record showed the debt
  had already been struck off the day Rosa's ring went missing —
  and Bruno's fist opened on that ring, which he had been
  holding for her the whole time. Laura wrote the true line
  herself: paid.
- **Why it matters, to someone who knows nothing:** A woman
  nearly had a man written down as a thief over a debt that was
  already forgiven, and the only person who could prove it was
  the man himself — who would not defend himself with anything
  but a closed hand, because he wanted to put what he'd found
  back into her hand himself.
