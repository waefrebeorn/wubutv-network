# HOLD ON — Episode 1: "Write That He Owes Me"

**Status:** LOCKED — Showrunner lock declared 2026-11-16 (room
session record in DECISION_LOG.md; Critic rounds 1–2 complete,
rulings recorded there).
**Show:** HOLD ON (morphed from THE TENTH SECOND) · Bible: `new-era/morph/MORPH_SHOW-2.md` (LAW)
**Format:** 60s · 6 units × 10s · 16:9 · one conversation
**Episode title** = the docket entry, in the accuser's words (Bible §1, Rule S9).

**Premise (Gate E, on its face):** The world restarts every ten
seconds and changes everything — except whatever you're holding,
which stays exactly as it was — so in this town every debt, theft,
and love affair is fought at the chalk wall that keeps the town's
only record, over one question anyone can understand: *what will
you be caught holding when the bell rings?*

**The chain (B1, one only):** proof shown (the key) → lie told
(two statements face off, each false as stated: Rosa's demanded
docket line — a debt the record already killed — and Bruno's
relayed claim that he *paid*; the wall will break both) →
entrance that breaks the lie (the Watcher reads the wall) →
authority reveal (the debt was struck the day the ring vanished;
he has held it since) → consequence begun, cut at the peak (the
ring in the open hand; the question unanswered).

**Speaking cast (cap 3, X3):** ROSA (accuser, guest — the diner
landlady) · LAURA (co-lead, chalk-hand, questioning job) · THE
WATCHER (authority). BRUNO (the accused lodger) never speaks; his
entrances and his fist are the chain's third beat (B10 exemption).

**Storyline letters (A5):** A = the debt accusation (Rosa ↔ Bruno)
· B = Laura's questioning job — her first true line · C = the wall
can be wrong (the Watcher / line nine). A spends U1, U2, U5 ·
B spends U2 (notice), U4 (name), U6 (act) · C spends U5, U6.

**Word budget (A1, binding):** 86 words total (band 80–90, cap
95) · 14 lines (cap 14) · per-line max 8 words (cap 12) ·
FULL-TALK ×2, non-adjacent (U1, U4) · QUIET ×1 (U3) ·
SILENCE-BEAT ×1 (U6, 12 ∈ 10–13) · MIXED ×2 (U2, U5).

## U1 — FULL-TALK · 18 words · Hook + Setup

**BLAM (Bible §6):** Frame 0 is mid-motion — ROSA's hand snatches
Bruno's room key off the wall's nail as the Cull Clock ignites
inside 0–1.0s. First-second sound event: the key's clatter on the
stone counter + the bell's snap. The grab and the bell teach the
rule before the first line lands (Slate §1): held things stay;
everything else is about to change. First line inside 3s — the
accusation already in flight, naming a visible object and an
action. No fade-in, no crackle head, no ramp from rest. U1's PEAK
panel is the grab itself.

**Staging:** The chalk wall, depot corner, morning light from
frame left. Rosa jerks her chin at the wall; LAURA stands at it,
chalk raised. On "Write it," the chalk touches the wall and
starts a stroke — **BR—** — and stops there when Laura answers
with the counter-claim instead of the line. The half-stroke stays
on the wall all episode (post insert): the visible block — the
chalk-hand will not finish. She completes no line until U6; that
restraint is her want, staged: *to write one true thing on the
wall.* Down the street, small in frame, Bruno himself stands
watching the wall. The wall's older docket lines run numbered
down its left edge (post) — the record's shape is established
from the first unit.

> ROSA *(want-statement, accusation-with-proof; tactic: ACCUSE)*:
> "Write it: Bruno owes me three months." — 7
>
> LAURA *(relays the counter-claim; tactic: RELAY)*:
> "He swears he paid you, Rosa." — 6
>
> ROSA *(holds the key high; tactic: CHALLENGE)*:
> "Then why's his key mine?" — 5

*Turn: opens on an unchallenged accusation; closes with the proof
contested — a claim against a key.*

## U2 — MIXED · 15 words · The proof; NOTICE

**Staging:** The key turns in Rosa's fingers for the camera — the
proof object shown before it is argued (B8). Her other hand
worries her bare ring finger: a pale band where a ring sat (the
ring is planted in picture two units before it is named). Down
the street, Bruno pats his coat with his left hand — pocket,
pocket — and comes up empty: the lodger has no key. His right
hand stays closed at his side. Laura sees the fist before anyone
says it.

> ROSA *(tactic: INSIST)*:
> "Three months. Not one coin crossed my counter." — 8
>
> LAURA *(questioning job: NOTICE; tactic: INTERROGATE)*:
> "Then what's he holding down there, Rosa?" — 7

**GAP 1 (after the turn, 2–3s):** held close on Bruno's closed
fist, bell ticking, no words.

*Turn: opens on Rosa certain; closes with a second object in the
story — whatever is in that fist.*

## U3 — QUIET · 7 words · Twist

**The one-detail reset (Slate §5 — designed BIG and singular):**
at the joint's bell, exactly ONE detail changes — the depot's
hanging sign flips from ROOMS to NO ROOMS. It fires on the bell,
at the joint, while Bruno crosses: the world changes around a
man who doesn't — the reset is the stage law made visible, not
a story beat; the unit's twist is the refusal below. Everything
else is locked: same street, same light, same hands, same key.
The audience hunts the detail; the detail is large enough to
find at phone size. This is not a world-swap (Bible §6: swaps
are reserved, max two, earned — E1 fires zero).

**Staging:** Bruno crosses the street in silence — Laura's demand
lands mid-crossing, as he reaches the wall's shadow, so the
unit's pressure starts at second zero — and he stops at the
wall, at the exact spot, like a man who has stood there before —
fist still closed, and does not offer it. Rosa watches,
unsurprised. The twist reads off the picture alone (B9): the one
act that could clear him is the act he refuses.

> LAURA *(tactic: DEMAND)*:
> "Bruno. Open your hand and show her." — 7

*Turn: opens on a demand; closes on a refusal that confirms the
fist is the story.*

## U4 — FULL-TALK · 19 words · Clash; NAME

**Staging:** Rosa presses with the key, close to Bruno's face.
Bruno does not step back. His fist, in frame, is trembling.
Thwarted at the fist, Rosa turns the demand on the wall instead —
she orders the charge escalated, debt to thief, and it is that
order Laura answers. (Not a third demand to open: a new action,
an escalation, and the pressure that makes the naming happen.)

> ROSA *(tactic: ORDER)*:
> "Write 'thief,' Laura. Now." — 4
>
> LAURA *(questioning job: NAME — a positive naming, not a
> negation; tactic: CORRECT)*:
> "He's guarding it, Rosa. Look — his hand shakes." — 8
>
> *[Bruno's fist trembles — picture beat, no words.]*
>
> LAURA *(tactic: EXPOSE, the peak turn)*:
> "He'd drop dead before he drops it." — 7

**GAP 2 (after the peak turn, 2–3s):** held reaction on Bruno's
face, fist in frame, no words.

*Turn: opens with pressure on Bruno; closes with the suspicion
redirected — it was never money, and Rosa's frame breaks.*

## U5 — MIXED · 15 words · The entrance breaks the lie; the reveal

**The episode's one scripted unprompted entrance (C8/X5,
declared here):** THE WATCHER arrives and goes straight to the
wall, one hand flat on line nine. The record itself was the
third party in the room all along. Rosa reads in silence — the
struck line fills an insert (post): **BRUNO OWES ME THREE
MONTHS**, struck through, beside her own unfinished **BR—**. Her
face does the beat. The causal link is spoken whole this time:
struck — when the ring vanished — and the Watcher has watched
him hold it since, so U6's open hand confirms a spoken fact
instead of introducing an unexplained object.

> WATCHER *(entrance-that-contradicts; tactic: SUMMON)*:
> "Line nine." — 2
>
> *[Rosa reads in silence. The insert. Her face.]*
>
> WATCHER *(authority reveal, B10; tactic: REVEAL)*:
> "Struck when your ring vanished." — 5
>
> *[His hand leaves the wall and points down the street — at
> Bruno's fist.]*
>
> WATCHER *(tactic: TESTIFY — first-person witness; the link,
> spoken before the fist opens)*:
> "I've watched him hold it four hundred bells." — 8

*Turn: opens with the record as Rosa's weapon; closes with the
record acquitting Bruno.*

## U6 — SILENCE-BEAT · 12 words · Consequence begun; ACT; the freeze

**Staging:** In silence, Bruno opens his hand at last — palm up,
held out toward Rosa, steady: an offering, not a surrender.
Rosa's lost ring, held through four hundred bells to be returned
in person — he had paid, in the only coin she counts, and waited
to put it in her hand himself. Only once the ring is visible to
the whole room does Laura speak — in U1 she had a claim; now she
has a sight, and that difference is the rule she writes by.
LAURA acts (questioning job: ACT): she takes the chalk and
writes the true line herself — her first. The chalk line is
post-composited (production notes): **PAID. ASK HIS HAND.**
Rosa steps in and takes the ring from his open palm.

> LAURA *(signature line, S5; tactic: DECLARE)*:
> "I saw what you were holding." — 6
>
> ROSA *(the open question, B11 — on screen over the freeze;
> tactic: ASK)*:
> "Why didn't you just knock, Bruno?" — 6

**GAP 3 — the freeze (final 2–3s):** the reveal has landed; we
hold Bruno's face, Rosa's hand, the ring between them. Bruno
does not answer — the episode ends on the not-answering. No
words. The episode ends mid-consequence, on the unanswered
question —
a viewer can type it into a comment. The closing street carries
one changed detail of its own: the key is back on its nail, where
frame 0 tore it off. The sign stays NO ROOMS.

## PRODUCTION NOTES (carrying Slate §5 flags for this show)

1. **The one-detail reset is a precision ask.** The U2→U3 joint
   asks for near-duplicate worlds differing in exactly one detail.
   The detail is designed BIG and singular to survive it: the
   depot's hanging sign, ROOMS → NO ROOMS — a two-face physical
   prop flip on the bell (or a PEAK-panel pair + post flip).
   Everything else at the joint is a CARRY match: same hand, same
   object, same street. Expect a higher refire rate on this joint;
   if the take changes five details or none, it fails the beat
   silently — QC hunts the sign explicitly. Never ask the
   generator to render the sign's text (see note 2).
2. **All chalk is post.** The wall is generated BLANK. Every
   docket line — the demanded line, struck line nine, "PAID. ASK
   HIS HAND." — is composited in post, keyed to measured beats
   (Slate production declaration; Split Total precedent). The
   wall's older docket lines run numbered down its left edge, so
   "line nine" points at a feature the viewer has seen since U1.
   No docket line is ever a generation request.
3. **BLAM discipline (Bible §6):** U1's PEAK panel is the grab.
   Cull Clock (cadmium, Method C) ignites inside 0–1.0s,
   furniture-corner, sound-off legible, never competing with
   speech. First line lands inside 3s. Loudness pass on the
   master; never fade in.
4. **Joints:** CARRY matches on all five joints (key in Rosa's
   hand U1–U5; ring at U6). Full world-swaps fired: ZERO (Bible
   §6 reserves them for tenser episodes). The closing detail —
   the key back on its nail — is a staging carry, not a swap.
5. **Front-loading defense (Method B A3):** turns are separate
   quoted blocks with picture turns between them; lines are
   written to land wherever the generator puts them; U6's tail is
   speech-independent (the chalk insert + the freeze).
6. **Anchors:** CHAR panels for Laura, the Watcher, Rosa — every
   unit (pilot law). Bruno is anchored by CHAR panel too: his
   face carries GAP 2 and the freeze, so his identity must hold
   without a single spoken line. Wall units are two-handers
   (Rosa + Laura): combined two-character anchor panel per unit
   (PILOT-4 lesson).
7. **Wordmark law:** "~ WuBu ~" glyph-spelled, UNC
   small/shadowed/frame-edge; full-span sweep wherever the chest
   is visible. UNC does not appear in E1 (Bible §3: benched) —
   no exception needed.
8. **Palette/world:** INK / COBALT / BONE + CADMIUM accent; the
   depot-corner wall; morning light from frame left — untouched
   from SHOW-2 (recognition is spent, not re-minted, S2).
9. **Vernacular (S4):** flat, deadpan, matter-of-fact about the
   absurd — nobody marvels at the bell; they check their hands.
   Carrier words: *holding*, *the bell*. The register does not
   drift.
10. **Timing note (Pass 4):** at the genre rate (~2.5 w/s) —
    U1 18w ≈ 7.2s + the grab ✓ · U2 15w ≈ 6.0s + GAP 1 (2–3s)
    ✓ · U3 7w ≈ 2.8s + Bruno's silent crossing carries the rest
    ✓ · U4 19w ≈ 7.6s + GAP 2 — the gap rides the U4→U5 joint
    and is protected: if a take runs long, trim the Watcher's
    entrance walk in U5, never the gap · U5 15w ≈ 6.0s + entrance
    + read insert ✓ · U6 12w ≈ 4.8s + the silent hand-opening
    (~2s) + GAP 3 freeze (2–3s) ✓. All six atoms close inside
    10s with the gaps intact.
