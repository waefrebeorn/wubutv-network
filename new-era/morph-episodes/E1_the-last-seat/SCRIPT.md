**Format:** 60s, 6 units × 10s, 16:9, one master. Speaking cast:
3 (X3 cap met) — the Dancer, the Usher, the Occupant.

**Premise (Gate E on its face):** a broke little theatre will be
closed by its landlord unless its star dancer can win over the
stranger sitting in the critic's seat — and tonight's stranger is
holding a ticket stub that hasn't been printed in twenty years.

## CHARACTER CARDS (A4 / C1 — dialogue written from Personality)

- **THE DANCER** — *History:* has opened every season since she
  was nineteen with the first-night number, the only thing the
  theatre owns outright; last episode (spent canon) she bought
  back an entrusted step with a withheld gaze. *Goal tonight:*
  get the stranger out of the critic's seat — or prove she
  belongs there — and keep the verdict hers. *Entering emotion:*
  mid-leap confidence, insulted on her own stage. Response Style:
  **LONG** (the rambler — her length is physical as much as
  verbal). Catchphrase (S5): **"Watch this."** — reserved from
  tonight; Night One earns it by the test she invents in U5.
- **THE USHER** — *History:* forty years in the building; the
  founder's stage manager; has never asked anyone for anything
  (carried plant, E2-P1). *Goal tonight:* open the night by the
  book and keep the doors open without asking. *Entering
  emotion:* deadpan routine, one degree off — the seat is already
  taken. Response Style: **SHORT** (3–6-word lines). Signature
  entrance line (C9): **"House of one."** **Holds the questioning
  job:** notices U2, names U4, acts U6.
- **THE OCCUPANT — the woman with the white stub** — *History:*
  somebody promised her this seat on an opening night; she holds
  the stub to prove it. *Goal tonight:* be given a reason to stay
  in that seat. *Entering emotion:* unhurried amusement; she has
  waited longer than anyone in the room has been in it. Response
  Style: **MEDIUM**, deadpan from the dark. Shot by silhouette
  identifiers only (hat, gloves, programme, lamp) — face never
  front-lit (bible §3; slate §5).

**Relationships declared before any line (C3):** Dancer ↔
Occupant — rival claimants: the stage's present vs the seat's
past. Dancer ↔ Usher — the star needs the keeper's book and
resents needing it. Usher ↔ Occupant — the keeper vs the one
entry his book cannot file.

**Shared wrong belief (A4):** the founder vanished; the real
critic is still to come; every occupant is a rehearsal. The
viewer can see tonight that she is not a rehearsal for anything.

## THE SCRIPT

Dialogue is VERBATIM. Lines are written to land wherever the
generator's front-loading puts them (bible production law); every
unit's tail carries a speech-independent event — the look at the
seat — so no cliff depends on speech placement. Turn 1 and Turn 2
are separate quoted blocks with the picture turn written between
them (Method B A3). Tactic margins per Gate D — no speaker repeats
a tactic consecutively; every speaker's final tactic ≠ their
opening tactic.

### U1 — HOOK + SETUP · FULL-TALK · 19 words · letter A

**Picture / BLAM:** Frame 0, FROM BEHIND THE OCCUPANT'S HAT: the
Dancer mid-leap at the seat, already moving; landing thud + the
Occupant's programme snapping shut ≤0.5s (named first-second
sound event); the seat's reading lamp clicks on inside 0–1.0s
(the Method C device firing in the BLAM window). Background,
box-office door: the landlord's lease notice, paper shown, never
read (visible stake, B8-adjacent). Accusation already in flight.

UNSAID — Dancer: *"If she's nobody, I can throw her out. If she's
somebody, I'm finished."* · Occupant: *"I have waited twenty
years to be accused in this seat."* · Usher: *"The book will tell
me what she is. I will not ask her."*

> TURN 1 — **DANCER** → Occupant | act: accuse | want: the seat
> back | tactic: ACCUSE | status: raise-self
> **"That seat's saved for the critic. You're not her."** (9w)

PICTURE TURN: the Occupant does not move. The lamp tilts up a
fraction — she is looking back.

> TURN 2 — **OCCUPANT** → Dancer | act: deflect | want: to stay
> seated | tactic: DEFLECT | status: lower-other
> **"Then she won't mind me warming it."** (7w)

TAIL EVENT (speech-independent): the Usher enters down the aisle
with the guest book; his entrance line lands on the tail of hers
(declared interruption point, C8).

> **USHER** → the house | act: declare | tactic: DECLARE
> **"House of one."** (3w)

DELTA: the night is formally open *under* the claim — the ritual
runs, the stranger stays. STATUS: Occupant, unchallenged-in-fact.

### U2 — SETUP / THE PROOF · MIXED · 14 words · letter B · GAP 1

**Picture:** the Occupant lays a ticket stub on the brass
armrest — shown in frame before anyone explains it (B8). The
Dancer dances the first-night phrase at her as a challenge
(danced TURN 1 of the challenge pair). Crossing behind the seat,
the Usher clocks the stub's colour and does not stop — **the
notice beat** (questioning job, U2).

UNSAID — Dancer: *"Dance is the only lie-detector I own."* ·
Occupant: *"Pink. She still thinks the colour is the point."*

> TURN 1 — **DANCER** → Occupant | act: accuse-with-proof |
> tactic: CHALLENGE
> **"Our stubs are pink. That one's white."** (7w)

PICTURE TURN: the phrase ends, aimed at the seat. The Occupant
looks at the stub, not at the dancer.

> TURN 2 — **OCCUPANT** → Dancer | act: lie/denial | tactic: LIE
> **"It was white when I bought it."** (7w)

**GAP 1 (2–3s, carrier: the non-applause):** she does not applaud;
the lamp stays cold; the Dancer holds the phrase's final pose,
waiting, in the non-answer. Danced pair banks its delta on the
refusal: TURN 2 of the pair is a person not answering.

DELTA: the claim is doubted by its own proof. KNOWS (audience):
white is wrong; nobody yet knows why.

### U3 — TWIST · MIXED · 12 words · letter C

**Picture:** the entrance that breaks the lie is the book itself,
carried into the light: the Usher opens the guest book at the
counter, forty years of nights in his hands.

UNSAID — Usher: *"I wrote this page. I know what white means."* ·
Dancer: *"If the book excuses her, I lose my stage to a ghost."*

> TURN 1 — **USHER** → Dancer | act: entrance-that-contradicts |
> tactic: CORRECT
> **"White stubs were never sold. They were given."** (8w)

PICTURE TURN: the Dancer comes off the stage to the counter —
the fight moves from the seat to the record.

> TURN 2 — **DANCER** → Usher | act: question | tactic: QUESTION
> **"Given to whom, Usher?"** (4w)

TAIL EVENT: the Occupant, unanswered and unanswering, turns one
page of her programme in the dark. (Her silence this unit is the
withheld beat the slate's F3.1-style cast discipline wants: three
speakers, but the third is present by act, not voice.)

DELTA: doubt becomes a category — given, not sold. The lie of U2
is broken by paper, not by argument (B1, third beat).

### U4 — CLASH / AUTHORITY REVEAL · FULL-TALK · 18 words · letter A · GAP 2

**Picture:** the keeper reads his own book aloud (B10 — his role
is his license to know). **The naming beat** (questioning job,
U4): the claim goes on the record.

UNSAID — Usher: *"I have read this page a thousand nights. Never
aloud."* · Occupant: *"Vanished. Is that what they told the
child?"*

> TURN 1 — **USHER** → the room | act: authority reveal |
> tactic: NAME
> **"Opening night. Seat one. The woman who built this room."**
> (10w — a book-read in three fragments, 4+2+4; see DECISION_LOG
> D-5)

PICTURE TURN: the Dancer looks from the page to the hat. ~1s
micro-hold (A2, uncounted).

> TURN 2 — **DANCER** → Usher | act: testify | tactic: TESTIFY
> **"She vanished before I was born."** (6w)

> TURN 3 — **OCCUPANT** → Dancer | act: entrance-that-contradicts
> (of the room's whole story) | tactic: CHALLENGE
> **"Did she."** (2w) — the episode's clippable line (A5):
> complete, strange, quotable out of context.

**GAP 2 (2–3s, carrier: the held look):** Dancer's gaze holds,
page to hat; the lamp warms a fraction; nobody speaks.

DELTA: the dispute stops being about a ticket. KNOWS (Dancer):
the stub was given to the founder. STATUS: the Occupant's claim
is on the record, and she has told the room its own story is
wrong.

### U5 — THE TEST PROPOSED · QUIET · 7 words · letter B

**Picture:** the Dancer returns to the stage and makes the only
offer that can settle it. The slate's swipe point lives here
(SLATE §3): the hook for a viewer who hasn't bought
dance-as-verdict is the *judge changing*, staged visibly — as the
dance begins, the Occupant's programme lowers by degrees and her
lamp warms: the room watches her decide to watch.

UNSAID — Dancer: *"If she looks away once, she's a fraud and I
win."* · Usher: *"Whatever she is, the house still needs its
dark."*

> TURN 1 — **DANCER** → Occupant | act: offer (the test) |
> tactic: OFFER
> **"One dance. Stay for it."** (5w) — **slate fix F5.1 verbatim;
> pre-locked by the slate pass, not punch-up eligible.**

> TURN 2 — **USHER** → the house | act: command | tactic: COMMAND
> **"House lights."** (2w)

TAIL EVENT: the house drops; the first-night number begins —
danced TURN 1 of the test pair, straight at the seat, the
Dancer's gaze held on the hat throughout.

DELTA: identity claimed → test accepted. The argument is over;
the verdict is now physical.

### U6 — CLIFFHANGER / THE FREEZE · SILENCE-BEAT · 12 words · letter A · GAP 3

**Picture:** the dance completes — and mid-phrase the Occupant's
foot taps the number's exact rhythm **on the brass armrest cap**,
and her gloved hand rises, conducting one bar from memory. The
tell is staged big (slate §5): foot on brass + programme edge
tapping + lamp warming in the same beat, so an *approximate* tap
still reads — she is keeping time she should not know. ~1s
micro-hold on the tap (A2, uncounted). Per X2, the device-cliff
(the lamp, the rhythm) is carried by a social beat in the same
unit: the whisper, her command, and the Usher's act.

UNSAID — Dancer: *"Nobody alive knows that rhythm. Nobody."* ·
Occupant: *"Again. I have been waiting to ask for it again."*

> TURN 1 — **DANCER** → Usher (whispered) | act: confess |
> tactic: CONFESS
> **"She knows it, Usher. Every step."** (6w)

> TURN 2 — **OCCUPANT** → Dancer | act: authority reveal, inverted
> — the judged becomes the judge | tactic: COMMAND
> **"Once more. From the top, darling."** (6w) — **slate fix
> F5.2 verbatim; pre-locked by the slate pass.**

**THE USHER'S ACT (questioning job, U6):** he does not clear the
seat. He turns the house light up himself, and lets her stay —
forty years of non-interference broken by a stagehand's gesture,
no line spent on it.

**GAP 3 / FREEZE (final 2–3s, carrier: the freeze):** her face
still in shadow, programme lowered one inch too slowly and
stopped mid-descent; the reaction holds; the question card goes
up (B11, Method C post graphic, never a generation request):
**WHO IS THE WOMAN IN SEAT ONE?**

DELTA (episode totals, forecast vs actual reconciled in
DECISION_LOG): Occupant STATUS intruder → sanctioned guest;
Dancer KNOWS the stub's category + the stranger knows the number;
Usher ACTS for the first time in forty years. Consequence begun,
not finished (B3): she stays — what she wants is Night Two's
problem.

## PRODUCTION NOTES (handoff — slate §5 flags + standing defenses)

1. **The rhythm tell is staged approximation-proof** (slate §5):
   foot on the brass armrest cap, programme edge tapping, lamp
   warming — three carriers in one beat; an approximate tap still
   reads. It is a tell, not a measurement.
2. **The Occupant is never generated front-lit.** Identifier
   anchors only (hat, gloves, programme, lamp); the face reveal
   is the season's withheld asset. The rotating-claimant anchor
   problem is solved by design, per the bible.
3. **PEAK panel per unit is the generation anchor** (the pilot's
   CHAR-only volley collapsed on frame 0; PEAK anchoring is what
   shipped, twice). The Dancer's CHAR panel locks the dress: dark
   hair in a tight bun, deep vermilion sleeveless dress, locked
   SHORT swirling skirt (E2 dress-drift fought here).
4. **Wordmark** is discharged by the post-production corner bug,
   as in both shipped episodes. UNC does not appear in this show.
5. **Silence-beat compression defense:** the three gaps are
   carried by action (non-applause, held look, freeze), never
   bare pauses — the generator cannot compress a held pose it is
   asked to perform.
6. **Coverage per unit (A5):** speaker → object/hand insert
   (stub, book page, brass cap) → silent listener reaction. Do
   not assume a 10s two-person dialogue master.
7. **The question card is a Method C post graphic**, keyed to the
   freeze; the lease notice in U1 is set dressing — shown, never
   legible, never a generation text request.
8. **Joint grammar:** every unit ends on the look at the seat and
   opens FROM the seat (the gaze spine, E2-proven); joints are
   declared BREAKs at production, as E2 ran them.
