# BOARD — THE LAST SEAT · Night One: The Woman with the White Stub

**Room:** Writers Room, Episode 1 session, 2026-11-16. Board drafted
by the Storyliner from MORPH_SHOW-5.md §8 (starting point, not law);
bible premise / cast / engine are law. Approved before beat sheets
(Method §2 gate). Cards ≤7 words; Gilligan line under each card.
Letters = storyline/location track (A5): **A** = the seat (the
claim), **B** = the stage (the dance), **C** = the book (the
record). Pattern: **A B C A B A** — alternates; no unit repeats its
neighbour's track.

**Shared wrong belief (A4):** the room believes the founder
vanished and the real critic is still to come — so every occupant
is treated as a rehearsal. The viewer can see from tonight that
the woman in the seat is not a rehearsal for anything.

**Questioning job (A4/X8): THE USHER** — notices U2 (clocks the
stub's colour crossing behind the seat), names U4 (reads the book
aloud — the authority reveal *is* the naming), acts U6 (raises the
house light himself; does not clear the seat). He rides the
accusation chain as its authority.

**One chain only (B1):** proof shown (white stub) → lie told ("It
was white when I bought it") → entrance that breaks the lie (the
book, carried into the light) → authority reveal (the Usher reads
his own book) → consequence begun, not finished (she stays).

## UNIT 1 — A · HOOK + SETUP · FULL-TALK (19w)

- **Card:** Dancer lands, accuses the stranger
  *Gilligan — Dancer: "That seat is the critic's. Who does she
  think she is?"*
- **Card:** Stranger answers like she belongs
  *Gilligan — Occupant: "Let her be angry. I have waited longer
  than she has been alive in this room."*
- **Card:** Usher opens the night anyway
  *Gilligan — Usher: "The book will tell me what she is. I will
  not ask her."*

## UNIT 2 — B · SETUP / THE PROOF · MIXED (14w) + GAP 1

- **Card:** Stub shown before explained
  *Gilligan — Occupant: "Pink. She still thinks the colour is the
  point."*
- **Card:** Dancer dances the challenge phrase
  *Gilligan — Dancer: "Dance is the only lie-detector I own."*
- **Card:** No applause; lamp stays cold
  *Gilligan — Dancer: "She didn't clap. Nobody has ever not
  clapped."*

## UNIT 3 — C · TWIST · MIXED (12w)

- **Card:** Book carried into the light
  *Gilligan — Usher: "I wrote this page. I know what white
  means."*
- **Card:** White was given, never sold
  *Gilligan — Dancer: "If the book excuses her, I lose my stage
  to a ghost."*

## UNIT 4 — A · CLASH / AUTHORITY REVEAL · FULL-TALK (18w) + GAP 2

- **Card:** Keeper reads his book aloud
  *Gilligan — Usher: "I have read this page a thousand nights.
  Never aloud."*
- **Card:** Book names the founder
  *Gilligan — Dancer: "She's older than the building. She can't
  be."*
- **Card:** "Did she."
  *Gilligan — Occupant: "Vanished. Is that what they told the
  child?"*

## UNIT 5 — B · THE TEST PROPOSED · QUIET (7w)

- **Card:** One dance, watched to the end
  *Gilligan — Dancer: "If she looks away once, she's a fraud and
  I win."*
- **Card:** House drops; programme lowers slowly
  *Gilligan — Occupant: "She dances it like her. Let us see if
  she finishes it."*

## UNIT 6 — A · CLIFFHANGER / THE FREEZE · SILENCE-BEAT (12w) + GAP 3

- **Card:** Her foot taps the exact rhythm
  *Gilligan — Dancer: "Nobody alive knows that rhythm. Nobody."*
- **Card:** "Once more. From the top."
  *Gilligan — Occupant: "Again. I have been waiting to ask for
  it again."*
- **Card:** Usher lets her stay; freeze; question card
  *Gilligan — Usher: "Whatever she is, she is house now. I will
  not be the one to empty that seat."*

**Gate A (connector test) — PASS.** Summaries join only by
but/therefore: she accuses the stranger, *but* the stranger
answers like she owns the seat; *therefore* the Dancer challenges
her with the dance, *but* the proof is the wrong colour and earns
no applause; *therefore* the book is opened, *but* the book names
the founder, and the stranger's "Did she" turns a ticket dispute
into an identity claim; *therefore* the Dancer stakes the one test
that can settle it; *but* the stranger passes it from memory —
*therefore* the Usher lets her stay, and the night ends on who
she is.

**Gate B (turn test) — PASS.** Every unit opens and closes on a
different value: claim disorder → ritual order imposed (U1);
claim unproven → claim doubted by its own proof (U2); doubt →
category (given, not sold) (U3); category → a named identity and
a tell (U4); identity claimed → test accepted, house dark (U5);
test running → test passed by the wrong person; consequence begun
(U6).

**Delta forecast (Pass 1):** ≥2 state changes promised —
(1) Occupant STATUS: intruder → sanctioned guest; (2) Dancer
KNOWS: the white stub's category + the stranger knows the
first-night number; (3) Usher: forty years of non-interference
broken by an act. Reconciled at lock in DECISION_LOG.md.
