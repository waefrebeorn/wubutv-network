Every vehicle restates the SKELETON verbatim (style, LOOK blocks,
palette/light/camera/texture); only the framing line, the action

## SKELETON (verbatim in every call)

"Exactly 10 seconds, landscape 16:9, 24fps photoreal video at a
small counting office. THE OFFICE: a long bone-white counter
across the middle of the room, a black paneled door with a brass
bell at frame left, dark shelves and ledgers behind the counter,
two dark blank split-flap board windows hanging over the counter,
always completely blank. Palette: ink black, bone white, cadmium
yellow lamp-glow; cold morning light from the door side; fine
film grain; a locked counter-wide camera; deadpan bureau comedy
played completely straight. THE CLERK: a woman with a sharp black
bob and round spectacles, a bone-white blouse, an ink-black vest,
cobalt-blue ink staining her fingertips; she stands behind the
counter with her big book. THE COUNTER: a gaunt bald man in a
black suit, a brass tally counter hanging on a chain at his
chest; he works the floor side of the counter. MR HALE: a
heavyset man in a dark suit carrying a clipboard. THE CHAMPION: a
very broad man in a dark tracksuit with a huge completely blank
white foam paddle strapped to his forehead. No text, no letters,
no numbers rendered anywhere in the scene; the only speech is
the quoted dialogue, spoken deadpan."

Action: "The Counter lays the folding chairs in neat rows of six
across the floor, two full rows inside this shot, working without
pausing. The Clerk speaks over the work. Then the Counter speaks
over the work. At the very end the door bell rings again and the

far end of the uncounted pile of chairs at frame right, arms

speaks first:
'I want one settled count by five o'clock.'

'One count, settled — that's all I want.'
Last, the broad man at the pile announces himself, loudly:
'I'm five — certify my five.'"

## U3 — resume from U2
Opening restatement (SR): "Continue in the same office: two full

folding chair now standing in the uncounted pile at frame right
where it was not a moment ago, the Champion planted at the pile's

Action: "At the head of the shot the Counter pulls the first
chair of the third row open — and the whole uncounted line of
folding chairs snaps open one after another in a domino wave
around the counter, bang-bang-bang; the last chair pops open
under Hale and sits him down hard. Nobody comments. The wave's
last chair pops open in front of the Champion and knocks him
back one step; his foam paddle tips sideways and he straightens
it without a word. Then the Counter counts the third row aloud,
batch by batch; as the wobbly chair comes up again in the pile
his hand stops over it, and he chalks one big white X on its
seat with a stick of white chalk. The Counter speaks first,
reciting the count:
'Six, twelve, eighteen — wobbly again.'
Then the Clerk answers from behind the counter:
'He needs twenty-four paid, Hale.'"
