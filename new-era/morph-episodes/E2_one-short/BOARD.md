## ST-1 — BEFORE / AFTER PAIR (one sentence)

Before: a hall manager with twenty-three chairs and a claim of
twenty-four is in control of the count. After: he has signed for
twenty-three, lost the chair he moved, and one of tonight's
guests will stand.

## ST-2 — THE ONE ARGUMENT (one sentence)

You cannot certify a number you wish for — only the chairs that
are actually there, each marked once.

## ST-7 — THE INVERSION (in words, before spectacle)

The man who came to prove the office comes up short was short
himself — and tried to borrow the missing chair from the count
by moving one chair back to be counted twice. The accuser of the
office is the author of his own shortfall.

- Want (never changes): to close the book with one settled

  was wrong. [GE-R: plain words — "settled count by five

**THE COUNTER** — counts the loads aloud, floor side.

**MR HALE, THE HALL MANAGER** (Ep. 2 customer — the antagonist,
