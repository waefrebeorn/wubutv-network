## PICTURE SUMMARIES (per 10-second unit, from frame inspection)

U1 (0–10s): A counting office: a long counter, shelves, a ledger.
A heavyset man bursts in with a trolley of folding chairs; the
trolley crashes against the counter and chairs spill. He shouts his
demand while the crash is still happening. A bald clerk-type catches
a falling chair. A woman with a black bob and glasses sits behind
the counter writing in a big book, unmoved. A black umbrella stands
on the shelf. An on-screen counter graphic appears reading
"CLAIMED 24".

U2 (10–20s): The bald man lays folding chairs in neat rows on the
floor. The woman states her want; the bald man states his. A very
broad man wearing a blank white foam paddle strapped to his
forehead enters, plants himself by the remaining pile of chairs,
and announces his claim. After he speaks, the heavyset man quietly
slides one chair out of the counted rows and back into the
uncounted pile with his foot while the bald man's back is turned.

U3 (20–30s): A line of folding chairs snaps open one after another
in a wave; the last one pops open under the heavyset man and seats
him hard. Another chair knocks the broad man back a step; his foam
paddle tips and he straightens it. The bald man counts the rows
aloud, stops at one chair, and chalks a big white X on its seat.
The on-screen counter reads "COUNTED 18".

U4 (30–40s): The bald man walks the rows chalking a white X on
every counted chair, speaking the marking rule to the camera and
asking the viewers what they counted. The heavyset man lunges for
a pay envelope on the counter; the woman's hand pins it flat. The
bald man then lifts one chair high: it bears TWO chalk X's.

U5 (40–50s): The bald man accuses the heavyset man, pointing at the
two-X chair. The woman tells the camera the viewers counted
twenty-three, same as the office. The heavyset man protests. The
woman stamps a certificate; the counter graphic settles at
"COUNTED 23 / CLAIMED 24". The bald man carries the two-X chair
behind the counter, out of reach. The heavyset man lets go of the
envelope. The broad man's foam paddle now bears a chalk X across
it. No certificate is given to the broad man.

U6 (50–60s): The heavyset man signs the certificate, the woman
pays him from the envelope, and he wheels the trolley out with
the remaining chairs — one chair fewer than he signed for; the
graphic reads "TAKES 22 / KEPT 1", and the two-X chair stays in
the office. He asks what happens when the office itself gets
counted. Nobody answers; the bald man begins counting the
office's own furniture aloud — shelf, umbrella, chair, book.
As this begins, the broad man walks out through the door in
profile, nodding once; the back of his foam paddle bears a
chalked "4" beside the chalk X. The final shot holds on a shelf:
one black umbrella beside one folded chair bearing chalk X's,
while the woman opens her book to a fresh page.
