## 1. CANON FACTS ADDED (append-only)

- **F-009** — Ep. 2 load: Mr Hale, a hall manager who rents
  chairs to weddings by the chair, brought folding chairs for
  a wedding that night; CLAIMED 24; COUNTED 23 (batches
  6 + 6 + 6 + 5). One of his chairs had broken at the hall;
  he brought twenty-three and claimed twenty-four.
- **F-010** — During the count, Hale moved the wobbly chair
  (one leg short) out of the counted rows and back into the
  uncounted pile, to have it counted twice. No character saw
  the move; the viewer did (GAP 1). He never admitted it in
  words.

  a chalk X. The wobbly chair turned up uncounted already
  bearing an X, and the marking round gave it a second X.
  Two X's on one chair was the evidence that settled Ep. 2.
- **F-012** — A recount has a price, stated by the Clerk
  before it was applied: "Recounts cost what got counted
  twice." The price for Ep. 2's recount was the moved chair
  itself. (The book's price doctrine, E1 F-004, now has a
  second, spoken form: prices in this office are stated
  before they are collected.)
- **F-013** — Ep. 2's transaction COMPLETED on screen: the
  Clerk stamped the certificate CERTIFIED 23; Hale signed
  it in his own hand, was paid at twenty-three, and left
  with twenty-two chairs. The wobbly chair stayed in the
  office. One guest at that night's wedding stands.
- **F-014** — The Counter named a move, not just a pattern,
  in front of a customer: "You moved it back to count
  twice." The Clerk heard it and answered with a verdict
  and a stamp, not a denial. (Advances E1 F-007.)
- **F-015** — [INVASION, 2026-11-16] During Ep. 2's count a
  second claimant entered: THE NUMBER CHAMPION, a broad
  competitor with a huge foam FIVE strapped to his forehead,
  who announced his number aloud — "I'm five — certify my
  five." — and queued at the uncounted pile to be counted
  in. The office processed his claim with its own machinery:
  the U4 marking round chalked a big X across his foam five
  (counted in, as an item); no certificate was issued to him
  in U5 (the office certifies what it counted, and what it
  counted was chairs); and the begun office count in U6
  reached him FOURTH, whereupon the Counter chalked a 4 on
  the foam beside the X. He accepted the counted number,
  nodded, and left. He claimed five and left a four. He left
  nothing behind in the room.
