## Transcript

HALE: Certify twenty-four chairs — hall pays by the chair.
CLERK: I want one settled count by five o'clock.
COUNTER: One count, settled — that's all I want.
CHAMPION: I'm five — certify my five.
COUNTER: Six, twelve, eighteen — wobbly again.
CLERK: He needs twenty-four paid, Hale.
COUNTER: Mark each chair, or one counts twice.
COUNTER: You counted with us — what'd you get?
CLERK: Recounts cost what got counted twice.
COUNTER: You moved it back to count twice.
CLERK: You counted twenty-three — same as us.
HALE: One broke; hall pays twenty-four.
CLERK: Twenty-four's a claim. Twenty-three's chairs.
HALE: I'll sign twenty-three, lose one chair, Clerk.
HALE: Keep one per recount, Counter — what happens counting this office?
COUNTER: Shelf: umbrella one, chair two, book three.
