## GATE E — locked script, cold read (2026-11-18)

**Input discipline:** the gate ran on the script's 11 lines plus
one-line picture summaries ONLY (listed below). No staging notes,
no bible, no brief were in the reader's hand. Every answer cites
a line or a picture beat. "PASS WITH RULING" does not exist.

**Gate input — lines:**
1. PLANNER: Thirty-one umbrellas. Count them before noon.
2. CLERK: I want the book closed even by noon.
3. COUNTER: Eighteen, nineteen, twenty — batch one stands now.
4. COUNTER: One count even — that's all I want today.
5. CLERK: We count what's brought.
6. CLERK: Receipts say what was paid, not what came.
7. PLANNER: You lost one in the crash. I packed thirty-one.
8. PLANNER: Recount them. My deposit rides on it.
9. CLERK: Every count in this book came up one short.
10. CLERK: Thirty is the count. The book closes even.
11. PLANNER: Then where does the one go?

**Gate input — picture summaries (one line per unit):**
U1: a door slams, a crate of umbrellas crashes across a counter,
a woman in peach demands a count; an office woman answers from
behind a big book. U2: a gaunt man counts umbrellas aloud in a
row, then stops with his hand in the air over a small remaining
pile. U3: the finished row reads thirty against her thirty-one;
the office woman holds up the receipt and rules on it. U4: the
woman accuses the office, slams a deposit envelope on the
counter; a held shot shows an empty shelf and her own umbrella
standing by the door. U5: a recount confirms thirty; the man
quietly puts her door umbrella on the shelf; the office woman
opens the book, stamps a certificate at thirty, and files the
deposit inside the book. U6: she signs at thirty, takes the
crate, reaches at the door for an umbrella that is not there,
and asks her question; the man turns and counts the shelf.

**Questions and answers:**
- *What is this episode about, in one sentence?* A customer
  claims thirty-one umbrellas; the office counts thirty, proves
  the claim was the thing that was short, and charges her for
  the recount. (Lines 1, 10; pictures U3, U5.)
- *What does each speaker want, and where do they say it?*
  PLANNER: thirty-one certified before noon (line 1). CLERK:
  the book closed even by noon (line 2). COUNTER: one count
  that comes out even (line 4). All three wants are spoken by
  their owners by U2. ✓
- *Who blocks them, and do they act or only speak?* The PLANNER
  is the present-tense antagonist: she accuses (line 7) and
  stakes her deposit to force a recount (line 8 + picture U4) —
  an act, not a speech. The office blocks her with the count
  and the book (lines 9–10). ✓
- *What is won or lost irreversibly, on screen?* The claim of
  thirty-one is stamped and signed dead at thirty (picture U5
  stamp; picture U6 signature). The stamp cannot be un-stamped
  inside the episode, and U6 does not revoke it — it completes
  it. ✓ (F-2 payoff quota.)
- *What does it cost, and who pays?* The PLANNER pays the
  recount's price: her deposit stays inside the book and her
  own umbrella stays on the shelf (pictures U5, U6). ✓ (F-7
  cost beat.)
- *Does every line parse alone, out of order?* Yes — each line
  is a complete claim, want, ruling, or question; no line needs
  the previous line's wording to parse (the shuffle test).
  Numbers are carried by the board in picture, so no line is a
  fragment of another line. ✓
- *Is any line twined or echo-formed?* No. Content recurs
  (thirty-one → thirty) as new social events — a claim, an
  accusation, a verdict — never as repeated form. ✓ (F-17.)
- *Does anyone speak a prompt or production detail?* No. The
  board, the book, and the shelf are objects in the world; no
  character names a mechanism. ✓ (F-16.)
- *Where does the episode end, and what is owed next?* On her
  unanswered question (line 11) over a freeze that shows the
  shelf holding her umbrella. Owed: the audience's own count
  of the load, which Part 2 must open by answering (hanger
  H-1). ✓

**GATE E VERDICT: PASS** — first read, no repair pass required.
