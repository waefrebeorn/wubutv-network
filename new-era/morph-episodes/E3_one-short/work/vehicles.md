SHARED FRAMING (all units, adapted per unit):
"Exactly 10 seconds, 16:9 widescreen, 1280x720, 24fps. Photorealistic live-action
scene in a counting office: a long white counter, dark shelves of ledgers and
boxes, cold morning light raking in from a door at frame left, warm lamp glow
over the counter. Every in-world surface is blank and unmarked: the split-flap
board's two windows are blank dark shutters, book pages are blank ruled texture,
papers, envelopes and cards are blank cream — no text, no letters, no numbers
anywhere in the scene."

VOICE SPECS (verbatim from BRIEF §1):
CLERK: "age ~55 · gender female · timbre dry, low, unhurried · pace measured, complete sentences · accent neutral bureau · trait never raises her voice; prices and verdicts land flat · breaks never"
COUNTER: "age ~60 · gender male · timbre flat, dry, low · pace slow, numbers evenly spaced · accent neutral · trait fragments and counting chains; verdicts in five words or fewer · breaks never — he stops mid-count instead of erring"
PLANNER: "age ~32 · gender female · timbre bright, carrying, theatrical · pace fast, sentences tumble · accent fruit-soap melodrama register, everything at full expression · trait states wants as deadlines · breaks never — she escalates, she does not crack"
