## Law-tension rulings (the room ruled; the parent may overrule)

1. **Episode word band.** E2 law F-5's 95–110-word episode
   band conflicts with the pass-2 per-unit caps (target 14–17
   words/unit ⇒ ~84–102 ceiling, and this show's own bible
   band is 80–90, hard cap 95, with a QUIET unit and a
   SILENCE-BEAT unit by design). RULING: the per-unit caps and
   the bible band govern (pass-2 supersedes where they
   conflict; the bible is the show's own law). Episode total:
   **80 words** — inside the bible band, under every cap.
2. **Speaker count.** F-14 allows two designed speakers; the
   bible's engine needs the rotating customer to speak (her
   want is line 1 — B6), and the invasion law needs the
   invader to hold ≥2 real lines. RULING: the two designed
   speakers are the CLERK and the COUNTER; the PLANNER speaks
   under F-14's logged-exception path as ONE exception voice,
   and she is simultaneously the customer and the invader —
   merging the chairs keeps the voice count at three instead
   of four (E2 ran four; its ear-test observation about the
   one-line invader is answered by giving the merged chair
   four lines and the episode's antagonist acts). No fourth
   voice exists in this episode.
3. **Camp A at U1.** Camp B is the default; U1 runs Camp A
   under the template's noted-exception path (director note
   in BRIEF §4.1-U1) because the BLAM ritual — claim and
   answer in one breath — is the show's opening shape, and
   14 words / 2 turns / 1 turn change sits inside every Camp
   A cap. All other units are Camp B.
4. **VOICE_ROUTE.** No pin/TTS/lip-sync route exists in the
   house toolset, so the route is declared NATIVE_TEXT_ONLY
   with the template's waiver line filled, spec text demoted
   to drift-reducer, and enrollment anchors cut from the E2
   master for the two designed voices. The Planner, new, is
   capped at PASS-EAR-ONLY this part; her enrollment is
   banked from Part 1's master for Part 2.
5. **Signature short lines.** The carrier line (4 words) and
   the hanger question (6 words) sit under the F-26 8–14-word
   line-economy target. RULING: they are the show's audio logo
   and its cliff form respectively — the bible's own line law
   (carrier + catchphrase discipline) keeps them; every other
   line in the episode is 6–9 words, and no line is a fragment
   that needs another line to parse.
