## THE SCRIPT

Board chain (joint grammar; the board is a Method C post
graphic on a declared-blank split-flap board — the generator
never renders a number): U1 flips CLAIMED 31 on the slam →
U2 cliff COUNTED 20 → U3 cliff COUNTED 30 | CLAIMED 31 (the
one-short fact lands in picture) → U4 board holds → U5 board
settles COUNTED 30 | CLAIMED 31 and the certificate is stamped
30 → U6 board unchanged over the freeze; the streak card flips
41 → 42 (post). The office persists across every joint; the
pile shrinks, the shelf gains one.

### U1 — 14 words · 2 lines · BLAM · Camp A exception (see BRIEF)

**Picture / BLAM:** FRAME 0 IS MID-SLAM — the office door
SLAMS, the bell rings, and a crate of umbrellas CRASHES across
the counter, three sound events stacked inside 0.5s (named
impact ≤1.0s, E1 satisfied by construction). The board flips
at ~0.3s on the slam to **CLAIMED 31 | COUNTED —**. No dead
head (A2), no ramp-from-rest (V2): the camera pushes in WITH
the load. In the CCTV corner of the same wide: **the shelf,
EMPTY**, and by the door the PLANNER's own umbrella, standing
where she shook it off — she came in under it, out of the
rain. She is mid-stride behind the crate, peach-satin suit,
giant foam peach fascinator, maximum-expression fruit-soap
register against the office's deadpan (the invasion's first
fact is visual: her world is louder than this room). Her
demand is the episode's first speech at ~0.5s, in flight off
the slam. The CLERK is already behind the counter, book open;
the COUNTER is floor-side, righting spilled umbrellas.

> PLANNER: Thirty-one umbrellas. Count them before noon.
> — 6 words (want-statement; her want is her first line;
> conflict stated in line 1)
>
> CLERK: I want the book closed even by noon.
> — 8 words · CONNECTIVE (her want, plain, spoken by her in
> U1; the office's terms answer the claim in the same breath)

*Physical moment:* the crash itself — one umbrella slides off
the crate and the Counter catches it by the handle without
looking up.

**Joint U1→U2 (strong reference):** last frame = counter-wide,
crate left spilling umbrellas centre, board CLAIMED 31, Clerk
behind counter right at the open book, Counter floor-side left
holding the caught umbrella, Planner centre behind the crate,
her own umbrella standing by the door, shelf empty in the CCTV
corner. U2 opens on that exact frame, same positions, same
light; only the work has started.

### U2 — 15 words · 2 lines · the count begins

**Picture:** The Counter counts batch one aloud, in frame —
umbrellas laid in a row along the counter, honest piles,
countable-looking, never framed as an exact-count promise:
**the board carries the arithmetic.** His first line is a
number, per his card. The board climbs to **COUNTED 20** at
the cliff. On the counter between the Clerk and the Planner
sits the hire receipt, slapped down with the load in U1 — the
proof object, shown before it is ever explained.

> COUNTER: Eighteen, nineteen, twenty — batch one stands now.
> — 7 words (the count chain; his first line is a number)
>
> COUNTER: One count even — that's all I want today.
> — 8 words · CONNECTIVE (his want, plain, spoken by him by
> U2: to finish one count that comes out even)

**GAP 1 (after U2's turn) — carrier: THE COUNTER'S STOPPED
HAND.** 2–3s, no dialogue. Starting batch two, his hand stops
mid-air over the pile: he has felt it run light — the pile is
smaller than eleven more umbrellas should make it (his
questioning-job NOTICE, unspoken). The board holds at COUNTED
20, one beat behind the pile in front of it. The carrier
cannot collapse into dead air: a stopped hand over a short
pile is a picture, not a pause.

**Joint U2→U3:** last frame = one full row of twenty along
the counter, the remaining pile beside the crate visibly
smaller than the claim needs, board COUNTED 20, Counter's
hand stopped over the pile, Clerk at her book, Planner
watching the pile, not the hand. U3 opens on the same wide;
only the Clerk has turned a page.

### U3 — 12 words · 2 lines · QUIET unit (slate fix F4.1 stands)

**Picture:** Batch two is counted in frame behind the Clerk's

> CLERK: We count what's brought.

### U4 — 16 words · 2 lines · accusation + the stake

**Picture:** The one accusation chain turns (only one per
episode). The Planner accuses the office of losing one in the
crash — and then ACTS: she slams her deposit envelope onto
the counter and stakes it on a recount. Money seen at risk,
in picture. Her demand is the present-tense antagonist move
the episode's payoff answers: the recount she forces is the
recount whose price she will pay. Mid-unit, one physical
moment: an umbrella rolls off the counted row and she catches
it against her hip without breaking her line — the load is
still moving even while the talk is still.

> PLANNER: You lost one in the crash. I packed thirty-one.
> — 9 words (accusation-with-proof: the receipt is her proof;
> the crash is her theory)
>
> PLANNER: Recount them. My deposit rides on it.
> — 7 words · CONNECTIVE (the stake stated before it is
> applied; her action in the same unit: the envelope is on
> the counter)

**GAP 2 (after U4's peak turn) — carrier: THE CCTV SHELF
ANGLE.** 2–3s, no dialogue. The CCTV corner holds on the two
addresses of the missing one in a single frame: **the shelf,
still EMPTY — and by the door, the Planner's own umbrella,
standing where she left it.** Nobody in the room looks at
either. The viewer does the arithmetic the cast won't: the
thirty-first umbrella was never in the crate. The carrier is
evidence, not air — compress the seconds and the two
addresses still read.

**Joint U4→U5:** last frame = CCTV hold resolving back to
counter-wide: deposit envelope on the counter under nobody's
hand yet, Planner facing the Clerk across it, board COUNTED
30 | CLAIMED 31, the Counter already turning back to the pile
to begin the recount. U5 opens on that wide; the recount is
already moving.

### U5 — 17 words · 2 lines · the reveal + the verdict

**Picture:** The recount runs in frame behind the dialogue
and confirms thirty. **THE PRICE IS COLLECTED IN PICTURE:**
mid-recount, the Counter passes the door with the last batch,
lifts the Planner's own umbrella from the door stand without
a word, and sets it on the shelf as he passes — eyes on the
pile, never on the shelf. The Clerk opens the book — every
count the office has ever run, each one short, in her hand —
and delivers the authority reveal (she keeps the book, so the
deepest reveal is hers). She stamps the certificate **30** —
ink on paper, irreversible — and slides the deposit envelope
into the book as its marker: the recount's price, kept in the
record. The board settles **COUNTED 30 | CLAIMED 31**.

> CLERK: Every count in this book came up one short.
> — 9 words · CONNECTIVE (the authority reveal: the pattern
> is older than this customer, this load, this office's day)
>
> CLERK: Thirty is the count. The book closes even.
> — 8 words · CONNECTIVE (the verdict: the count was never
> short — the claim was; her want from U1 is won, on screen)

~1s reaction hold on the Planner's face after the verdict
before U6.

**Joint U5→U6:** last frame = certificate stamped 30 on the
counter, book open with the deposit envelope inside it as a
marker, the Planner's umbrella now standing ON THE SHELF in
the CCTV corner, the counted row of thirty along the counter,
Planner facing the certificate, pen beside it. U6 opens on
that frame; the pen is put into the Planner's hand.

### U6 — 6 words · 1 line · payoff completed + the hanger

**Picture:** The payoff completes and is NOT revoked: the
Planner signs the certificate — **30**, her own hand — takes
the crate of thirty umbrellas, and goes to the door. Rain is
audible beyond it. Her hand rises for the umbrella that is
not there. The COUNTER acts (his questioning job, U6): he
turns from the pile and counts the shelf instead — one item —
and stops on her umbrella. He says nothing. **THE RATIONED
SELF-AWARENESS BEAT (F-21, this episode's one):** in the
freeze, the Counter's eyes lift from the shelf to the lens
and hold — the beckon, unspoken: the home cast knows, and the
viewer is the only one who can answer for them. The board
holds COUNTED 30 | CLAIMED 31; the streak card flips to 42
(post). Her question is spoken and carried on screen as the
part's end card (post text): it is the count-along call —
*post your count of the load* — booked as hanger H-1.

> PLANNER: Then where does the one go?
> — 6 words (THE HANGER — her unanswered question; the cliff
> is a character's, never the board's alone)

**GAP 3 (final 2–3s of U6) — carrier: THE SHELF FREEZE.**
Freeze on the CCTV corner: the shelf now holds **ONE
UMBRELLA — hers** — the Planner at the open door in the
foreground edge, hand still raised, the Counter mid-count
turned toward the shelf with his eyes on the lens, the
Clerk's face at the frame's other edge, book open, deposit
envelope inside it. Nobody moves. The world state in the
freeze is visibly different from U1's first frame: a shelf
fuller by one, a customer poorer by one umbrella and one
deposit, a certificate signed, a streak one longer.

## PRODUCTION NOTES (binding on the build; full spec in BRIEF.md)

1. **ONE SPEAKER PER UNIT** (Camp B), except U1's noted Camp A
   exception. No unit carries a second voice "for colour."
2. **THE BOARD CARRIES THE ARITHMETIC:** the split-flap board
   is a Method C post graphic (PIL plate) on a declared-blank
   board — CLAIMED 31 at ~0.3s on the U1 slam, COUNTED 20 at
   the U2 cliff, COUNTED 30 | CLAIMED 31 at the U3 cliff,
   settle held through U5–U6. FOREST #1F6B3A accent, furniture
   bottom-left, bug bottom-right, phone-legible. Piles and
   rows are honest piles; never ask the generator for
   exactly-countable umbrellas. The certificate, the book's
   pages, the receipt, the streak card's number and the end
   card are likewise post plates on blank surfaces.
3. **SHELF CONTINUITY:** dedicated CCTV-corner angle, own
   shelf PEAK panel. Part 1 shelf state: OPEN empty → CLOSE
   one umbrella (peach-handled, the Planner's own). Verify
   against DECISION_LOG state ledger before assembly.
4. **BLAM OPENING:** frame 0 mid-slam; slam + bell + crash
   stacked inside 0.5s; first speech onset ~0.5s; board flip
   ~0.3s. No fade-in, no locked frames, no crackle intro.
5. **GAP DEFENSE:** all three gaps carry plot (a short pile
   felt / the two addresses of the missing one / a shelf that
   has grown). A gap whose carrier does not reach the screen
   is a story-gate failure — repaired, never
   disclosure-shipped.
6. **JOINT LAW (strong reference):** every joint above is a
   last-frame → first-frame continuation (world state,
   wardrobe, positions, light); the outgoing unit's END STATE
   is quoted verbatim at the head of the next unit's vehicle
   (BRIEF §4). Per-joint EYE-verified cohesion logged in the
   production GATE_LOG; seam numbers alone never clear a
   joint.
7. **THE PLANNER'S UMBRELLA IS A TRACKED PROP:** peach-handled,
   visibly distinct from the hire umbrellas (black, uniform).
   Its three addresses — door (U1–U4), Counter's hand (U5),
   shelf (U5 tail → U6 freeze) — are state changes staged
   ACROSS cuts or in plain view, never as a generated morph.
8. **CAMERA UNIFORM / PALETTE / FORMAT:** counter-wide master,
   CCTV corner owns the shelf, close angle owns the book;
   INK/BONE/CADMIUM, COBALT hers, FOREST the board; one grain
   pass; 1280×720, 24fps, 1440 frames, CRF 18 by size
   differential, AAC, faststart; one assembly pass owns the
   joined intermediate; probe before and after the master
   encode.
9. **NO IN-WORLD TEXT FROM THE GENERATOR:** board, book,
   receipt, certificate, streak card, end card — all declared
   blank in the vehicles, all lettered in post. A take with
   legible invented text on a declared-blank surface is a
   reject class, not a nit.
