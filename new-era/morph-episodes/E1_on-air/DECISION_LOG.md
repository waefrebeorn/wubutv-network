## PASS 6 — CRITIC (fresh context; located objections only)

**Round 1 — cold read:** followed every beat cold; told all four
voices apart; stumbled in two places, filed as located objections:

1. **U2, Laura's notice line** ("Strip's ahead of the picture") —
   *Objection:* a premiere viewer has never been taught the strip
   rule; the line risks reading as lore (Gate E / C2 / B9).
   *Resolution:* the fact is staged, not explained — the strip is
   live and leading from frame 0 (bible BLAM shape), and U2's
   staging now specifies the strip's peak firing visibly ~0.5s
   before the monitor's mouth moves, in the same frame as the
   line. The line names what the picture is already showing
   (B8 order: object first, name second). Lines unchanged;
   staging note added. **Resolved.**
2. **U5, Peter's collapse line** — draft read "I didn't sell a
   singer. I sold UNC." *Objection:* "sold UNC" can read as
   literal person-selling to a cold viewer (Gate E plainness).
   *Resolution:* Dialogue Writer rewrote to the bible outline's
   own wording — **"I sold my boss"** — which is colder-readable
   (the relationship is the joke and the wound). Recount: U5
   14 → 15 words, still MIXED (at ceiling), episode 86 → 87,
   still in band. Pass 4 re-run on U5: ✓. **Resolved by rewrite.**
3. **U1, Laura's "By hand"** — *Objection (considered, not
   upheld):* unexplained at first hearing. *Room's answer:* it is
   an intra-episode plant that fires in U6, fifty seconds later,
   in picture (the lever pull) — legibility is retroactive inside
   the same minute, and the plant is registered (PL-7) with its
   payoff cell filled. **No change.**

**Round 2:** re-read cold after the U5 rewrite; no stumbles; the
legibility test (state each speaker's unsaid sentence from the
dialogue alone) matched Pass 3's recorded unsaid sentences for
all four speakers. **No further objections. Critic closed at 2
