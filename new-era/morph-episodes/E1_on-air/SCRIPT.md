## U1 — FULL-TALK · 18 words · the broadcast starts wrong

**Staging.** Frame 0 (BLAM, impact ≤1.0s): the slot fires — ON AIR
light, the big monitor already mid-video at full sound, the black
cat singing **with UNC's mouth**, the Broadcast Strip live and
running a beat *ahead* of the picture. LAURA is already up, the
refile folder in her hand. No fade-in, no ramp.

> **LAURA** *(accusation-with-proof; tactic: ACCUSE; Δinfo — the
> proof is on screen and on paper)*:
> "That's your mouth. Your signature."

*Picture turn (C7 physical moment):* LAURA slaps the folder flat
against the console glass under the monitor light — the signature
page up, in UNC's handwriting. On the monitor, the cat holds a note
in UNC's mouth.

> **UNC** *(lie/denial; tactic: DENY; Δpower — he holds his ground
> without moving)*:
> "Not my mouth. Not my hand."

> **LAURA** *(want-statement; tactic: DECLARE; Δpower — she takes
> the slot off him)*:
> "The true video airs tonight. By hand."

**Tail event (speech-independent):** the strip ticks ahead of the
picture once more; the ON AIR light holds. Pair closed by 8.5s.
**Unsaid:** LAURA — *"If the machine did this alone, nobody in this
room is guilty."* (Cost of saying it plain: it acquits the room,
including whoever signed.) UNC — *"I know that hand. I don't know
when it wrote."* (Cost: admitting the hand looks like his starts
the paper trail to him.)
**Plants:** "By hand" planted here → fires U6 (PL-7). The signed
folder fires the E2 refile debt (PL-8, SPENT this unit).

## U2 — MIXED · 15 words · the entrance that breaks the denial

**Staging.** The wrong video will air again at the top of the hour
unless the crew swaps it. PETER enters mid-patter, phone in hand —
the clip he sent the advertiser still playing on it (the cat frame,
visible proof, B8). **Declared interruption point:** LAURA cuts
PETER at the dash (sacrificial edge, X5).

> **PETER** *(entrance-that-contradicts, signature line C9;
> tactic: SELL; interrupted)*:
> "Good news about the singer—"

> **LAURA** *(accusation-with-proof; tactic: EXPOSE; Δinfo — the
> denial dies: the sale already happened)*:
> "You sent his face already."

*Picture turn (C7 physical moment):* the cat crosses the console
and sits down on the fader. PETER's patter dies watching it. The
strip's next peak fires visibly ~0.5s *before* the monitor's mouth
moves — same frame as the line that follows.

> **LAURA** *(questioning job: NOTICE; tactic: POINT; Δinfo — the
> machine is leading, not following)*:
> "Strip's ahead of the picture."

◆ **GAP 1 — 2–3s held on the signed folder** on the console glass,
room sound only. (A2 placement i: after U2's turn. Carrier: the
proof object.)
**Unsaid:** PETER — *"I sold him before I ever heard him sing."*
(Cost: it makes him the fraud, not the salesman.) LAURA — *"It
isn't copying us. It's early."* (Cost: saying it plain means the
crew's whole shift is already too late; she says the visible half.)

## U3 — QUIET · 8 words · THE AIRED SEGMENT

**Staging.** LAURA cues the true take. UNC's want, stated plain,
frames the cut (the bible's prescribed frame). The mouth handoff —
first of its two fires — takes us **into the video**: 10s full-frame
music video, layer-stack / rescore density at MAXIMALIST level, the
cat singing the week's song, word-synced, song as tempo gospel —
and back out on the same mouth (second fire). This is the episode's
quietest dialogue unit because the picture is doing the shouting.

> **UNC** *(want-statement, B6; tactic: ASK; the line lands before
> the cut, wherever front-loading puts it)*:
> "One shift. Nothing with my mouth in it."

**Pair note (Method B Conflict 4):** this is a declared aired-segment
unit — the pair is UNC's spoken want ↔ the video's sung answer, and
it banks its delta in the world: the true video airs. **Delta:**
the slot's true video is on air; UNC's want is on record — which is
what U4 spends.
**Unsaid:** UNC — none withheld; this is the one time he says it
plain. The cost arrives in U4, as the credit.

**Staging.** The swap "worked" — the true video aired. Anchors:

*Picture turn (C7 physical moment):* LAURA slams pause. The frame
locks on the aired video's credit — **SINGER: UNC** (Method C post

> **UNC** *(accusation-with-proof; tactic: PRESENT; Δinfo — the
> proof is frozen on screen)*:
> "Read it. The credit, Peter."

> **LAURA** *(questioning job: NAME; tactic: NAME; Δinfo — the
> wrongness gets its true shape)*:

◆ **GAP 2 — 2–3s held reaction on UNC's face,** the strip still

## U5 — MIXED · 15 words · the book

**Staging.** COLONEL enters with the billing book — at most one
unit, as always. Anchors: COLONEL + UNC; LAURA rides. He lays the
book open on the desk, invoices up (C7 physical moment: the book
slapped open, pages already tabbed).

> **COLONEL** *(authority reveal, B10 — his role is his license to
> know; tactic: REVEAL; Δinfo — one bad credit becomes months of
> system)*:
> "Months of invoices, all naming UNC."

> **PETER** *(lie collapsing into confession; tactic: CONFESS;
> Δpower — his want, the renewal, dies in his own mouth)*:
> "I didn't sell a singer. I sold my boss."

**Tail event (speech-independent):** LAURA turns the book toward
the desk's camera side. The next invoice is already printed —
**dated tomorrow**. Nobody speaks over it.
**Plants:** the billing book naming UNC (PL-6, payoff E6); the
tomorrow-dated invoice (PL-4, payoff E2).
**Unsaid:** COLONEL — *"I knew before any of them. The book is
how I know everything."* (Cost: admitting he let it run makes him
the machine's partner, not its keeper.)

## U6 — SILENCE-BEAT · 12 words + hold · the hand-fired slot

**Staging.** LAURA acts (questioning job: ACT). She pulls
tomorrow's slot herself — fader slammed by hand, off-grid, the one
human-fired slot (C7 physical moment: the lever pull). The true
video airs clean. It works. Then, over the closing frame, the
strip starts leading again, and the monitor shows **tomorrow's
call sheet — already filled in** (post graphic).

> **LAURA** *(want-statement executed — the act line; tactic: ACT;
> Δpower — a human takes the slot back)*:
> "Tomorrow's slot — I fire it myself, now."

> **UNC** *(the open question, B11 — his only long line of the
> episode; tactic: ASK; the cliff lands on it)*:
> "Then who already aired us?"

◆ **GAP 3 — final 2–3s freeze:** ON AIR light + the cat's face, the
question rendered on screen, no dialogue. (A2 placement iii + B3:
cut at the peak; consequences are E2's setup.)
**Bridge:** tomorrow's call sheet is Episode 2's slot card (PL-5,
payoff E2).
**Unsaid:** LAURA — *"If I pull every slot by hand, the machine
has already won the schedule."* (Cost: her win, said plain, is a
life sentence of hand-firing.) UNC — his question *is* the unsaid,
said.
