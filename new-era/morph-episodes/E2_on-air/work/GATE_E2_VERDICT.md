# F-9 GATE E (on the finished master) — COLD-READER VERDICT

Reader note: these answers come only from the transcript and
picture summaries in GATE_E2_INPUT.md. No script, bible, or
production notes were consulted.

## 1. What does each named person want?

- **Laura:** She wants UNC's name off the call sheet.
  Citation: U1 transcript — LAURA: "This sheet knew
  yesterday. I want his name off it." The "his" is only
  identified later, in U5, when the Colonel "crosses UNC's
  name off the call sheet with a thick marker" (U5 picture).
  She also acts to expose Peter live: in U5 she "slams [the
  fader] open and locks it down, then turns to the camera"
  (U5 picture) and says "Viewers, red means live. You heard
  his confession?" (U5 transcript).

- **UNC:** He wants to not sing / not perform.
  Citation: U2 transcript — UNC: "I want one quiet shift.
  My mouth stays shut, mine." This is consistent with U3,
  where he says "I don't know how. Show me how, Peter."
  (U3 transcript) — although what exactly he doesn't know
  how to do is not spelled out (see Q5).

- **Peter:** He wants the advertiser renewed, and he wants
  UNC heard live (i.e. to sing/perform live) to get that.
  Citation: U1 transcript — PETER: "Then hear him live.
  I want the advertiser renewed." He restates the pressure
  in U2: "Your mouth shut pays nobody. Red means live,
  UNC." (U2 transcript). In U1 picture he "wheels in a
  microphone stand and locks it in front of the seated UNC
  like a product on display."

- **The Colonel:** He wants "billable truth."
  Citation: U5 transcript — COLONEL: "I want billable
  truth. Advertiser cancelled. Name comes off." The same
  line is heard earlier, partly, on a desk speaker in U2:
  "I want billable truth." What "billable truth" actually
  means is never defined (see Q5).

- **The showman:** He wants the singer's place.
  Citation: U2 transcript — a showman shouting through
  glass, partly heard: "The singer's place is mine."
  In U2 picture he is "a broad showman with a giant foam
  number FOUR strapped to his forehead [who] points at
  the desk and shouts."

## 2. Who wins something, who loses something, and is any of it undone by the end?

- **Laura wins her stated want.** The Colonel in U5
  "crosses UNC's name off the call sheet with a thick
  marker" (U5 picture), matching her U1 demand "I want
  his name off it."

- **UNC loses, then partly recovers, and escapes singing.**
  Loss 1: U2 picture — "Peter takes the name badge off
  UNC's chest and drops it into a grey OUT tray."
  Loss 2: U5 picture — his name is crossed off the sheet.
  Partial undo: U5 picture — the Colonel "picks UNC's
  badge out of the tray and clips it back onto his
  chest." So the badge loss IS undone; the name crossed
  off the sheet is NOT undone within the episode (the
  crossed-out sheet is still on the desk in the final
  freeze, U6 picture). He does keep his mouth from
  singing: the singing in the episode is done by the cat
  (U3, U6) and by Peter's demonstration (U4), not by UNC.

- **Peter loses the advertiser and loses his own game.**
  Loss 1: U5 transcript — COLONEL: "Advertiser cancelled,"
  the opposite of Peter's U1 want, "I want the advertiser
  renewed."
  Loss 2: U6 picture — the fresh call sheet "reads
  SINGER: PETER on the first line," i.e. Peter himself is
  now booked as the singer, the role he was trying to
  force on UNC. His stunned face is in the final freeze
  (U6 picture). Neither loss is undone.

- **The showman loses his claim.** He claimed "The
  singer's place is mine" (U2), but the new sheet names
  Peter as SINGER (U6 picture), and in U6 the showman
  "loses his grin and backs away down the corridor."

- **The Colonel gets to impose his outcome** — invoice
  stamped CANCELLED and torn, name crossed off, badge
  restored (all U5 picture) — but whether he got the
  "billable truth" he said he wanted is never shown in
  words; the material does not say what the truth was.

## 3. Why is Peter's name on the new call sheet at the end — what caused that, in the episode's own terms?

This chain IS followable from the material, in four steps:

1. Peter states the rule himself. U4 transcript —
   PETER: "Whoever sings here goes in the next video and
   sheet."
2. Peter then sings/performs himself. U4 picture —
   "Peter performs a huge one-man music-video
   demonstration — chair spin, jazz hands, strutting
   around the desk — while monitors show costumed
   versions of himself singing along." He also supplies
   the lyric: U4 transcript — PETER: "A singer is just a
   mouth with paperwork, Laura!"
3. It was live/recorded. A red microphone light is
   glowing from U1 onward, Peter and Laura both state
   "Red means live" (U2 and U5 transcripts), and in U5
   Laura "slams [the fader] open and locks it down"
   (U5 picture) and asks the viewers "You heard his
   confession?" (U5 transcript).
4. The result plays back and is printed. U6 picture —
   "the cat singing Peter's 'mouth with paperwork' line
   in a man's singing voice" on the big monitor, and the
   printer produces the sheet reading "SINGER: PETER."

In short: Peter is caught by his own stated rule because
he, not UNC, did the singing, and Laura made sure it went
out live.

## 4. What is the showman with the number four doing in this episode, and what happens to him?

What the material supports: he is an outside rival
claimant for the singer's job. In U2 he is behind the
glass, points at the desk and shouts "The singer's place
is mine" (U2 transcript and picture), while "people on a
bench behind him stand up to watch" (U2 picture). He
never enters the room and never speaks to anyone directly.

What happens to him: he loses. In U6, after the new sheet
prints SINGER: PETER, "the showman with the foam four
loses his grin and backs away down the corridor"
(U6 picture).

What the material does NOT explain: what the number FOUR
means, who or what "NUMBER FIVE: CHAMPION" on the second
line of the new sheet (U6 picture) refers to, and whether
that line — rather than Peter being named singer — is
what defeats him. His want and his defeat read clearly;
the numbering system around him does not.

## 5. Does anything in this episode not make sense to you?

Yes, specifically:

1. **"This sheet knew yesterday."** (U1, Laura.) U1
   picture says the monitor was already playing the cat
   video and the sheet already had that song as its first
   line "before anyone touches the desk." A prophetic /
   pre-written sheet is shown but never explained.
2. **"You wrote this demo, Peter. The sheet never did."**
   (U3, Laura.) What "this demo" is, and what it proves
   about Peter, is not established in the transcript or
   pictures given.
3. **"Billable truth."** The Colonel's stated want (U2,
   U5) is never defined, and the material does not say
   what truth was billed, found, or confessed, or how it
   causes "Advertiser cancelled."
4. **"You heard his confession?"** (U5, Laura.) Peter
   makes no statement labelled or shaped as a confession
   in the transcript. The nearest candidate is "A singer
   is just a mouth with paperwork" (U4), which reads as a
   jibe, not a confession; the link has to be guessed.
5. **UNC's "I don't know how. Show me how, Peter."**
   (U3.) How to do what — sing, work the desk, keep
   quiet? Not specified. Peter's U4 demonstration
   appears to be the answer, but the question itself is
   unanchored.
6. **The cat.** A cat sings on the monitor (U1, U3), a
   "real cat duets silently on the console" (U4 picture),
   and the cat sings Peter's line "in a man's singing
   voice" (U6). Who the cat is in this workplace, and why
   its video is the sheet's first line, is not explained.
7. **NUMBER FOUR vs NUMBER FIVE: CHAMPION.** The
   showman's foam FOUR (U2) and the new sheet's second
   line "NUMBER FIVE: CHAMPION" (U6) imply a ranking
   contest that is never stated. Who Five is cannot be
   answered from this material.
8. **The Colonel's authority** is shown in action (he
   cancels, crosses off, restores the badge — U5) but
   never introduced; a first-time viewer must simply
   accept that this entering older man with a billing
   book can overrule Peter.

## OVERALL VERDICT: PASS

A first-time viewer can follow the core story from
transcript + pictures alone:

- **Who wants what:** all five wants are stated in
  dialogue — Laura: name off the sheet (U1); UNC: a
  quiet shift, mouth shut (U2); Peter: advertiser
  renewed via UNC live (U1); Colonel: billable truth
  (U5); showman: the singer's place (U2).
- **What is won/lost:** Laura's removal happens on
  screen (U5); the advertiser Peter wanted is cancelled
  (U5); UNC's badge is taken (U2) and returned (U5)
  while his name stays crossed off; the showman's claim
  fails (U6).
- **Why the ending happens:** Peter's own rule —
  "Whoever sings here goes in the next video and sheet"
  (U4) — catches Peter because he performed the
  demonstration himself (U4 picture) while Laura held
  the fader open live (U5), so the printer names him:
  SINGER: PETER (U6).

The pass is for that central chain. The items in Q5 —
above all the unexplained numbering (FOUR / NUMBER
FIVE: CHAMPION), the undefined "billable truth," and
the prophetic sheet — are genuine comprehension gaps
around the edges, but they do not break the
want / win-loss / ending chain the episode turns on.
