# ON AIR — Episode 2 BOARD (Passes 1–2: storyline + breakdown)

**Working title (ST-10):** "Show Me How" — states the setup (a
demonstration is demanded) and the promised turn (the demonstration
is the trap). Frame one is the contrast: yesterday's printed sheet,
already coming true on the monitor.

**Skeleton (named, §2 menu): S7 — THE DEMONSTRATION TRAP.**
The captive (UNC, ordered to sing into the live desk mic to prove
he is "the singer") answers with a plain statement of incompetence
— "Show me how" — and the captor (PETER, this episode's present-
tense antagonist) demonstrates, narrating his own competence, and
confesses the sale into a microphone that is already live. The
captive hands the captor's own lesson back as a silent pointing
gesture in U5 (his spoken restatement was cut in the Gate E
repair — see DECISION_LOG).
Engine rotation (F-6): E1 ran B1 (proof → denial → entrance →
authority reveal). E2 does NOT. E2's engine is a want-vs-want
contest run as a demonstration trap: PETER acts on screen in the
present tense (wheels in the mic, puts UNC's badge in the out-tray,
fires the demonstration himself) and LAURA/UNC oppose him with a
trap, not an accusation chain.

**Before/after pair (ST-1):** "UNC is billed as the singer and
booked to be fired at six → UNC's name is crossed off the sheet,
his badge is back on, and PETER is the name on tomorrow's sheet."

**One-sentence argument (ST-2):** Who owns a mouth — the man who
sells it on paper, or the man it belongs to?

**Persisted situation + one new element (SR-4):** Persisted: the
broadcast slot and its printed call sheet (E1's system). The ONE
new element: the live desk demonstration for the advertiser.
Nothing else is new.

**Audience secret (ST-5):** From frame 0 the desk mic's red light
is ON in the foreground — the desk is already live. The viewer
sees it in U1; no character states that THIS mic is live until U5.
PETER states the general rule in U2 ("Red means live") without
looking at his own desk — the rule is stated before it is tested
(ST-4), and the gap between rule and notice is the episode.

**Test stated before tested (ST-4):** The live-mic rule is spoken
by PETER in U2. It is first applied in U4 (his demonstration) and
settled in U5 (the advertiser heard it).

**Escalation, itemized (ST-6):**
1. U1 — the sheet's first item has already aired by itself.
2. U2 — PETER puts UNC's badge in the OUT tray: the sheet's last
   item (UNC leaves at six) is made physical.
3. U3 — the demonstration is ordered; UNC refuses by requesting it.
4. U4 — PETER demonstrates and brags the fraud into the live mic.
5. U5 — the advertiser cancels; the name is crossed off; the badge
   comes back. (The account settles in this order: aired → badge →
   demo → confession → cancellation.)
6. U6 — per the desk rule PETER stated in U4, the machine remixes
   his demonstration into a new video and onto the next sheet:
   the debt transfers, larger.

**Relationship inversion (ST-7):** The seller becomes the product.
PETER, who sold UNC's mouth, ends the episode with the cat wearing
HIS mouth and his name on tomorrow's sheet.
