# THE NIGHT LIST — Episode 2: "The Keeper Night"
### BOARD — storyline + breakdown (Passes 1–2, Writers Room, 2026-11-16; conformed to the Gate E repair script, 2026-11-16; conformed again to Gate E repair round 2, 2026-11-16; conformed again to Gate E repair round 3, 2026-11-16 — see DECISION_LOG "Gate E repair round 3")

**Show:** THE NIGHT LIST (bible `new-era/morph/MORPH_SHOW-3.md`).
**Format:** 60s, 6 units × 10s, 16:9. Palette INK + BONE + CADMIUM
lamp, VERMILION device. Register: deadpan.

**SKELETON (named, Addendum §2): S1 — THE DOOR TEST.** The test is
stated as a rule about beds and names: *a bed is held when a name
is spoken at the door and its bag is carried through it — spoken
and carried, same hands.* Claimant attempts fail on the stated
evidence, adapt exactly one thing, and try again; the Keeper
upgrades the protocol aloud before the resolving attempt; the
third attempt passes and the door decides. Engine rotation is
mandated: E1 ran the B1 accusation chain; B1 may not spine two
consecutive episodes (F-6). E2's engine is a want-vs-want contest
with a winner.

**ST-1 BEFORE/AFTER PAIR:** *The Keeper holds a bed and the pen →
the Keeper holds only the pen; his bed belongs to an absent
brother, and the Keeper sleeps downstairs at his desk.*

**ST-2 ONE-SENTENCE ARGUMENT:** A bed in this house is earned by
presence — a name spoken and a bag carried by the same hands —
not bought with money carried by somebody else's.

**ST-7 RELATIONSHIP INVERSION (in words, before spectacle):**
The keeper of the door becomes the bedless man at his own desk;
the owner, who holds every deed in the building, cannot pass the
door test his own house runs on, because everything he owns was
carried in by other people's hands.

**ST-4 TEST, STATED BEFORE TESTED:** The door test is stated in
U1 (Keeper, line 5) before the first attempt is ruled on (U2,
line 8). The U4 upgrade — *carrying hands hold no money* — is
spoken in U4 before the resolving attempt in U5.

**THE FLOOR RULE (named house rule — round 3):** *Staying after
refusal earns dry floor — no bed, no name.* The house's third
standing rule, beside the door test and the money rule: a
refused claimant who stays the night is not put back in the
wet; he gets the dry floor place (the dry corner under the
stair). It is stated as world text on the painted HOUSE SIGN
beside the door, legible in picture from U2 onward (the blob's
refusal happens under it), spoken by the Keeper in U5 over the
entered verdict as the episode's principle (the moral slot),
and named by the Keeper in U6 — he taps the sign as he drags
the mat — at the moment he applies it. Peter's case is
consistent with it: his man never came, so nothing was stayed
and nothing was earned.

**ST-5 AUDIENCE SECRET (held by end of U1):** In U1's wide frame,
unseen by Keeper and Laura, UNC stands in the doorway holding a
cash envelope and a stranger's carpet bag that is still OUTSIDE
the threshold. The viewer knows the freed bed already has a buyer
before anyone in the room does. Characters learn it in U2 when
UNC steps in and speaks his want.

**ST-6 ESCALATION, ITEMIZED (one step per unit):**
1. U1 — a name struck: the Keeper strikes his own name off.
2. U2 — a name spoken without a bag: cash's candidate is called
   at the door; the bag stays outside. (Both wants and the test
   are already on the table from U1.)
3. U3 — a bag carried by the wrong hands: UNC hauls the bag in
   himself; the mouth that must speak it never does.
4. U4 — a bed stripped: UNC rolls the Keeper's mattress toward
   the door to deliver the sale, and says the destination and
   the count aloud over the bed: it is Peter's, eleven names,
   ten beds, test or none. *(Repair: the freshen-strikes slip
   is cut; P-001's remind re-sites to U6.)*
5. U5 — the test passed honestly: Laura carries her brother's
   bag through the door and speaks his name, hands empty of
   money; the name is written on; the ropes are tightened
   under the stated grant rule before the tag is hung; the
   FLOOR RULE is spoken aloud over the verdict as the
   episode's principle (the moral slot — round 3).
6. U6 — a name written back by its owner: on his way to the
   board the Keeper settles the refused Blob Prince first —
   under the FLOOR RULE, which he names at the moment he
   applies it (he taps the house sign beside the door; the
   blob stayed, Peter's man never came at all): the worn
   doormat dragged to the
   dry corner under the stair beside the door, where the
   eave's drip does not reach — dry floor, no bed, no name,
   no tag. Then, on screen, by his own hand, the Keeper
   writes a fresh KEEPER at the board's foot — his U1 strike
   is not un-struck (ELIAS keeps that slot); the desk-sleeper,
   living under no name and uncounted for tomorrow, accepts
   the arguing a live name brings so he counts again. ELIAS
   above it is untouched and HELD. The account settles item
   by item in reverse: money failed, hands failed, presence
   won — and the shortage is back as a new debt, with the
   clerk himself as the extra name.

**SR-4 PERSISTED SITUATION / ONE NEW ELEMENT:** The situation
persists (the desk, the board, eleven-names pressure). The single

**SR-5 STATE-CARRIER (worn continuity):** Elias's carpet bag,

## CHARACTER CARDS (CH-1 — complete before dialogue)

**THE KEEPER — night clerk.**
- Want: one quiet night, and his rule kept — the list decided by
  the test, not by money.
- Stake: his own bed. He lives where he works; if his name comes
  off, he sleeps at the desk.
- Wound / wrong belief: believes the pen makes him the judge.
  The shared wrong belief (A4): everyone in the room believes
  holding the pen is holding the power; the episode proves the
  test, not the pen, decides — and it decides against him.
- Unbreakable rule: a name he strikes, he does not un-strike.
  *(Round 2, U6: he honours it literally — the struck slot
  stays struck and ELIAS keeps it; what he writes is a fresh
  line at the board's foot.)*
- House rules he keeps (all three stated on screen): the door
  test (U1); carrying hands hold no money (U4); THE FLOOR
  RULE — staying after refusal earns dry floor, no bed, no
  name (signed beside the door from U2; spoken by him U5;
  applied by him U6).
- Tell: caps and uncaps his pen when cornered (planted U2,
  before his U5 deed — CH-5).
- Voice rule: SHORT, flat; states rules as formulas; his longest
  beat is a rule, never a feeling.
- Signature line: "Door test: spoken and carried, same hands."
- Function phrase (CH-4): **the judge who pays.**
- Deed (CH-3): strikes his own name off (U1); writes ELIAS on
  and tightens the held bed's ropes (U5); states the floor
  rule over the verdict (U5); settles the refused claimant
  who stayed with the dry-corner floor place — tapping the
  house sign and naming the floor rule as he drags the mat —
  and writes his own name back, fresh, at the board's foot
  (U6).

**LAURA — long-stay guest.**
- Want: the freed bed held for her brother, Elias, before he
  arrives.
- Stake: if the bed sells tonight, her brother sleeps outside
  when he comes — and she will have watched it happen from her
  chair. *(Repair: the stake is now SPOKEN in U1, in plain
  words — "Without it, Elias sleeps in rain." Gate E Q4.)*
- Wound / wrong belief: believes a rule honestly used cannot be
  beaten by money; the episode tests exactly that.
- Unbreakable rule: she never asks a favour she is accusing
  someone else of selling — she wins by the test or not at all.
- Tell: her hand rests on the handle of a second carpet bag kept
  under her chair (planted U1, held U2–U3, before her U5 deed —
  CH-5).
- Voice rule: MEDIUM, even; asks rulings, not favours; speaks in
  verdicts when she names a cheat.
- Signature line: "Carrying is not speaking, boss."
- Function phrase: **the sister who passes the test honestly.**
- Deed: carries Elias's bag through the door and speaks his
  name at the threshold (U5) — her questioning job lands here:
  notice U1, name U3, act U5 (shifted off the E1 U2/U4/U6
  pattern per F-8).

**UNC — the owner. Present-tense antagonist (F-6).**
- Want: the freed bed sold tonight, for Peter's cash.
- Stake: an empty bed is a night of rent lost, and Peter's cash
  is already promised against it.
- Wound / wrong belief: believes every bed in the house is his
  because every deed is; the door test is a clerk's game he can
  buy his way past.
- Unbreakable rule: he never carries anything in this house
  himself — until the test makes him (U3, his tell breaking).
- Tell: thumbs the edge of the cash envelope when he is pricing
  something (planted U1, in the audience-secret frame).
- Voice rule: LONG by register, spent here in short bursts —
  the lecture compressed to pricing language; money stated
  plainly, never decorated.
- Signature line: "Eleven names, ten beds — Peter's, test or none."
- Function phrase: **the seller who cannot pass his own door.**
- Deeds: hauls the stranger's bag through the door himself
  (U3); strips Bed 10 — mattress rolled toward the door (U4).

**THE DISPATCHER — day clerk. The authority (speaks U2, U5).**
- Want: her log believed — the count in her book is the count
  in the room.
- Stake: if the board and the log disagree and nobody cares,
  her book is worthless and so is her word.
- Wound / wrong belief: believes the written record settles
  arguments by itself; learns it settles them only when someone
  reads it aloud at the bite.
- Unbreakable rule: she never writes a name she has not seen
  carried in.
- Tell: closes her hand on the log's edge before she speaks.
- Voice rule: SHORT, flat — numbers first, always.
- Signature line: "Ten names, ten beds. Keeper's downstairs."
- Function phrase: **the record that rules.**
- Deed: hangs the HELD tag on Bed 10 — after the ropes, per
  the grant rule — and closes the log on the verdict (U5
  picture). Her log decides the grant: she reads board against
  book before the tag moves.

Speaking cast: Keeper + Laura + UNC (three) + the Dispatcher as
authority — inside the F-5 cast structure. No fifth voice. The
night's buyer, Peter, never appears and never speaks: he is a
