## PASS 1 — STORYLINE

- **Skeleton: S2 THE COUNT THAT WON'T COME OUT** (unchanged
  from the blocked room — the skeleton was never the failure).
  Hook: the count is wrong (eleven names, ten beds). Denial:
  the wrongness is somebody else's fault. Evidence: the
  board itself, in lamplight. Turn: the wrongness is the
  keeper's own name. Payoff: the count comes out — and the
  keeper is the one left standing.
- **Before/after pair (ST-1):** eleven names for ten beds,
  argued over → ten names, ten beds, and the eleventh name
  written fresh at the foot of the board by the man it
  displaces.
- **The one argument (ST-2):** a name on the board is not a
  bed until the house's test says so — spoken and carried,
  same hands. Every beat pulls on it: the strike, the test,
  Peter's two failures, the attempted purchase, the grant,
  the self-writing.
- **Value change (ST-1/F-2):** the eleventh name's bed —
  fought over by Laura (for Elias), UNC (for Peter, then for
  cash), and the house's own test; WON by Elias through
  Laura's hands in U5 and NOT revoked at the final frame:
  the last image of the beds is Elias's bag on Bed 10 with
  the HELD tag on it. What the win costs is paid in the same
  frame-sequence: the Keeper's own name goes to the foot of
  the board and the Keeper sleeps at his desk.
- **Audience secret (ST-5):** pictured in U1 — the Keeper
  strikes his OWN name off the board in vermilion at frame
  0. The viewer knows whose name made eleven before Laura
  finishes her complaint.
- **Escalation, itemized (ST-6):** (1) the count is wrong
  and the wrong name is the keeper's; (2) the test prices
  Peter out twice — spoken-not-carried, carried-not-spoken;
  (3) the test is attacked with money — the bed stripped,
  the envelope offered, the rule stopping the sale in
  picture; (4) the test is passed by the one claimant who
  meets it exactly. The payoff settles the list in order.
- **Inversion (ST-7):** the keeper who strikes names to make
  room ends by writing his own name where the struck names
  go — the bottom of the board, bedless. The clerk who
  serves the list becomes its eleventh problem.
- **One new element (SR-4):** the door test itself. The
  board, the beds, the rain persist from E1; nothing else
  is new.

## CHARACTER CARDS (CH-1 — complete before Pass 3)

**THE KEEPER** (clerk; every episode).
Function: the house's test made flesh — and its cost.
Want (spoken U1): quiet — his name off the board ends the
arguing. Stake: the list's authority; if names can be
bought or carried in by other hands, the board is a price
list. Wound/wrong belief: that the list, kept exactly,
keeps everyone — including, somehow, him. Rule he will not
break: a name is held only as the test holds it — spoken
and carried, same hands; and carrying hands hold no money.
Tell: he strikes in vermilion and writes small. Voice rule:
rulings, counts, and rules, stated flat; questions only at
the end, and only to camera. Signature entrance line:
"Name."
Episode deed (CH-3): he hangs the HELD tag on Elias's bed
himself and speaks the count that leaves himself bedless —
then writes his own name at the board's foot, on screen,
and takes the desk.

**LAURA** (fighter; E1's claimant, returning).
Function: the claimant who meets the test exactly.
Want (spoken U1): the eleventh name's bed — for her
brother, Elias. Stake (spoken U2): without it, Elias sleeps
in rain. Wound/wrong belief: that carrying a thing for
someone is the same as bringing them — the test teaches her
the difference in Peter's failures before she passes it
herself. Rule she will not break: she will not buy what the
test can grant. Tell: she says "boss" when she is telling
the clerk a truth he doesn't want. Voice rule: plain,
first-person, facts over pleas. Signature: "You put him
back on, Keeper."
Episode deed (CH-3): she speaks Elias's name at the door
carrying his bag in her own hands — the pass that wins Bed
10 irreversibly.

**UNC** (pressure; the house's own — see the bible's cast
note and the E1 ledger: the old man of the house, whose
history with the board is E3's business).
Function: the present-tense antagonist (F-6) — he ACTS:
demands, strips a bed on screen, offers money on screen.
Want (spoken U2): Bed 10 sold — to Peter, for cash tonight.
Stake: his word to Peter, and whatever Peter's arrival
means to him (unspoken; his U4 slip is the crack in it).
Wound/wrong belief: that everything in the house has a
price because everything in his life has had one. Rule he
will not break: he never asks twice the same way — demand,
then deed, then money. Tell: he calls the claimant by name
when he wants the room to hear the claim ("Peter!"). Voice
rule: short orders and offers; denials too specific for the
question asked. Signature: none banked; his slip is the
plant (P-001).
Episode deed (CH-3): the strip + the offer — the attack the
money rule visibly stops.

**PETER** (absent principal — never appears, never speaks).
Function: the control case. His bag is carried in by
another man's hands (fails carried-not-spoken in reverse:
spoken for, carried for — neither by him); his envelope is
the money the rule refuses. His absence is the fact Laura's
U3 line plants and U6's picture pays: the bag still
unclaimed at the stair foot at the end — its man never came.

**THE BLOB PRINCE** (invader; see DECISION_LOG INVASION
LOG). Function: the second control case — a claimant who
arrives with everything except the test's terms: maximum
expression, a colony behind him, a Queen's claim in his
mouth — and no hands that can carry his own sack in while
he speaks his own name. Want (spoken U2, twined on the
Peter ruling): a dry bed. His settlement is the floor rule
— pictured from U2 (the painted sign), spoken by the Keeper
in U5, and cashed in U6: asleep in the dry corner under the
stair on the dragged doormat, his sack for a pillow. The
house processed the empire's heir by its own rules and the
house won — and the floor, dry, is more than the rain gave
him: the invasion costs the house a corner and costs him
his rank for the night.

**THE DISPATCHER** — CUT as a speaker in the re-room (see
header). She exists in the house (E1 canon) but E2's cast
follows the bible's rotation — Keeper + one fighter + one
pressure — and her log is not this episode's reveal. The
count and the HELD tag are the Keeper's own acts now.
