## TRANSCRIPT (master ASR, faster-whisper, no VAD)

U1: Write us married, or noon loses our room. Then hold at the bell, both hands one ring. I want that hold believed without the wall.
U2: I want couples written, not hired hands. Write us coupled first... One room, first written keeps it. Bouquet, veil, sleeve, drop them, be first.
U3: Bouquet's down, why's it still opening? The snap startles fingers, lock thumbs.
U4: Loaded, then bare, I opened. Write us married, thumbs locked. Hold yours with both hands. Tell us, who else can?
U5: Hold held, wall rights married.
U6: Nobody holds your life for you. Nobody. Next, the cook. Who brought his tin back empty?
