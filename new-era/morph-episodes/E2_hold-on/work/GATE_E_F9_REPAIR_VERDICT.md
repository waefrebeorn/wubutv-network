## 2. Q2 — What blocked them? — PASS
The obstacle is concrete and repeatedly pictured: the rule is "one ring, both hands, at the bell," but in U1 "June's fingers slip and curl on the ring" as the bell rings, in U2 "her fingers jump at the sound" under her load, and in U3 "only June's hand moves, her bare fingers flying open at the bell's snap." ## 3. Q3 — What changed by the final unit? — PASS

The end state in U6 is the opposite: the Watcher "puts it in June's hand," "the ring is now on June's finger," the couple carry their bags into the room, and the wall shows "MARRIED standing finished across the couple's chalked names."
