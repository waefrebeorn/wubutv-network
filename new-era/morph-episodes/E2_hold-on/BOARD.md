## PASS 1 — STORYLINE

- **Skeleton: S1 THE DOOR TEST** (Addendum §2). CHANGED from
  the blocked candidate's S3 Wish Ladder — S3's degrading
  meter (the bouquet turning to brass) was one of the machines
  the cold readers could not file, and its snap-back grammar
  fought F-2. S1 is the folk shape for a guarded threshold:
  keeper states the test aloud → claimant fails on the stated
  evidence → adapts exactly one thing → tries again → third
  attempt resolves at the door. The wall is the door; the bell
  is the knock.
- **Before/after pair (ST-1):** an unmarried couple about to
  lose their room → a married couple carrying their bags back
  into it, written on the wall in the Watcher's own chalk.
- **The one argument (ST-2):** whose hands does the wall
  write — the couple's own, or a hired Holder's? Every line
  pulls on that rope: the test, the refusal of the hired hold,
  the rival claim, the two failures, the teach, the win.
- **Engine (F-6 rotation):** a want-vs-want contest under a
  worsening constraint (the noon bell). B1 (proof → lie →
  entrance → record reveal) is NOT the spine — E1 ran B1. The
  antagonist-function is the Watcher's rule, wielded by him on
  screen in the present tense (he states it, rules with it,
  and is finally bound by it), with the Fruit Couple as rival
  claimants who act.
- **Value change (ST-1/F-2):** June's marriage — fought over
  (her hand vs the rule vs the rival claim), WON irreversibly
  in U5 (MARRIED chalked; U6 confirms with the room key in her
  hand and the bags carried in — the stake resolved ON
  SCREEN, in picture, not inferred).
- **Audience secret (ST-5):** pictured in U1 — June's fingers
  already slipping on the ring inside the first second. The
  viewer knows her hand is the problem before anyone says it.
- **Escalation, itemized (ST-6):** (1) attempt with loaded
  arms fails — the load; (2) attempt with bare hands fails —
  the startle; (3) the deadline made flesh — the bags set out
  of the room (U4 cost beat); (4) attempt with locked thumbs
  holds. The payoff settles the list in order: load removed
  (U3), startle answered (teach, U3), deadline beaten (U5).
- **Inversion (ST-7):** the professional Holder — the woman
  paid to hold things for other people — cannot hold this one
  for them; the couple priced as hopeless holders (her hand
  opened twice) end as the only true hold in the street. The
  re-rank is relational, not spectacle.
- **One new element (SR-4):** the marriage test itself. The
  bell, the wall, the queue persist; nothing else is new. The
  bouquet is a plain bouquet. There is no brass, no cake, no
  pocketed object.

## CHARACTER CARDS (CH-1 — complete before Pass 3)

**JUNE** (guest, the bride — Bible §4 archetype).
Function: the claimant whose own hand is the obstacle.
Want: to be written married with Eli before the room is
lost. Stake: the couple's room at Rosa's depot — unwritten
by the noon bell, they lose it to the next written couple.
Wound/wrong belief: that holding more is the same as holding
— she loads her arms to prove devotion. Rule she will not
break: she will not let anyone else's hands make her
marriage. Tell: she clutches — bouquet, veil, sleeve — when
she is afraid (planted U1, named U2, dropped U3). Voice rule:
plain and first-person; she reports her own hand honestly
("Two bells I opened"). Signature line: "Write us married,
or noon loses our room."

**THE COURIER** (co-lead, even episode — the Holder for hire).
Function: the professional whose want the rule refuses — and
the teacher the refusal makes of her.
Want (series, Bible §4): to be believed without the wall — a
held thing should speak for itself. Episode form: she wants
this hold, June's, believed on her record. Stake: her record
is her living; a public ruling that her hands don't count is
a public price cut. Wound/wrong belief: that a held thing is
the only truth, so a written thing is a lesser one. Rule she
will not break: she never drops a thing she has taken in
hand. Tell: she counts on her fingers when a hold matters
(U1 palm; U5 the count). Voice rule: short, deadpan, prices
everything; speaks in offers and verdicts about hands.
Signature line: "I want one hold believed without the wall."

**THE WATCHER** (authority, every episode).
Function: the keeper whose rule is the door — and who is
bound by it when it is beaten fairly.
Want (series): the wall's word to stay final. Episode form:
one couple written by noon, by the rule, no exceptions —
exceptions are what unwrites the wall. Stake: if he bends
the test for a hired hand, every line he has written is
negotiable. Wound/wrong belief (the town's A4 belief, his
most of all): the wall can't be wrong. Rule he will not
break: he writes only what the bell proves. Tell: he wipes
chalk dust from his fingers before he rules. Voice rule:
rulings and rules, stated flat. Signature line (banked, NOT
spent — as the blocked room ruled): "The wall says what the
wall says."

**ELI** (silent principal — F-3's one allowed; the groom).
Function: the steady hand — the control in June's experiment.
Want: the marriage, and June believed about her own hand.
Deed (CH-3, decisive, with cost): in U4 he carries the
couple's bags back inside the room's threshold against the
porter's eviction and stands in the doorway. His hand never
opens in three pictured bells (the U1 slip is hers, beside
his stillness; U2; U5).

**LAURA** (silent presence, Bible §4 rotation — the
non-speaking co-lead appears, holding something: the chalk).
Function: the witness whose restraint is the remind (PL-3).
Deed: in U5 she hands the chalk to the Watcher — because the
hold was true, the keeper's own rule requires his hand.

**THE FRUIT COUPLE — THE PEACH / THE LIME** (invaders; see
DECISION_LOG INVASION LOG). Function: the rival claimants
whose failure is the control experiment for June's win —
same test, same bell, opposite result. Want (spoken by them,
in unison, U2): to be written coupled, first. Their hold:
one shared bouquet. Their failure mode: at a bell both their
hands slip off the stem together, and each lets go entirely
to blame the other for letting go first — pictured U2, run
wordless through U3–U5.
