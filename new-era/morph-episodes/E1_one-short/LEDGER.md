## 1. CANON FACTS (append-only, F-###)

- **F-001** — The office counts loads for the town, for money.
  The Clerk keeps the book; the Counter counts aloud, floor
  side. *(founding, Ep. 1)*
- **F-002** — Every count this office has ever run has come up
  exactly one short. The book records all of them. The Clerk
  stated it on record, Ep. 1 U5: "This office has never
  closed even."
- **F-003** — Ep. 1 load: a wedding planner brought hire
  umbrellas for a noon wedding; CLAIMED 31 (hire receipt, paid
  in full); COUNTED 30 (batches 20 + 10). Her fee rode on 31.
  She packed them herself.
- **F-004** — The book opens at a price, and the price is a
  concession. Ep. 1: the Planner paid by surrendering her
  accusation — she laid the hire receipt face-down on the
  counter and was written into the book as one short.
- **F-005** — The shelf behind the Clerk holds ONE UMBRELLA
  from Ep. 1's load, standing, handle up. No character has
  looked at it, named it, or acknowledged it. *(Viewer-only
  fact; see the shelf inventory, §5.)*
- **F-006** — The Counter once lost count — stepped backward
  (a back-step) — and ordered it kept secret. Only he knows.
  *(Carried canon from THE COUNT, its F-006; backstory, not
  yet spoken in ONE SHORT.)*
- **F-007** — In Ep. 1 the Counter named the pattern out loud,
  in front of a customer, for the first time: "Every pile
  comes up one light. Every one." The Clerk heard it and
  answered with the book, not a denial.
- **F-008** — In Ep. 1 U6 the Counter counted the shelf
  ("One umbrella.") and stopped. He has the observation. He
  does not yet have the conclusion, and he has said nothing
  about it to anyone.
