# VEHICLES — THE LAST SEAT · E2 "Night Two: The Landlord's Test"

Written BEFORE firing (2026-11-17, production coordinator). SCRIPT.md is
law; this file implements it. Panel Spec v2 + Strong Reference (SR1–SR4)
govern every fire. Image doctrine per the measured video-api lab: an
image input is a strong conditioner, not a frame source; image 1 (the
unit's PEAK panel) is the anchor; composition carries when ref aspect =
output aspect; always state aspect in text.

## GLOBAL LAWS (in every video prompt)

- 16:9 landscape, exactly 10 seconds, 24 fps (stated in text).
- Prompts open "at <place>".
- All dialogue for a unit is ONE merged quoted speech block at the END
  of the prompt (dialogue-last). Speakers named in narration order.
  Nothing else is spoken: "No other speech, no narration, no singing."
- No text of any kind inside the picture: every piece of paper (notice,
  tally card, slip, letter, programme, guest book) is BLANK paper or
  illegible texture; all counts/words are post/prop work.
- The Occupant is NEVER front-lit: hat, gloves, programme, lamp,
  white stub on the brass armrest are her only identifiers.
- UNC does not appear in this show. The "~ WuBu ~" wordmark is the
  post corner bug only — no in-generation text.
- Dialogue lines are VERBATIM from SCRIPT.md (109 words, 13 lines).

## LOOK BLOCKS (verbatim in every prompt where the figure appears)

- THE DANCER — adult woman in her late twenties, dark hair in a tight
  bun, deep vermilion sleeveless stage dress with a SHORT swirling
  skirt, soft dance shoes, athletic build, focused expression. A flat
  key on a metal ring on her RIGHT wrist (U1–U4 and into U5's handover;
  the ring is EMPTY from the U5 payoff on).
- THE USHER — tall thin man in his late fifties, slicked-back
  grey-streaked dark hair, long ink-navy usher's coat with brass
  buttons, white gloves, thin deadpan face, calm unsmiling expression.
  Guest book under his arm at U1's open.
- THE OCCUPANT — a woman in the front-row critic's seat, seen only in
  shadow: wide dark hat, dark elegant coat, gloved hands, a theatre
  programme; her face is NEVER shown or front-lit. White ticket stub
  resting on the brass armrest beside her in every unit.
- THE COLLECTOR — a heavyset man in his fifties in a grey overcoat,
  flat cap, carrying a small wooden cash box; from U3's joint he wears
  a heavy metal chain over one shoulder; in U4 he adds a padlock.
- THE FRUIT COUPLE — two adult performers in handmade fruit costumes of
  our own design: the PEACH, a round fuzzy peach-pink suit with a green
  leaf tilted on her head like a hat, blushing cheeks, huge expressive
  eyes; the LIME, a bright green lime suit with a wedge-shaped grin,
  equally expressive. Costumes IDENTICAL in every unit (U2–U6). They
  are SILENT in every unit — no speech, no captions, no direct address.
- THE SEAT — one old cinema seat, faded vermilion velvet, brass armrest
  caps, worn, alone in its row, with a small reading lamp on its arm.
- THE THEATRE — a small old theatre: warm dim house, rows of ordinary
  worn seats, a low stage downstage, a box-office door stage-left with
  a brass aisle table beside the stage.

### U1 — HOOK / THE TEST STATED (21w, 3 lines)
PEAK: PNL-PEAK-U01.png (new). CHAR: PNL-CHAR-DANCER-hero.
Open FROM BEHIND THE OCCUPANT'S HAT: the Dancer mid-leap at the seat,
ALREADY DESCENDING toward her landing (never at the apex — E1's V1/V2
lesson); landing thud + programme snapping shut inside 0.5s; the seat
lamp clicks ON inside 0–1s. Down the aisle behind her the Collector
enters carrying the cash box and a folded paper notice.
Action: she lands in a low crouch facing the seat, touches the key
ring on her right wrist once as she recovers; her line to the seat.
The Collector reaches the box-office door stage-left and slaps the
blank notice flat on it; lifts the cash box, shakes it once. The
Usher crosses to the door, reads the blank notice, states the test to
the room, then turns to the seat and delivers the refrain.
Dialogue (merged, dialogue-last):
DANCER: "I want this stage open; shall I dance again?"
USHER: "Landlord's test: dance for fifty cash before last bar."
USHER: "House of one."
Last-frame target (J1): Dancer in the low crouch downstage centre
facing the seat, right wrist (key ring) resting on her knee; lamp ON
warm; notice whole on the door; Collector standing at the door, cash
box in his left hand.

### U2 — SETUP / ATTEMPT 1 FAILS (14w, 2 lines) · GAP 1
PEAK: PNL-PEAK-U02.png (new). CHAR: PNL-CHAR-DANCER-face.
Open FROM THE SEAT's point of view on the identical crouch (J1 state);
the only change: the Collector sets the box down on the aisle table.
Action: the Usher opens the box, lifts a BLANK tally card and holds it
toward the room; his line to the Dancer. The Occupant looks at the
card, not at the Dancer; her conducting hand stops mid-bar; the lamp
cools a fraction; her line from the dark. Tail: down the aisle from
the back of the house the FRUIT COUPLE enters arm in arm and stops
behind the Dancer — the Peach points at the critic's seat, at the two
of them, mimes the two of them sitting in it together, beaming; the
Lime points at the Peach, at a plum-dark programme on a back seat,
folds his arms and turns half away; the Peach rolls her eyes to the
ceiling. The Occupant does not turn; the Usher glances once from the
couple to the notice. GAP 1 carrier rides the tail: the Collector
counts coins out one by one onto the brass armrest (a clink every
~0.7s) while the theatre cat climbs onto the open box lid and sits in
the money.
Dialogue: USHER: "I want that test passed without begging him."
OCCUPANT: "I want a reason to stay."
Last-frame target (J2): box open on the table, coins in a SHORT row on
the brass armrest, cat on the lid, blank tally card propped against
the box; Dancer standing centre; lamp cooled; fruit couple standing
at the back of the aisle, Lime arms folded.

### U3 — EVIDENCE / ATTEMPT 2 FAILS (17w, 2 lines) · COST BEAT BEGINS
PEAK: PNL-PEAK-U03.png (new). CHAR: PNL-CHAR-USHER-face.
Open on the identical table/box/coin-row/cat/tally state (J2); the
Dancer in the same spot takes a wage envelope from her skirt pocket;
the Collector steps in from the door side with the chain over his
shoulder (the one new prop, introduced at this joint, carried to U5).
The couple holds its aisle position (Peach hopeful, Lime arms folded).
Action: the Dancer touches the key on her wrist, then empties the
wage envelope into the box coin by coin; her line straight to camera
(direct address). Behind the seat the Collector loops the chain
through the box-office door handles. The Occupant leans one inch out
of the dark toward the box; her verdict line.
Dialogue: DANCER (to camera): "Empty your pockets for your job — would you?"
OCCUPANT: "Forty-one is not fifty, even in a pretty dress."
Last-frame target (J3): chain looped through the door handles behind
the seat row; blank tally propped on the box; wage envelope empty and
flat on the table; Dancer centre facing the seat, empty envelope in
her left hand; couple at the back of the aisle unchanged.

### U4 — TURN / PROTOCOL UPGRADED (20w, 2 lines) · TEACH · GAP 2
PEAK: PNL-PEAK-U04.png (new). CHAR: PNL-CHAR-USHER-hero.
Open FROM THE SEAT: same chain, tally, envelope in the same hand (J3);
the Collector hands a second, smaller blank slip to the Usher at the
table — the slip enters in the Usher's white glove, nowhere else.
Action: the Usher reads the slip; his line to the room. Behind him
the Collector snaps the padlock through the chain. As it locks, a
white envelope slides under the chained door and stops against the
chain, half in, unopened; the Usher sees it and leaves it. The
Occupant's foot under the armrest begins a soft rhythm; she raises
one gloved hand and teaches the phrase; her line. Then the fruit
couple, hand in hand, comes down the aisle to a spot BESIDE the
Dancer's mark and attempts the phrase together: a leap together, a
turn in opposite directions tangling their arms, a landing IN A HEAP
on the floor in each other's arms, the Peach's leaf-hat fallen over
the Lime's eyes. The Occupant's foot does NOT tap; her hand does NOT
rise; the lamp does NOT warm. The Usher shakes his head once, slowly.
GAP 2 carrier rides the tail: her foot taps the rhythm three times —
tap, tap-tap, tap — the lamp warms one step with each tap; the
Dancer, crouching into her start position, answers the last tap with
one heel on the boards; the padlocked chain sways once and settles.
Dialogue: USHER: "Forty-one short, he adds: critic stays to last bar."
OCCUPANT: "A phrase is leap, turn, land — land clean and I'll stay."
Last-frame target (J4): Dancer in her crouch at the exact downstage
mark, key ring catching the lamp; Occupant's foot still; lamp two
steps warmer than U3; padlocked chain on the door; tally on the box;
fruit couple in their heap at the aisle's edge, leaf-hat over the
Lime's eyes.

### U5 — VERDICT / ATTEMPT 3 PASSES (19w, 2 lines) · IRREVERSIBLE PAYOFF
PEAK: PNL-PEAK-U05.png (new). CHAR: PNL-CHAR-DANCER-hero.
Open on the identical crouch/mark/lamp/door state (J4), the Dancer
already rising into the first leap as the frame opens (motion from
the joint, no ramp from rest); the couple in the same heap, the Peach
sitting the Lime up, hands still linked.
Action — THE SEAT-WAVE: the Dancer dances leap-turn-land three times
straight at the seat; each clean landing slams one row of house seats
down in a wave, programmes flap up off laps like startled birds; the
Collector crossing behind the rows with his chain must duck each
wave; the third duck drops the chain at the Dancer's feet. The
Occupant taps all three landings; on the third, as the Dancer's
balance wavers half an inch, the Occupant's gloved hand shoots out
and steadies her elbow without leaving the seat. The Dancer picks up
the dropped chain's end, sets it aside, takes the cash box in both
hands, unhooks the flat key from her wrist ring; her line to the
Collector, holding out box and key. PAYOFF (irrevocable): the
Collector takes box and key, weighs the key once in his palm, tears
the notice in half off the door, unlocks the padlock, unloops the
chain, and walks out up the aisle carrying box, key and chain; the
torn notice halves fall to the floor and stay; as the chain comes
off, the white envelope slips fully inside and stops against a torn
notice half, still unopened. The Occupant's verdict line to the room.
Tail: the fruit couple rises from the heap, straightens the Peach's
leaf-hat, and takes two ordinary house seats together at the back,
the Lime's arm around the Peach.
Dialogue: DANCER: "Take the box and my flat key — nine, and my home."
OCCUPANT: "No begging, then: she chose the price herself."
Last-frame target (J5): aisle empty; door unchained and ajar, warm
light through its crack; torn notice halves on the floor either side
of the door; Dancer centre, box-less, empty ring raised on her right
wrist as the final pose settles; Occupant seated, lamp fully warm,
hand still extended; couple seated together in two back-row seats.

### U6 — HANGER / THE TEST HANDED ON (18w, 2 lines) · GAP 3 / FREEZE
PEAK: PNL-PEAK-U06.png (new). CHAR: PNL-CHAR-OCCUPANT.
Open FROM THE SEAT with every J5 element identical — door ajar with
the same light crack, halves in the same spots, empty ring on the
same wrist, lamp at full warmth — and the white envelope resting
against the nearer notice half, unopened. The couple sits in the
same back-row seats, out of the freeze's focus.
Action: the Usher crosses, picks up the envelope, opens it at the
table, reads; his line to the room. The Dancer looks from her empty
ring to the seat. The Occupant takes the letter from the Usher's
glove, slowly, and holds it over the lamp, its light coming through
the paper; her last line to the Dancer. FREEZE (final 2–3s, built in
post as in E1): the letter glows over the lamp in her glove; her
programme in her other hand lowers one inch and stops mid-descent;
the Dancer's empty ring catches the light; the torn halves lie
either side of the open door. No post question card (script: "if
used" — not used; the debt line is the hanger).
Dialogue: USHER: "The letter says the real critic comes final night."
OCCUPANT: "This house of one owes him its seat, darling."
Last frame: the freeze composition above.
