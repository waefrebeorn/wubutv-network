### STORY SENSE

**(a) Causality**
No objection found.
Checked in particular, as instructed, the newcomers' arc: their arrival is caused by their own stated claim — they enter and immediately claim the seat: "a couple in peach and lime costumes enters arm in arm, points at the seated woman's seat and then at themselves to claim it as theirs for tonight, and waits in the aisle" (Picture, U2). Their attempt is caused by the phrase rule stated in dialogue in the same unit before the picture: "A phrase is leap, turn, land — land clean and I'll stay." (OCCUPANT, U4), followed by "the fruit couple attempts the leap-turn-land phrase together in the aisle" (Picture, U4). Their settlement is concluded, not evaporated: "so their claim to the seat is lost" (Picture, U4), and it persists through "the fruit couple gets up and sits together in two ordinary seats at the back."
