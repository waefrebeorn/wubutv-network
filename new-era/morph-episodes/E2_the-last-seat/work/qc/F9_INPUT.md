to camera; behind the seat, the grey-coated man loops a chain

woman raises a gloved hand and teaches the dance phrase; the
fruit couple attempts the leap-turn-land phrase together in the
aisle and lands in a heap in each other's arms, not clean; the

U5: The dancer performs the leap-turn-land phrase three times

the box, the key, and the chain; the dancer's wrist key ring is
now empty; the fruit couple gets up and sits together in two
ordinary seats at the back.

U6: The usher picks up the white envelope from beside a torn

together in the back row; the picture freezes on this tableau
for the final three seconds.
