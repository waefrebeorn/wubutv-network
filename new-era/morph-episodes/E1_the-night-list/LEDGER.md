## 1. CANON FACTS (append-only, F-###)

- **F-001.** The building has **ten beds**. The night list — the
  names on the board — decides who sleeps in them. A name struck
  off sleeps outside. *(Premiere premise, on screen U1–U6.)*
- **F-002.** SOL's name was struck from the list at **noon** on
  E1's day, while no clerk was at the desk. The strike is entered
  in the Dispatcher's entry log, noon column, initialed "U." The
  night column for that day is blank. *(U3, the log — authority
  reveal, B10.)*
- **F-003.** UNC sold Sol's bed at noon. Basis on screen: the log
  entry (F-002), the day's receipts laid on the desk in U4 — two
  of them for the same bed, **Bed 7** — Laura's naming (U4), and
  UNC's silence in the face of it. The Keeper acts on it as true
  (F-004).
- **F-004.** In E1 U5 the Keeper wrote SOL back onto the board, in
  full, under his stated rule: *"One on. One off. That's the
  rule."* The board now carries **eleven names for ten beds**.
- **F-005.** The name at the bottom of the board is **KEEPER**. The
  Keeper's own name is on the list he keeps: he lives where he
  works. *(On screen U6; the bible's §2 transformation of his
  fear: "this desk is the only bed I have.")*
- **F-006.** Laura's dare — *"Then strike yours, Keeper. Or strike
  mine."* — is **unanswered** at E1's end. The pen was last seen
  hovering over KEEPER. No name has come off.
- **F-007.** The Keeper is never at the desk at noon. *(His alibi,
  U3; corroborated by Sol, U4: he knocked at noon and the desk was
  empty.)*
- **F-008.** Peter is a broker who pays cash for vacancies — a
  struck name is a bed he can rent twice. UNC is Peter's creditor.
  *(Bible §4, carried as founding canon; Peter does not appear in
  E1.)*
- **F-009.** The board strike on SOL was **still wet at ~2 a.m.**,
  though the strike was logged at noon (F-002). Unexplained on
  screen. Somebody touched that board tonight. *(Planted P-001.)*

**CLAIMED (contested, NOT canon — recorded as positions):**

- **C-001.** Laura claims she watched Peter pay money at the desk
  at midnight, the night before E1 (U2: *"I watched Peter pay you
  at midnight"*). She believed the recipient was the Keeper. The
  Keeper denies receiving Peter's money (U2). The recipient is
  **unverified**. *(Planted P-003.)*

## 10. SEASON PROJECTION
*(This section settles SHOW-3's owed season projection — the debt
THE ROSTER never paid. Source: bible §7, adopted whole.)*

The Keeper works the desk all season while the contested names
climb the ladder toward his own (bible §5 escalation, S7):
a stranger's name → a friend's → Laura's brother's → Laura's →
the Keeper's own. The audience can predict the ladder and come
back to check.

- **Midseason turn:** Laura's brother arrives — the name she has
  wanted on the list since the premiere — and the Keeper discovers
  the log for the night the book began is missing its page, in the
  Dispatcher's own hand.
- **Season's final image:** the first page of the book, in
  lamplight — the first name ever written on the list, in the
  Keeper's young hand, is his own — and the Keeper picks up the
  pen, strikes it through himself, and hands Laura the desk.
