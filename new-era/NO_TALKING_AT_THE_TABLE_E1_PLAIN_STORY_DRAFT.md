# NO TALKING AT THE TABLE — E1 plain story (draft for user verdict)

Written by the parent, 2026-11-27. Bible: new-era/shows/NO_TALKING_AT_THE_TABLE.md
(rulings applied: ZERO spoken words, no exceptions; what breaks the silence is
a SOUND; exactly ONE vocal sound in the episode, at the turn; an almost-sound
costs one chair-inch sawn off on camera; a full sound costs the whole chair;
the episode ends on the NEXT CHALLENGER button).

Premise: At this table nobody may make a sound. Two rival hosts defend the
rule against a guest who has come to break it. Every mistake is paid for in
chair, in front of everyone.

Cast: BRUNO (big host, tall chef's hat), KIP (small host, bow tie),
MANGO (the guest, an invader fruit character), the diners (who bet with
bread rolls, piled on the side they back).

1. A long table under a big wall sign: a mouth, crossed out. Bruno and Kip
   are already miming insults at each other across it. Their two chairs stand
   tall and whole, and a saw hangs on the wall beside the sign. Mango arrives
   grinning, carrying a covered dish, and takes the guest chair. The diners
   start two piles of bread rolls — one for the hosts, one for the guest.
2. Soup course. Mango mimes the funniest face he has. Kip's shoulders shake;
   a tiny squeak escapes him before he clamps his mouth shut. Bruno points
   at the sign, takes down the saw, and saws one inch off Kip's chair leg,
   on camera. Kip sits down again, lower. Rolls pile onto Bruno's side.
3. Main course: a crab, alive, under the cover. It pinches Bruno's finger.
   Bruno's mouth opens in a huge silent scream — no sound comes out. The
   diners applaud without a sound and pile rolls onto Bruno's side. Mango's
   grin fades.
4. Mango cheats. He slides his phone under the table and it shouts "HELLO!"
   in a bright voice. Both hosts freeze — then point at Mango, not each
   other. Bruno fishes the phone out and holds it up, still talking, for
   the whole table to see. The trick is exposed. Bruno saws one inch off
   Mango's chair. The rolls avalanche onto the hosts' side.
5. Dessert. Mango climbs onto the table and dances the funniest silent dance
   he has in him — hat over his eyes, spoon in his teeth. The whole room
   shakes. Kip finally bursts: one huge "HA!" — the episode's only sound.
   Total silence after it. Bruno stands, lifts Kip's whole chair, and
   carries it out of the room.
6. Kip eats his dessert standing, his face filling the frame, the empty
   space where his chair was beside him. He starts to shake with silent
   laughter again and clamps his own mouth shut with both hands. The door
   opens: a tall pepper character stands there cracking his knuckles,
   looking at the empty place at the table. NEXT CHALLENGER.

Legibility check: the stake is printed in frame 1 (the crossed-out mouth,
the whole chairs, the saw on the wall). The body is the ledger — chair
height and the roll piles carry the score, readable in any frame. There is
no dialogue at all; every move is an act answered by an act (squeak → saw,
trick → exposure → saw, laugh → chair carried out). Proof is always a
positive picture: the phone held up still talking, the chair leaving the
room. The premise is re-spent in all six units.
