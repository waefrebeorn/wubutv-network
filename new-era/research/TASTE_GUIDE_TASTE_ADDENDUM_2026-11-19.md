## The verdict, stated as a rule

**The fidelity gates measure correctness, never flavor.**
From this addendum forward, no floor check — the Stranger
Test included — may be cited as evidence that an episode is
*good*. They certify that an episode is *correct*. Flavor is
a construction property: built at card stage and storyline
stage, protected at harvest, and tested by the Quote-Back
Test.

## The new standard, in brief

- **THE RIZZ CARD** (replaces SPEECH_RULE-as-prose): six
  fields per speaking character — `CARRIER_WORDS`,
  `CATCHPHRASE`, `BRAG`, `AUDIBLE_TELL`, `SPECIMEN_LINES`
  (≥3, blocking), `REGISTER_DEVIATION` — plus the episode's
  designated **SHOW-OFF**, licensed to play to the room
  (target class DELIGHT, unrationed).
- **Story inputs:** the one-sentence **PETTY WANT** (a person
  wants something petty from another person; a procedure is
  never the want) and the **STATUS CHANGE** (a change in
  standing between people; someone must be able to lose
  face — `FACE_AT_RISK`).
- **THE BIT:** every unit declares the named repeatable
  mechanism it runs; a unit with no bit is pictured, not
  spoken.
- **THE KEEP-LIST HARVEST:** flavor carriers are marked and
  harvested first; compression falls on connective and
  procedural lines; the tell is never what gets trimmed.
- **REGISTER:** every show declares its vernacular at bible
  stage; the invaders' register infecting the home cast
  across a season is the gentrification made audible — spent
  deliberately, never explained.
- **THE QUOTE-BACK TEST — the headline gate** (script lock
  and master stage): a cold reader, dialogue only, read aloud
  once, must — without looking back — (1) quote one line
  back, (2) name the show-off and their verbal signature,
  (3) retell the scene as gossip carrying an attitude. PASS
  requires all three. Full protocol: DIALOGUE_METHOD.md §3A;
  THE_NEW_STANDARD.md §6.

## Conflicts with standing law — named explicitly

1. **F-26's hear-before repair gradient — CAPPED.** The
   hear-before check stands (its meaning-failure definition
   unchanged), but its repair loop is capped at **one round
   at script stage**; further flags are adjudicated by the
   showrunner in writing, not by another re-banter or repair
   pass. *Why:* iterated cold-reader repair is a one-way
   gradient — every repair subtracts, nothing adds
   (TASTE_DIAGNOSIS T-0). A repair may not remove a keep-list
   line, a catchphrase instance, or a tell without a written
   showrunner ruling naming what the removal buys (the
   REPAIR CAP, DIALOGUE_METHOD §3).
2. **The Stranger Test's headline-gate status (dialogue
   addendum, 2026-11-18) — SUPERSEDED.** The test is demoted
   to a **floor check** (`FLOOR_CHECK (STRANGER)`) with the
   **SIGNATURE EXEMPTION**: a declared CATCHPHRASE or carrier
   construction arriving after its earning event is marked
   SIGNATURE, not STAGE; a world-rule maxim stated before its
   teaching event remains STAGE. The headline gate is the
   **QUOTE-BACK TEST**. *Why:* Q3's mechanic cannot
   distinguish a dead maxim from a live signature and executed
   both (T-2) — the gate banned the genre's repeatable
   devices by construction.
3. **DIALOGUE_METHOD Pass 3.2's audience-target cut rule —
   RETIRED.** A line whose target was "the room / the record
   / the audience" was cut by rule; that rule banned charisma
   by construction (T-4). It is replaced by the target class
   **DELIGHT**: a line aimed at the audience's delight is
   legal for the show-off without ration, and for any other
   character as the episode's one licensed grievance beat.
   The LEDGER LINE ban stands untouched — a ledger line's
   addressee is the *gate*, which is a different defect from
   playing to the room.
4. **B-8's draft-double/cut-half (online-research wave,
   2026-11-18) — SUPERSEDED as the harvest law.** The ratio
   was executed against the flavor carriers, which are
   surplus by definition: compression removed the tell and
   kept the claim (T-1). The harvest is now the **keep-list
   harvest** (DIALOGUE_METHOD Pass 3.5; template §4.4
   `KEEP_LIST` / `cut_record`). Template §7 row 37 stands as
   the historical record of B-8.
