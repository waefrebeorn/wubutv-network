## U1 — MARKET DAY (board 12)

STATE IN: board 12 · hat ON · coat ON · sack near-empty · the
garden chained, DORO inside.
PICTURE: the full set in one wide shot — banner over the stall,
cart frame-right, garden gate frame-left. PETT rains petals over
his cart. LILA stands centre with her board held up: 12. MOSS,
PIA and PIO buy bread at the next stall, watching sideways.
DIALOGUE:
- PETT (offer — names the garment price): "Pink petals! A
  hundred petals for one good coat!"
- LILA (want, stated as her terms): "I have twelve petals. I
  need one million."
- PETT (demand — pats her coat sleeve): "Then start with the
  coat, little buyer."
- LILA (refusal + counter-demand, hand on her hat): "The coat
  stays. Name a smaller price."
END STATE: PETT's eyes go to her HAT. He smiles.

## U2 — THE HAGGLE, THE MELT, THE BET (board 62)

STATE IN: board 12 · hat ON · coat ON.
PICTURE: close at the cart. The haggle is hands and garments:
his palm out, her chin up. Then the sale — he lifts the hat off
her head with a flourish, pours fifty petals into her sack; a
witness-hand repaints the board: 62. LILA lifts one petal from
the sack, delighted — and it MELTS in her palm, pink dripping
through her fingers, in front of the whole stall. PIA and PIO
point and laugh. MOSS winces. LILA holds the dripping hand up,
high, so everyone sees — and bets.
DIALOGUE:
- PETT (demand): "The coat. One hundred petals. Fair price."
- LILA (counter-offer — takes off her own hat, holds it out):
  "Not the coat. My hat. Fifty petals."
- PETT (accepts — hat in his hand, petals pouring): "Done!"
- (the melt — no line; laughter only)
- LILA (THE BET — cap-exempt; loud, to the witnesses as much
  as to him): "Every petal in my sack is real by sunset — or
  the coat is yours. For nothing."
- PETT (accepts — hand out): "Shake on it."
END STATE: handshake held · hat in PETT's hand · board 62 ·
her bare head and the dripping palm in the same frame.

## U3 — THE TEST AND THE WISH (board 31 REAL)

STATE IN: board 62 · hat GONE · bet live.
PICTURE: LILA's small table beside the garden gate. A bowl of
clear water. One by one she dips her petals: fakes melt pink
and sink; real ones float. The melt-pile grows beside the bowl;
the sack shrinks. Mid-unit, THE PICTURED WISH: she looks
through the chain at her garden, her eyes close for one beat —
the SAME composition, dreamed: the gate stands OPEN, the beds
flowered thick, LILA inside in her red coat, hat on, sack full
and heavy, morning light. Hold it. (This exact composition is
the season finale's target frame.) Back: the bowl, the count.
She repaints the board herself: 31 REAL. Behind the chain,
DORO hangs another garland and watches her work.
DIALOGUE:
- DORO (taunt through the chain — he is spending her garden):
  "Your garden likes my flowers better."
- LILA (claim, answered by her hands — she dips the next
  petal without looking up): "My garden. I am buying it back."
END STATE: board 31 REAL · melt-pile high · sack smaller than
when she came · DORO's second garland hung.

## U4 — THE BOWL GOES BACK (board 31 REAL — 19 FAKE)

STATE IN: board 31 REAL · hat GONE · the bowl full of pink
melt-water.
PICTURE: LILA carries the bowl with both hands through the
square and sets it down ON PETT's cart, in front of his
customers. Two customers lean in, look at the pink water,
look at their own handfuls of petals — and walk away. PETT
sees them go. LILA plants her brush: she repaints the board
herself, on his cart, in his square: 31 REAL — 19 FAKE.
DIALOGUE:
- LILA (proof presented — to him and the customers): "Look.
  Your petals. Pink water."
- PETT (deflect + counter-offer — scooping fast): "Bad luck!
  Nineteen new petals. Free."
- LILA (demand): "My hat back. And real petals. All
  nineteen."
- PETT (refusal — hugs the hat to his chest): "No. The hat
  is mine. Take the nineteen."
END STATE: board 31 REAL — 19 FAKE in LILA's paint · the hat
against PETT's chest · his customer side of the square empty.

## U5 — THREE FACES (board 231)

STATE IN: board 31/19 · hat GONE · witnesses in the square.
PICTURE: LILA calls the witnesses over with one arm — MOSS,
PIA, PIO come to the cart. FIRST, THE THREE FACES: the camera
gives the witnesses' faces the frame as LILA reaches up,
takes the BEST petal from PETT's own display crown, and dips
it in the bowl. It melts. MOSS: shock. PIA: shock. PIO:
delight. Only then the consequence, same square, same
witnesses: PETT unlocks the box under his cart, takes out
her hat and gives it back, then pours his true stock — 200
real petals — into her sack. Board repainted: 231. LILA puts
her hat on.
DIALOGUE:
- LILA (summons — a move, not a remark): "Moss. Pia. Pio.
  Watch his best petal."
- (the dip — the melt — the three faces; no lines)
- LILA (THE DEMAND — cap-exempt; quiet, in the silence after
  the melt): "Open the box, Pett."
- PETT (surrender — key already turning): "...The hat. And
  two hundred real."
END STATE: board 231 · hat BACK on LILA · box open and empty
of its secret · the three witnesses still watching.

## U6 — THE DISPOSAL (board 231, and tomorrow)

STATE IN: board 231 · hat ON · coat ON · sack heavy.
PICTURE: walking home through the square at dusk, past the
gutter stream. LILA stops. From her pocket she takes the one
melted fake petal she kept — limp, pink, dead — looks at it
once, and drops it in the gutter. It washes away pink and is
gone. THE DISPOSAL. She repaints the board one last time:
231 — and chalks the first line of tomorrow's number under
it. Behind her, inside the garden, DORO hangs one BLACK petal
on his garland.
DIALOGUE:
- DORO (from inside the garden — a gift that is a threat):
  "One black petal, Lila. For your garden."
- LILA (defiance, answered by her chalk — she does not turn
  around): "Tomorrow I buy more."
END STATE (held): board 231 with tomorrow chalked under it ·
her hat on, her coat on, the sack heavy on her back · the
black petal hanging in her garden.
