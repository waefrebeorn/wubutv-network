## BOARD SUMMARY

- **Premise:** The door the Clerk never opened opens itself from the far side, and the Counter completes owed 1006 a beat too late to own it. Beyond the threshold the second desk stands unattended, counting on its own — and keeping, exactly, the Clerk's totals. Its tally roll proves the back-step was recorded outside her ledger, so the Clerk locks her book and names his concession as the price of reopening it. The door then seals from the far side with the desk still counting behind it, and the Counter must speak the cliff alone and unaudited.
