# DRAFT — SHOW-4 THE COUNT, Episode 2 (Pass 3 — Dialogue Writer)

- **Input:** BREAKDOWN.md (approved board, Pass 2), the pitch bible's
  voice notes (SHOW-4 treatment + pilot LOOK blocks), PILOT-4's final
  unit, and the ledger's next-episode anchors for E2.
- **Drafting rule observed:** units drafted serially, in order, under
  the carry-forward rule — each unit's final line and exit state are
  pasted at the head of the next unit's draft block.
- **Voice notes (from the pilot's shipped lines):**
  - SUPER NUMBER HEAD (the Counter): deadpan, exact; declarative
    arithmetic claims and accounts; short sentences; the dread is
    under the exactness, never on top of it. Pilot register: "Your
    ledger counted that parcel twice." / "It went back one. Tell no
    one." / "Open it, Clerk. It's counting without us."
  - THE TALLY CLERK: flat verdicts and imperatives; cites her book;
    speaks the count's numbers like entries, not exclamations. Pilot
    register: "Say the number between ours, Counter." / "Write the
    missing digit, Counter. Now." / "We agree, Counter. So we're both
    wrong."
- **A3 construction (every unit):** turn 1 and turn 2 are separate
  quoted blocks with the picture turn written between them; a late
  beat is parked on paper at ≥6.5s (declared per unit in BREAKDOWN);
  the tail carries a speech-independent event (declared per unit in
  BREAKDOWN). Turn 1 in every unit is about what the picture shows
  late, so it stays true whenever the generator lands it.
- **Cliff fragments** (the spoken numbers) are the show's device —
  uncharged to the pair budgets, per Method B §1.

## Gate D — tactic audit (run at draft)

- **Clerk's tactic sequence:** PRESS → CLAIM → NAME → CORRECT →
  CLAIM → PRICE → VERDICT. No consecutive repeat for the speaker
  (the two CLAIMs are separated by NAME and CORRECT; PRICE and
  VERDICT are adjacent speeches but different verbs). Final tactic
  (VERDICT) ≠ opening tactic (PRESS). ✓
- **Counter's tactic sequence:** ACCOUNT → COUNTER-QUESTION →
  DEMAND → MISNAME → CHALLENGE → CONCEDE. No consecutive repeat.
  Final (CONCEDE) ≠ opening (ACCOUNT). ✓
- **See-saw named per turn** in every draft block above. ✓
- **Swap test:** every turn 2 breaks in transit — each answers its
  own turn 1's specific words (the deadline, the kept total, the
  who-question, the "slipped" misnaming, the shut book, the price).
  ✓
- **Cost test:** the Counter pays in all six units (ownership,
  premise, status, the secret, a debt, the premise again); the
  Clerk pays in U5 (her wall becomes a priced offer — the book can
  reopen). ✓
- **Narrator test:** every turn is a speech act to a named
  addressee with a named want; no turn restates its unit's hook
  fact — each does something about it to the other character. ✓
