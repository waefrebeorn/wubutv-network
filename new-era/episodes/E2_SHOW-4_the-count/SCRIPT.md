## Bible lines (carried from the pilot, locked)

- **Palette:** INK (hall, suits, desk) + BONE (paper, blouse, chalk)
  + CADMIUM (the board's glowing digits, the tally brass) + one
  COBALT accent (the Clerk's ink). No other hues.
- **Light:** one motivated source per frame — the board's glow, one
  overhead lamp; through U6's seal, the door's narrowing light-gap.
  Flat, deadpan, diagram-clear.
- **Camera:** locked medium-wide on the desk for anchor units; no
  camera move in the first 12 frames of any unit; moves only as
  events (track to the door, push to the roll).
- **Texture:** fine film grain finish; clean render is a fail.
- **The world rule, visualised:** at every joint the hall behind the
  held elements (desk, board, ledger, door) visibly swaps — light,
  chairs, dressing — while the count survives. The characters know;
  nobody lectures it.
- **LOOK blocks** are the pilot's, unchanged: SUPER NUMBER HEAD —
  tall gaunt adult man, very large round bald head, pale skin,
  heavy-lidded eyes that never blink, ink-black suit, white shirt,
  brass mechanical tally counter on a chain at his chest, long
  fingers, deadpan. THE TALLY CLERK — adult woman, sharp black bob,
  round spectacles, bone-white blouse, ink-black vest, cobalt-blue
  ink stains on her fingertips, deadpan.

## Script (one conversation; turn 1 rides the picture turn, turn 2
## at 6.5–8.7s, cliff number at 9–10s)

**U1 — "A locked door whose handle turns itself, and the owed
number finished too late."** *(P6 · STANDARD · silence beat)*
HOOK 0–1.5: frame 0 = a brass key tag in a locked door reads 1006;
the Clerk's hand rests still on the handle; she does not turn it.
STATE: the hall swapped behind the held door (new light, chairs
reversed); she watches the handle, not him. TURN 4–7: the Clerk
presses — **"Finish it, Counter. Before it does."** — then the
SILENCE BEAT (2.5s): the handle turns itself under her still hand,
from the far side; the door opens inward; nobody beyond it. LINE
(Counter → Clerk, ACCOUNT): **"One thousand six. Late, not
mine."** CLIFF (Clerk, flat): "One thousand seven."

**U2 — "A threshold, and a second board keeping the Clerk's
total."** *(P5 · FULL)*
HOOK: the threshold plate reads 1007 as the pair crosses; the
Clerk's ledger lies open at her standing total. STATE: the
unattended door swings between the pair and the desk beyond,
hiding and revealing its board. TURN: revealed — the other board
reads 1008, exactly her ledger's standing total. LINE (Clerk →
Counter, CLAIM): **"It keeps my total, Counter. Exactly."**
LINE 2 (Counter → Clerk, COUNTER-QUESTION): **"Then whose hand
keeps it, Clerk?"** CLIFF (Counter — he speaks her number): "One
thousand eight."

**U3 — "An empty desk that answers by counting."** *(P2 · FULL)*
HOOK: an enamel sign reads 1008 above the second desk; its chair
is empty; no one sits at it. STATE: the Counter faces the desk;
the Clerk at his shoulder; the desk's board holds 1008. TURN:
LINE (Counter → the desk, DEMAND): **"Who sits here? Let them
count."** — the desk bell strikes itself; its board advances to
1009 with no hand near it. LINE 2 (Clerk → Counter, NAME):
**"Nobody sits there. It counts alone."** CLIFF (Clerk, reading
its board): "One thousand nine."

**U4 — "A tally roll, and the step he ordered unwritten, written."**
*(P8 · STANDARD · one marked callback: F-006, as a record)*
HOOK: a paper tally roll feeds out of the unattended desk, its
header printing 1009. STATE: the Counter draws the roll through
his long fingers, reading down it; the Clerk watches his hands,
not the paper. TURN: a backward mark at 1003 — his back-step,
recorded. LINE (Counter → Clerk, MISNAME): **"It slipped, Clerk.
Rolls do."** LINE 2 (Clerk → Counter, CORRECT): **"It records.
Your step, in order."** Reaction beat: his hand stops on the
roll; he does not tear it. CLIFF (Counter): "One thousand ten."

**U5 — "A ledger locked, and a price named for its reopening."**
*(P1 · FULL)*
HOOK: the ledger's clasp stamps 1010 as the Clerk closes the book
on the desk between them. STATE: behind her, past the swinging
door, the other desk's board reads 1011; she does not turn to it.
TURN: LINE (Clerk → Counter, CLAIM): **"My book shuts on my
word."** LINE 2 (Counter → Clerk, CHALLENGE): **"Unaudited counts
are guesses, Clerk."** FRAGMENT (Clerk, PRICE): **"Concede the
book counts."** Her hand rests on the locked clasp — the book is
shut, not destroyed. CLIFF (Clerk): "One thousand eleven."

**U6 — "A door sealing itself, and a count spoken with no one to
audit it."** *(P3 · STANDARD)*
HOOK: the inner door face reads 1011; the pair stands on the hall
side of the threshold, her locked ledger under her arm. STATE:
the door begins to close on its own; the light-gap narrows. TURN:
LINE (Clerk → Counter, through the gap, VERDICT): **"Count alone,
then. The price stands."** — the door seals from the far side;
in the closing gap, the desk's board is glimpsed at 1012, still
counting. LINE 2 (Counter → the sealed door, CONCEDE — the pilot
line re-priced): **"It's counting without us, Clerk."** CLIFF
(Counter, alone, unaudited): "One thousand twelve."
