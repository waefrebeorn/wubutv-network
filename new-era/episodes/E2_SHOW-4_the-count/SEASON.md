## The final image (E8)

The count reaches the number it skipped. The Counter speaks it —
alone in the hall, no ledger open, no door to hide behind — and
behind the door, for the first time in the series, the desk's board
goes dark mid-count. Whether the count completed because the
station earned its viewer (his theory) or because it ran out of
things to count (hers), the show does not say. The last spoken
number of the season is the skipped one.

## Character arcs (start → end)

- **SUPER NUMBER HEAD:** E1 — the count is his and true, and its
  errors are other people's. → E8 — he speaks the skipped number
  knowing it ends the count; the count completes at the moment he
  stops owning it. (E2 moved him: ownership CONTESTED, counting
  alone and unaudited, a priced debt unpaid.)
- **THE TALLY CLERK:** E1 — the audit shadow: her book disagrees by
  one and nobody has to care. → E8 — her book and the board agree
  at the completion, and her +1 is resolved as the count's own
  skipped beat — the gap was the count protecting itself, and her
  book (and the desk) were keeping the missing number safe.
  (Direction of the resolution is loose-band; the resolution itself
  is firm.) (E2 moved her: UP — she holds the audit, the price,
  and the last word at the door.)
