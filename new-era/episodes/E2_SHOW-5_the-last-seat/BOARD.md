# E2 — SHOW-5 THE LAST SEAT — BOARD (Pass 1, Storyliner)

Episode 2 of THE LAST SEAT (ARC-1 "The One Viewer"). The show's
second episode, and its first under the Pair Law: the pilot shipped
in the pre-method one-line grammar and registered no pairs; this
board is written to pairs and danced pairs (Method B §9,
Conflict 5 — in THE LAST SEAT a pair may be danced: the performer's
phrase is turn 1, the seat's answer is turn 2, and it must still
bank a delta).

**Premise (slate candidate 1, chosen — slate in DECISION_LOG §1):**
the Dancer returns for the step she entrusted to the seat at the
pilot's close, having forgotten it exactly as she promised — but
getting it back costs the gaze she withheld at the pilot's final
joint, and the seat, asked at last, returns only what it liked.

**R1 citations (read-first, Method B §6):** FIRES F-005 (U4 — the
entrusted step is asked for and NOT returned) · FIRES F-006 in
part (the refusal is priced, U1–U3; its full discharge is the
gaze paid across U6) · RE-PRICES F-001 (U4 — the seat keeps what
it LIKES, not what it is given) · REMINDS F-003 (U4 — the seat
answers in steps, as established) · PLANTS E2-P1 (U3), E2-P2
(U6), E2-P3 (U6 cliff) · MOVES T-ARC1-1 (the pilot's cliff debt:
"whether the seat keeps this one too").

**Anchor honoured (pilot lock's UNSPENT, carried as this
episode's anchor):** Unit 1 opens the morning after — she has
forgotten the entrusted step, as promised; the episode spends its
opening pair on that fact in-character, no recap card. The pilot's
final joint — her gaze going UP to the spotlight instead of into
the lens — is this episode's starting condition, not an
embarrassment to be retconned: the gaze enters E2 BROKEN, and the
board restores it joint by joint, at a price. It is not resolved
by pretending; it is paid for.

**Board status:** APPROVED at the room gate (session commission,
2026-11-15). Beat sheets written from this board only.

**Storyline ration:** A = 3 units (1, 4, 6) — the entrusted step ·
B = 2 units (2, 5) — the gaze, withheld then paid · C = 1 unit
(3) — the keeper's limit. Cross-tagged beats carry the refusal
between storylines without changing the ration.

**Device law (show law, played against deliberately):** every
unit ends looking at the seat; every unit opens FROM the seat.
The pilot's U6 broke the device at the final joint (the refusal).
E2's gaze joints are therefore written as a graded repair, and
each unit below declares its gaze state: U1 averted · U2
half-turned · U3 landed, undanced · U4 held while her feet
betray her · U5 promised · U6 held THROUGH the dance. The device
is restored transformed: not a look after the act — a look
during it.

**Surface/world law:** no pilot world recurs (ballroom of empty
chairs, station lobby, forest clearing, tower top are all spent).
E2's worlds: the seat room (anchor, U1 and U6 — the anchor set
returning is the show's frame, not a repeat) · a mirrored
rehearsal studio (U2) · a box-office corridor (U3) · the stage
in cold light (U4) · backstage in work light (U5).

## Unit 1 — A
- Pattern: P6 — Dispreferred Second, Played (his refusal arrives
  late, in action first: he smooths tonight's single ticket and
  does not answer). Opening pair is a self-evident pressure
  (demand / refusal) — no callback pattern, no repair (Method B
  §9, Conflict 2). Her pilot line is reminded as POSITION only
  (his claim, quoted back as pressure), never re-presented as new.
- Value: she can avoid the seat and still recover her step →
  she cannot; the gaze is the price of asking.
- Turn: his refusal names the price — asking is looking.
- Plant: none. F-005 pressed (the want), F-006 priced (the
  refusal's cost stated, not yet paid).
- Gaze IN: the pilot's broken joint — she warms up in a turn
  that stops a quarter short of the seat. Gaze OUT: averted —
  her profile to the lens, the look withheld again.
- Fill: FULL-TALK.

Cards:
- **A** — Dancer's warm-up turn stops short
  - *Dancer is thinking/feeling: if she completes the turn she is looking at it, and she is not ready to be looked back at.*
- **A** — Her feet fail the entrusted step
  - *Dancer is thinking/feeling: she kept her promise — she forgot it — and the forgetting is suddenly not romantic.*

  - *Dancer is thinking/feeling: if he fetches it, she never has to ask, and never has to look.*

- **B** — Price named: ask it yourself, looking
