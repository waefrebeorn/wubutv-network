## Unit 2 — B · DANCED PAIR (refused second) · Fill WORDLESS (declared)

Interruptions: none.
1. FROM THE SEAT: a mirrored rehearsal studio (the world has
   culled). The Dancer dances the ask with her back to the
   seat, facing the mirror — she performs the steps she still
   owns, full out, at her own reflection. (OFFER — Δpower)
2. She reaches the hole — the place where the entrusted phrase
   belongs — and dances AROUND it: the surrounding steps,
   bracketing an absence, twice, smaller the second time.
   (EXPOSE — Δinfo: the gap is shown as choreography)
3. TURN 2 (the seat's answer): nothing. The stage light stays
   flat and cold; no warmth, no shift. A held 2s of
   non-answer while she waits, back still turned, breathing.
   (REFUSAL — Δpower: a second part withheld is a state

4. Her head begins to turn toward the seat — the body deciding
   before the pride does. (YIELD — Δinfo)

flat light (the picture turn is an absence). CLIFF — gaze
joint HALF-TURNED: her head mid-turn at the cut; tail event:

Motif: dancing around a gap.
Word budget: WORDLESS — exempt from word gates (Method B §9,

## Unit 3 — C · P5 Counter-Question That Concedes · Fill FULL-TALK
Plants: PLANTED E2-P1 (keepers don't ask). Interruptions: none.
1. FROM THE SEAT: a box-office corridor (culled world); the
   seat stands in it, unculled, as it stands everywhere. The
   Usher performs the keeper's ritual: the back of his hand
   hovers a breath from the velvet, reading its warmth,
   touching nothing. (TEND — Δinfo: his knowledge is real and
   it is all like this — near, never spent)
2. The Dancer enters the corridor's far end and presses him:
   he tends it daily — ask it. (PRESS — Δpower)
3. His account: keepers don't ask; we keep. The line is flat,
   not unkind — a rule recited, not a weapon. (ACCOUNT —
   Δinfo: the refusal gains its reason; E2-P1 planted in the
   account's plain form)
4. Her counter-question concedes his premise — the seat
   answers SOMEONE — and attacks only the conclusion: then
   who does it answer? His turn line gives the premise its
   name: the one who looks at it. (CONCEDE — Δinfo: premise
   enters shared KNOWS / NAME — Δpower: the rule now aims
   her)
Picture: HOOK — the hovering hand over the velvet, sound-off
legible: the keeper and his whole religion in one gesture.
STATE — her approach down the corridor. TURN — his hand
withdraws from the seat as he recites the rule (turn 1 rides
her press; the withdrawal is the picture turn). CLIFF — gaze
joint LANDED: she faces the seat fully, first time since the
pilot's refusal; tail event: the corridor's work light clicks

Motif: the keeper's hand testing the seat's warmth.
Word budget: FULL-TALK 22–25 · silence beat: none · late beat
parked: the turn line (beat 4) at ≥6.5s.

## Unit 5 — B · P1 Concession-With-A-Price (+P3 callback) · Fill FULL-TALK
Plants: none. OWES born (the full gaze). Interruptions: none.
1. FROM THE SEAT: backstage in work light (culled world) —
   ropes, a bare bulb, the billing sheet pinned to a board:
   tonight's act, her name, in the Usher's hand. The Usher
   offers the safe road: dance the kept one tonight.
   (OFFER — Δpower)
2. She refuses yesterday's step — the room has seen it; she
   will not repeat it. (REFUSE — Δpower: the recursion

   billing as written — tonight is hers to risk — and names

   Δpower: STATUS — hers rises; PRICE — Δpower: an OWES is
   born)
4. Her acceptance re-prices her pilot line: yesterday she
   promised to forget it looking away; tonight — watch me
   forget it looking. (ACCEPT — Δinfo: the callback lands at

Picture: HOOK — the billing sheet, her name, his pencil

Motif: the pencil set down unused.
Word budget: FULL-TALK 22–25 · silence beat: none · late
