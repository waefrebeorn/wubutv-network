# E2 — SHOW-5 THE LAST SEAT — "Ask the Seat"

Logline: The Dancer comes back for the step she entrusted to
the seat — but getting it back means giving the seat the gaze
she withheld, and the seat returns only what it liked.

6 units × 10s = 60s · 1440 frames @ 24fps · one conversation:
USHER ↔ DANCER, with the seat as the third party — addressed
directly in U6, answering throughout in its own language
(light, steps, silence). Arc: ARC-1 "The One Viewer", thread
T-ARC1-1 (the pilot's cliff debt) moved. Stake: the Dancer's
act — and whether she performs to be KEPT or to be WATCHED.

R1 citations (read-first, Method B): FIRES F-005 (U4) ·
FIRES F-006 in part (U1–U3 price the refusal; U6 discharges
it) · RE-PRICES F-001 (U4) · REMINDS F-003 (U4) · PLANTS
E2-P1 (U3), E2-P2 (U6), E2-P3 (U6 cliff) · MOVES T-ARC1-1.

Transition device (show law): every unit ends looking at the
seat; the next unit opens FROM the seat. This episode plays
the device as a graded repair of the pilot's broken final
joint (her gaze went UP to the spotlight — a refusal, shipped
canon, GATE_LOG): U1 averted · U2 half-turned · U3 landed ·
U4 held while her feet betray her · U5 promised · U6 held
THROUGH the dance. The refusal is not retconned; it is paid
for, joint by joint.
First-second doctrine (opening beat): frame 0 = FROM THE
SEAT, the Dancer mid-warm-up-turn, already moving — and the
turn stops a quarter short of the seat. A dancer who will
not face the one seat: legible with the sound off, in motion
at the cut.
Lore shown, never explained: a ticket for a house of one,
unsold; a keeper who reads warmth with the back of his hand
and has never asked for anything; a step that arrives in a
dancer's feet uninvited; a light that answers before the end
of a dance for the first time. The seat's occupant is never
shown. Nothing resolves about who sits.

Word budgets (Writers Room, layered per §7 Conflict 2):
U1/U3/U5 FULL-TALK 22–25 · U4 STANDARD pair-gated (spoken
9–12 + danced coverage) · U2 declared WORDLESS (danced pair;

## Units

### U1 — the price of asking (A · P6 Dispreferred Second, Played · FULL-TALK · 25 words)
Frame sentence: *the seat room from the seat's view, the
dancer's warm-up turn stopping short — and her feet failing
the step she came back for.*
Opens: FROM THE SEAT. The seat room, morning. The Dancer is
mid-warm-up-turn — the turn stops a quarter short of facing
the seat; she covers the stop by shaking out her shoulders.
She tries the entrusted step's first move from memory; her
feet produce a smaller, wrong thing, and stop. Beside the
seat, the USHER holds tonight's single ticket.
Motion motif: the turn that stops short.
- DANCER → USHER | DEMAND — "Bring my step back, Usher." (5)
  [DELAY BEAT — P6, action not words, 0.8s: the Usher looks
  at the seat; he smooths the ticket flat against his palm;
  he says nothing]
- USHER → DANCER | REFUSE (with account) — "I keep the seat,
  Dancer. Not steps." (7)
- DANCER → USHER | PRESS — "You told this room it kept it."
  (7) [Pass 3b form — see Production notes]
- USHER → DANCER | PRICE — TURN LINE — "Ask it yourself,
  Dancer. Eyes first." (6)
  [Tail beat, unspoken: the Usher sets the single ticket
  upright against the seat's armrest — unsold, unhanded]
UNSAID: DANCER — "I don't want to look at it. It might not
look back." · USHER — "I have never asked it for anything. I
will not begin with her step."
DELTA: her want (recovery by proxy) is refused and marked
CONTESTED; the price of asking is named — the gaze. F-005
pressed; F-006 priced, not yet paid.
CLIFF (gaze joint): AVERTED — her profile to the lens; the
look withheld a second time, now knowingly.

### U2 — the back turned (B · DANCED PAIR, refused second · WORDLESS, declared)
Frame sentence: *a mirrored rehearsal studio, and the dancer
asking her own reflection for the step — with her back to
the only audience.*
Opens: FROM THE SEAT. The world has culled: a mirrored
rehearsal studio. The Dancer dances the ask with her back to
the seat, facing the mirror — the steps she still owns, full
out, at her own reflection.
- TURN 1 (danced): she reaches the hole — the place where
  the entrusted phrase belongs — and dances AROUND it: the
  surrounding steps, bracketing an absence, twice, smaller
  the second time.
- TURN 2 (the seat's answer): NOTHING. The stage light
  stays flat and cold; no warmth, no shift. A held 2s of
  non-answer while she waits, back still turned, breathing.
Motion motif: dancing around a gap.
UNSAID: DANCER — "It kept steps before it ever saw my face.
Why should my back matter?"
DELTA: KNOWS — she learns dancing is not asking: the seat
answers an address, not a performance. Her want CONTESTED,
deepened. Seat-signs log gains the NON-answer (a refused
second part, recorded as a sign class at lock).
CLIFF (gaze joint): HALF-TURNED — her head begins the turn
her body refused; tail beat: in the mirror, her eyes find
the seat's reflection before she turns — she sees it seeing
her, second-hand.

### U3 — the keeper's limit (C · P5 Counter-Question That Concedes · FULL-TALK · 25 words)
Frame sentence: *a box-office corridor, the seat standing
unculled in it, and the keeper's hand hovering over the
velvet — reading warmth, touching nothing.*
Opens: FROM THE SEAT. The world has culled: a box-office
corridor; the seat stands in it as it stands everywhere. The
USHER performs the keeper's ritual: the back of his hand
hovering a breath from the velvet. The DANCER enters at the
corridor's far end.
Motion motif: the keeper's hand testing the seat's warmth.
- DANCER → USHER | PRESS — "You tend it daily. Ask it,
  Usher." (7)
  [PICTURE TURN: his hand withdraws from the seat as he
  answers — the rule costs him the ritual]
- USHER → DANCER | ACCOUNT — "Keepers don't ask, Dancer. We
  keep." (6)
- DANCER → USHER | COUNTER-QUESTION — "Then who does it
  answer, Usher?" (6)
- USHER → DANCER | NAME — TURN LINE — "The one who looks at
  it." (6)
  [Tail beat, unspoken: the corridor's work light clicks
  off behind her, leaving the seat's end lit]
UNSAID: DANCER — "He is not refusing me. He is refusing for
himself — and that frightens me more." · USHER — "The day I
ask it for something, I stop keeping it. I am afraid of
what it would answer."
DELTA: the premise enters SHARED KNOWS — the seat answers
the one who looks at it. E2-P1 PLANTED inside the account:
keepers don't ask — the Usher has never asked the seat for
anything (remind Ep 4, payoff Ep 6).
CLIFF (gaze joint): LANDED — she faces the seat fully, the
first time since the pilot's refusal. The unit ends before
she dares the ask itself.

### U4 — the wrong step back (A · P2 Misdirected Answer, danced · STANDARD · 12 spoken words)
Frame sentence: *the stage in cold light, the dancer
offering the seat the shape of her missing step — and her
feet answering with yesterday's.*
Opens: FROM THE SEAT. The stage in cold light. The Dancer
stands in the seat's view and dances the ASK: the steps she
owns, then the held shape of the missing phrase, arms
bracketing the absence, gaze on the seat throughout.
- TURN 1 (danced): the offered gap — a complete, honest
  ask, faced.
- TURN 2 (the seat's answer): the cold light WARMS — and
  her feet take the returned step: the low slide-step, the
  snap head-turn, the shoulders dropping. YESTERDAY'S step,
  arriving in her body while she faces the seat. She dances
  two phrases before she can stop it; her face goes from
  gratitude to fury mid-phrase.
  [PICTURE TURN: the turn is in her shoes, not her face]
- DANCER → USHER | REPORT — "This isn't it. This is
  yesterday's." (6)
- USHER → DANCER | NAME — TURN LINE — "It kept the one it
  liked." (6)
  [Tail beat, unspoken: her feet settle, against her will,
  into the old step's closing pose]
Motion motif: the feet starting without her.
UNSAID: DANCER — "It heard me. Whatever comes back, it
heard me." · USHER — "It has never kept a thing out of duty.
I am sorry to be the one who knows it."
DELTA: F-005 FIRED — the entrusted step, asked for with the
gaze paid, is NOT returned. F-001 RE-PRICED — the seat
keeps what it LIKES, not what it is given; keeping is
taste, not custody. F-003 REMINDED — it answers in steps:
hers, moving.
CLIFF (gaze joint): HELD WHILE BETRAYED — she stops
mid-old-step and does not look down; she holds the seat's
gaze with the wrong step still in her legs.

### U5 — the price of tonight (B · P1 Concession-With-A-Price + P3 callback · FULL-TALK · 25 words)
Frame sentence: *backstage in work light, the billing sheet
with her name on it, and the keeper offering her survival —
yesterday's dance, kept and safe.*
Opens: FROM THE SEAT. Backstage in work light (culled
world): ropes, a bare bulb, the billing sheet pinned to a
board — tonight's act, her name, in the Usher's hand. His
pencil hovers over the sheet.
- USHER → DANCER | OFFER — "Dance the kept one tonight,
  Dancer." (6)
  [PICTURE TURN: she looks at the sheet, not at him]
- DANCER → USHER | REFUSE — "It's yesterday's. I won't
  repeat it." (6)
- USHER → DANCER | CONCEDE + PRICE — TURN LINE — "Then
  dance unkept. Looking. All of it." (7)
  [Reaction beat, unspoken: he sets the pencil down UNUSED
  — he changes nothing on the sheet]
- DANCER → USHER | ACCEPT (P3 — her pilot line re-priced)
  — "Then watch me forget it looking." (6)
  [Tail beat, unspoken: he takes the billing sheet down
  and carries it toward his place beside the seat]
Motion motif: the pencil set down unused.
UNSAID: USHER — "If she looks away once, I will call it off
to save her — and she will never forgive either of us." ·
DANCER — "He is not daring me. He is covering me. There is
a difference, and I am only just learning it."
DELTA: an OWES is born — the Dancer owes the full gaze for
tonight's dance, no turning away (discharged U6). STATUS —
the Usher concedes the billing: Dancer ↑. Her WANTS changes
on record: from recovering what is kept → being watched
making what is not.
CLIFF (gaze joint): PROMISED — she steps out of the work
light toward the stage end, already facing the seat's
direction.

### U6 — the dance under the gaze (A · DANCED PAIR, the answer during · silence-beat unit · 15 spoken words)
Frame sentence: *the seat room again, one warm light, and
the dancer beginning a step nobody has ever kept — straight
at the one seat, looking.*
Opens: FROM THE SEAT. The anchor set, changed: the stage
bare, one warm light — and the single ticket still upright
on the armrest (U1's tail event, carried across the
episode). The USHER stands beside the seat.
- USHER → DANCER | SEND — "The seat is watching, Dancer.
  Begin." (6)
- TURN 1 (danced): she begins a NEW step — never danced,
  never kept — first phrase, straight at the seat, eyes on
  the lens the whole time.
  [SILENCE BEAT — 2.5s: mid-phrase she STOPS. Holds.
  Looks. The exact spot where she looked away yesterday,
  stood in and occupied, in silence — immediately before
  the turn line]
- DANCER → THE SEAT | REFUSE-KEEPING — TURN LINE — "Don't
  keep it, Seat. Watch it happen." (7)
- TURN 2 (the seat's answer): she dances the phrase out,
  whole, once — and DURING its back half the light changes:
  warms, deepens, while she is still moving. The first
  mid-performance answer in the show's record.
- CLIFF FRAGMENT: USHER → DANCER | REPORT — "It's
  answering." (2)
  [Tail beat, unspoken: the ticket on the armrest has
  fallen flat — or been laid flat — unseen. Set dressing
  only: NOT a plant; no payoff is promised on it and none
  may be claimed (Pass 5 watch item, DECISION_LOG §5)]
Motion motif: the light changing mid-phrase.
UNSAID: DANCER — "Let it cull the step. I am not dancing
for the archive anymore." · USHER — "In all my years of
keeping, it has never once answered before the end of
anything."
DELTA: E2-P2 PLANTED — the seat can answer DURING a
performance (remind Ep 3, payoff Ep 5). E2-P3 PLANTED —
the unkept dance: did it survive the night? (payoff Ep 3,
opens E3). The U5 OWES is DISCHARGED — the gaze paid in
full, through the dance. Her WANTS completes its change:
she dances to be watched.
CLIFF (episode cliff, device — the gaze joint HELD
THROUGH): final frame is her looking down the lens,
breathing, the light warm around her, the step finished
and unkept. Ledger debt: the seat answered during the
dance — is it keeping this one AS SHE MAKES IT, or warning
her? T-ARC1-1 carries the question to Episode 3.
