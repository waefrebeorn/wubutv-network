# VEHICLES — E2 SHOW-2 THE TENTH SECOND, "The Chalk Wall"

One vehicle per unit. Every vehicle carries the same style line and
the same LOOK blocks verbatim (prompt discipline); only the world,
action and dialogue blocks change. Dialogue is given in separate
quoted blocks in script order, with the picture turn between them —
the lines are written to read wherever they land. Duration and
aspect are stated in text on every call: 10 seconds, 16:9.

STYLE (all units): Photorealistic live-action, cinematic, slight
codec softness, never ad-clean. 10 seconds, 16:9 widescreen,
1280×720. No captions, no subtitles, no titles. The only lettering
anywhere is chalk on the wall.

LOOK (all units, verbatim):
- COURIER: adult woman, 34; sharp black bob haircut with a straight
  fringe; calm deadpan face; long cobalt-blue courier coat to the
  knee; black cross-body strap; canvas satchel at her hip; dark
  trousers; scuffed boots.
- WATCHER: adult man, 68; short grey stubble beard; deep-set eyes;
  ink-black cardigan over a bone-white shirt; sleeves pushed up;
  white chalk dust on his fingers and forearms.
- LAURA: adult woman, early 30s; dark brown hair in a low ponytail;
  sharp, curious expression; forest-green work shirt with two chest
  pockets; dark trousers. No headphones.
- PARCEL: one mid-size parcel wrapped in brown paper, cross-tied
  with string, one gold stamp on top, no readable words on it.

## U1 — the unfinished name (image 1: PNL-PEAK-U1 · image 2: PNL-CHAR-LAURA-hero-e2)
World: the depot street at dawn — wide wooden depot door, brick
wall dense with white chalk tally marks, one brass lamp, cobbles,
morning light from frame left. On the wall, among the tallies: a
circled chalk drawing of a parcel, and two fresh chalk letters, L
and A — the start of a name — chalk dust still settling in the
light.
Action (already moving at frame 0): LAURA arrives mid-stride from
frame right, boots scuffing the cobbles, and stops at the fresh
letters, her right hand extending to trace the stroke. The COURIER
stands at the wall, the parcel under her left arm, a stub of white
chalk in her raised right fist beside the fresh letters; she turns
to the arriving woman. Sound in the first second: boots scuffing
to a stop on cobbles, chalk tapping brick.
Beats: Laura traces the stroke and knows her own hand. The Courier
looks from the unfinished letters to the chalk in her own fist.
Laura holds her hand out for the chalk. The Courier gives her the
chalk stub; Laura's fist closes on it as a warm cull light begins
to rise at the street's far end.
Dialogue (exact words, in order):
LAURA says: "The wall started my name, Courier."
COURIER says: "Names survive in holding hands, Laura."
LAURA says: "Then give me the chalk."
COURIER says: "Hold it through every cull today."
Final beat: Laura's fist closed on the chalk, cull light rising.

## U2 — the wall's rule (image 1: PNL-PEAK-U2 · image 2: PNL-CHAR-WATCHER-hero)
World: a narrow market lane at morning — stalls and awnings; at
the depot corner the same chalk wall stands, its tallies in frame.
At the street's far end a warm cull light glows early and wrong.
Action (already moving at frame 0): LAURA, the chalk stub in her
raised fist, crosses to the wall and turns to the WATCHER, asking.
The WATCHER does not answer at once: he counts the tally marks
fingertip by fingertip along the wall, unhurried — his finger
stops at one empty slot in the tallies and holds there. Sound in
the first second: market murmur, a fingertip tapping chalk marks.
Beats: the stopped finger holds at the empty slot; Laura's eyes
follow it. The Watcher refuses her, level and plain. Laura presses
what his finger already said. He gives the gap its age. At the end
Laura's chalk touches the wall and the first stroke of a letter
begins as the cull light rises.
Dialogue (exact words, in order):
LAURA says: "What does it want first, Watcher?"
[the Watcher counts in silence, finger stopping at the empty slot]
WATCHER says: "It writes changes, Laura. You're an arrival."
LAURA says: "The tally stops one short."
WATCHER says: "That gap was there before you."
Final beat: chalk on brick, a letter's first stroke beginning,
light rising.

## U3 — the question underneath (image 1: PNL-PEAK-U3 · image 2: PNL-CHAR-COURIER-face)
World: the depot door at morning; on the wall a single large chalk
letter L stands where a stroke landed.
Action (already moving at frame 0): the COURIER holds the parcel
up at chest height between herself and the WATCHER and turns it
slowly in her hands; the Watcher's hands slide under it, taking
its weight as it turns, and a second gold stamp on the parcel's
underside comes up into the light. Sound in the first second:
brown paper creaking, string creaking as the parcel turns.
Beats: the Courier asks across the parcel. The Watcher answers
the question underneath while the parcel turns stamp-up. She
names the dodge. He delivers the ruling plainly. At the end the
Courier extends the parcel toward LAURA at frame edge; Laura's
hands rise toward it — the offer held, not yet taken.
Dialogue (exact words, in order):
COURIER says: "Who signs for its own carrier, Watcher?"
WATCHER says: "The name arrived. The hands change."
COURIER says: "I asked who signs, Watcher."
WATCHER says: "Any hand holding it, Courier."
Final beat: the parcel extended toward Laura, her hands rising,
unaccepted, as the light shifts.

## U4 — the culled mark (image 1: PNL-PEAK-U4 · image 2: PNL-CHAR-LAURA-face)
World: a street of closed metal shutters; the chalk wall runs end
to end, tallies dense, one slot in the tally empty — chalk dust
and a faint ghost outline inside the empty slot.
Action (already moving at frame 0): LAURA walks the wall mid-
stride, the parcel cradled in her left arm, chalking a fresh
running mark at the tally's end with her right hand; the WATCHER
walks beside her, calling the count aloud. Sound in the first
second: chalk scratching brick, boots on cobbles.
Beats: Laura walks the wall end to end counting the standing
marks under her breath and stops at the empty slot; she crouches,
dust on her fingertip. The Watcher defends the wall. She repairs
the count. Then, unspoken: the Watcher takes the chalk stub from
her offered hand, chalks a small circle around the empty slot
himself, and gives the stub back into her fist. The COURIER
watches from the depot step at frame edge.
Dialogue (exact words, in order):
WATCHER says: "Four hundred twenty-one, Laura."
LAURA says: "I count twenty on the wall."
WATCHER says: "The wall keeps every mark, Laura."
LAURA says: "The cull took that mark, Watcher."
Final beat: the small chalk circle around the empty slot under
rising light, the stub back in Laura's fist.

## U5 — which you (image 1: PNL-PEAK-U5 · image 2: PNL-CHAR-COURIER-hero)
World: a quieter street; the chalk wall behind them in shadow,
the depot door open on darkness.
Action (already moving at frame 0): the two women walk mid-
street, LAURA holding the parcel at rest cradled in both arms;
Laura asks her question and the COURIER stops mid-step, one boot
still raised, caught by it. Sound in the first second: two sets
of footsteps on cobbles, one stopping short.
Beats: the Courier counter-questions without looking away. Laura
answers from the parcel in her arms. The Courier looks at the
parcel, not at Laura, and yields. At the end Laura turns the
parcel in her hands, signing side up, the chalk rising in her
other hand.
Dialogue (exact words, in order):
LAURA says: "Which you is it addressed to?"
COURIER says: "Which Laura is asking, Laura?"
LAURA says: "The one holding your parcel."
COURIER says: "Then sign as its holder, Laura."
Final beat: the parcel turned signing-side up, chalk rising.

## U6 — the receipt (image 1: PNL-PEAK-U6 · image 2: PNL-CHAR-WATCHER-face)
World: the home depot street at dawn again — depot door, chalk
wall, brass lamp, morning light from frame left.
Action (already moving at frame 0): LAURA holds the parcel
signing-side up in both hands at waist height, her other hand
raising the chalk stub above its paper; the COURIER stands
empty-handed beside her, watching — the first time her hands
are empty. The WATCHER stands at the wall. Sound in the first
second: dawn street quiet, paper settling in her hands.
Beats: Laura asks which name. The Courier directs her plainly.
Then a held silence of about two and a half seconds: the chalk
an inch above the parcel's paper, the street holding, nobody
speaking. Then Laura writes on the parcel's paper in chalk —
the Courier's name — as she speaks, and passes the signed parcel
into the Courier's hands. The Watcher speaks from the wall.
Final beat: both women turn to the wall — the name LAURA stands
complete on the wall in full chalk letters, finished, though her
hand wrote one letter; the signed parcel rests in the Courier's
hands as the light rises.
Dialogue (exact words, in order):
LAURA says: "Which name, Courier?"
COURIER says: "The parcel's own, Laura."
[held silence — chalk an inch above the paper, nobody speaks]
LAURA says: "You carried it to yourself, Courier."
WATCHER says: "Read the wall, Laura."
