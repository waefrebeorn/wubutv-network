## Gate A — connector test (summaries joined, one sentence)

Laura claims the chalk to finish the name the wall began in her
own hand, **but** the Watcher refuses her — the wall writes
changes, not arrivals, and its tally will not close — **therefore**
the Courier forces her own debt onto the table and asks who signs
for a parcel addressed to its carrier, **but** the Watcher answers
the question underneath it instead: any hand holding it may sign,
**therefore** Laura's counting repairs the count itself — the
missing mark was culled, and the slot is circled — and her
question repairs the premise, **therefore** she signs as the
holder, the record stands, and the wall finishes her name behind
her back.
