## Cast LOOK blocks (invariant traits, restated in every vehicle)

- COURIER: adult woman, 34; sharp black bob haircut with a straight
  fringe; calm deadpan face; long cobalt-blue courier coat to the knee;
  black cross-body strap; canvas satchel at her hip; dark trousers;
  scuffed boots.
- WATCHER: adult man, 68; short grey stubble beard; deep-set eyes;
  ink-black cardigan over a bone-white shirt; sleeves pushed up; white
  chalk dust on his fingers and forearms; holds a stub of white chalk.
- LAURA: adult woman, early 30s; dark brown hair in a low ponytail;
  sharp, curious expression; forest-green work shirt with two chest
  pockets; dark trousers. (No headphones in this show — that prop is
  her SHOW-1 carry and stays home.)
- PARCEL: one mid-size parcel wrapped in brown paper, cross-tied with
  string, one gold CADMIUM stamp on top, no readable words anywhere
  on it.
- WORLD: cobbled depot street — wide wooden depot door, brick wall
  beside it covered in white chalk tally marks, one brass lamp,
  morning light from frame left. Palette: INK / COBALT / BONE +
  CADMIUM accent. The chalk wall stands in every world the cull makes.

## Boundary classification (J1/J2 — written before volley 1)

This show's fiction swaps the street at every cull; the SCRIPT opens
every unit in a new world. All five boundaries are therefore declared
**BREAK** (a world change the story itself performs), each designed
per J14 with two named matches shared across the cut: (1) THE CARRY —
the held object (chalk / parcel) in the same custody state the script
specifies on both sides, present within 1.5s of the incoming head;
(2) THE CULL CLOCK snap — the invariant ring snaps full exactly on
the joint frame (Method C §3.2). Custody transfers the script places
AT a joint are staged as straddles (J9): the U3→U4 parcel offer is
held unaccepted across the flash at U3's cliff and is in Laura's
hands at U4's head. Every BREAK unit re-anchors (J7): image 1 = its
PEAK, image 2 = a CHAR sheet per the rotation table. No resume, no
chain calls — resume would continue the outgoing street, which is
the one thing this show's grammar forbids. Joint Gate: BREAK class
(no MAE bar; six-item eye checklist with item 5 read as "declared
matches present").

| boundary | class | carry across the cut | joint-frame action |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1→U2 | BREAK | chalk, Laura's fist (won at U1's cliff) | cull light rises on her closed fist |
| U2→U3 | BREAK | chalk, Laura's fist; her stroke has landed (letter L stands) | chalk touches wall, first stroke begins as light rises |
| U3→U4 | BREAK | parcel: offered (U3 cliff) → Laura's hands (U4 head) — STRADDLE | parcel extended, Laura's hands rising, unaccepted across the flash |
| U4→U5 | BREAK | parcel, Laura's arms | circled absence under rising light |
| U5→U6 | BREAK | parcel turned signing-side up, Laura's hands; chalk rising | the turn-over completes in frame |
