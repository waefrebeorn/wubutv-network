## Units

### U1 — the unfinished name (A · P1 Claim → Challenge → Concession-With-A-Price · FULL-TALK · 23 words)
Frame sentence: *the chalk wall at dawn, and one name on it is
unfinished.*
Opens: the wall — tallies, the circled drawing of a parcel,
and the fresh mark of the pilot's U5 become two letters of a
name, dust still settling. The Courier stands at it, parcel
under her arm, the chalk stub in her fist (the pilot's cliff
state). Laura arrives for her first shift and stops at the
stroke; she traces it and knows her own hand.
Motion motif: a fist closing on chalk.
- LAURA → COURIER | CLAIM — "The wall started my name,
  Courier." (6)
  [PICTURE TURN: the Courier looks from the unfinished stroke
  to the chalk in her own fist]
- COURIER → LAURA | CHALLENGE — "Names survive in holding
  hands, Laura." (6)
- LAURA → COURIER | PRESS — "Then give me the chalk." (5)
- COURIER → LAURA | CONCEDE-WITH-PRICE — TURN LINE — "Hold
  it through every cull today." (6)
UNSAID: LAURA — "I have held this chalk before, in a version
I don't remember." · COURIER — "I am afraid the next me is
already standing here."
DELTA: the mark is identified — LAURA, begun, in her own hand
(F-008 fired in part; F-013). The chalk changes custody under
a spoken price (F-014): an OWES is born — Laura owes the
carry, held through every cull today.
CLIFF (device): the cull light rises; Laura's fist is closed
on the chalk.

### U2 — the wall's rule (A · P6 Dispreferred Second, Played · FULL-TALK · 24 words)
Frame sentence: *a market street that wasn't there at dawn,
and the wall counting itself at its corner.*
Opens: new world — a narrow market lane; the wall constant at
the depot corner. Chalk in Laura's fist, raised toward the
wall. At the street's far end, cull light warms early and
wrong (F-011 reminded as picture only).
Motion motif: a fingertip counting marks.
- LAURA → WATCHER | ASK — "What does it want first,
  Watcher?" (6)
  [DELAY BEAT — P6, scripted action 0.8s: the Watcher counts
  the tallies fingertip by fingertip; his finger stops at
  the empty slot and holds. No words.]
- WATCHER → LAURA | REFUSE, with account — TURN LINE — "It
  writes changes, Laura. You're an arrival." (7)
- LAURA → WATCHER | PRESS — "The tally stops one short." (5)
- WATCHER → LAURA | ACCOUNT — "That gap was there before
  you." (6)
UNSAID: LAURA — "I think the wall started my name because I
asked it to, last time." · WATCHER — "I have counted past
that gap for years and called it weather."
DELTA: the wall's rule is spoken on screen — it writes
changes, not arrivals (F-015). Laura's want (her name
finished on request) is refused and marked CONTESTED. The
gap is now shared knowledge; its cause is not yet known.
CLIFF (device): Laura's chalk touches the wall; the first
stroke of a letter begins as the light rises.

### U3 — the question underneath (B · P2 The Misdirected Answer · FULL-TALK · 23 words)
