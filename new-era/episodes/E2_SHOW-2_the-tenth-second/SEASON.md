# SHOW-2 THE TENTH SECOND — SEASON PROJECTION (Season 1)

Written at the E2 lock, 2026-11-15, backward from the final
image (Writers Room §1.3 / §7 Conflict 7). Owned by the
Showrunner; updated at every lock. Firm for the next 2–3
episodes, loose beyond — the bail-out position is deliberate.

**The season's final image:** the wall covered in finished
names, every one of them completed in a hand nobody saw
writing; the street empty at dawn; and on the depot step one
parcel, unsigned, addressed to the wall itself.

**Where the principals end the season:**
- THE COURIER starts trusting only her own hands and ends
  having let the depot's hands keep her record — her last
  act of the season is to carry a parcel she is not allowed
  to sign.
- THE WATCHER starts as the wall's sole keeper and ends
  sharing it: his count corrected, his circle drawn, his
  verdicts coming back to him re-priced.
- LAURA starts unkept — a name the wall began and abandoned
  — and ends as the wall's chosen hand, or its next author;
  the season does not say which until she does.

**Midseason turn (E4):** the culls stop arriving on the
count. Time itself desyncs from the tally, and the depot
must choose which to trust — the called number or the wall.
