## 7. Critic (Pass 6 — fresh context; cold read first, then located objections)

**Cold read (reported stumbles only):** the reader stumbled
at U3's turn line on first pass ("which name arrived?") and
recovered on the picture turn; read U5's doubled name
("Which Laura is asking, Laura?") as a deliberate echo, not
an error; followed every other beat without a stumble;
told the three speakers apart throughout.

**Round 1 — located objections:**
1. **U4, turn line (BLOCKING — word choice / legibility).**
   "One mark was culled from it." A cold ear cannot name
   the agent or the object: "culled from it" — from the
   wall? the tally? the count? The unit's whole event is
   the discovery that the CULL takes things; the line must
   say so. Gate invoked: legibility (the repair must be
   statable by a stranger). **Answer (Pass 3c):** the line
   is replaced by the draft's own logged alternative —
   "The cull took that mark, Watcher." (6 words; U4
   recount unchanged at 22). The objection is sustained
   and closed.
2. **U3, turn line (advisory — legibility).** "The name
   arrived. The hands change." risks floating free of the
   parcel for a cold viewer. **Answer:** no text change.
   T1 names the situation in full ("Who signs for its own
   carrier, Watcher?") and the picture turn puts the
   parcel — stamp up — between the speakers as the line
   lands; "the name" has exactly one referent in frame.
   The stumble is real but self-healing inside the unit;
   recorded so production holds the stamp-up turn BEFORE
   the line, not after it.
3. **U1, Courier unsaid (advisory — legibility test).**
   The Critic could not re-derive the Courier's recorded
   unsaid ("I am afraid the next me is already standing
   here") from U1's dialogue alone; from her two lines the
   cold derivation was "she does not trust the girl to
   hold it." **Answer:** no text change. The unsaid's
   support is serial, and deliberately so: the episode's
   anchor state (the pilot's cliff — her refusal, spoken
   the night before) is cited in the script header, and
   her price line is the reluctance the cold read DID
   derive, one layer up. The mismatch is recorded as the
   known cost of a second episode's opening pair, which
   doctrine restricts to self-evident pressures — the
   pressure (a demand and its price) IS self-evident; the
   fear under it is for returners. Noted, not fixed.
4. **U2, turn line (advisory — voice).** "You're an
   arrival" uses the depot's dialect (people as deliveries)
   cold. **Answer:** kept. The dialect is the show's voice
   (the Watcher's treatment register: addresses, deadlines,
   custody), and U5 pays it off — Laura answers from
   custody, in the same dialect, as a native. Recorded.

**Round 2:** the Critic re-read U4 with the Pass 3c line:
the repair now states its agent and object cold; recount
verified (22). No further objections. **PASS.** Rounds
used: 2 of 2 allowed — the second was a verification round,
