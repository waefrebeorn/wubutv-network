## UNIT 1 — Storyline A · Pattern P6 (the dispreferred second, played)

*Why P6:* the opening pair must be a self-evident demand/refusal with
no P3 callback and no P8 repair — P6 IS the refusal pattern: UNC's
refusal arrives delayed and accounted for, and the delay is what the
audience reads. The premise (PL-5) is spent as present action, never
as referenced history.

**Beat sheet (4 beats):**
1. UNC's mouth shapes a word in the silent room; LAURA — headphones
   around her neck, watching him, not the screen — stops still.
   (WITNESS, Δinfo — LAURA gains private knowledge: he moved first,
   before any screen.)
2. The single monitor's mouth stays closed a full beat longer, then
   opens on the word. (FOLLOW — the machine, Δinfo — the order
   UNC → machine is visible to the audience and to LAURA.)
3. LAURA demands UNC admit his mouth moved. (DEMAND, Δpower — UNC
   must now answer on her terms; the question is about him.)
4. **Dispreferred delay, 0.7s (P6, marked — NOT the silence beat):**
   UNC looks at the dark monitor, hand still, before he refuses with
   an account. (REFUSE, Δpower — he holds his status, but the refusal
   concedes the episode's subject is now his mouth.)

**Interruption points:** none (no P4 in this unit).
**Plant marks:** PL-5 FIRED — first firing, as proof: the mirroring
escalates simultaneous → anticipatory on screen.
**Fill class confirmed:** STANDARD · 9–12 spoken words.
**A3 anti-front-load:** late beat parked at ≥6.5s on paper = UNC's
delayed refusal (turn 2). Speech-independent tail event = the
monitor's mouth opening late, after his — the order reads with the
sound off even if all speech lands early.
**Cliff:** sung onset — the monitor's mouth opens on the next word.

## UNIT 2 — Storyline B · Pattern P5 (the counter-question that concedes)

*Why P5:* the watched take must end with the premise in SHARED ground
or U3 has nothing to test. A counter-question lets UNC concede he
moved first while attacking what it means — the fight relocates
instead of stalling on denial.

**Beat sheet (4 beats):**
1. LAURA turns her chair from the monitor to face UNC and states the
   test: this take, she watches him. (FRAME, Δpower — she sets the
   conditions of proof; procedure itself changes hands.)
2. The take runs; LAURA's eyes stay on UNC's mouth while the monitor
   plays unwatched behind her. (WATCH, Δinfo — evidence collected
   under stated conditions, not glimpsed.)
3. UNC's mouth moves; a beat later the monitor's mouth follows with
   the same word. (FOLLOW — the machine, Δinfo — order proven to BOTH
   of them this time; UNC sees her see it.)
4. UNC counter-questions — accepting he was first, attacking the
   conclusion she draws from it. (COUNTER-QUESTION, Δpower — premise
   enters both KNOWS lists as shared ground; the dispute moves to
   meaning.)

**Interruption points:** none.
**Plant marks:** PL-5 FIRED — proof, second firing: anticipation
witnessed under test conditions.
**Fill class confirmed:** STANDARD · 9–12 spoken words.
**A3 anti-front-load:** late beat parked at ≥6.5s = UNC's
counter-question (turn 2). Speech-independent tail event = the
monitor's mouth following UNC's, a visible beat late — the lag itself
is the unit's evidence and survives total speech displacement.
**Cliff:** sung onset — monitor mouth opens, still following.

## UNIT 3 — Storyline A · Pattern P4 (the interrupted turn)

*Why P4:* the proof ladder needs its third rung to COST UNC something
in the room, not just in logic. His innocent explanation (copying) is
interrupted by the phenomenon itself happening mid-sentence — the
interruption is the evidence, and it takes the floor from him.

**Beat sheet (5 beats):**
1. UNC offers the copying account: a man echoes what he watches.
   (ACCOUNT, Δinfo — the innocent explanation is placed on record,
   where it can be killed.)
2. LAURA sets the counter-test: then turn around. (DARE, Δpower —
   submit to the test or refuse it in front of the running take;
   either answer costs him.)
3. UNC turns his back to the monitor and holds the position.
   (SUBMIT, Δpower — he accepts her conditions; the manager takes
   direction from the new hire.)
4. Back turned, UNC's mouth moves early again; the monitor follows.
   (ANTICIPATE — UNC, unwitting; FOLLOW — the machine. Δinfo —
   copying is now impossible; anticipation is the only account
   standing.)
5. UNC begins the explanation again; LAURA cuts him off as it
   happens. (INTERRUPT, Δpower — floor taken and kept; UNC's want
   'explain it away' is marked CONTESTED.)

**Interruption points (P4, marked):** LAURA's interruption lands ONLY
on the sacrificial edge words at the tail of UNC's renewed account
(its trailing filler/account tail). ESSENTIAL SPANS, protected: her
turn-1 test terms and the beat-4 event itself — no interruption may
touch the words that state the test or the moment his mouth moves.
**Plant marks:** PL-5 FIRED — proof completed: copying excluded.
After this unit the plant's proof phase is spent; only its causation
firing (U6) remains.
**Fill class confirmed:** FULL · 12–16 spoken words.
**A3 anti-front-load:** late beat parked at ≥6.5s = the interruption
and its held aftermath (UNC stopped mid-explanation, back still
turned). Speech-independent tail event = UNC remaining back-turned,
facing away, while the monitor's mouth opens behind him — the
geometry alone states the proof.
**Cliff:** sung onset — the monitor opens on the next word behind
UNC's back.

## UNIT 4 — Storyline B · Pattern P1 (claim → challenge → concession-with-a-price)

*Why P1:* this is the board's price unit — LAURA's claim (his rule
makes his mouth the record) is challenged at its joint and the
concession is demanded WITH its price named (the refile). P1 is the
only pattern whose delta class is an OWES entry, and the OWES birth
is this unit's forecast delta.

**Beat sheet (5 beats — fragment chain shape, FULL-TALK):**
1. LAURA states UNC's own rule back to him as a claim about him.
   (CLAIM, Δinfo — 'the mouth is the record' re-enters the room aimed
   at its author; callback of his PROCEDURE, not of a ledger fact —
   the rule is his standing policy, quoted, not re-priced history.)
2. UNC challenges the claim at its joint: the rule names the singer,
   not the crew. (CHALLENGE, Δpower — he defends the filing category,
   not the fact; the fact is no longer deniable after U3.)
3. LAURA names the price: every 'singer' take refiled under UNC.
   (PRICE, Δpower — the concession's cost is made concrete and
   clerical: his name, on every take.)
4. Overlapping fragment volley — categories and names thrown back and
   forth, each fragment cutting the last one's tail. (VOLLEY,
   Δpower — the see-saw flips per fragment; neither holds the floor
   for a full sentence.)
5. UNC refuses the refile outright, flat, ending the volley.
   (REFUSE, Δinfo — refusal on record: the OWES entry is born unpaid
   and UNC's want 'the record holds' is marked CONTESTED.)

**Interruption points:** no P4 pattern in this unit; the beat-4
fragment overlaps are chain joins, and production is marked to land
them ONLY on sacrificial edge words (fragment tails), never on the
words that name the price (beat 3) or the refusal (beat 5) —
both ESSENTIAL SPANS, protected.
**Plant marks:** none fired, none planted. PL-5's proof is complete;
this unit spends the board's B storyline and banks the OWES delta.
**Fill class confirmed:** FULL-TALK · 22–25 spoken words (episode's
only full-talk unit; research budget per board §6 declared exception).
**A3 anti-front-load:** late beat parked at ≥6.5s = the flat refusal
(beat 5), written as a separate quoted block after the volley.
Speech-independent tail event = UNC closes the take-file folder
UNSIGNED and sets it back on the 'singer' side of the desk — the
unpaid debt reads in props alone.
**Cliff:** sung onset — the monitor's mouth opens on the next word,
indifferent to the verdict.

## UNIT 5 — Storyline C · Pattern P2 (the misdirected answer)

*Why P2:* the absence test's revelation should come from UNC, not
from narration. LAURA asks about the take; UNC answers the unasked
question underneath it — whether the machine is waiting for HIM —
and the dodge records him as KNOWING it. Knowledge asymmetry is the
delta this unit banks.

**Beat sheet (3 beats — SPARSE by design):**
1. UNC walks out; LAURA, alone with the monitor, calls the take to
   run without him. (DISMISS, Δpower — she removes the suspect from
   the circuit and takes the room.)
2. The take does not run. The monitor's mouth stays closed past the
   beat where the song's grid says a word is due; the status light
   never turns. (WAIT — the machine, Δinfo — dependency is SHOWN:
   the machine's silence is its testimony.)
3. From the doorway, UNC answers the question she did not ask — not
   'will it run?' but the one about him. (ANSWER-THE-UNASKED,
   Δinfo — UNC is recorded as knowing the machine waits for him;
   LAURA's KNOWS gains his knowledge, not just the fact.)

**Interruption points:** none. **Dispreferred delay:** none scripted
— beat 2's held stall is the machine's action beat (A2 fill), NOT a
P6 delay and NOT the silence beat; the episode's only scripted
silence beat is U6's.
**Plant marks:** P-E2-1 PLANTED ('the machine will not sing while
UNC is out of the room') — planted by beat 2, shown, not spoken.
**Fill class confirmed:** SPARSE · 5–8 spoken words. Named non-speech
fill (A2): the stalled take itself — one held closed mouth, a status
light that never turns, room tone where the beat grid says a word is
due; LAURA alone in frame with the idle console.
**A3 anti-front-load:** late beat parked at ≥6.5s = UNC's doorway
answer (turn 2) — the unit's only late speech, written to still land
if turn 1 front-loads. Speech-independent tail event = the due word
passing unsung: the mouth stays closed through the unit's tail.
**Cliff (board-approved variant):** the handoff that fails to arrive
— the mouth stays closed at 9–10s and the deferred sung onset lands
as U6's opening frame, triggered by UNC's return. The stall IS this
unit's cliff; no dialogue closes it.

## UNIT 6 — Storyline C · Pattern P3 (the callback that re-prices)

*Why P3:* UNC's deliberate wrong word re-prices the episode's own
spent material — his pilot rule 'the mouth is the record' returns
with its value inverted: his mouth now literally MAKES the record.
The same words, a new price — the anti-recursion move, spent on the
cliff's own thread. (P3 is barred from opening pairs only; U6 is the
cliff unit.)

**Beat sheet (5 beats):**
1. UNC re-enters and takes his place facing the monitor; the stalled
   take is still waiting on the screen. (RETURN, Δpower — he steps
   back into the circuit voluntarily; the room's geometry restores
   itself around him.)
2. LAURA sets the dare's terms: one word, his choice, not the
   song's. (DARE, Δpower — she designs the test that can convict or
   clear him; he accepts by staying.)
3. **SILENCE BEAT — 2.5s, scripted, placed IMMEDIATELY BEFORE the
   turn line.** UNC stands still, mouth closed, choosing; the
   monitor waits on him; LAURA does not move. This is the episode's
   only silence beat — distinct from U1's 0.7s P6 delay, which was a
   refusal's hesitation, not a held choice.
4. UNC mouths the wrong word — deliberately, on his own timing.
   (FEED, Δinfo — authorship tested: a word with no source but him.
   This mouthing is the unit's turn line, played in action against
   the beat grid.)
5. The monitor sings his wrong word, full voice, on the grid.
   (CONCEDE — the machine, Δinfo — causation proven: PL-5's final
   firing; the machine follows the crew's mouth, not the song.)

**Interruption points:** none.
**Plant marks:** PL-5 FIRED — final firing, as causation (plant
closed: proof U1–U3, causation U6). P-E2-2 PLANTED at beat 5 ('UNC
can feed the machine a word not in the song, and it will sing it').
P-E2-3 PLANTED at the cliff ('the monitor's mouth can open on a word
UNC has not mouthed'; payoff due E3).
**Fill class confirmed:** SILENCE-BEAT unit · 15–18 spoken words
(budget already reduced for the 2.5s beat; pair closes by 8.5s).
**A3 anti-front-load:** late beat parked at ≥6.5s = the monitor
singing the wrong word (beat 5) — a device event, not dialogue, so it
cannot front-load. Speech-independent tail event = the cliff itself:
the monitor's mouth opening on a word nobody in the room has mouthed.
**Cliff:** sung onset — the monitor opens on an unmouthed word
(P-E2-3); UNC's mouth stays closed. INVERTED vs the pilot's
simultaneous-mirroring bridge cliff.

## OUTLINE (one paragraph per unit)

**U1.** The cutting room, monitor glow, one screen dark. Frame 0: UNC
in profile, mid-room, his mouth shaping a word in total quiet while
the monitor behind him sits black and closed-mouthed; LAURA, headphones
around her neck, catches it and freezes. The camera watches the room,
not the screen: the monitor's mouth opens a beat late, following him.
LAURA crosses into his eyeline and makes her demand; UNC's refusal
comes after a visible hesitation spent looking at the dark screen —
as if checking an alibi that isn't there. The pair closes by 8.5s and
the monitor's mouth opens on the next sung word, still a breath
behind him.

**U2.** Same room, geometry reversed: LAURA turns her chair away from
the monitor to face UNC, back to the screen, and announces the test
with her body before any words — this take she watches the man. UNC
works standing, exposed, with nowhere to put his face; the console
sits idle between them, untouched. The take runs unwatched on the
screen behind her. His mouth moves; the camera holds on LAURA's face
as she hears the monitor follow a beat later — proof arriving as a
sound behind her. UNC's counter-question concedes the order and
fights the meaning, and the monitor's mouth opens on the next word,
still following his.

**U3.** UNC, needled, offers the copying defence standing — then, on
LAURA's dare, turns his whole body away from the monitor, back to the
screen, arms folded, a man refusing an eye test. The camera watches
his back and LAURA's face beyond him: she can see both mouths, he can
see neither screen. His mouth moves early anyway; the monitor follows
behind his back. He starts the explanation again to the wall and
LAURA cuts him off mid-tail, taking the floor while he is still
facing away — the unit ends on that geometry: her voice, his back,
the screen's mouth opening on the next sung word behind him.

**U4.** The room tightens: LAURA comes around the desk with the take
files — the physical 'singer' takes — and puts UNC's own rule on the
desk between them like a document. The exchange accelerates into the
episode's only overlapping argument, fragments thrown across the
files, neither speaker finishing a sentence while the price is
named: every take refiled under UNC. UNC's hands stay off the files
until the end, when he shuts the folder unsigned and sets it back on
the singer's side of the desk — refusal as a clerical act. The pair
closes by 8.5s; the monitor, indifferent, opens on the next sung
word.

**U5.** UNC walks out — the door, the corridor light, the room down
to one person. LAURA alone with the single monitor calls the take to
run and settles to watch it prove her wrong. It doesn't run. The
camera holds the wide room: idle console, empty chair, LAURA small
beside the big screen, and on the screen a mouth that stays closed
while the song's grid ticks past the word that was due. The status
light never turns. From the doorway, half in corridor light, UNC
answers the question she never asked, and the unit tails out on the
still-closed mouth — the handoff that doesn't arrive.

**U6.** UNC comes back in and takes his place facing the monitor;
the stalled take is still waiting on screen, mouth closed, exactly
where he left it. LAURA sets the dare standing off to the side, out
of the machine's sightline, a referee. Then the silence beat: 2.5
seconds of held room — UNC still, choosing, the monitor waiting on
him, nobody speaking — before he mouths one wrong word, his word,
deliberately. The monitor takes it and sings it full-voice on the
grid. Both of them hear whose song it is now. And at the cliff the
monitor's mouth opens again — on a word UNC has not mouthed, while
his own mouth stays shut.
