## U1 — HOOK (frame 0: HUMAN-FIRST MOUTHING, MONITORS STILL)
Frame sentence: *UNC in profile, mid-room, his mouth shaping a word in
total quiet, while the monitor behind him sits black and
closed-mouthed; LAURA, headphones around her neck, catches it and
freezes.*
The monitor's mouth opens a full beat late, following him.
LAURA → UNC (demand): "Say it, UNC. You moved first." (6w)
[0.7s — UNC looks at the dark monitor, hand still]
UNC → LAURA (refusal, with account): "I follow the screen, Laura." (5w)
CLIFF: the monitor's mouth opens on the next sung word, still a
breath behind him.
Spoken: 11 words · Pattern P6 · PL-5 fired (proof, first firing).

## U2 — THE WATCHED TAKE
Frame sentence: *LAURA turned away from the monitor, back to the
screen, facing UNC; the take runs unwatched behind her.*
His mouth moves; the monitor's mouth follows a beat later — she hears
the proof arrive as a sound behind her.
LAURA → UNC (frame): "This take I watch you, UNC." (6w)
UNC → LAURA (counter-question): "First makes me the singer?" (5w)
CLIFF: the monitor's mouth opens on the next word, still following.
Spoken: 11 words · Pattern P5 · PL-5 fired (proof, second firing —
order now shared ground).

## U3 — THE BACK-TURNED TAKE
Frame sentence: *UNC's back to the monitor, arms folded; LAURA beyond
him, able to see both mouths; he can see neither screen.*
UNC → LAURA (account): "A man echoes what he watches." (6w)
LAURA → UNC (dare): "Then turn around." (3w)
[He turns. Back turned, his mouth moves early anyway; the monitor
follows behind his back.]
UNC → LAURA (account resumed, interrupted on its tail): "I only ever—" (3w)
LAURA → UNC (interruption/verdict): "Your back is turned." (4w)
CLIFF: the monitor opens on the next word behind UNC's back.
Spoken: 16 words · Pattern P4 · PL-5 fired (proof completed — copying
excluded).

## U4 — THE REFILE (the episode's only FULL-TALK unit)
Frame sentence: *LAURA around the desk with the physical 'singer'
take files; the rule laid on the desk between them like a document.*
LAURA → UNC (claim): "Your mouth is the record." (5w)
UNC → LAURA (challenge): "The rule names the singer." (5w)
LAURA → UNC (price): "Refile every singer take under you." (6w)
UNC → LAURA (volley fragment): "Singer takes." (2w)
LAURA → UNC (volley fragment): "Your takes." (2w)
UNC → LAURA (refusal): "I won't refile them." (4w)
Tail event: UNC closes the take-file folder UNSIGNED and sets it
back on the 'singer' side of the desk.
CLIFF: the monitor opens on the next word, indifferent to the verdict.
Spoken: 24 words · Pattern P1 · OWES born (UNC owes the refile).

## U5 — THE EMPTY ROOM (SPARSE)
Frame sentence: *the wide room — idle console, empty chair, LAURA
small beside the big screen; on the screen, a mouth that stays
closed.*
LAURA → UNC, as he exits (dismiss): "Out. It sings alone." (4w)
[The take does not run. The due word passes unsung; the status light
never turns.]
UNC → LAURA, from the doorway (misdirected answer): "It waits for me." (4w)
CLIFF: the handoff that fails to arrive — the mouth stays closed at
9–10s; the deferred sung onset lands as U6's opening frame.
Spoken: 8 words · Pattern P2 · P-E2-1 planted (shown, not spoken).

## U6 — THE WRONG WORD + BRIDGE CLIFF
Frame sentence: *UNC back in place facing the monitor; the stalled
take still waiting on screen, mouth closed, exactly where he left it;
LAURA off to the side, a referee.*
LAURA → UNC (dare): "One word, UNC. Yours. Not the song's." (7w)
UNC → LAURA (counter-question): "And when it sings it?" (5w)
LAURA → UNC (re-price fragment): "Your word. Your record." (4w)
[Silence beat — 2.5s: UNC still, mouth closed, choosing; the monitor
waits on him; LAURA does not move.]
UNC mouths one wrong word — deliberately, his own, on his timing.
The monitor sings his wrong word, full voice, on the grid.
BRIDGE CLIFF (final frame = next episode's first frame): the
monitor's mouth opens on a word UNC has not mouthed — while his own
mouth stays shut.
Spoken: 16 words · Pattern P3 · PL-5 fired (final firing, causation —
plant closed) · P-E2-2 planted · P-E2-3 planted at the cliff
(payoff due E3).
