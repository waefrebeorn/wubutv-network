### Round 1 — located objections

**Objection 1 — U3, TURN 1 (voice / cover-the-names). SUSTAINED.**
As drafted: UNC — "Men echo what they watch." (5 words). The generic
plural subject is any man's aphorism; UNC's established register is
the singular, certain case ("A man…" / "The mouth is the record" —
pilot lines and treatment chain). Cited gate: Pass 5 voice check
(cover-the-names, dialogue report §1.6) + pitch-bible voice notes
(UNC: short certain sentences). **Disposition:** returned to Pass 3;
Dialogue Writer rewrote the line (below); Pass 4 recount re-run.

**Objection 2 — U4 TURN 1 + U6 FRAGMENT (repetition of the re-price).
ANSWERED, not sustained.**
The objection: "the record" is re-priced twice (U4's claim "Your
mouth is the record"; U6's fragment "Your word. Your record.") —
is the second a repeat wearing a new shape? Room's answer, on the
record: the two uses are claim and verdict, not two claims. U4 aims
the rule at UNC as an accusation he can still refuse (and does);
U6's fragment is spoken BEFORE the feed, as the dare's price-tag,
and the unit then enacts it — his mouth literally makes the record.
P3 is assigned to U6 alone; U4 runs P1. Callback budget (doctrine
S6): one per unit, and each unit lands without the other. The
escalation is the episode's spine (rule quoted → rule enforced →
rule enacted). **No rewrite.** Logged here so the choice is the
room's, not an accident.

**Objection 3 — U1, TURN 2 (legibility of the account). ANSWERED,
not sustained.**
The objection: cold, "I follow the screen" is spoken while every
monitor is dark — does the account read? Room's answer: the darkness
is the tell. P6's account must be visibly insufficient — that is how
a cold viewer knows it is an account and not the truth — and U3
restates the same account in full ("A man echoes what he watches")
in the unit built to kill it. The legibility test was also run on
this unit: the Critic's own statement of UNC's unsaid sentence from
the dialogue alone matched Pass 3's recorded unsaid ("If I say my
mouth moved, the room changes" ≈ Critic's "he can't admit it without
becoming the problem"). **No rewrite.**

**Legibility test (all units):** the Critic stated each speaker's
unsaid sentence from the dialogue alone; all twelve matched Pass 3's
recorded unsaid sentences in substance. PASS.
