# SEASON PROJECTION — SHOW-1 'THE MACHINE' · SEASON 1

**Written:** 2026-11-15, at EPISODE 2 lock (the show had no season
projection; written now per Writers Room §1.3). **Owner:** Showrunner.
**Frame:** 8 episodes, projected backward from the final image.
Firm for E3–E4 (the bail-out position); loose beyond E4 — the
looseness is deliberate and is re-examined at every lock and at the
E5 re-pitch (guard G5). **Arc served:** ARC-1 The One Viewer —
the machine knows/uses the crew (thread T-ARC-1-1).

## The season in one movement

The crew starts by managing the machine's wrongness and ends by
being managed by it. The proof ladder of E2 (mirroring →
anticipation → authorship) is only the first direction of travel:
once the monitor opens unled (E2 cliff), the season's question
inverts — not "is UNC inside the song?" but "does the machine still
need anyone in the room at all?" Every episode moves the 'who leads'
counter one notch; nobody is allowed to win it twice in a row.

## Final image (the destination that disciplines choices)

The cutting room, a take running. On the monitor, the singer's mouth
opens — and for the first time all season, no mouth in the room
moves with it, before it, or after it. The machine sings alone,
correctly, to an empty chair. Whether that is the machine cured or
the crew culled is the shot the season refuses to answer. (Built
from E2's two poles: U5's stall — it would not sing without him —
inverted by F-020: it no longer waits.)

## Character start → end

- **LAURA:** start — new hire, notices first, credibility on loan.
  End — the room's prosecutor-turned-witness: she has proven
  everything and can stop nothing; her knowledge is the season's
  record and its burden.
- **UNC:** start — manager, keeper of the record, UP. End — the
  record's subject: his name on the takes he refused to refile,
  filed by someone else's hand (or the machine's); the procedure he
  defended now processes him.
- **COLONEL:** start — off-screen anchor of the official version.
  End — the official version's last believer, forced to announce
  the one thing it cannot cover (his entrance spends PL-4).
- **THE SINGER:** start — a mouth on a monitor. End — still
  unresolved (PL-2 pays off late by design): the season's last
  unanswered face.

## Midseason turn (E4)

P-E2-2 pays off: UNC's fed word stops being an event and becomes
vocabulary — the song now permanently contains a word only UNC
wrote, and the machine begins feeding words back. Authorship stops
being a test the crew runs and becomes a correspondence neither
side admits to starting.
