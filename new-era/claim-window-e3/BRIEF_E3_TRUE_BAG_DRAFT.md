## §1 The story (from SCRIPT_E3.md v2, verbatim in substance)

The Peach's train is at the platform NOW, doors open, and its ticket
is inside its lost bag. The Peach lies about the bag — big, black,
very strong — because a small bag is not its act. June works only
from true descriptions, so the lie pulls a wrong case full of boots,
and the train beeps. The Peach protects the act with a bigger lie —
black with gold — and gets a bag of sandwiches, and the conductor
calls all-aboard. Now the Peach sees its real bag on the shelf
behind June: small, red, a white string. Pointing is not a
description, so the only way to the bag is to say the true words out
loud and lose the act. It does. June hands the bag over, the ticket
is inside, and the Peach catches its train holding the small red bag
it said was big and black.

Chain: every joint reads therefore/but (record in SCRIPT_E3.md —
Therefore / Why / Shuffle / Fight-Back / Choice-Under-Cost /
Coincidence Direction / No-Assertion / Plant-and-Payoff, all PASS,
run by the parent on the beat list BEFORE dialogue was written).

  the form as she puts the big black case back). NOT "True is fast

- Standalone beat: U4 — the pointing that cannot claim ("Pointing
