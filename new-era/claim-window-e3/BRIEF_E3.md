## §0 Why this episode is built differently (msg 739 law)

User verdict on Ep2: "this is a good example but there was no story
actually — make the stories more direct and more ESL language friendly —
right now you are too BBC mysterious." Standing law from that verdict:
(1) a REAL STORY — a plain plot a cold viewer can follow: clear want,
clear problem, clear ending; (2) ESL-FRIENDLY language — short, simple,
direct English, no cryptic or mystery tone; (3) SETTING SHIFTS and STORY
ELEMENTS as first-class craft — the place changes, and each change of
place changes what can happen in the story. Pilot and Ep2 both played
entirely at the window counter; this episode moves three times, and
every move is a story beat, not decoration.

## §1 The story (five plain sentences — if it can't be told like this, it isn't ready)

1. The Peach lost a small red bag at the station.
2. The Peach's train ticket is inside the bag, and the train leaves at six.
3. The Peach says the bag is big and black, so June finds the wrong bag.
4. The clock says six is near, so the Peach tells the truth: the bag is
   small and red, with a white string.
5. June runs to the platform with the right bag, and the Peach gets on
   the train.

PETTY WANT: the Peach wants its bag back before six o'clock.
PROBLEM: its own lie makes the bag impossible to find.
STATUS CHANGE (irreversible): the Peach leaves the station on the train;
June leaves her window — she never leaves the window — and runs.
FACE AT RISK: the Peach's (the lie costs it the train) and June's (her
rule vs. the clock: a true description at 5:59 still counts, and she
proves it by running, not by bending the rule early).

## §2 Setting-shift map (each shift changes the story, not just the picture)

- U1–U2 · THE WINDOW (the old set, pilot blocking): the lie is told and
  believed-shaped — June works her normal system. The system fails here:
  the wrong bag comes up.
- U3–U4 · THE BACK SHELVES (June STANDS and goes back — the bible's one
  standing move): two red bags on the shelf. The Peach must choose with
  its eyes, not its mouth. It chooses the big one. Wrong — it is full of
  lost gloves. The lie is now a picture, not just words.
- U5–U6 · THE PLATFORM (new set, first time in the series): the train is
  there, the doors are open. No counter between them — June hands the
  bag over hand-to-hand, the first direct hand-off in the show. The
  story can only end here; the window could not finish it.
