## The story in plain sentences

The Peach's train is at the platform NOW, doors open, and its ticket
is inside its lost bag. The Peach lies about the bag — big, black,
very strong — because a small bag is not its act. June works only from
true descriptions, so the lie pulls a wrong case full of boots, and
the train beeps. The Peach protects the act with a bigger lie — black
with gold — and gets a bag of sandwiches, and the conductor calls
all-aboard. Now the Peach sees its real bag on the shelf behind June:
small, red, a white string. Pointing is not a description, so the only
way to the bag is to say the true words out loud and lose the act.
It does. June hands the bag over, the ticket is inside, and the Peach
catches its train holding the small red bag it said was big and black.

## The chain (the whiteboard — every joint must read therefore/but)

U1 want + clock on screen
  —therefore→ U2: because June needs a description and the Peach
    lies, she pulls the wrong case; the lie costs the first beep.
U2 —therefore→ U3: because the first lie failed and the train is
    leaving, the Peach protects its act with a bigger lie — and the
    second wrong bag costs it the all-aboard call.
U3 —but→ U4: but now the real bag is in sight and pointing cannot
    claim it; the only road left is the truth, and the truth costs
    the Peach its face.
U4 —therefore→ U5: therefore the Peach says the small true words,
    quietly, with no act.
U5 —therefore→ U6: therefore June can hand the bag over, the ticket
    is in it, and the Peach runs — the ending is caused by the
    confession, by nothing else.

Plants: the small red bag sits on the shelf behind June from U2 on,
in plain sight of the camera (fires U4–U5). June's rule — a claim
needs a description; pointing is not one — is stated by her actions
in U1–U2 and spoken once, at the lock, in U4. The train is visible
through the glass behind the counter in every window unit: the clock
is a picture, never a summary claim. No coincidence rescues anyone;
the bag was there all along, unreachable only because of the lie.

## Dialogue (ESL: one idea per line, common words, most lines ≤7 words)

U1 (the window — the train at the platform behind the glass, doors
open; the Peach bursts in mid-shout, first spoken word at 0.00s):
  PEACH: "My bag! The train is here!"
  JUNE:  "Where is the ticket?"
  PEACH: "In my bag! In my bag!"
  JUNE:  "Describe the bag."
  PEACH: (squeak) "Big. Black. Very strong."

U2 (June hauls up a big black case. The Peach opens it: big black
boots. No ticket. On the shelf behind June, unseen by both: a small
red bag with a white string):
  JUNE:  "Boots. No ticket."
  PEACH: "That is not my bag."
  (Outside, the train beeps once.)
  JUNE:  "Describe it again."

U3 (the Peach doubles down — the act before the train):
  PEACH: "Okay, so technically — black! With gold!"
  JUNE:  "Black with gold. Noted." [thunk-thunk]
  (June brings a black bag with gold trim. The Peach opens it:
  sandwiches.)
  PEACH: "Sandwiches!"
  JUNE:  "Two wrong bags."
  (The conductor, outside: "All aboard!" The doors beep, faster.)
  JUNE:  "The train is going."

U4 (the Peach stops. On the shelf behind June — it sees it now — the
small red bag. It points, hard):
  PEACH: "That one! There! That one!"
  JUNE:  "Pointing is not a description."
  (The beeping stops. A bad silence. The Peach looks at the small
  bag. It speaks quietly, no squeak:)
  PEACH: "It is not big."

U5 (the choice — the Peach pays with its act, out loud):
  PEACH: "Small. Red. White string. It is mine."
  (June turns. She takes the small red bag off the shelf. She steps
  out from behind the counter — no counter between them — and puts
  the bag in the Peach's hands.)
  JUNE:  "Small. Red. White string."
  (The Peach opens the bag. The ticket. It holds the ticket up.)

U6 (the platform — doors closing; the Peach runs with the small red
bag and gets in; the train pulls out. June, back at her window, puts
the big black case back on the shelf and stamps the form):
  JUNE:  "Big. Black. Nobody's." [thunk-thunk]

QUOTABLE (declared): "Big. Black. Nobody's." — a gag built out of
the lie itself; it is the last line because the chain built it, and
the episode still lands with it deleted (Test 7).
STANDALONE BEAT: U4 — the pointing that cannot claim, and the
quiet "It is not big."
