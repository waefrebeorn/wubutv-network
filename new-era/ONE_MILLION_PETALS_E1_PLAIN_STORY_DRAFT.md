# ONE MILLION PETALS — E1 plain story (draft for user verdict)

Written by the parent, 2026-11-27. Bible: new-era/shows/ONE_MILLION_PETALS.md
(rulings applied: the haggle serial is the episode engine; the tally board is
repainted every unit and the count moves six times; unit 2 is a public
humiliation plus a spoken doubling-down bet before named witnesses; unit 5's
proof is the witnesses' three reaction faces first; the episode ends on the
DISPOSAL).

Premise: She needs one million petals to buy back her garden. Every petal
costs her something she is wearing — and the sellers cheat.

Cast: LILA (the buyer, home character), OLD PETT (petal seller of the day),
MOSS the baker and the twins PIA and PIO (named witnesses), DORO (invader
rival seller, inside the chained garden, redecorating it).

1. Market day. A banner over the stall reads "1,000,000 PETALS = THE
   GARDEN". Lila's tally board reads 12. Her petal sack is nearly empty; the
   garden gate behind her is chained, and inside it Doro hangs a garland
   that never wilts. Old Pett's cart carries a mountain of pink petals.
   Lila wears a fine red coat. Moss, Pia and Pio buy bread at the next stall.
2. The haggle. Pett wants her red coat for 100 petals. Lila offers her hat
   instead; he takes it — 50 petals. The board is repainted: 62. Then one
   petal melts in Lila's palm in front of the whole stall. The twins point
   and laugh. Lila lifts the dripping petal and makes her bet out loud,
   before Moss, Pia and Pio: "Every petal in my sack is real by sunset —
   or the coat is yours for nothing." Pett shakes her hand.
3. Lila tests her petals one by one in a bowl of water. Fakes melt pink;
   real ones float. The bad pile grows. The board is repainted: 31 REAL.
   Her hat is gone, her sack is smaller than when she came, and the bet is
   losing. Behind the chain, Doro hangs another garland and watches.
4. Lila carries the bowl of pink melt-water back to Pett's cart and sets it
   down where his customers can see it. Two customers look into the bowl
   and walk away. She demands her hat back and the real petals the fakes
   replaced. Pett refuses — and offers her 19 replacement petals, free.
   She paints the board herself: 31 REAL — 19 FAKE.
5. Lila calls the witnesses over. First, the three faces: Moss, Pia and Pio
   lean in as she dips the best petal from Pett's own display crown into
   the water — it melts. Shock. Shock. Delight. Then the consequence, in
   the same square: Pett opens the locked box under his cart, gives back
   her hat, and pays 200 real petals from his true stock. The board is
   repainted: 231.
6. Walking home, Lila takes the one melted fake petal she kept in her
   pocket and drops it in the gutter. It washes away pink and is gone.
   Her hat is back on, her coat is still hers, the sack hangs heavier.
   She repaints the board one last time — 231 — and chalks the first line
   of tomorrow's number under it. Inside the garden, Doro hangs one black
   petal on his garland.

Legibility check: the want is printed in frame 1 (the banner, the chained
garden, the board at 12). The body is the ledger — Lila visibly diminishes
(hat gone) as the sack and the board grow; the count moves in all six units
(12 → 62 → 31 → 31/19 → 231 → 231 repainted as a promise). Every spoken
line is a move and is answered (offer → refusal → counter-offer; bet →
handshake; demand → refusal → proof). Proof is a positive picture: the
melting petal and the witnesses' three faces, never a deduction. The
premise is re-spent in all six units.
