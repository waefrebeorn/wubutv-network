## U1 — THE LAW (evening, the dining room)

FRAME: the long table, candlelit. BRUNO frame-left and KIP
frame-right are already mid-insult-mime across it — thumbs down,
tongues out, finger guns. Between them on the wall, huge: the
crossed-out mouth sign; beside it the saw on its hook and the row of
sawn-off trophy blocks. Both host chairs stand tall and whole.
SOUND: score and room tone only. Cutlery, cloth, a clock. No voices.
ACTION: the door opens — MANGO enters grinning, covered silver dish
held high, and takes the centre guest chair facing camera. He sets
the dish down and mimes a huge delicious smell — eyes closed, hands
waving the steam to his face. BRUNO points at the sign, then at the
saw, then at the trophy blocks: the law, and its price, in three
points. MANGO nods, still grinning, and mimes zipping his own mouth.
The diners start the betting: two bread rolls placed on the hosts'
side, one on MANGO's side. KIP mimes one last insult at BRUNO behind
MANGO's back; BRUNO catches him at it and raises one finger — a
warning. KIP freezes, hands up, innocent.
END STATE: all seated, all silent, dish covered between them, roll
piles started (hosts 2, guest 1), the saw on its hook, chairs whole.

## U2 — THE SQUEAK (soup course)

FRAME: soup bowls down the table. MANGO centre, KIP frame-right one
chair over, BRUNO frame-left watching both. Roll piles: hosts 2,
guest 1.
SOUND: score, room tone, spoon-and-bowl sounds — and ONE tiny squeak
from KIP at the beat (cap-exempt event). Nothing else vocal.
ACTION: MANGO performs for KIP: he mimes the funniest face he has —
cheeks blown out, spoon balanced on his nose, eyes crossed. The
diners shake silently. KIP's shoulders begin to shake; he presses
his lips flat; a tiny SQUEAK escapes him before he clamps both hands
over his mouth. Freeze. BRUNO stands, points at the sign, takes the
saw down from the wall, kneels by KIP's chair and saws ONE INCH off
its front leg, on camera — sawdust, the little block falls. He holds
the block up to the room like evidence, then nails it to the wall
row. KIP sits again — one inch lower, tilted, furious and silent.
The diners pile three more rolls onto BRUNO's side.
END STATE: KIP seated lower on his shortened chair, MANGO grinning
wider, roll piles hosts 5, guest 1, the new block on the wall.

## U3 — THE SILENT SCREAM (main course)

FRAME: the covered dish now open between them: a whole cooked crab
on a platter — except one claw still moves. BRUNO frame-left, KIP
lower frame-right, MANGO centre watching BRUNO.
SOUND: score and room tone only. The scream is SILENT — mouth wide,
no sound at all. That is the gag and the proof.
ACTION: BRUNO reaches for the crab with the serving tongs. The live
claw snaps shut on his finger. BRUNO's whole body goes rigid; his
mouth opens in a huge silent scream — eyes bulging, chef's hat
trembling — and NOT A SOUND comes out. He holds it, shaking, while
the crab dangles from his finger. Slowly he prises the claw open
with a spoon, sets the crab down, and sits, breathing hard through
his nose, finger held high and throbbing. The diners rise in silent
applause — clapping with their palms an inch apart, no contact, no
noise. MANGO's grin fades for the first time. Rolls pile onto
BRUNO's side: hosts 8, guest 1.
END STATE: BRUNO triumphant and silent, finger in the air, KIP
looking at him with respect, MANGO's grin gone flat.

## U4 — THE PHONE (the cheat)

FRAME: plates cleared, coffee cups. Under the table, unseen by the
hosts, MANGO's hand slides his phone onto his knee, screen glowing.
KIP lower frame-right, BRUNO frame-left, both watching MANGO.
SOUND: score and room tone — then the phone's bright recorded
"HELLO!", loud and cheerful, from under the table (cap-exempt
event; a device voice, nobody at the table speaking). The phone may
chirp once more while held up. No other voice anywhere.
ACTION: the HELLO! bursts out. Both hosts freeze — then turn and
point, together, at MANGO. MANGO throws his hands up, all innocence,
and mimes "who, me?" BRUNO ducks under the table, fishes the phone
out, and holds it HIGH for the whole room: the phone, still lit,
still chirping, in his fist — the cheat produced, a positive
picture. The diners point too. BRUNO hands the phone to a diner,
takes down the saw, kneels by MANGO's chair and saws ONE INCH off
its leg, on camera. The block goes up on the wall beside KIP's.
MANGO sits again — one inch lower, grin finally gone. The rolls
AVALANCHE onto the hosts' side: hosts 12, guest 1.
END STATE: MANGO low and beaten, phone confiscated on the sideboard,
two blocks on the wall, KIP and BRUNO level-ish and watching him.

## U5 — THE LAUGH (dessert; the turn)

FRAME: dessert — one tall, wobbling jelly cake under a glass dome at
centre table. MANGO lower centre, KIP lower frame-right, BRUNO
frame-left. Roll piles hosts 12, guest 1.
SOUND: score and room tone; the dance is silent; then KIP's ONE
HUGE "HA!" (cap-exempt event — the episode's only full vocal sound),
and after it TOTAL silence, held.
ACTION: MANGO stands, climbs onto the table, and dances the funniest
silent dance he has in him — hat of napkins over his eyes, a spoon
in his teeth, hips going, nearly stepping in the jelly and saving
himself at the last inch. The whole room shakes silently — diners
bent double, BRUNO's hat bouncing, KIP purple, both hands clamped,
tears coming. KIP bursts: one huge "HA!" — the only sound. It dies
into total silence. Every face turns to KIP. KIP's own hands fly
back over his mouth, too late. BRUNO stands, walks around the table,
lifts KIP's whole chair — KIP scrambling off it — and carries it out
of the room on his shoulder, past camera. The door shuts.
END STATE: the empty place at the table where KIP's chair stood;
KIP standing beside it, hands still on his mouth; MANGO still on the
table, frozen mid-dance; nobody moves.

## U6 — THE EMPTY PLACE (the button)

FRAME: dessert served. KIP eats STANDING at the table, his plate
where his chair was, his face close and huge in frame — the loss
picture at poster size. The empty place beside him. BRUNO seated,
MANGO seated lower, diners quiet. Roll piles final: hosts 12,
guest 1.
SOUND: score and room tone only. KIP's laughter is silent — body
shaking, mouth clamped by his own hands. No vocal sound.
ACTION: KIP takes a bite of jelly cake standing up. Under the
table's edge, MANGO catches his eye and does the tiny first move of
the dance again — just the hips. KIP starts to shake with silent
laughter, clamps his own mouth shut with both hands and holds it,
eyes watering, victorious over himself this time. BRUNO nods at him,
once — respect. Then the door opens. PEPPER stands in it: tall, red,
stem cap, cracking his knuckles, carrying a covered dish that STEAMS
and RATTLES faintly on its own. He looks at the table, at the sign,
at the empty place where the chair was — and pulls up at the empty
place, miming sitting on nothing, then mimes eating, prim and
perfect, without a sound: the challenge issued in the show's own
grammar. KIP, hands still clamped on his mouth, looks at camera.
END STATE, final frame: PEPPER mid-mime at the empty place, dish
rattling beside him; KIP clamped and staring out; the sign above
them all. NEXT CHALLENGER.
