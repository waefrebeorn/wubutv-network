## PREMISE

Sentence (ships as the caption; the episode itself is wordless):
"At this table nobody may speak. The first one who talks loses his
chair."
Schema: ijeonguu zero-word pantomime. Frame 1: a long table, one
chair at the head, a big crossed-out mouth sign everyone can see,
two rivals already miming insults, mid-gesture. Hunger, cheating
and revenge are all mime + props; the proof beat is the cheat
produced (the sawn chair leg held up, the hidden drumstick pulled
from a sleeve) — never a deduction. Loss: the final unit is the
loser eating standing up, chair gone, face at poster size.

## ENGINE

Zero-word pantomime in anthology rhythm: each unit is one complete
gag beat at the table (setup → cheat → proof picture), and the
chair ledger chains the units into an episode. The premise is
re-spent every unit because every act happens at the table.

## LEDGER RULE

Silence is kept or lost on bodies and chairs: a caught almost-sound
costs ONE CHAIR-INCH, sawn off on camera; a full sound costs the
whole chair, carried out of the room by the winner — and the chair
count in the room is the score, readable in any frame.

## THE SILENCE LAW (ruling, G4)

ZERO spoken words. No exceptions. The ijeonguu transplant's "one
short transactional line" (Part B) is RETIRED for this show.
What breaks the silence is a SOUND, not a word: a gasp, a hum, a
swallowed word, a covered cough. (Annex Part E's under-table phone
survives as a silent GESTURE — a picture leaving a phone — never
as speech.)

**Sound budget (Annex Part I #14):** exactly ONE vocal sound per
episode, placed at the turn. The whole table's reaction to the
sound — not to any word — is the unit's payoff picture.
Almost-sounds below the one budgeted sound are costed by the
ledger rule (chair-inches), never by dialogue.

## TEXTURE

The invader guest is the gentrification arriving at the table: a
fruit or brainrot character takes a seat in a home dining room and
tries to make its hosts break their own law. Shown in casting and
behaviour, never explained.
