## ENGINE (ruling, G4)

**The episode engine is THE HAGGLE SERIAL** (the premise): each
episode one seller, one scam, one real cost; she pays with things
she is wearing and visibly diminishes as the sack visibly grows.
**The season shape is THE ARENA LADDER** (Annex Part B, adi_8xx):
one opponent per episode up a visible ladder, and a foul is named
aloud in the unit where it happens.
**RETIRED as an engine: GUILT AS A GARDEN** (Annex Part G PETALS
#1) — its body math is opposite to the haggle engine's (growing
guilt-flowers vs visible diminishment), and two engines cannot
share one body. Its flowers survive ONLY as the chained garden
itself: the garden's chain of flowers is set dressing and prize,
never a body ledger.

**Mandatory structure — THE FACE-SLAP CYCLE (Annex Part I #13):**
unit 2 carries a public humiliation AND the seller's spoken
doubling-down bet in front of the named witnesses; unit 5's proof
is shot as the witnesses' THREE REACTION FACES first — the crowd
re-rating — and the consequence is collected in the same square,
same witnesses, in the same episode.

## LEDGER RULE

The sack grows by exactly what LILA's body and life lose: every
petal in the sack is paid for with a named thing off her back, her
cart, or her house — and the tally board is repainted with the new
total every unit.

**Count movement (ruling, G4):** the board is repainted EVERY unit
with the new total; each unit's transaction moves the count by a
named amount, spoken or shown inside the unit. The number visibly
moves six times per episode or the episode is not written yet.

## STAKE DISPLAY (ruling, G12.4)

## TEXTURE

The invader is inside the prize: DORO redecorates LILA's chained
garden behind the chain while she pays to buy it back — new paint,
new furniture, his garland on her gate. Gentrification is not the
backdrop of this show; it is the thing being purchased, one petal
at a time.

## FORMAT + COMPRESSION

6 units × 10 seconds, 16:9 landscape, one 60-second episode.
The sources spread the arena ladder across many posts and parts.
The compression device is the face-slap cycle's mandatory beat
pair: one full public reversal per episode — humiliation and bet
in unit 2, proof-by-witness-faces and collection in unit 5 — so
each 60 seconds completes one whole ladder step the sources take
a part to climb, and the repainted board carries the running
total across episodes.
