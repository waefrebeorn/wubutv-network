## ENGINE

The body-ledger serial: ONE feature per episode, re-spent in ≥4 of
6 units; every line is a demand/refusal answered by a feature
moving or refusing to move. E1's feature is the SMILE — the wedding
photo is E1's convergence room. The VOICE is E2's theft. Every
episode ends in the PHOTO STUDIO (Annex Part I #10): the whole
family assembled for a group photograph, the one room where a
stolen feature must perform in front of everyone; the photograph
becomes next episode's printed stake on the wall.

## LEDGER RULE

A feature exists on exactly one face at a time: what VELLA gains,
NINO's face loses in the same unit — and the loss SPREADS carrier
by carrier (Annex Part I #11), readable off faces in any frame.

Contagion spread map (the season's escalation shape): E1 — VELLA
takes the smile. E2 — the smile appears on the PHOTOGRAPHER.
E3 — NINO's voice is heard from the baby sister's RADIO.
Mid-season — the thief's own mother tries the stolen laugh and
cannot stop. Last — MADAME ORA catches it: the enforcer's own face
fails her own law.

## TEXTURE

Gentrification worn on a face: the invader's shopfront and product
labels colonise the street with stolen features while home faces
flatten. The takeover is the theft, shown on bodies — never
explained.

## FORMAT + COMPRESSION

6 units × 10 seconds, 16:9 landscape, one 60-second episode.
The source melodramas run 84–185 seconds per part. The compression
device is the CONVERGENCE ROOM: every episode closes in the photo
studio, where the whole ledger must perform at once in front of the
full cast — so each 60-second episode ends with its complete state
re-read in a single tableau, and the season's spread carries the
long-form escalation.

## SEASON COLUMNS (Annex Part I #5)

- **Hook rotation:** E1 identity · E2 realisation · E3 emotion ·
  then rotate; two identical hook types in a row are a script-lock
  failure.
- **Answer-lag ledger:** every episode answers the PREVIOUS
  episode's hanging question inside unit 1 and raises its own.
  The master question — whose face is it really, and will NINO
  collect in person — is scheduled to land TWO episodes after the
  audience most wants it, never on the episode that raises it
  hardest.
