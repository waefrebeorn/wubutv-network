# BIBLE — MY LANDLORD IS A BABY

Written 2026-11-27 (writing loop fold, user msg 1253) — packet First
Move #2, executed. This bible carries the show's fixed cast and the
parent rulings that close GAPS G4/G11/G12 for this show. The packet
premise (NEW_SLATE_PACKET_2026-11-25.md §5) is the show's spec; this
file is where the §1 cast cards are built from.

## PREMISE

Sentence (also the narration's first line): "My landlord is a baby.
Today he came to collect the rent himself."
Schema: catyfavorite storybook track — the one sanctioned narration
form. Frame 1: a baby in a landlord's hat at the door, hand out;
the tenant's last coin visible in a glass on the shelf behind him.
Printed stake: the coin glass — coins leave it one by one, on
screen. Proof beat: what the rent buys appears in the room as an
object (a leak fixed becomes a dry patch + a bucket retired; a lie
becomes a second baby at the door). Loss: an episode ends with the
tenant paying with the thing itself (his bed carried out) or the
baby shrinking to pocket size and being carried home in a cup.
Serial engine: one demand per episode, escalating objects; the
tenant's room visibly empties/fills as the season's ledger.

## CAST

- **ELIAS** — the tenant (male, HOME, adult). The narrator; the
  narration is his alone. Costume token: the coin glass on his
  shelf — his whole fortune, readable in any frame.
- **BABY BOSS** — the landlord (male baby, INVADER). Costume
  tokens: the landlord's hat, and the teething-ring crown
  (Annex Part G LANDLORD #2) readable at any scale. He does not
  speak: he is cast-carded by ACTS, not lines — hand out, rattle
  raised, the fat marker for the re-stamping (Annex Part E). His
  §1 card carries demands as gestures; the template's speaker
  fields are marked NON-SPEAKING PRINCIPAL.
- **THE COLLECTOR** — the impostor collector (recurring threat):
  a fake rent-collector who knows ELIAS's name and history and
  takes payment/keys before the real landlord arrives
  (Annex Parts E + H). Costume token: a rent book identical to
  the real one, one detail wrong.

Home vs invader: ELIAS is HOME — the flat is his. BABY BOSS is the
invader who owns the paper. See TEXTURE.

## ENGINE — FORM RULING (G4)

The catyfavorite narration track STAYS as the show's engine, ruled
as this show's sanctioned narration exception: first person, one
plain sentence per shot, every sentence exactly what the frame
shows, followable with the sound off.
**Exchange-Rule reconciliation:** narration does not exempt the
show from the Exchange Rule (synthesis §3 item 4). Every narration
sentence states a MOVE — a demand, a payment, a refusal, a lie —
and BABY BOSS ANSWERS it with an act inside the same shot (grows,
shrinks, points, takes, turns away). A sentence the baby does not
answer is cut.

## LEDGER RULE (growth ruling, G4)

The baby grows ONE VISIBLE SIZE each time he is paid, and shrinks
when he is not: rent pressure is read off his body, and the coin
glass empties coin by coin on screen. (Premise governs.)
RESERVE: growth on every "no" (Annex Part A LANDLORD #11);
growth on rent paid in fun, shrinking on cash-only months
(Annex Part G LANDLORD #4).

## TEXTURE

The building's other flats fill with fruit tenants — one more door
per episode: a fruit family's nameplate, their music through the
wall, their pram in the hall. ELIAS's flat is the home being
surrounded, shown door by door, never explained.

## FORMAT + COMPRESSION

6 units × 10 seconds, 16:9 landscape, one 60-second episode.
The source storybook tales run 105 seconds and up, one want drawn
across many shots. The compression device is OPEN ON THE
DESTINATION (Annex Part I #2) plus the coin glass: E1 opens on the
tenant's room ALREADY EMPTIED — bed gone, ELIAS sleeping in the
bath, the baby huge — then cuts to "ONE MONTH EARLIER" printed on
the rent book, and every unit is one payment closer to the room
the viewer has already seen. Sixty seconds carries the whole
month because the viewer is never asking what happens, only which
payment does it.

## SEASON COLUMNS (Annex Part I #5)

- **Hook rotation:** E1 identity · E2 realisation · E3 emotion ·
  then rotate; checked at script lock.
- **Answer-lag ledger:** every episode answers the previous
  episode's hanging question inside unit 1 and raises its own.
  The master question — will the room empty, and will ELIAS ever
  hold the night-light back — is scheduled to land two episodes
  after the audience most wants it.

## RULINGS

- **The aside (Annex Part I #6):** the narration track carries
  exactly ONE aside per episode — the one sentence that tells the
  audience something the baby doesn't know.
- **The pictured wish (Annex Part I #20):** one licensed
  future-tense daydream per episode — "Next month the room is
  warm and the baby knocks like a guest." — over the pictured
  wish (the room full, the baby small and welcome). The finale's
  real last shot re-shoots the daydream's composition with the
  wish granted or quietly altered.
- **The villain's love (Annex Part I #8):** BABY BOSS has one
  beloved thing — a night-light he cannot sleep without. Holding
  it is ELIAS's one moment of power per season, and he always
  gives it back: that is the relationship the rent war sits on.
- **Phone-call recap (Annex Part I #1):** available from E2 —
  ELIAS phones the baby's "office" (a pram with a toy phone) and
  the recap is the rent history read back as an itemised bill;
  the call ends with a new demand, not a goodbye.
- **Help card (Annex Part H LANDLORD #4):** rationed — at most
  once per episode, only when every in-world move has failed.
- **Title/caption:** per the template ruling (2026-11-27) —
  TITLE = the question the episode answers; CAPTION = the
  episode SENTENCE (for this show, also the narration's first
  line).
