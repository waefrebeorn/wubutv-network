## The story in plain sentences

1. A giant empty glass jar stands between two houses. A line painted near
   the top says FAIR DAY. Both neighbours are already dropping things in.
2. The tall neighbour puts in his roof tiles, one by one. His roof gets
   emptier. The short neighbour puts in his coat buttons. His coat falls open.
3. The short neighbour finds one of the tall neighbour's tiles hidden in his
   own wood pile — the tall neighbour has been putting the same tile in
   twice, taking it out at night. He holds the tile up in an open hand where
   everyone can see it.
4. The tall neighbour must pay for the cheat on screen: he puts in his
   dinner, his chair, and his hat. The jar passes the short neighbour's side.
5. The short neighbour puts in the last thing he has — his door key. The jar
   reaches the FAIR DAY line. The fair bell rings.
6. The tall neighbour carries the full jar home on his head, past both
   houses, while the short neighbour walks beside him holding the door key
   he won back. The jar is home. The race is over and somebody lost.
