## U1 — HE CAME FOR THE BATH (glass ONE · baby KNEE-HIGH · the coin REFUSED)

STATE IN: new month · room empty but for the bath · glass ONE
(the coin from under the bath, E1's aside, now in the glass) ·
baby SHRUNK to KNEE-HIGH overnight.
PICTURE: the FIRST-3 frame. A rope on the bath — the baby has
been dragging it toward the door and got it to mid-room. ELIAS
stands in front of it, arms spread. The baby opens the rent
book: a drawn bathtub, FIVE drawn coins. ELIAS takes the one
coin from the glass and holds it out. The baby looks at it a
long time. He shakes his head, slow, and points at the bath.
NARRATION (ELIAS, to camera):
- "He came for the bath. Just like I said he would. First
  knock of the month."
- "He shrank in the night, see. Nobody paid him. Overnight
  counts."
- (the confession, habit 8) "That coin? It sat under this
  bath all last month. He knew. Course he knew."
THE BABY'S ANSWERS: the point at the bath answers the first
line; his small size IS the second line's proof; the slow
head-shake refuses the coin as the third line lands.
END STATE: coin back in ELIAS's fist · the book open at five
coins · the bath mid-room, rope still on it.

## U2 — THE NEIGHBOUR PRICES IT (hall · baby KNEE-HIGH · a second door fills)

STATE IN: the hall outside · the second door now wears a
nameplate, PLUM, and a pram stands by it · glass ONE (in the
room, behind us).
PICTURE: ELIAS in the hall with a cup of tea he made to think.
MRS PLUM comes out of her door with washing. The baby, waiting
in the hall, stamps a tiny RENT MARK on her door with the fat
marker, businesslike, and holds out his hand to her too. She
looks at the mark. She looks at ELIAS. She is not surprised.
NARRATION (ELIAS — her lines quoted in her own shape):
- "Mrs Plum moved in Tuesday. Plums. Nice people. She took
  one look at my bath rope and she said—"
- (quoted, dry and kind) "'He cries, you pay, love. That's
  the whole lease.'"
- "The man before me, she said, he paid with his door.
  Sleeps in the hall now. Lovely hall, she said."
THE BABY'S ANSWER: the stamp on her door — rent spreads while
they talk; his hand stays out to her the whole unit.
END STATE: the tiny rent mark on PLUM's door · the baby's
hand out · ELIAS looking at his own door, doing the maths.

## U3 — HE WANTS IT FOR SLEEPING (glass ONE · baby KNEE-HIGH · sympathy)

STATE IN: the room · the bath mid-room · the baby alone with
it for one beat while ELIAS watches from the doorway.
PICTURE: the baby climbs into the bath. Too big to drag,
small enough to fit. He curls up in it with the night-light
— and for one beat he is asleep, thumb in, hat slipping.
ELIAS watches. The baby wakes, sees him, and points at the
bath again — but slower. Less landlord. More asking.
NARRATION (ELIAS, quieter — temperature drops):
- "He wasn't dragging it to sell it."
- "He wants to sleep in it. Like me. That's… that's the
  whole thing, isn't it."
- "Five coins for a bed. His bed. Mine too."
THE BABY'S ANSWERS: the curl and the sleep answer the first
line before it ends; the slower point answers the second —
the same gesture, asking instead of demanding.
END STATE: baby sitting in the bath, night-light in his lap ·
ELIAS in the doorway, arms no longer spread.

## U4 — THE GIFT (glass ONE · the CARDIGAN given · buys nothing)

STATE IN: later · the bath still mid-room · ELIAS in his
cardigan.
PICTURE: ELIAS takes off his brown cardigan — his one warm
thing — folds it once, and lays it in the bath as a blanket.
No book. No coin. He steps back with his hands up: free. The
baby stares at it. He climbs in, pulls the cardigan over
himself, and hugs it like the night-light. Then THE PICTURED
WISH: ELIAS pictures Friday — himself handing over two coins,
the baby small and asleep in the bath under the cardigan, the
room warm, two neighbours, nobody owing anybody. Hold it.
NARRATION (ELIAS):
- "So I gave him my cardigan. Not rent. Nobody wrote it
  down. Just… here. It's cold in a bath."
- "He looked at me like nobody ever just gives him a thing."
THE BABY'S ANSWERS: the stare answers the gift; the hug
answers the second line — he holds the cardigan the way he
holds the light.
END STATE: baby in the bath under the cardigan, night-light
glowing · ELIAS in his shirt, rubbing his arms · the wish's
composition banked (bath, cardigan, two coins, warm room).

## U5 — THE PROMISE (night · the page STAMPED · baby KNEE-HIGH → HUGE)

STATE IN: NIGHT · the book open on the floor between them ·
the fat marker beside it · glass ONE.
PICTURE: ELIAS kneels at the book. He writes, big and slow,
on a clean page: I PROMISE — TWO COINS — FRIDAY. He reads it
aloud to the baby as he writes. The baby watches the page,
not the glass. A long beat. The baby picks up the fat marker
and STAMPS the page — a fat ring-mark right under the words.
Then he GROWS — one whole size on camera, KNEE-HIGH to HUGE,
fed on a promise like a coin — and sits down beside the bath,
satisfied, in the cardigan.
NARRATION (ELIAS):
- "I don't have five. I don't have two. But I've got a
  word, and I've never paid with it. Not once."
- (cap-exempt — reading the page aloud, slow) "I promise.
  Two coins. Friday. My word."
- "He stamped it. Just like that. A promise is a payment.
  Who knew."
THE BABY'S ANSWERS: his eyes on the page answer the first
line; the stamp answers the promise as it's read; the growth
answers the third line — proof, on his body, in the same shot.
END STATE: the stamped page in the open book · baby HUGE in
the cardigan, night-light beside him · glass still ONE.

## U6 — FRIDAY, TONIGHT (glass ONE → EMPTY · the PAGE taken · the bath STAYS)

STATE IN: the same night, later · ELIAS asleep sitting
against the bath · the book closed on the floor.
PICTURE: a knock — soft. The baby at the door, HUGE, book
under his arm. He opens it to the promise page and holds out
his hand. ELIAS looks at the page. He looks at the glass:
one coin. He pays it — glass EMPTY. The baby looks at the
coin. He looks at the page. Then he tears the page out of
the book, careful, folds it once, and puts it under his hat.
He nods at the bath — yours — and goes. ELIAS climbs into
the bath, his bed, in his shirt. Quietly, only to us, the
aside. Then the last line.
NARRATION (ELIAS):
- "Friday, I said. He nodded like Friday was tonight.
  Maybe it was. Baby days are short."
- (THE ASIDE — bible-protected; quietly, only to us) "I
  never told him the bath leaks. He'll find out."
- (cap-exempt) "He didn't take the bath. He took my word."
THE BABY'S ANSWERS: the open book and the outstretched hand
answer the first line; the tear-out answers the payment —
one coin accepted against two promised, the page itself
taken as the rest; the nod at the bath answers the aside's
secret with a mercy Elias never learns about.
END STATE (held): the bath, stayed · the glass empty · the
book with a page missing · ELIAS in the bath, looking at us.
HANGING QUESTION FOR E3 (emotion hook): a promise is spent —
what does the baby do with a man's word, and what happens
the first time Elias can't keep one?
