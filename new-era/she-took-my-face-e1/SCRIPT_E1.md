## U1 — THE STAMP (day, photo studio)

FRAME: the flash fires. NINO stands in his wedding suit and red scarf,
his face smooth and mouthless. Beside him VELLA smiles with TWO smiles,
one above the other. Behind them, a shelf of cream bottles — every
label printed with the same smile. ORA stamps a framed certificate on
the studio wall: SOLD — LEGAL, under a drawing of the smile.

- ORA: "One smile, one face. Stamped. Sold. Legal." [certifies — the
  theft made lawful]
- VELLA (to the photographer): "Take it now. We are both smiling."
  [sells the picture]
- NINO (flat, quiet): "One of those is mine." [accuses — the want,
  stated in unit 1]

ACTION: the flash fires again. VELLA turns her best smile — both of
them — to the camera. NINO touches the smooth place where his smile
was. The certificate hangs straight and official behind them.
END STATE: the theft certified; the wall holds the stamp; NINO's hand
on his empty face.

## U2 — THE WALL (evening, VELLA's shop) — SCORE ONLY, no dialogue

FRAME: VELLA alone, closing her shop. On the wall, a row of photographs
of VELLA as a girl — in every one, the girl's face is plain and
unsmiling. VELLA looks at the girl. Then at herself in the mirror:
two smiles.

ACTION (no lines): she peels the stolen smile off her face and holds
it in her hands like a warm mask. Alone, she tries it on again —
higher, lower — it never sits right on her. She practices smiling at
nobody. The smile slips a fraction, by itself. She pushes it back,
quick, and looks at the girlhood wall again.
TEXTURE: outside the dark window, her shop sign glows — a big printed
smile over the door, colonising the empty street.
END STATE: the smile back on, slightly crooked; the girl on the wall
still unsmiling. RUNG 1: alone, it fits her badly.

## U3 — THE BOTTLE (day, the street)

FRAME: TOMI stops dead at VELLA's shop window. A whole row of bottles,
every label smiling the same smile. He looks at the label, then runs.

- TOMI (dragging NINO to the window, pointing — label, face, label):
  "Brother. Your smile. She sells it." [shows him — the discovery]
- NINO: "Then she can give it back." [decides — the demand is loaded]

ACTION: NINO presses his flat face close to the glass. Inside, his
smile looks back at him from twenty bottles at once.
TEXTURE: neighbours pass the window flat-faced and ordinary; every
bottle in the window smiles. The street's faces are losing; the labels
are winning.
END STATE: NINO walking toward the shop door, TOMI at his shoulder;
twenty printed smiles watching him come.

## U4 — THE REFUSAL (day, VELLA's shop)

FRAME: inside the shop. The stamped certificate hangs here too, behind
the counter — the same SOLD — LEGAL stamp, bigger. Bottles everywhere.

- NINO: "Give me my smile back." [demands]
- VELLA: "Today the bride buys only from a smiling face." [refuses —
  names her price]
- VELLA (hand flat on the certificate): "A face is just a sticker.
  I peel it. I sell it." [her law — the most confident line]

ASIDE — NINO turns to the camera, once, flat and quiet:
- NINO: "Then watch me peel it back." [ASIDE — the promise the plot
  has not proven yet]

ACTION: VELLA smiles both smiles at a customer and sells a bottle on
the spot, coins down, never looking at NINO. NINO backs out of the
shop — not beaten; measuring the room.
END STATE: the sale made; the certificate untouched; the promise made
to the audience only.

## U5 — THE LAUGH (day, the wedding shoot)

FRAME: the studio dressed for the wedding shoot. VELLA works the room,
wearing the smile, selling bottles to the guests. The bride sits ready,
happy to tears.

- GUEST (staring): "Your smile moved." [notices — RUNG 2, the company
  slip: the stolen smile twitched by itself]
- VELLA (too bright): "It does that when it is happy." [covers — a lie]

ACTION: the bride begins to cry with happiness. In the quiet room,
NINO's smile on VELLA's face bursts out LAUGHING — loud, at the wrong
moment. Every guest stares. The smile laughs again. VELLA clamps both
hands over it.
- VELLA (behind her hands, to the smile itself): "Stop it. Stop
  laughing." [orders her own face — the theft confessed by behaviour]

REACTION BEATS: the bride's tears stop. The guests look from VELLA's
clamped hands to NINO's flat face, and back. NINO does not move.
END STATE: the room silent; the smile still shaking under her hands;
every eye in the room on the two faces. RUNG 3: in public, it is not
hers at all.

## U6 — THE PHOTO (day, photo studio)

FRAME: the photographer lines up the whole wedding group. NINO steps
in beside VELLA and holds one of her bottles up next to her face —
the label's smile and the smile she wears, side by side, identical.
The camera flashes.

- ORA (looking at the fresh print, then up at her own certificate):
  "Two smiles. One face. My stamp is wrong." [the authority revokes
  her own stamp — the reckoning]

ACTION: ORA takes the certificate down off the wall. The printed photo
is passed hand to hand: two matching smiles, and NINO's flat face
between them. NINO takes the photo home and pins it on his own empty
wall — the first picture in his collection.
- NINO (at his wall): "First picture for my wall." [starts the
  collection]

FINAL BEAT (no line): in her shop, VELLA touches the stolen smile —
and a small crack opens at its corner. She stares at it in the mirror.
FINAL FRAME: the cracked smile in the mirror; across town, one photo
on an empty wall.
END STATE: the proof printed and pinned; the stamp revoked; the smile
still on her face — but cracked. The collection has begun.
