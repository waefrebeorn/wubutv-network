## U1 — THE DESTINATION (glass EMPTY · baby HUGE)

STATE IN: room EMPTY (no bed, no table) · baby HUGE, asleep
in the corner, hugging the glowing night-light · glass EMPTY
on the shelf · the rent book open on the floor.
PICTURE: the whole episode in one frame. ELIAS sits in the
bathtub in the corner, fully dressed, holding his pillow. He
looks at us. The baby breathes, enormous. On the floor the
rent book lies open; its page reads: ONE MONTH EARLIER.
NARRATION (ELIAS, to camera — cap-exempt):
- "My landlord took everything. It started with one coin."
THE BABY'S ANSWER (act, same shot): in his sleep, the huge
baby hugs the night-light tighter — the size of him IS the
answer: this is what the coin bought.
END STATE: held on the book's page — ONE MONTH EARLIER.

## U2 — ONE COIN, ONE SIZE (glass 6 → 5 · baby DOLL → KNEE-HIGH)

STATE IN: ONE MONTH EARLIER · room FULL and warm (bed made,
table set) · glass SIX coins · baby DOLL-SMALL.
PICTURE: a knock. The door opens: BABY BOSS at the door,
doll-small, landlord's hat straight, hand out. He climbs the
door frame and points at the coin glass. ELIAS takes ONE coin
from the glass, kneels, and pays it into the small hand. The
baby GROWS — one whole size, on camera — knee-high, hat still
straight. Then THE PICTURED WISH: ELIAS looks at the room and
pictures it — the SAME composition dreamed: rent paid, the
room warm, ELIAS asleep in his bed, the baby tiny and asleep
in a cup beside the glowing night-light. Hold it. (This exact
composition is the finale's target frame — to be re-shot with
the wish granted or quietly altered.)
NARRATION:
- ELIAS: "My landlord is a baby. Today he came to collect
  the rent himself."
THE BABY'S ANSWER: the climb, the point, the growth — each
in the same shot as the sentence it answers.
END STATE: baby KNEE-HIGH, coin in fist · glass FIVE · the
wish's composition banked.

## U3 — NOT PAYING (glass 5 · baby KNEE-HIGH → DOLL-SMALL · pillow TAKEN)

STATE IN: a week later — a calendar page tears itself off
the wall (designed object) · baby KNEE-HIGH · glass FIVE.
PICTURE: a knock. The baby at the door, knee-high, hand out.
ELIAS folds his arms and shakes his head — no coin moves. The
baby SHRINKS one size in front of him, doll-small — then
takes hold of ELIAS's pillow, drags it to the door, and sits
on it outside, facing the room, waiting. Unpaid, he does not
leave empty-handed.
NARRATION:
- ELIAS: "He only grows when I pay him. So I tried not
  paying."
THE BABY'S ANSWER: the shrink, the drag, the sit — refusal
answered by a taking, in the same shot.
END STATE: pillow OUTSIDE the door, baby on it · glass FIVE ·
ELIAS looking at his bare bed.

## U4 — THE WRONG BOOK (glass 5 · THE COLLECTOR refused · THE ASIDE)

STATE IN: glass FIVE · pillow back inside (leaning by the
door) · day.
PICTURE: a second knock — different: three neat taps. A
SECOND BABY at the door, grey hat, holding out a rent book
identical to the real one. He opens it to the picture page:
the drawn landlord — and in the drawing, the little hat sits
on the WRONG SIDE. The camera pushes in on the wrong detail.
ELIAS, coin already in his fingers, stops. He shuts the
door. A beat — then a BLACK RENT SLIP slides under the door
and the small footsteps go. ELIAS picks up the slip, looks at
it — then turns to us, quiet, only to us.
NARRATION:
- ELIAS (refusal, stated as he shuts the door): "His hat
  was on the wrong side. I shut the door."
- ELIAS (THE ASIDE — bible-protected; quietly, only to us):
  "I keep one coin under the bath. He does not know."
THE ANSWER (act): the impostor's hand, held out and left
empty; the black slip — his answer to the shut door.
END STATE: black slip in ELIAS's hand · glass FIVE · the
bath in the corner behind him as he says the aside.

## U5 — THE LIGHT AND THE BED (glass 5 · baby DOLL-SMALL → KNEE-HIGH · bed PAID)

STATE IN: NIGHT · baby DOLL-SMALL · the night-light GONE
(fallen behind the shelf in the day's dragging and pulling).
PICTURE: dark room, one lamp low. BABY BOSS sits small in
the dark where his light should be, crying without a sound —
shoulders shaking, no noise. ELIAS wakes, searches, finds
the night-light behind the shelf, kneels, and GIVES IT BACK.
The baby hugs it, glow returning — then, still hugging it,
he opens the rent book to the circled date: the whole month,
due at once. ELIAS looks at the book. He looks at his bed.
He nods once. The baby, fed on the payment, GROWS one size
dragging the bed out through the door.
NARRATION:
- ELIAS: "His light was gone. I gave it back."
- ELIAS (as the bed moves): "He wanted the whole month. I
  paid with the bed."
THE BABY'S ANSWERS: the silent crying answers the first
sentence before it ends; the book opened to the circled
date answers the gift; the growth answers the payment.
END STATE: bed GONE out the door · baby KNEE-HIGH in the
doorway, hugging the light · ELIAS standing in the
half-empty room.

## U6 — THE DESTINATION, AGAIN (glass ONE · baby HUGE)

STATE IN: the U1 picture, re-shot exactly — room EMPTY ·
baby HUGE asleep in the corner hugging the night-light ·
ELIAS in the bathtub, fully dressed, the returned pillow
under his arm · the rent book closed on the floor.
PICTURE: everything the viewer now understands. ELIAS looks
at us. On the shelf, the coin glass — with ONE coin in it:
the coin from under the bath. In his sleep, the huge baby's
hand opens on the floor, palm up. Waiting.
NARRATION (ELIAS, to camera — cap-exempt):
- "One coin left. Tomorrow he comes for the bath."
THE BABY'S ANSWER: the sleeping hand, opening.
END STATE (held): the glass with one coin · the open hand ·
ELIAS in the bath, looking at us.
