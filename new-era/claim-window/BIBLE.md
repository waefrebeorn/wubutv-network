## Premise (one sentence a stranger can picture)

A lost-and-found window at the station: a clerk who releases nothing she can't get described
properly, and a peach who lost an umbrella and will say anything except the truth about it.

PETTY WANT: THE PEACH wants its umbrella back before the storm breaks.
STATUS CHANGE: by the end the umbrella is RELEASED — stamped and handed over, irreversible —
and June has broken her own rule to do it (she stamps the form after the handover and writes
the true detail in herself).
FACE AT RISK: the Peach's — every lie it tells is one June has already heard; June's — her
perfect record ("nobody gets anything out of this window they couldn't describe") is the thing
she gives up.
COLLISION: claimant who can't describe + clerk who won't release + a storm on the clock.

## Register

Dry municipal deadpan (June) against fruit melodrama (the Peach). June never raises her voice;
the Peach performs every feeling at full volume. REGISTER DEVIATION is the season arc: June's
flatness cracks exactly once per episode, late, small.

## Cast (two designed speakers — the ceiling)

### JUNE — the clerk (home character)
LOOK: woman in her 40s, dark hair in a low knot, plain grey station cardigan over a white
shirt, no name tag, sleeves pushed to the elbow. Sits behind the claim-window counter; a
rubber date stamp and a stack of claim forms live at her right hand. Never stands except to
go to the back shelves.
RIZZ CARD:
- CARRIER WORDS: "again", "the form", "noted"
- CATCHPHRASE: "Describe it again." — declared before the episode; earned instance is U1's
  procedure (she asks because the description changed), payoff instance is U6, where the
  Peach throws it back and June answers with the Peach's own U1 lie, now true.
- THE BRAG: "Nobody's ever gotten anything out of this window they couldn't describe."
- AUDIBLE TELL: two stamp thunks — thunk-thunk — on the counter before she answers a claim
  she doesn't believe.
- SPECIMEN LINES: "You said blue. Then you said red. Pick a life." · "That's not a
  description, that's a wish." · "I don't lose things. People lose things at me."
- REGISTER DEVIATION: fills in the true detail on the form herself, in her own handwriting,
  after the item is already gone.

### THE PEACH — the claimant (invader, the SHOW-OFF)
LOOK: a peach character in a soaked yellow rain slicker two sizes too big, leaf stem bent
flat by the rain. Arrives wet, stays wet. Hands always moving — pointing, pleading, offering.
RIZZ CARD:
- CARRIER WORDS: "technically", "okay so", "mine"
- CATCHPHRASE: "Okay so technically—" — starts every claim and gets cut off every time;
  lands clean exactly once, in U5, when it finally tells the truth.
- THE BRAG: "I have never lost anything I didn't find louder."
- AUDIBLE TELL: a small squeak before each lie.
- SPECIMEN LINES: "It's red. It was always red. I dream in red." · "I didn't lose it, the
  rain took it, there's a difference." · "She's an umbrella with commitment issues."
- REGISTER DEVIATION: U5 — drops the act entirely for one plain sentence about its mother.

SHOW-OFF: THE PEACH (default invader). DELIGHT unrationed for it; June is the wall it plays
against.

## The bit

THE PULL: June ducks behind the counter and hauls out the wrong umbrella — beach umbrella,
cocktail umbrella, umbrella hat — each presented dead-serious as a candidate match. Setup in
U3, paid off in U6 when the pull is finally right and the Peach opens it indoors.
