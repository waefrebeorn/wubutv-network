# PILOT-4 — THE COUNT: "The Number Between Ours"

**Show:** SHOW-4 THE COUNT (ARC-3, thread state: OPEN → DEVELOP).
**Format:** 6 units × 10 s = 60 s · 1440 frames @ 24 fps · 1280×720 · one 16:9 master.
**Workflows demonstrated:** panel kit locked before firing (PANEL WORKFLOW SPEC

every line · motion law · motif axis checked across units.

## Bible lines (locked for this pilot)

- **Palette:** INK (hall, suits, desk) + BONE (paper, blouse, chalk) + CADMIUM
  (the board's glowing digits, the tally brass) + one COBALT accent (the
  Clerk's ink). No other hues.
- **Light:** one motivated source per frame — the board's glow from behind the
  desk, one overhead lamp. Flat, deadpan, diagram-clear (SATIRE channel
  signature: tallies as set dressing, the frame an encyclopedia page that
  misbehaved).
- **Camera:** locked medium-wide on the desk for anchor units; no camera move
  in the first 12 frames of any unit; moves only as events (push to badge,
  track to door).
- **Texture:** fine film grain finish; clean render is a fail.
- **The world rule, visualised:** at every joint the hall behind the held
  elements (desk, board, ledger) visibly swaps — light, chairs, dressing —
  while the count survives. The characters know; nobody lectures it.

## LOOK blocks (invariant traits, restated in every vehicle)

- **SUPER NUMBER HEAD ("the Counter"):** tall gaunt adult man; very large
  round bald head; pale skin; heavy-lidded eyes that never blink; ink-black
  suit, white shirt; a brass mechanical tally counter on a chain at his chest;
  long fingers. Deadpan. Counts with small precise hand movements.
- **THE TALLY CLERK:** adult woman; sharp black bob; round spectacles;
  bone-white blouse, ink-black vest; cobalt-blue ink stains on her fingertips;
  deadpan. Keeps the audit ledger. Her totals are always exactly one higher
  than his — until unit 5.

## Script (one conversation; line at 7–9 s, cliff number at 9–10 s)

**U1 — "The desk, and two totals that disagree by one."**
HOOK 0–1.5: frame 0 = PEAK panel state: board glows 1000, ledger open on
1001, both legible in one frame; the Counter's hand already hovering over
the brass tally button, mid-press. STATE: he counts the hall's chairs
aloud under his breath, tally clicking. TURN 4–7: a chair at the back
vanishes (cull) — the total should drop; the board does not move. LINE
(Clerk → Counter, DARE): **"Say the number between ours, Counter."**
CLIFF: he looks at the board, then at her ledger. "One thousand one."

**U2 — "The counting desk, and two parcels stamped with the same number."**
HOOK: 1001 stamped big on a parcel on the desk (the cliff number, shown,
new place). STATE: the Counter weighs the parcel, stamps it again —
1001. TURN: a second parcel slides in from the dark, already stamped
1001. The number arrived twice. LINE (Counter → Clerk, ACCUSATION):
**"Your ledger counted that parcel twice."** CLIFF (Clerk, flat):
"One thousand two."

**U3 — "The counting hall rebuilt, and its chalked total missing a digit."**
HOOK: the hall swapped (new light, chairs reversed, dressing changed);
1002 chalked huge on the wall. STATE: the Clerk writes in the ledger;
the Counter counts chalk tally marks under the number. TURN: the chalk
smears — the last digit culls; the wall reads 100_ . LINE (Clerk →
Counter, DEMAND): **"Write the missing digit, Counter. Now."** CLIFF:
he raises the chalk, does not write. "One thousand three."

**U4 — "The counter's chest tally, and its number stepping backward."**
HOOK: push-in already moving at the cut: the brass tally on his chest
reads 1003. STATE: he walks the desk line, tally clicking once per
chair. TURN: the tally clicks — and steps BACK to 1002 for a held beat,
then returns to 1003. LINE (Counter → Clerk, WARNING/refusal): **"It
went back one. Tell no one."** CLIFF (Clerk, writing it down anyway):
"One thousand four."

**U5 — "The board and the ledger in agreement, and one chair nobody counted."**
HOOK: board 1004 and the open ledger 1004 in one frame — agreement, the
strangest state yet. STATE: both stare; the Counter recounts the chairs
to prove the agreement. TURN: mid-count, a chair appears at the end of
the row that neither of them counted — the total is wrong even when they
agree. LINE (Clerk → Counter, CONFESSION/dare): **"We agree, Counter.
So we're both wrong."** CLIFF (Counter, quietly): "One thousand five."

**U6 — "A chalked door, and through its window another desk counting ahead."**
HOOK: 1005 chalked on a door at the hall's end; camera already tracking
with the Counter as he approaches. STATE: the Clerk follows with the
ledger; the door's small window glows CADMIUM. TURN: through the window:
a second counting desk, a second board — already showing 1006. It is
counting without them. LINE (Counter → Clerk, DEMAND): **"Open it,
Clerk. It's counting without us."** CLIFF: the Clerk's hand on the
handle; the Counter opens his mouth — "One thousand—" — CUT on the
onset, mid-word. Nothing resolves.

## Motif axis (no motion motif repeats across units)

U1 pressing (hand/tally button) · U2 sliding (parcel enters) · U3 erasing
(chalk smear) · U4 stepping-back (tally digit reverses; light flicker) ·
U5 appearing (chair pops in) · U6 approaching/opening (track to door,
handle turn begins). All distinct. ✓
