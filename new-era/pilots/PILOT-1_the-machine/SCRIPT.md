# PILOT-1 — THE MACHINE — "The Mouth That Moved" (Chain M1-1 spine, extended to 6 units)

Format: 6 units × 10s = 60s · 1440f @24fps · 16:9 · one conversation, one spoken
line per unit at 7–9s, ≤9 words, named speaker → named addressee.
Transition device: THE MOUTH HANDOFF — every unit ends on a mouth opening on a
word; the next unit opens on the SAME mouth mid-word, different angle/singer.
Stake: LAURA's credibility with UNC — if she is wrong she is the new hire crying
wolf; if she is right, the machine is lying. (SHOW-1 Chain M1-1, OPEN.)
Bible: palette INK black / CADMIUM yellow-gold / COBALT screen-blue / FOREST
green accent · light = monitor glow key + one warm practical · camera locked or
slow push · texture: slight codec softness, cinematic, never ad-clean.
Cull-survivor PROP: LAURA's studio headphones (one pair, carried every unit).

## U1 — HOOK (doctrine frame 0)
Frame sentence: *the cutting room monitor, and the singer's mouth moving in
total silence.*
0.0s: the main monitor fills the frame — a black cat with a red collar mid-song,
mouth wide on a note that is NOT sounding. In the dim room at frame edge, LAURA
holds one headphone cupped to her ear, staring at UNC, not the screen.
LINE 7–9s — LAURA → UNC (accusation): "The mouth moved, UNC. Nothing came out." (7w)
Unspent fact: the mouth is singing a song nobody can hear.
CLIFF: the cat's mouth opens on the next word — cut mid-opening.
Motif: held stare / monitor push-in.

## U2 — STATE
Opens on the SAME cat mouth mid-word, side angle on the monitor. UNC works the
console faders, deadpan, trying to bring the sound up. A waveform on the second
screen draws itself in total silence.
LINE 7–9s — UNC → LAURA (command/deflection): "Cut to the mouth, Laura. Mouth is the record." (9w)
Unspent fact: the waveform is drawing a song that has no sound.
CLIFF: the cat's mouth opens on a word — cut.
Motif: hands on faders / console work.

## U3 — TURN
Opens on the cat mouth mid-word, reflected angle. LAURA holds the printed lyric
sheet beside the monitor; the mouth is forming a shape that is not the printed
word.
LINE 7–9s — LAURA → UNC (accusation): "It sang a word that isn't written, UNC." (8w)
Unspent fact: the singer is singing words that do not exist in the song.
CLIFF: the mouth opens wider — cut.
Motif: sheet-beside-screen comparison lean-in.

## U4 — TURN (the wrong-singer flip)
Opens on the cat mouth mid-word — and mid-word the face on the monitor is a
young woman's face, same word, same pitch, impossible mouth; the collar is
still there. UNC reaches over and files the take without looking up.
LINE 7–9s — UNC → LAURA (dodge): "File it under the singer. Singer's the record." (8w)
Unspent fact: the singer changed faces mid-line and UNC filed it as routine.
CLIFF: the woman's mouth opens on the next word — cut.
Motif: the face flip (transformation).

## U5 — DEVELOP (the fix arrives before the mistake)
Opens on the woman's mouth mid-word, new angle. Every monitor in the room now
shows a DIFFERENT mouth, all singing silently. A status light turns green on a
take the crew has not shot yet. LAURA and UNC both turn to look at it.
LINE 7–9s — LAURA → UNC (warning): "It fixed a take we haven't broken yet." (8w)
Unspent fact: the machine's fixes now arrive before the mistakes they fix.
CLIFF: one mouth, centre screen, opens on a word — cut.
Motif: the room-wide turn / wall of mouths.

## U6 — LAND + BRIDGE CLIFF
Opens on the centre mouth mid-word; slow pull-back reveals the whole monitor
wall showing the SAME mouth in sync, still silent. UNC stands, crosses to the
main monitor.
LINE 7–9s — UNC → LAURA (deadpan resolve): "It knows our names now. Cut it anyway." (8w)
BRIDGE CLIFF (final frame = next chain's first frame): the main monitor's mouth
opens on a word — and UNC's own mouth opens with it, same word, in the room.
Nothing resolves.
Motif: the pull-back / the standing crossing.
