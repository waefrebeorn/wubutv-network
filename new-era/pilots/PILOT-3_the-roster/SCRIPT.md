# PILOT-3 — THE ROSTER — "The Name That Circles Itself"

One episode, one name across the whole episode: the KEEPER's own.
6 units × 10s = 60s · 1440 frames @ 24fps · one conversation chain.
Arc: ARC-2 "The Roster That Keeps Everyone", Chain 1 (OPEN) extended to
pilot length. Stake: the Keeper's own name — if the roster kept him, he
was never free; if it didn't, he came back for nothing.
Lore shown, never explained: the circled name, the kept shift, the chair
that is occupied on the monitor and empty in the room, UNC's refusal to
erase. The pen is never found. Nothing resolves.

Transition device (show law): every unit ends with a name spoken aloud;
the next unit opens with that name ALREADY WRITTEN somewhere new.
Joint device (brief): the NAME changes state in frame at every joint.
Escalation: each unit's peak stranger than the last.

## The roster page (PROP design — layout locked)

A large handwritten shift roster on heavy paper, names in different
hands, one column of names, one of shifts:
- WREN — top entry, oldest hand, faded ink (the first name; never read
  aloud until the final cliff)
- VASQUEZ
- OKONKWO
- HALE
- KEEPER — freshly circled in wet, shining ink

## Units

### U1 — HOOK (frame 0 = doctrine beat)
Frame sentence: *the roster page, and one name freshly circled in wet
ink with no hand in the room.*
0.0s: close on the open roster — KEEPER circled, ink wet and shining,
pen lying across the page, no hand in the room. The Keeper's hand
enters and stops above the circle, not touching. Camera eases back:
the Keeper leaning over the page, UNC behind him at the frame edge.
Motion motif: the hovering, stopped hand.
LINE 7–9s — KEEPER to UNC (accusation, wants the culprit):
"The ink is wet, UNC. Who held the pen?" (9 words)
CLIFF: UNC, flat, speaks the next name: "VASQUEZ."
Unspent fact: a name was circled moments ago in an empty room.

### U2 — the name on the door
Opens: VASQUEZ already written somewhere new — chalked in a different
hand on the roster-room door's shift board. The Keeper crosses to it;
UNC stays by the roster. Behind them the room's dressing has changed
(different lamp, an extra chair) — the roster has not.
Motion motif: the crossing walk.
LINE — UNC to KEEPER (dodge/command, wants the reading to continue):
"The roster doesn't circle itself, Keeper. Someone kept you." (9)
CLIFF: the building speaker crackles; the DISPATCHER's voice speaks a
name: "OKONKWO."
Unspent fact: VASQUEZ's shift on the door board never ended.

### U3 — the name in the log
Opens: OKONKWO already written — on the wall monitor, in the
Dispatcher's typed log that is itself a roster. But this entry is not
typed: it is in handwriting, and the handwriting is the Keeper's own.
LAURA stands at the frame edge (third point of the triangle), watching
the Keeper, not the screen.
Motion motif: monitor light pulsing on faces.
LINE — LAURA to KEEPER (the avoided question, wants what he left):
"What did you leave here, Keeper?" (6)
CLIFF: the Keeper, quietly, speaks his own name: "KEEPER."
Unspent fact: the Keeper wrote a name he does not remember writing.

### U4 — the name on the chair
Opens: KEEPER already written — on the shift tag of the one chair.
The chair is empty in the room. On the wall monitor, the same chair
is OCCUPIED — a seated figure from behind, face never visible, still.
The Keeper looks room → monitor → room.
Motion motif: the slow push toward the chair.
LINE — KEEPER to UNC (demand, wants the sitter named):
"Who is sitting my shift, UNC?" (6)
CLIFF: the reading machine on the desk speaks the name in a flat
machine voice: "KEEPER."
Unspent fact: someone is keeping the Keeper's shift right now.

### U5 — the roster circles itself
Opens: close on the roster page — KEEPER circled. While Keeper and
UNC watch, a SECOND circle draws itself around the name, ink
spreading wet across the paper. No hand. The pen lies still across
the page and does not move. This contradicts UNC's U2 line, on screen.
Motion motif: liquid ink spreading.
LINE — UNC to KEEPER (warning/refusal, wants the name left alone):
"I was asked to erase it once. Never again." (9)
CLIFF: the Keeper reads the next name down and speaks it: "HALE."
Unspent fact: the roster circles itself — or something holds the pen
that isn't a hand.

### U6 — the name in his own hand (final)
Opens: HALE already written — on the last line of the page, in the
Keeper's own handwriting, dated tonight, the ink still wet. The
roster's pages lift and settle in a draft; no window is open.
Motion motif: paper moving in still air.
LINE — KEEPER to UNC (resolve/demand, wants a witness):
"Then I keep the shift. Read with me, UNC." (9)
FINAL CLIFF (bridge cliff — next episode's first frame): the camera
tilts up the page to the top entry. UNC speaks the first name for the
first time: "WREN." Final frame holds on WREN, in the oldest hand.
Unspent fact: who wrote the first name — and why it is at the top.

  yellow-gold), BONE paper, FOREST accent (Laura's coat). Six-colour
  station system; no foreign colour except the wet ink's shine.
- Light: one motivated lamp, low and warm; monitor glow as second
  source in U3/U4. Gothic, dim, still air.
- Camera: locked or near-locked; one slow move per unit at most.
  Reaction holds: faces receive news, camera stays.
- Texture finish: slight film grain, found-footage softness —
  recovered, not rendered. Never clean corporate.
- Register: deadpan. The wronger the world, the flatter the response.
