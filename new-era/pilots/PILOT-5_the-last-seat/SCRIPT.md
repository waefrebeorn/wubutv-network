# PILOT-5 — THE LAST SEAT — "The Dance the Seat Liked"

Pilot episode built on show chain S1-2 (DEVELOP), expanded to the pilot
format: 6 units × 10s = 60s / 1440f @ 24fps, one 16:9 master.
One act across the episode: the dance. One conversation: USHER ↔ DANCER,
with the seat addressed directly in U1 and U6 (doctrine §3 M4 — a look at
the seat is a speech act that must want something).

## Show bible lines (locked for this pilot)

- PALETTE: INK darkness; BONE warm stage light; VERMILION = the Dancer;
  CADMIUM brass/ticket accents. One motivated light per unit (the stage
  light); the seat end of the room stays dark.
- CAMERA: the seat's point of view — locked-off from the back of the
  room, looking at the stage. No camera move in the first 12 frames of
  any unit. The seat never culls; the world behind the act does.
- TEXTURE: warm tungsten stage light, soft theatrical haze, subtle film
  grain. Deadpan register: nobody on stage is surprised by the cull.
- THE SEAT: one old cinema seat, faded vermilion velvet, brass armrest
  caps, worn, alone. It is the camera's home and a cull-survivor prop.
  Its occupant is NEVER shown, never generated. The seat answers with a
  light, a warmth, a kept step — never a voice.

## Cast LOOK blocks (restated verbatim in every vehicle)

- THE USHER: a tall thin man in his late fifties, slicked-back
  grey-streaked dark hair, long ink-navy usher's coat with brass
  buttons, white gloves, thin deadpan face, calm unsmiling expression.
- THE DANCER: an adult woman dancer in her late twenties, dark hair in
  a tight bun, deep vermilion sleeveless stage dress with a short
  swirling skirt, soft dance shoes, athletic build, focused expression.

## The units

### U1 — THE LEAP (world: the seat room, anchor set)
Frame sentence: *the stage from the seat's view, and the dancer already
mid-leap toward the one viewer.*
- 0–3s: FROM THE SEAT. Frame 0: the Dancer mid-leap down the lens,
  already moving; near foreground, the Usher's white-gloved hands hold
  two paper tickets for a house of one seat. She lands close, huge in
  frame, and dances the first phrase of a step straight at the seat.
- 3–7s: the phrase builds — she dances FOR the seat, eyes on it.
- 7–9s LINE — USHER (voice beside the seat, hands in frame) → the seat
  (warning): "You kept her step. She dances it tonight." (8 words)
- 9–10s CLIFF: she stops mid-phrase and looks straight down the lens at
  the seat. Gaze taken.
- EVENT: a leap lands for an audience of one. MOTIF: aerial leap toward
  camera. UNSPENT: the seat kept a step — which one, and why her?

### U2 — THE FORGETTING (world culled: a ballroom of empty chairs)
Frame sentence: *a stage ringed by a hundred empty chairs, and the
dancer's step dying under her.*
- 0–3s: FROM THE SEAT. The world has culled: the stage is ringed by a
  hundred empty chairs. The Dancer is mid-step — and the step falters,
  loses its rhythm, dies under her feet.
- 3–7s: she tries again, smaller; the steps shrink and stop. She stands
  in the wreck of her own dance, looking at her feet.
- 7–9s LINE — DANCER → USHER (demand): "Teach me the step the seat
  liked, Usher." (8 words; canon chain line, trimmed to grammar)
- 9–10s CLIFF: she raises her head and looks at the seat, waiting.
  Gaze offered.
- EVENT: a dancer forgets her own step mid-dance. MOTIF: small
  faltering steps that stop. UNSPENT: she forgot it — or it was taken?

### U3 — THE REFUSAL (world culled: the station lobby, the monitor)
Frame sentence: *the station lobby with its monitor playing to nobody,
and the usher standing closer than the stage.*
- 0–3s: FROM THE SEAT. The world has culled: the lobby — a monitor on
  the wall plays the station to the empty room. Far down the room, the
  Dancer waits on a marked spot of floor. The USHER walks slowly into
  the seat's view, close to camera, large in frame, and stops.
- 3–7s: he does not turn to her. He speaks past the seat, down the room.
- 7–9s LINE — USHER → DANCER (refusal/correction): "You didn't forget
  it, Dancer. The seat kept it." (9 words; canon chain line)
- 9–10s CLIFF: the Usher turns his head and looks at the seat — down
  the lens. Gaze returned to the seat; the question is handed over.
- EVENT: the keeper refuses to teach; the seat is named as the keeper.
  MOTIF: a slow walk-in, then stillness. UNSPENT: if the seat kept it,
  will the seat give it back?

### U4 — THE ANSWER IN STEPS (world culled: a forest clearing, one shaft of light)
Frame sentence: *a forest clearing lit by one shaft of light, and the
dancer turning in a step she never learned.*
- 0–3s: FROM THE SEAT. The world has culled: a night forest clearing,
  one shaft of warm light. The Dancer is already turning — slowly, on
  the spot — in a step she does not know, her face changing as her feet
  remember for her.
- 3–7s: the turns widen; the returned step assembles itself under her,
  phrase by phrase.
- 7–9s LINE — DANCER → USHER (report): "I asked. The seat answered in
  steps, not words." (9 words; canon chain line verbatim)
- 9–10s CLIFF: mid-turn she comes around facing the seat, gaze down
  the lens, still turning as the cut lands. Gaze taken, mid-motion.
- EVENT: the seat answers — the step returns through her feet. MOTIF:
  turning on the spot. UNSPENT: the answer is in her feet now — what
  does the whole dance look like?

### U5 — THE ANSWER AT FULL SIZE (world culled: the tower top, MEGA scale)
Frame sentence: *the top of a transmission tower at night, and one
small dancer crossing a stage built for giants.*
- 0–3s: FROM THE SEAT. The world has culled to the biggest wrongness: a
  vast tower-top stage in the night sky, the city dark far below, the
  Dancer small in the huge frame — already traveling across it in the
  returned step, low and sliding.
- 3–7s: she crosses the whole stage on the move — the kept step: a low
  slide-step forward, a snap head-turn toward the seat, shoulders
  dropping — repeated, bigger, unmistakably a move that can be stolen.
- 7–9s LINE — USHER → DANCER (command): "Then dance the answer,
  Dancer. The seat is watching." (9 words; canon chain line verbatim)
- 9–10s CLIFF: she plants, crouches, and SPRINGS — the cut lands on the
  onset of the biggest leap of the piece, her eyes on the seat.
  Gaze held through the spring.
- EVENT: the answer danced at full scale, the move revealed whole.
  MOTIF: the traveling slide-step. UNSPENT: the leap she just began —
  where does it land?

### U6 — THE ENTRUSTING (world culled back: the seat room, emptied)
Frame sentence: *the seat room again, emptied, and the dancer landing
out of the dark toward the one seat.*
- 0–3s: FROM THE SEAT. The anchor set, changed: the stage is bare, one
  warm light — the room kept the act. She lands out of the dark,
  completing the leap, close to the seat.
- 3–7s: she performs the kept step once, complete, grounded and exact,
  straight at the seat: the low slide-step, the snap head-turn, the
  shoulders dropping. The whole move, once, owned.
- 7–9s LINE — DANCER → the seat (dare/entrusting): "Keep this one too,
  Seat. I'll forget it." (8 words)
- 9–10s CLIFF (bridge): she holds the gaze down the lens, breathing;
  behind her the stage lights begin, almost imperceptibly, to shift —
  the next cull coming. Nothing resolves. Final frame.
- EVENT: a new step entrusted to the seat. MOTIF: the grounded
  slide-step + the held gaze. UNSPENT (held past the episode): whether
  the seat keeps this one too — answered only by whether she remembers
  it tomorrow.

## Motif ledger (no repeats across units)
U1 aerial leap · U2 faltering steps that stop · U3 slow walk-in +
stillness · U4 turning on the spot · U5 traveling slide-step ·
U6 grounded slide-step performed once + held gaze.
(U5/U6 share the kept step by design — it is the episode's act, shown
traveling then owned; their camera events differ: crossing vs landing.
Flagged here so the gate judges it knowingly, not by accident.)
