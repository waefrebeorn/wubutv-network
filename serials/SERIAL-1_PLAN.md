## The arc — "THE UNFILED CLAIM"

A blank card arrives in the lost-and-found tray. Nobody owns it. Everybody
files.

- **S1-1 "The Tray"** — the card arrives in the lost-and-found tray. Laura
  picks it up and reads nothing; it is blank.
  *Beat:* the arrival. *Tagline:* "It has no name."
  *Cliff:* the form has a line for the finder's name. Laura leaves it blank.
- **S1-2 "The Receipt"** — UNC files a receipt form for the card.
  *Beat:* the filing. *Tagline:* "Filed."
  *Cliff:* the form asks for the owner's name. He leaves it blank.
- **S1-3 "The Second Card"** — Laura returns the card to the tray.
  *Beat:* the return. *Tagline:* "There are two now."
  *Cliff:* a second identical card is already in the tray.
- **S1-4 "The Wrong Count"** — UNC counts the cards at the tray.
  *Beat:* the count. *Tagline:* "The count is wrong."
  *Cliff:* the count is wrong — there are three. THE PIPS enters.
- **S1-5 "The Claim"** — Laura claims one of the cards.
  *Beat:* the claim. *Tagline:* "It needs a witness."
  *Cliff:* the claim form requires the other party's signature. There is no
  other party.
- **S1-6 "The Night Audit"** — UNC audits the tray at night.
  *Beat:* the audit. *Tagline:* "Four."
  *Cliff:* all cards are blank, and there is a fourth.
- **S1-7 "The Ink"** — Laura writes her name on one card.
  *Beat:* the signing. *Tagline:* "It won't take."
  *Cliff:* the ink fades before the form dries.
- **S1-8 "On File"** — UNC files the cards under "UNCLAIMED — SELF."
  *Beat:* the filing. *Tagline:* "On file."
  *Cliff (series cliff):* the file is already full of blank cards.
