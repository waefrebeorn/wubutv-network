# musicvideo/ — WuBuTV music videos ledger

Music videos are their own series: `MV-<n> "<Title>"`. They do not take bumper,
episode, or commercial numbers. First track decides MV-1's title.

Per track: `musicvideo/<slug>/` holds the Suno track + lyrics, the section map,
the timed shot list, takes refs, and `masters/` (16:9 network cut + 9:16 reel).

Method: `notes/SLOPCANNON_SLERM_STUDY.md` (slerm of the slopcannon approach).
Staff playbook: `notes/MUSIC_VIDEO_WORKFLOW.md` (intake → direction → firing →
QC → assembly → ship). Tracked under `goal_c0fd34939e31`.

Standing rules: no-dub (the Suno track IS the audio; video native silent),
`~ WuBu ~` hoodie wordmark gate on every UNC frame, no licensed material.
