- Camera & focus — dolly, dolly zoom, pan, tilt, tracking, rack focus, close-up,
  deep focus, shallow depth of field, soft focus, hard focus, blocking, action
  geography, frame within a frame, leading lines, symmetry, negative space,
  motivated camera movement, mise-en-scène.
- Light & color — chiaroscuro, hard light, high-key, low-key, soft light,
  practical light, color palette, silhouette.
- Editing & rhythm — 180-degree rule, continuity editing, eyeline match, match
  cut, montage, J-cut/L-cut, jump cut, frame rate, shutter angle, aspect ratio,
  color grading, impact and reaction, lyric video, sound bridge.
- Writing & story — beat sheet, logline, scene objective, setup and payoff,
  subtext, visual storytelling, conflict and stakes.
- Genre & style — Afrofuturism, analog horror, body horror, cosmic horror,
  cyberpunk, dark fantasy, documentary, film noir, folk horror, gothic horror,
  horror genres, magical realism, mockumentary, music video, performance video,
  post-apocalyptic, retrofuturism, science fiction, solarpunk, space opera,
  surrealism, visualizer, western.
- Animation & anime — 2.5D, anime background art, anime styles, cel, chibi,
  claymation, collage, cutout, limited animation, mecha, pixel art, rotoscoping,
  sakuga, stop motion, anticipation and follow-through.
- Prompting & planning — AI video prompting, camera movement prompts, character
  consistency, negative prompts, shot list, storyboarding.
- Audio & performance — diegetic/non-diegetic sound, how silence works, voice
  conversion, lip-sync, phonemes and visemes, pitch shifting, room tone, sound

- AI video & vision — short history of AI video, depth map, diffusion model,
  computer vision, optical flow, image-to-video, text-to-video, video-to-video,
  temporal consistency.
- Inside the Cannon — the product shelf (below).

## Inside the Cannon shelf (the product guides)

- Cannon Credits — 8 credits per finished second at Quality 2K, exact total
  shown before firing.
- Caption styles — twelve caption looks, karaoke sweep to one big word per beat.
- Chat and Studio mode — talk to the Producer, then switch to Studio for
  timeline, prompts, references, takes. Both edit the same project.
- Final assembly — chosen takes cut in timeline order with the original song,
  optional captions, MP4 out. Included.
- Firing the Cannon — every ready scene as one coordinated volley, exact credit
  total first, clips attach to the timeline.
- Make films with an agent — MCP server, published skill, HTTP API.
- Start from your own footage — upload a video, restyle scene by scene against
  references, keeping the source's cuts and timing as the structure.
- Takes and rerolls — every scene keeps take history; reroll only the scenes
  that missed; choose the take the cut uses; download any single clip.
- The Producer — project-aware creative partner: brainstorms, plans scenes
  against the song, revises ranges, explains status.
- The reference bank — one reference pool per project, a different subset in
  every scene.
- The scene timeline — the song as an editable sequence of numbered scenes,
  each with timing, words, direction, references, take history.
- Video captions — timed to every sung word, drawn in the chosen look, burned
  into the finished MP4.
