# MV-8 — Asset Bank (Stage 2: locks before a single frame is spent)

Project: MV-8 "Wipe and it's still there". NO singing character anywhere in this
build — no mouth sync, no performance windows. Sync = cuts on stabs/hits/chant
onsets + a kinetic-type layer added in post. Every vehicle in Stage 4 names
locks from THIS file; nothing may be fired that names a character, outfit,
prop, or place with no row here (Slopcannon Stage 2 gate).

## Global style lock (restated in every still and every Stage 4 prompt)

- **Style:** glossy stylised 3D cartoon, soft studio lighting, rounded forms,
  clean graphic shapes. Deadpan heroic sincerity — the tropes are played
  straight; the objects are the joke.
- **Brown rule (substitution substance):** all brown is CHOCOLATE — glossy,
  smooth, soft-serve-smooth, graphic-design brown (a paint stroke / marker
  line / chocolate smear). NEVER texture-realistic, never lumpy, never gross.
  (Squatty Potty soft-serve rule + Hitchcock chocolate-syrup rule, research §1.4.)
- **Palette:** porcelain white + warm gold light are the world; chocolate
  brown is the antagonist colour; **yellow is the SOLE accent colour**
  (rubber-glove gauntlets + kinetic type accent). No other accent colours.
- **Format:** ONE 16:9 (1280×720 plates), 24 fps master. Aspect stated in
  text on every generation call (video-api-mastery: unstated aspect follows
  the attached images).
- **Text:** ZERO readable text inside any still or clip — no letters, no
  words, no logos, no watermark. All typography is a post layer (Stage 6).
- **Licensed material:** none. Every character below is WuBu-native. The
  research names reference *recipes* (mascot design grammar, camera moves),
  never designs to copy.

## Character locks

### C1 — THE CHAMPION (hero)
A cheerful, earnest young knight-hero, stylised cartoon proportions, warm
determined smile, plays everything completely straight. Wears the TP ARMOUR
(pieces P1–P5 below, assembled one per brass stab in the arming montage,
scene H1-D). Body under the armour: simple white tunic. He is the audience's
champion against the Brown; he never winks at the camera.

### C2 — THE MOUNT ("Sir Swirl") — the flying turd, STYLISED MASCOT ONLY
A giant glossy chocolate-brown SOFT-SERVE SWIRL character: smooth swirled
ice-cream silhouette with a curled tip, two big round cartoon eyes, small
cheerful smile, tiny aviator goggles pushed up on its "forehead" and a cream
scarf that streams behind in flight (the scarf is the speed cue in every
flight shot). Cute mascot, full stop — never realistic, never textured,
never gross. Big enough to ride: the Champion sits astride the swirl's
mid-body. Eyes + one accessory + cheerfulness = the mascot recipe
(research §1.5, Mr Hankey row — recipe only, design is original).

### C3 — THE SQUIRE (sidekick / audience surrogate)
A small round toilet-paper-roll squire: a living TP roll with a cute face,
tiny arms, carrying a spare roll nearly as big as itself. His job is
REACTION — he is the one who cracks at wipe #30 while the Champion stays
deadpan (the reaction sells the loop, research §1.3). Present from the
Interlude on; rides behind the Champion on the Mount or clings to the cape.

### C4 — THE BROWN RIBBON SERPENT (the curse, personified)
The marker-wipe made flesh: a long, smooth, glossy chocolate-brown RIBBON
that draws itself through the air like a marker line being written — flat,
graphic, elegant, slightly smug. After every wipe it simply re-draws
itself. No face needed except two smug dots for eyes at the head of the
ribbon when it duels (scene FH-A). It is the devil's-Sharpie curse: elegant,
not slimy.

### C5 — THE CLOG COLOSSUS (final boss)
A colossal dam of compacted brown blockage walling off the Great Cistern —
built of smooth chocolate-brown rounded masses (soft-serve geology, never
realistic), with two heavy-lidded eyes near the top and a slow, sleepy,
immovable expression. Scale is the joke: the Champion is tiny against it.

### The grail and the curse (story objects)
- **THE GHOST WIPE — the quest grail.** One perfectly clean sheet of toilet
  paper: spotless, glowing faintly white-gold, held up like a holy relic.
  The hero is not chasing cleanliness; he is chasing the *mythical* clean
  wipe (research §1.2). The last sheet of the film must be ghost-white.
- **THE MARKER WIPE — the curse.** A fresh white sheet that comes back with
  one bold chocolate-brown marker stroke across it, every time, drawn by an
  unseen hand. The opening gag, the Squire's despair, the Serpent's calling
  card.

## Anchor-still doctrine (Stage 4 inherits this)

WuBu's generator treats an image input as a STRONG CONDITIONER, never a
frame source (video-api-mastery, measured): outputs are fresh generations —
"same scene, re-rendered", MAE never near 0, end anchors land recomposed
(MAE ~52 vs ~43 for starts). So the F-chain below is planned as
**anchor pairs**, not pixel-pinned frames: each clip is fired with
start-anchor Fi (+ end-anchor Fi+1 where the journey recipe is used),
Stage 4 QC judges identity/composition carry, and assembly cuts on the
landing. Where a frame must appear EXACTLY (the loop close F0), the still
itself is laid in during editing — the generator will not reproduce it
verbatim. Every still is generated at 16:9 and the aspect is ALSO stated
in text on every call. Read every ref back before use (a drifted ref
poisons every downstream take).
