# MV-17 "Fridge Light Mystery" — ASSET BOARD (Stage 2)

Premise (from the song): a man runs a one-man investigation into why the
refrigerator light is never on when he looks. Floor-sitting door-crack
stakeout, phone-recording experiment, creeping paranoia ("it's not just
the fridge anymore"). Event axis per the intake queue: INVESTIGATION /
STAKEOUT — camera traps, door POV, evidence rituals. One world (the night
kitchen), events change inside it.

## Motifs (return ≥3 times)
- THE DOOR CRACK: the few-centimetres gap and its light slit on the tiles.
- THE CHECK RITUAL: open slow → peer → close fast → floor-sit verdict.
- THE FLASHLIGHT: cadmium-yellow, the investigation's tool; its beam is
  the second light source.
- THE COUNT: "checked twice" — tallies/checks accumulate (post layer:
  magnet letters + tally marks on the door, Family 12 post, never in-gen).

## Section colour map
- Verses: cool/desaturated teal-blue night (5000–10000K feel), moonlight.
- Pre-chorus: same world, tighter framing, flashlight joins.
- Chorus: fridge light at full spill — brightest cold white-blue, saturated.
- Bridge: inside the fridge — the colour inverts (he is IN the light).
- Final chorus: warmest the night gets — fridge glow + first grey of dawn
  at the window edge (light-shift, not set-shift).
- Outro: near-dark, fridge closed, one magnet word left on the door (post).
