Photorealistic live action, shot on 35mm film, visible fine grain. The
man is a Taiwanese-American man in his early sixties, born in Taiwan,
ethnically Taiwanese of Han Chinese heritage; his features present
plainly as East Asian. His skin is a warm medium tone with smooth even
texture and natural age lines. His eyes are almond-shaped and dark brown

rectangular black metal eyeglasses with straight temples, sitting
level, always the same glasses.

His build is broad and powerful — the BUFF PASS read for this build:
wide shoulders, a thick chest, a commanding upright carriage, straight
posture; the exaggeration lives in the body's breadth and presence,
never in the face, which stays faithful. He wears his signature black
leather jacket over a black crew-neck t-shirt, with dark trousers. The
black leather jacket is his silhouette: no scene dresses him in
anything else. There is NO microphone on him — no lapel mic, no clip,
no device at his collar — in any frame, ever (the source photo carries
a lapel mic; it is a defect of the photo, not a trait of the man, and

Overall feel: the boss who left and came back — unbothered, vindicated,
operating at full size.
