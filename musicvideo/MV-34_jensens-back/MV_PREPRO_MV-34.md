# MV_PREPRO_MV-34 — "Jensen's Back" (prepro, 2026-11-24)

**Plan shape: THE FULL RESTAGE.** The news-cycle story in plot order —
the dark office, the verdict, the fall, the apology, the eleven days,
the call, the walk back in, the reversal, the chair again — every
section its own world, no section revisiting another's location,
staging, cast or event. The shape this cycle's two viral restages and
the house's own best builds share; locked by the MV-33 precedent and
not re-argued here.

**⚠ The plan covers the PERFORMED track, not the full sheet.** Dual-ASR
measurement (LYRIC_TIMELINE.md) proves the render performs 25 of 60
sheet lines: hook intro + Verse 1 + Pre-Chorus + Chorus + closing echo.
Verse 2, Pre-Chorus 2, Chorus 2, Breakdown, Final Chorus and Outro are
not sung and are not planned. Every scene below maps to performed
lines only; spans are the measured ASR spans (planning values —
assembly re-anchors by forced alignment, ribbon law).

## 1. World, cast, motif

- **World:** one company, one day that lasts eleven days — the office
  tower: dark office, boardroom, trading floor, grey waiting room,
  lobby doors, press wall. Brightness is the plot: the film starts in
  a lights-off office and ends in the same office at full daylight.
- **Cast:** the Return (Huang likeness, shared-cast pack) sings EVERY
  sung line — nobody else sings, ever. Everyone else is staff, board,
  traders, press: mouths closed, they watch, bow, ring phones, cheer.
- **Story spine (intake, trimmed to the performed lines):** the empty
  office, lights off → the board's verdict → the ticker falling, phones
  ringing → the apology → eleven days of waiting → the call comes →
  the office stands → the doors open, he walks back in → the ticker
  reverses, +30% → the chair again, in daylight.
- **Motif axis:** THE DOOR AND THE CHAIR — he is framed by doorways
  (out, then in) and the desk chair is empty, then waited beside, then
  sat in. One motif, stated at S01 (empty chair), varied at S06 (the
  chair he waits in is a plain chair, not the desk chair), resolved at
  S10 (the desk chair, occupied, daylight). THE RETURN WALK (S08) is
  the motif's kinetic form and the chorus's event.
- **Colour map by section:** Intro = blue-black dark → flash white ·
  Verse 1 = cold slate (boardroom) → red (the fall) → warm bone (the
  apology) → flat grey (the wait) · Pre-Chorus = warm white/green ·
  Chorus = glass daylight (the walk) → saturated green (the reversal) ·
  Tail = warm gold daylight. The arc is the ouster told in light:
  dark → red → grey → green → gold.

## 6. Tension / release per section

- **Intro:** held breath (dark, still) → strobe release (the press
  storm) — the return announced before it's explained.
- **Verse 1:** a five-beat descent — verdict (cold), fall (red),
  apology (soft), wait (grey, the still point) — release valves are
  L4's first flare of returning light and L8's ringing phone.
- **Pre-Chorus:** the room stands — tension converts to motion.
- **Chorus:** full release, twice: the walk (kinetic) and the
  reversal (spectacle). Peak loudness (t=48) sits inside S09's first
  line, where the green wall is brightest.
- **Tail:** the settle — one soft line in the chair, in daylight.
  The film exhales in the room it started in.
