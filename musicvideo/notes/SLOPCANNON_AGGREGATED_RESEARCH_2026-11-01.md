## 2. What Slopcannon actually is (verified core, hype stripped)
- A song-to-video *workstation*, not a one-prompt generator. Song + named reference images + idea in; numbered, timed scene plan out; scenes fired as one volley with the exact credit total shown first; bad scenes rerolled singly with full take history; chosen takes cut to the *original song* with optional word-timed captions into an MP4 master.
- Scene timeline = the heart: each scene carries its own timing, direction, references subset, words, take history, and its own audio excerpt "which is what the Cannon uses when it renders that scene." One clear action, one place, one camera move per scene ("if a scene's direction needs the word 'then' more than once, it is probably two scenes"). Cuts planned to land on the music; a performance scene "knows which line is being sung"; mouth movement toggleable per scene.
- Reference bank = the consistency mechanism: one reusable pool per project (people, outfits, props, places); each scene gets only the subset it needs; identity described separately from outfit and mood; story changes become *variants* assigned only to later scenes, never edits to the original.
- Assembly rule (the key design decision): "The original song, not the audio of any clip, becomes the soundtrack." Generated clip audio is dropped. Captions are built from word timing in post (12 looks; 9:16 safe band 14–65% of frame height, clear of side rails); captions drawn on the user's device, server assembly makes the film without captions.
- Rights gate: "You need the rights or permission for every face, logo and image you upload" — confirmed once per project; "AI generation does not create those rights." Human confirmation remains required for content rights and paid generation actions.
- Product timeline [verified live]: MCP connection for Claude/Codex/Grok 24 Sep 2026; own-footage restyle mode 27 Sep 2026; "Inside the Cannon" guide push 29–30 Sep 2026; Sloptoberfest Oct 2026. Legacy Studio link still on the homepage — the chat-first Producer workflow evidently replaced an earlier Studio; cutover date not published.
- Creator: Bone / @boneGPT is presented in secondary coverage as founder, and Sloptoberfest names him funder ($9,000) and council head. No legal name, biography, interview, or launch date was found in any public source searched — [unknown], not a gap to fill. Do not conflate three "Muse"s: Meta Muse Wonder (model family, released 12 Oct 2026); muse.slop.cc (Slopcannon's own earlier surface); unrelated Muse course/DAO/Adobe products.

## 4. What survives all three devil's-advocate rounds (the rebuild's load-bearing walls)
1. Audio-first timing grid, computed from the track — never guessed by a language model. BPM/downbeat/section analysis + forced word alignment; tempo-map (not single BPM) for tempo changes; boundaries snapped to beats; section labels are heuristic, boundaries are the reliable part.
2. Timed scene plan from lyrics + sections as the planning unit (scene count follows the song — typically 12–20 for 3 minutes, not a fixed 8; the "8 scenes" pattern is a community template, not a product rule).
3. Reference *selection* per scene (locked pool, subset only) — mitigates drift, does not solve it. Cross-shot consistency still degrades with time since last appearance; past ~16 s models lose the character. Falsifier: blind identity score ≥95% across scenes 1/5/10 with and without a bank.
4. Non-destructive, capped, per-scene rerolls with a named best take — beats whole-video regeneration on cost and learning. One variable per reroll; exact cost shown before firing; failed renders not counted.
5. Master-audio assembly with the user's track untouched — video conformed to audio, never vice versa; assembly itself free to repeat.
6. Hook + motif + escalation in the plan itself (the banger test): hook in the first 0.5–3 s; one repeatable motif returning ≥3 times; chorus escalation with the peak saved for the *last* chorus; bridge contrast; final frame rhyming with the first. A video passing all technical gates but failing this goes back to *plan*, not to reroll lottery.
- KILLED in Round 3: "cut on every beat" (clinical; Murch's Rule of Six — emotion first, rhythm ~1/10); "8 scenes for every song"; "tag a famous founder for distribution"; "reference bank = solved consistency"; "views = quality"; "parody label = legal safety"; "captions timed to words = lip-sync proof" (category error — Slopcannon's wiki promises captions, not lip-sync).
- Partially surviving: exact-cost-before-fire (helps the volley, not the project total — needs a project budget + hard stop); "agent runs the whole workflow" (yes, only with caps, take history, and human-chosen takes); Sloptoberfest proves demand (partially — payout/judging transparency still open, winners due 7 Nov 2026).

## 5. Performance-window lipsync (user-supplied sauce, audited against WuBu's tools)
The sauce as supplied (boneGPT posts + slop.cc wiki): a line's window = first word to last word; during it the singer is on screen, facing camera, MCU or closer, mouth fully visible and unobstructed; entrances, reveals, pull-backs, big action go BEFORE the first word or AFTER the last word, never stacked on the vocal; loud SFX/one-shots stay out of the window or quieter than the vocal; delivery clear and articulated, never whispered/mumbled; 1–2 singers max in close-up; restrained head movement, steady camera; facing + motion in parentheses after the name; auto lyrics corrected, never trusted; review checks a clear consonant near start and end; distinguish fixed offset vs growing drift vs wrong performance; phonemes ≠ visemes.
- Audit honesty (from the MV-2 audit + lip-sync KB note): the four wiki pages (lip-sync, phonemes-and-visemes, ai-video-prompting, character-consistency) confirm the sauce's core in SOFTER form. "Performance window non-negotiable," the SFX-steals-sync mechanism, and "1–2 singers max" are NOT literally in those pages — they rest on bone's posts/direction notes as supplied.
- KILLED on transfer to WuBu's tools: "model lip-syncs to supplied audio chunk" (WuBu's media tool passes NO audio — text-to-video only); "correct auto lyrics / lipSyncMode: required" (no such parameter exists here; transferable remainder = the lyric timeline itself must be true); "loud SFX steals mouth sync" as a generation claim (downgraded to an edit note — generated audio is dropped anyway).
- DOWNGRADED: "entire window, non-negotiable" → a SUSTAINED passage judged by majority of samples (the model does not hold framing 10 s; S13 opened close-up then went back-view); "1–2 singers max" → readability, not a sync law; "check a clear consonant" → mouth-open + facing (frames alone cannot score consonants).
- SURVIVING RULES (9, now the skill's hard gate — see references/performance-window.md): (1) every vocal scene contains a sustained to-camera vocal passage (framing IS the sync here; no audio-conditioned mouth exists to fall back on); (2) order of events — choreography/gesture/reveal/pull-back BEFORE first word or AFTER last word, never stacked (T2V models front-load the first action described; MV-2 S18 proves it works, S07 t1 proves the failure); (3) articulated open-mouth delivery wording, never whisper/mumble, never deadpan closed mouth across a line; (4) restrained head movement + steady camera during the vocal; facing + motion in parentheses after the name; (5) declare who visibly performs; non-vocal scenes are declared non-vocal (SINGING vs CUTAWAY at plan time); (6) diagnose before rerolling — facing fail vs delivery fail vs staging fail; mouth-open near start and end; (7) phonemes ≠ visemes — judge held vowels and line-start/end closures, never letter-by-letter; (8) profile/hands/blur/fast head turns hurt REVIEW — use intentionally, never by accident; (9) one singer, one readable event per shot (framing real estate).
- Lip-sync KB note's honest core (survives): prompt-led silent generation can never truly lip-sync. It can only (a) make the mouth easy to read, (b) keep the face stable enough that a later repaint pass has clean material, (c) spend face time where sync matters and cut away where it doesn't. Real sync needs audio-driven generation or a mouth-repaint pass (MuseTalk best future fit — real-time, sharp mouth, MIT/commercial OK, 256px face region, flat-cartoon faces UNTESTED; LatentSync second choice for hero close-ups, 18 GB VRAM; Wav2Lip draft only, blurry + non-commercial licence). The repaint test is NOT user-approved (needs GPU or cloud spend) — do not build it without approval.
- Coverage budget (adopt now, free): spend face time on chorus hooks, held notes, emotional close-ups; cutaways (hands, ticket, lights, wide plaza, silhouette) for fast runs, dense choruses, hard lyrics. MV-2 already did this by accident (S26 silhouette, S27 tear) — now deliberate: every prepro scene marked SINGING or CUTAWAY; never promise sync on a CUTAWAY.
- MV-2 proof the discipline works: volley 1 of the redo (S05/S07/S09/S12/S13/S20, performance-window staging) QC'd 5 KEEP / 1 REJECT — and the reject (S12 t2) failed on TEXT (gantry "TOLL" sign), not facing. Facing failures that killed five t1 takes are fixed by staging; the text gate remains a separate, independent gate.
- Honest limit (keep stating at delivery): this maximizes the PLAUSIBILITY of mouth motion against the Suno master; it does not produce phoneme-accurate lipsync. This pipeline has no audio-conditioned generation and must not claim otherwise.

## 6. Failure-mode catalog (with WuBu countermeasure)
- F1 Character drift across scenes → locked reference bank (WuBu: vehicle identity + outfit + pose blocks verbatim wherever they repeat); per-scene subset only; QC stills at first/mid/last appearance.
- F2 Lip-sync failure on performance close-ups → declare SINGING vs CUTAWAY per scene; performance scenes get performance-window staging + majority-sample QC; otherwise avoid singing close-ups (wide/abstract/montage); never promise lip-sync from captions.
- F3 Timing drift vs beat/section → LYRIC_TIMELINE.md measured from the delivered track (frame-pinned line times) BEFORE planning; scene boundaries on section/line boundaries; cuts on beats/drops; never a uniform 10 s grid (MV-2 v1's root failure: verse lines ~10 s, chorus lines ~5 s, outro landed ~10 s early).
- F4 Generic-looking output → one distinctive motif + one unusual camera/palette choice locked in the plan; motif returns ≥3 times; forbid the model's default palette/wardrobe.
- F5 Cost/credit burn on rerolls → WuBu analogue: 6-video cap per user message; per-scene reroll cap (two denied rerolls → retire + fresh staging from scratch); one named axis per reroll; exact volley contents stated before firing.
- F6 Unwanted on-screen text → anti-text stack named in EVERY vehicle (incl. no single glyphs/numerals anywhere, no sign panels, blank tickets); 2s/5s/8s frame QC; any legible text = automatic reroll reason; captions only as a separate post layer, never in generated frames.
- F7 Rights → original characters only unless user directs otherwise; no licensed characters/plots/dialogue/logos; user attests track rights at intake; parody label is not a licence.
- F8 No clear chorus / dense lyrics / tempo change / long instrumental → energy-curve scenes when sections are weak; instrumental scenes carry visual story (motif development, location change); dense-lyric scenes use wider shots + sparse captions, not lip-sync.
- F9 Review fatigue on a full song → triage: QC scores takes, user-facing review surfaces worst scenes + first/chorus/last; auto-advance the rest with best takes logged.
- F10 Agent runaway (slop cannon with a wallet) → hard caps (6 videos/message, 2 denied rerolls/scene); every volley stated before firing; no silent plan changes; uncertainty labelled [confirmed]/[inferred]/[assumed].
- F11 Audio damage → master-audio rule: user's track muxed untouched; video conformed to audio; final QC checks full-length alignment, not just the first 30 s.
- F12 Platform/scam adjacency → never use Slopcannon's name, cannon branding, or token tickers; verify any third-party tool on its own domain before paying.

## 7. The rebuilt WuBu pipeline (what the skill now enforces)
1. INTAKE: track + verbatim lyrics + chat direction; copy + md5 + log; ffprobe duration; rights check. Never send a track back for a timing miss.
2. TIMELINE FIRST: measure the true lyric timeline from the delivered track (lyric-video frames / forced alignment) into LYRIC_TIMELINE.md — section boundaries + per-line start/end times, with confidence notes. The plan is built FROM this file. A uniform grid is a planning bug, not a default.
3. PLAN: MV_PREPRO from the timeline — one scene per section/line beat in song order, each with song in/out, lines covered, SINGING or CUTAWAY tag, hook/motif/escalation, one beat, one camera move. Short lines (~2–3 s) are pickups inside longer sections — never stretched to a full 10 s scene. Long holds get explicit coverage decisions.
4. VEHICLES: identity + outfit + pose blocks verbatim; performance-window staging for SINGING scenes (vehicle names its line; singer (facing camera; static, MCU-or-closer) during the line; all gesture/camera movement strictly before first word or after last word; open articulated mouth shapes, relaxed jaw on held notes); shared anti-text stack; CUTAWAY scenes carry no vocal burden.
5. FIRE: at most 6 videos per user message (incl. delegated agents); vehicle fired verbatim; every take kept with md5; one named axis per reroll; retire after two denied rerolls → fresh staging from scratch.
6. QC: bumper gates (wordmark where UNC appears, zero text, no licensed material) + performance-window gate for SINGING scenes (2s/5s/8s majority: ≥2/3 face-to-camera with mouth visible AND ≥1 open-mouth vocal passage; diagnose facing vs delivery vs staging before rerolling; widen the sample when reads disagree; a still open mouth is checked against its speaking beat before being called a break).
7. ASSEMBLY (window-first, section-timing — the cheap peak-tween stage is DEAD and removed): QC-passed takes only, trimmed to their true lyric windows with uniform fit per scene, cuts on line/section boundaries, NO peak warp, NO time-stretch of the song; Suno track sole master audio; captions in post as a subtitle track; ~ WuBu ~ corner bug; 16:9 master + 9:16 reframe from the same cut; ffprobe + md5 verification; CUTLIST.md + MUSIC_VIDEO_LEDGER.md.
8. DELIVERY: masters as chat attachments (never bare links); announce to #wubu-mv-6a90e1; disclose flags + the honest lipsync limit in the delivery message.
9. FUTURE (needs user approval, not built): MuseTalk/LatentSync repaint test on one flat-cartoon take (does it track a cartoon face?); audio-driven generation if the media tool ever accepts audio input (Meyer black-frame method). Until then, prompt-steering + coverage budget is the whole sync story.
