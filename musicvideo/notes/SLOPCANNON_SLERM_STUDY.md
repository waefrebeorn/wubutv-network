## What SlopCannon actually does (verified from slop.cc + slop.cc/faq, 2026-10-19)

An AI music-video workstation, five stages — a whole project, not a prompt box:

1. **Load it** — the song (audio file), the people/visual references, the idea. One audio file minimum, at least one clear reference image; rights to the files confirmed.
2. **Direct it** — a Producer (project-aware creative assistant) turns the materials into a **timed scene plan**: the song is analyzed for timing/structure/words; every scene gets a number, real timing, direction, and assigned references. The story follows the song.
3. **Fire it** — the exact credit total is reviewed BEFORE firing; the Cannon launches every ready scene as one coordinated volley; each scene keeps its own timing, prompt, audio excerpt, and references; finished clips attach to the timeline automatically.
4. **Keep the hits** — watch the timeline; change and reroll ONLY the scenes that missed; earlier takes stay available (every scene has its own take history). Failed renders release their hold; nothing fires without credits.
5. **Finish it** — active takes are trimmed into timeline order **with the original song**, optional captions added in post (never drawn into the AI footage), MP4 master downloaded.

Supporting principles: the song is the timing authority (story, performance, cuts, captions all follow it); reference bank with per-scene subsets (variants for costume/state changes); projects survive refreshes and resume without paying twice.

## What we reject

- Their UI, their credit system, their branding — we build our own project structure in the repo (see MUSIC_VIDEO_WORKFLOW.md). No copying of their Director's Wiki content or their prompt formats.
- Lip-synced lyrics inside generated footage — slopcannon disables mouth movement scene by scene; we go further: generated mouths never sing the lyrics (our voice rules), captions carry the words in post.
- Engagement-bait CTA grammar — never our voice, per the @clipcraft7 ruling.
