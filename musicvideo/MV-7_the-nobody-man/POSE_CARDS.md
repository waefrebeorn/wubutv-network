# MV-7 "I'm The Nobody Man" — POSE CARDS

One card per signature pose. SFW throughout. Poses are selected from
this file per scene; story changes become variants for later scenes
only, never edits to the original card.

## PC-1 — Desk lean-in (the singing pose)
Medium close-up, S1 seated at his desk facing camera, leaning slightly
in toward the monitor glow, mouth clearly visible and unobstructed,
tired busted grin between phrases, candle flame visible at frame edge
on the desk. Static camera. THE default singing pose for verses and
pre-choruses.

## PC-2 — The grin at the glow
Close-up, S1 looking straight at camera with the full tired busted
grin, monitor glow washing one side of his face, darkness behind him.
Hook/chorus title-line pose.

## PC-3 — Hand near the flame
Medium close-up, S1 at the desk, one hand resting near the small
candle, flame steady between his fingers' shadow and the desk edge,
singing toward camera. Verse 2 "small flame" pose.

## PC-4 — Pinning the receipt (bridge, non-singing detail)
Close detail: a hand pins a blank paper receipt onto a cork strip
already crowded with blank slips. No face, no singing. Bridge montage
only.

## PC-5 — Hands posting (bridge, non-singing detail)
Close detail: hands typing on a keyboard in the dark, phone glowing
face-up beside them, monitor glow from above frame. No face, no
singing. Bridge montage only.

## PC-6 — The kingdom (final chorus peak)
Medium-wide, S1 at his desk turned to camera, the whole wall behind
him lit with hundreds of tiny glowing screens like sparks, one steady
light among them slightly brighter, candle still burning on the desk,
his grin quiet and certain. Visual peak — Final Chorus only.

## PC-7 — Final frame rhyme
Same framing as the opening frame (PC-1 pulled to the opening wide):
man at desk, candle + phone glow in the dark — now with the spark wall
lit behind him. Held, mouth closed, gentle stillness. Last shot only.
