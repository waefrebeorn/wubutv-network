# MV-4 POSE_CARDS.md — signature poses (SFW only)

Every scene in MV_PREPRO_MV-4.md is assigned to exactly one card plus a
one-line variation. Vehicles carry [identity block] + [outfit block] +
[pose block] in that order, verbatim wherever they repeat. One character
per card; a card never introduces a second person.

Identity block (NOVA, verbatim): "NOVA, a tall cartoon space-crooner
host, indigo-violet swept-back pompadour hair, warm open smile,
cobalt-blue retro-future uniform jacket with cadmium-gold trim,
bone-white shirt, white gloves, chrome microphone."
Identity block (BOLT, verbatim): "BOLT, a squat round-bodied retro
robot, bone-white shell with cobalt panels, rectangular head with one
horizontal visor eye strip, spring neck, roller feet."
Identity block (ZIB, verbatim): "ZIB, a tall thin forest-green alien
with three round eyes in a row, long noodle arms, bone-white party
tunic."

## P-CROON — NOVA singing to camera (front)
- Viewpoint: front, medium close-up.
- Stance: feet planted shoulder-width, weight even, knees soft.
- Torso: upright, chest open to camera, slight lean-in.
- Arms/hands: one gloved hand on the chrome mic stand, other hand open
  at chest height.
- Head/gaze: chin level, eyes to camera, warm open smile that becomes
  the singing mouth.
- Depth: near — mic stand; mid — NOVA; far — spaceport terminal lights,
  soft.
- Motion: start at rest, lean in slightly before the line; hold the
  lean through the line; mouth closes after the last word, stable pose.

## P-POINT — NOVA pointing at the viewer (front)
- Viewpoint: front, medium close-up.
- Stance: feet planted, weight forward on the front foot.
- Torso: leaning toward camera, chest open.
- Arms/hands: right arm extended straight at the viewer, index finger
  pointing; left hand open at the hip.
- Head/gaze: chin level, eyes locked to camera, big inviting smile.
- Depth: near — pointing hand, slightly large in frame; mid — NOVA;
  far — dance floor crowd, soft.
- Motion: arm rises before the line, points through the line, lowers
  after the last word into an open-palm invitation.

## P-LAUNCH — NOVA launching the paper ship (three-quarter)
- Viewpoint: three-quarter, medium shot.
- Stance: feet in a lunge, weight shifting forward.
- Torso: turned three-quarter, chest toward the throw direction.
- Arms/hands: right arm cocked back holding the folded paper ship,
  then thrown forward in a smooth underhand launch; left arm
  counterbalances.
- Head/gaze: eyes following the paper ship, smile widening.
- Depth: near — launching hand and paper ship; mid — NOVA; far — the
  dome and the ringed planet.
- Motion: cock back before the line, launch on the beat after the line,
  arm follows through and holds.

## P-DANCE — NOVA dancing with the crew (front, wide)
- Viewpoint: front, medium-wide shot.
- Stance: feet in a dance step, knees bent, weight shifting side to
  side.
- Torso: loose, shoulders rolling with the beat.
- Arms/hands: arms swinging in a simple two-step, hands open and
  relaxed.
- Head/gaze: chin up, eyes to camera, joyful grin.
- Depth: near — dance floor edge; mid — NOVA center; far — BOLT and
  ZIB dancing, crowd behind.
- Motion: continuous two-step through the shot, one clear step change
  only.

## P-HOLD — NOVA farewell, arms open (front)
- Viewpoint: front, medium shot, slight low angle.
- Stance: feet planted wide, weight even, grounded.
- Torso: upright, chest open, arms spread wide.
- Arms/hands: both arms open to the sides, palms up, welcoming and
  letting go at once.
- Head/gaze: chin level, eyes to camera, gentle smile.
- Depth: near — empty space at the frame edges; mid — NOVA; far —
  the ship rising past the dome.
- Motion: arms rise slowly after the line into the open hold and stay.

## P-BOLT — BOLT robot dance (front)
- Viewpoint: front, medium shot.
- Stance: roller feet in a tap step, body bobbing on the spring neck.
- Torso: upright, shell catching the light.
- Arms/hands: short arms swinging, claws open in rhythm.
- Head/gaze: visor eye strip bright, head bobbing on the spring.
- Depth: near — dance floor; mid — BOLT; far — crowd and lights.
- Motion: bob and tap through the shot, one spin only, ending facing
  camera.

## P-ZIB — ZIB alien dance (front)
- Viewpoint: front, medium shot.
- Stance: long legs in a loose sway, weight drifting side to side.
- Torso: swaying, shoulders loose.
- Arms/hands: noodle arms waving overhead in slow arcs.
- Head/gaze: three eyes wide and happy, chin up.
- Depth: near — dance floor; mid — ZIB; far — crowd and lights.
- Motion: sway and wave through the shot, arms rising on the beat.
