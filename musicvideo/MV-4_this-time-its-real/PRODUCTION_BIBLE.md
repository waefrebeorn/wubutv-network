# MV-4 Production Bible — "This Time It's Real (Futurama is cancelled)"

User direction (this track only): recreate the Futurama stuff — dancing,
celebrating, spaceships, a music video banger. HARD GATE: original
WuBuTV-native characters and designs only. No Fry/Leela/Bender/Farnsworth
likenesses, no show logos, no copied designs. The song's own lyrics name
Futurama and quote "Good news, everyone" — those are the user's sung words
and stay; the CHARACTERS on screen are original.

Standing rules: ONE 16:9 master only. The song is tempo gospel — the song's
tempo and word times are the fixed grid; the video warps to the song, never
the reverse. Always fire, never ask. Volley cap 6 videos per user message.

## World
A retro-future cartoon spaceport in the 31st-century SPIRIT: neon terminal,
glass dome dance floor, a big friendly delivery spaceship in the hangar,
observation deck with a ringed planet outside. Flat cartoon, bold ink
outlines, flat fills, WuBuTV palette only: COBALT #1B3FD1, VERMILION
#E23A1B, CADMIUM #FFC61E, BONE #F4EEDC, INK #141414, FOREST #1E4D33.
Two primaries max per frame plus bone and ink. No on-screen text in
generated footage, ever; captions live in post.

## Cast (immutable speaker IDs, never remapped)

- **NOVA (S1) — LEAD, the space-crooner/host.** A tall cartoon humanoid
  variety-show host. Indigo-violet swept-back pompadour hair (NOT orange,
  NOT a Fry likeness), warm open smile, cobalt-blue retro-future uniform
  jacket with cadmium-gold trim and a bone-white shirt, white gloves,
  chrome microphone on a stand. Identity lock, verbatim in every vehicle:
  "NOVA, a tall cartoon space-crooner host, indigo-violet swept-back
  pompadour hair, warm open smile, cobalt-blue retro-future uniform
  jacket with cadmium-gold trim, bone-white shirt, white gloves, chrome
  microphone." NOVA sings every vocal line unless the plan says CUTAWAY.
- **BOLT (S2) — robot dancer.** A squat round-bodied retro robot, bone-white
  shell with cobalt panels, rectangular head with one horizontal visor eye
  strip, spring neck, roller feet. No cigar, no antenna ball, no Bender
  silhouette. Dances, never sings. Mouth: a thin speaker grille that stays
  closed and still.
- **ZIB (S3) — alien dancer.** A tall thin forest-green alien with three
  round eyes in a row, long noodle arms, bone-white party tunic. Dances,
  never sings. Mouth stays closed and still.
- **CROWD (S4) — spaceport travellers.** Background dancers, simple flat
  cartoon travellers in bone/cobalt/vermilion. Mouths closed and still,
  always.

## Motif (returns 3+ times, one sentence)
**THE PAPER SHIP**: NOVA folds a little paper spaceship, launches it at
the hook; it returns in every chorus; in the final chorus the paper ship
becomes the real ship lifting off. First frame: paper ship in NOVA's hand.
Final frame: the real ship rising past the dome, paper ship taped to the
window — the rhyme.

## Camera grammar
SINGING: singer facing camera, static, medium close-up or closer during
the line; all gesture and camera movement strictly before the first word
or after the last word. CUTAWAY: wide, profile, or reaction — mouth
closed or turned away, never promised sync. One singer, one readable
event per shot. No text, no logos, no extra limbs, no morphing.

## Forbidden (every vehicle)
No licensed characters, plots, dialogue, logos, or real-person likeness.
No on-screen text, glyphs, numerals, sign panels, or rectangular
border/box/frame/emblem/patch enclosing anything. No whispering or
mumbling in singing prompts. One primary singer per prompt; every other
visible character's mouth remains closed and still, restated twice.
