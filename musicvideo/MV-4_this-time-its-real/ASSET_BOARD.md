# MV-4 ASSET_BOARD.md — reference board (mechanics only, SLERM-transformed)

Source lyrics: lyrics.txt, sections per LYRIC_TIMELINE.md. Search
context per section: concrete nouns (TV, door, file, ship, couch,
hallway, paper), verbs (lean in, close the door, seal it, binge, quote,
rise again), roles (the announcer, the keeper of the file, the crowd
that stayed). Motif candidate, repeated across the song: THE PAPER
SHIP (folded, launched, flown) — outranks one-off images.

Pop-culture mechanics used (from web research this run):
- Dance-party video conventions (Prezi/Slideshare codes-and-conventions
  research): bright colours, lively party/club location, fast cuts on
  the beat, MCU + long-shot mix, establishing shot first, lighting that
  moves with the beat. Taken as CONVENTIONS, not a look to copy.
- Graphic match cut (zooop.ai / screenweaver.ai match-cut research):
  a shared shape in the same screen position carries the cut across a
  jump in time or scale. 2001's bone-to-spacecraft is the famous
  precedent — the MECHANIC (small thing becomes big thing across one
  cut), not the film. WuBu translation: paper ship in hand → real ship
  in the sky, same screen position, same drift direction.
- Slow-motion launch appreciation (NASA SpaceFlight launch footage
  discussion): a launch reads as power when the camera holds on it and
  the music carries the pace — WuBu translation: the ship-rise is a
  held wide, never a fast cut.

Devil's-advocate passes: PASS 2 killed the licensed-character rows
(Fry/Leela/Bender likenesses, Planet Express ship, Farnsworth
impersonation of "Good news, everyone") and the Suno cover-art door as
a set. PASS 3 defended the paper-ship motif (attack said "too cute";
defense: it is the only object that can be folded, launched, and
flown — it carries the whole arc). PASS 4 tested survivors against
the worst case for this track (dense repeated choruses, slow final
lines, 17s soft outro): crowd-dance-every-chorus was DEMOTED (same
shot type three times); the couch wide set was DEMOTED to a tight
corner so NOVA stays MCU; the 17s outro got an explicit two-take
coverage decision instead of a generation pull.
