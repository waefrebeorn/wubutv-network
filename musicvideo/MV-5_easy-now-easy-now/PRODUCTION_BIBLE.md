# MV-5 Production Bible — "Easy now, easy now (THE VOICE)"

User direction (this track only): a black cat singing to a girl on a bicycle,
having a boardwalk-on-a-beach moment — mostly like those anime intros with
walking montages, except bicycle riding across the beach. Anime-intro
montage feel, riding/walking interchange between the two. The song OPENS
with a chorus intro (final-chorus closing block: "Easy now, easy now / We
ride, we ride / Easy now, easy now / Till the morning comes") before Verse 1
— confirmed from the Suno lyric video frames and the audio transcription.

Standing rules: ONE 16:9 master only. The song is tempo gospel — the song's
tempo and word times are the fixed grid; the video warps to the song, never
the reverse. Always fire, never ask. Volley cap 6 videos per volley, a
batching limit, never a user-interaction gate. Generated clip audio is
always dropped; track.mp3 (md5 0c1bcafa568547e2d4fa36e133ad3626) is the sole
master audio.

## World
A sunny beach boardwalk in a soft anime style: wooden planks, salt air,
the pier, a vendor corner, steps down to the sand, kids with a boombox,
golden-hour sun by the final chorus. Flat anime cel style, clean ink
outlines, soft pastel sky (peach to teal), warm sunlight. No on-screen
text in generated footage, ever; captions live in post.

  cat with blue eyes, a red collar with a small round YELLOW BELL. Rides

  and the sand. Identity lock, verbatim in every vehicle: "a black cat

- **BACKGROUND (S3) — boardwalk people.** Vendor at the corner, kids with

## Vocal ownership (measured from the audio, LYRIC_TIMELINE.md)
Every scene's vehicle names exactly ONE primary singer — CAT or GIRL —
per the per-line ownership table in LYRIC_TIMELINE.md. Never ask one
prompt to switch singers mid-clip. The other character's mouth stays
explicitly closed (positive phrasing, restated twice in every vehicle).

## Motif (returns 3+ times, one sentence)
**THE YELLOW BELL**: the small yellow bell on the cat's red collar —
it rings (visible glint / close-up) at the hook, returns at every chorus,
and closes the video in the final frame. First frame: the bell glinting
in the basket as the cat sings the opening hook. Final frame: the bell
glinting again as the girl and the cat hold the sun — the rhyme.
Secondary recurring home base: **THE BASKET** — the cat's place on the
bike; every riding scene starts or ends with the cat settled in it.

## Camera grammar
SINGING: singer facing camera, static, medium close-up or closer during
the line; all gesture and camera movement strictly before the first word
or after the last word. Riding shots are CUTAWAY or montage: the bike
moves, the singer's face is in profile or wide, mouth closed or turned
away — never promised sync while riding. One singer, one readable event
per shot. No text, no logos, no extra limbs, no morphing.

## Forbidden (every vehicle)
No licensed characters, plots, dialogue, logos, or real-person likeness.
No on-screen text, glyphs, numerals, sign panels, or rectangular
border/box/frame/emblem/patch enclosing anything. No whispering or
mumbling in singing prompts. One primary singer per prompt; every other
visible character's mouth remains closed and still, restated twice.
SFW only. The sheet's "blue shirt, red bicycle" is superseded by the
user's visual direction: red jacket, blue bicycle — the picture follows
the direction, the sung words stay as performed.
