choruses are layered DUETS in the audio.

## Staging law from this map
- Solo line → singer framed singing (moving, gaze on the other /
  the road / the lyric's object); the non-singer is out of frame or
  visibly mouth-closed in a motivated action.
- Split line (→) → cut or in-shot handoff at the split point.
- Duet/chorus → wide riding two-shots; both mouths legitimately
  move; no solo close-up claims a duet line.
- No-evidence lines (L89, L90) → montage covers, no mouth claims.
