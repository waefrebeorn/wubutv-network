# MV-5 Pose Cards

One card per signature pose; every scene in MV_PREPRO_MV-5.md is assigned
to exactly one card plus a one-line variation. SFW only. Identity and
outfit blocks come from PRODUCTION_BIBLE.md verbatim; only the pose block
changes, and only to a card from this file.

## P-BASKET-SING (front, cat in basket, singing)
1. Name: P-BASKET-SING. Viewpoint: front, slightly low.
2. Stance: the cat sits upright in the front basket of the parked blue
   bicycle, front paws resting on the basket rim, tail curled around its
   paws.
3. Torso: upright, chest open to camera, red collar and yellow bell
   centred on the chest.
4. Arms and hands: front paws on the basket rim, still during the line.
5. Head and gaze: head level, blue eyes to camera, mouth open in song;
   ears up, relaxed.
6. Depth layering: near — basket rim and bell; mid — the cat; far — the
   girl standing behind the bike, boardwalk and sea beyond.
7. Motion inside the 10 seconds: start — cat settles into the basket;
   end — cat holds the singing pose, bell glinting, mouth closing after
   the last word.

## P-GIRL-SING (front, girl singing, cat listening)
1. Name: P-GIRL-SING. Viewpoint: front, eye level.
2. Stance: the girl stands beside the parked blue bicycle, weight on one
   leg, one hand resting lightly on the handlebar.
3. Torso: open to camera, red short jacket over the black crop top,
   relaxed lean toward the bike.
4. Arms and hands: one hand on the handlebar, the other loose at her
   side; the cat sits in the basket beside her hand.
5. Head and gaze: sun hat shading her eyes, chin level, purple eyeshadow
   and red lipstick visible, mouth open in song, gaze to camera.
6. Depth layering: near — handlebar and basket; mid — the girl; far —
   boardwalk planks and the sea.
7. Motion inside the 10 seconds: start — girl turns to camera from the
   bike; end — girl holds the singing pose, mouth closing after the last
   word, hand still on the handlebar.

## P-RIDE (three-quarter side, riding montage)
1. Name: P-RIDE. Viewpoint: three-quarter side, tracking.
2. Stance: the girl pedals the blue bicycle along the boardwalk, the cat
   sitting in the front basket facing forward.
3. Torso: girl leaning gently forward over the handlebars, jacket open,
   hair and hat ribbon moving in the breeze.
4. Arms and hands: both hands on the handlebars; the cat's paws on the
   basket rim.
5. Head and gaze: girl looking ahead down the boardwalk, profile to
   camera; cat looking ahead, mouth closed and still.
6. Depth layering: near — boardwalk planks passing; mid — bike, girl,
   cat; far — pier, sea, sky.
7. Motion inside the 10 seconds: continuous gentle pedalling and forward
   tracking; the camera holds a steady side track, no zoom during the
   ride.

## P-RAIL (front, cat on the rail, ice cup)
1. Name: P-RAIL. Viewpoint: front, slightly low.
2. Stance: the cat sits on the boardwalk rail by the steps, tail hanging
   down, an ice cup sweating on the rail beside it.
3. Torso: upright, facing camera, bell centred.
4. Arms and hands: front paws tucked neatly, still.
5. Head and gaze: head level, blue eyes to camera, mouth open in song
   (or closed and still on a GIRL line, listening).
6. Depth layering: near — rail and ice cup; mid — the cat; far — steps
   down to the sand and the sea.
7. Motion inside the 10 seconds: start — cat hops up onto the rail;
   end — cat holds the pose, tail swaying once, mouth closing after the
   last word.

## P-WALK (three-quarter, walking the bike)
1. Name: P-WALK. Viewpoint: three-quarter front.
2. Stance: the girl walks the blue bicycle along the boardwalk, one hand
   on the handlebar, the cat riding in the basket.
3. Torso: relaxed, upright, jacket open, walking pace.
4. Arms and hands: one hand on the handlebar, the other swinging gently;
   the cat's paws on the basket rim.
5. Head and gaze: girl glancing toward the cat in the basket, then to
   camera; cat looking up at her, mouth closed and still.
6. Depth layering: near — boardwalk planks; mid — girl, bike, cat; far —
   vendor corner and beach walkers.
7. Motion inside the 10 seconds: slow walking pace forward; the camera
   tracks beside them at a steady distance.

## P-SUNSET-HOLD (wide front, final hold)
1. Name: P-SUNSET-HOLD. Viewpoint: front, wide to medium.
2. Stance: the girl stands beside the parked blue bicycle at sunset, one
   hand on the handlebar, the cat sitting up in the basket.
3. Torso: both facing camera, relaxed, golden light on their faces.
4. Arms and hands: girl's hand on the handlebar; cat's paws on the
   basket rim, bell catching the sun.
5. Head and gaze: both to camera; the singer's mouth open on the final
   line, closing after the last word; the other's mouth closed and still.
6. Depth layering: near — basket and bell; mid — girl and cat; far —
   the sun low over the sea, boardwalk stretching behind.
7. Motion inside the 10 seconds: start — wide sunset shot; end — gentle
   push to medium, bell glinting in the last frame (rhymes with the
   first frame).

## P-VENDOR (three-quarter, vendor corner)
1. Name: P-VENDOR. Viewpoint: three-quarter side.
2. Stance: the girl leans on the vendor's corner counter, the bike
   parked beside her, the cat in the basket watching the vendor.
3. Torso: relaxed lean on the counter, jacket open.
4. Arms and hands: one forearm on the counter, the other hand holding
   nothing; the vendor's hands busy with fruit behind the counter.
5. Head and gaze: girl looking toward the vendor, then to camera; cat
   looking at the fruit, mouth closed and still.
6. Depth layering: near — counter and fruit; mid — girl and bike; far —
   boardwalk and sea.
7. Motion inside the 10 seconds: start — girl arrives at the counter;
   end — girl holds the lean, a small nod, mouth closing after the last
   word.
