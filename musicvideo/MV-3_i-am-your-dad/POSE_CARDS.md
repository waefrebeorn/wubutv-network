# MV-3 POSE_CARDS.md — monster man in a suit, big yellow ball smiley head

Identity block (verbatim in every vehicle, in this order: identity + outfit + pose):
- IDENTITY: "A monster man in a tailored dark suit, slightly too tall, broad shoulders, solid and present. His head is one big smooth yellow ball with a simple smiley face: two small solid black oval eyes and one wide curved smile. The smile IS his mouth: when he sings it opens wide and articulates clearly, and when he is silent it rests as the painted smile. No hair, no hat, no mask seam, no human face underneath."
- OUTFIT: "Dark charcoal suit, crisp white shirt, cadmium-yellow tie, polished dark shoes. The tie's yellow rhymes with his head; nothing else on him is yellow."
- HUE NOTE: the head's yellow is the motif color. Two primaries max per frame plus bone and ink: yellow head + cadmium tie against ink suit and bone shirt. No other saturated color competes.

Pose cards (one signature pose per card; every MV_PREPRO scene assigns exactly one card plus a one-line variation). SFW only. One character per scene keeps its own card.

## P-CROON — front, medium close-up, mic on stand
1. Viewpoint: front.
2. Stance: feet shoulder-width apart, weight even, planted.
3. Torso: upright, slight lean toward the mic, chest open.
4. Arms and hands: one hand resting lightly on the mic stand, the other open at waist height, palm up on the claim line.
5. Head and gaze: chin level, oval eyes straight to camera, smile-mouth open and articulating during the line, closed to the painted smile after the last word.
6. Depth: mic stand near (soft, out of focus at frame edge), monster man mid, plain wall or street backdrop far.
7. Motion: start at rest with hands down → one hand rises to the stand before the first word; no motion stacked on the vocal.

## P-LEAN — three-quarter, leaning in
1. Viewpoint: three-quarter, body angled, head turned to camera.
2. Stance: one foot forward, weight on the front foot, knees soft.
3. Torso: leaning in toward camera, shoulders angled, conspiratorial but warm.
4. Arms and hands: both hands open toward the viewer, palms up, offering the line.
5. Head and gaze: head tilted slightly, oval eyes to camera, smile-mouth open on the line.
6. Depth: foreground shoulder or doorway edge near, monster man mid, room far.
7. Motion: start upright → lean in before the first word, hold through the line.

## P-POINT — front, pointing at the viewer
1. Viewpoint: front, medium shot.
2. Stance: feet planted, weight even, one step toward camera before the line.
3. Torso: square to camera, slight forward pitch.
4. Arms and hands: one arm extended, index finger pointing straight at the viewer on the invitation ("Say it with me"); other hand at side.
5. Head and gaze: chin slightly down, oval eyes locked to camera, smile-mouth open on the line.
6. Depth: empty space near (the point travels through it), monster man mid, crowd-less street far.
7. Motion: step forward before the first word → point held through the line → hand lowers after the last word.

## P-LISTEN — three-quarter, head tilted, listening to the echo
1. Viewpoint: three-quarter, head in profile-to-camera turn.
2. Stance: feet planted, weight even, still.
3. Torso: upright, turned slightly away from camera.
4. Arms and hands: one hand cupped near where an ear would be (no ear visible — hand hovers beside the ball head), other hand at side.
5. Head and gaze: ball head tilted, oval eyes looking off-frame toward the echo source, mouth closed to the painted smile (CUTAWAY pose — never a singing pose).
6. Depth: speaker or doorway near, monster man mid, empty room far.
7. Motion: start facing camera → head turns to listen before the echo, holds still.

## P-MARCH — full body, walking toward camera
1. Viewpoint: front, full body, street or hallway.
2. Stance: mid-stride, one foot forward, arms swinging gently.
3. Torso: upright, moving forward, suit unruffled.
4. Arms and hands: arms relaxed at sides, swinging with the stride; no prop.
5. Head and gaze: oval eyes to camera, smile-mouth closed while walking (CUTAWAY) or opening only on a declared singing line.
6. Depth: street/hallway stretching far behind, monster man mid moving toward near.
7. Motion: start far → walks toward camera through the shot, one continuous approach.

## P-HOLD — front, arms open, final-frame pose
1. Viewpoint: front, medium shot, same framing as the first scene.
2. Stance: feet planted, weight even, grounded.
3. Torso: upright, chest open, welcoming.
4. Arms and hands: both arms spread wide, palms up, embracing the room.
5. Head and gaze: chin level, oval eyes to camera, smile-mouth closed to the painted smile, held.
6. Depth: plain backdrop far, monster man mid, nothing near to occlude him.
7. Motion: arms rise before the final line → hold, still, through the fade.
