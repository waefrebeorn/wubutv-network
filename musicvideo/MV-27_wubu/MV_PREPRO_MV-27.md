- LEAD (S1) = THE HOST: a grown man in his mid-thirties, short dark hair,
  trimmed beard, navy broadcast jacket with gold trim over a black t-shirt,
  dark jeans. Signature prop: a chunky GOLD broadcast microphone. Same face,
  same outfit, every take.
- SUPPORT (all grown adults, named in every vehicle): MARA, the producer —
  a woman in her forties, dark bob haircut, red blazer, headset around her
  neck. DEX, the engineer — a man in his fifties, grey flat cap, denim work
  shirt. SOL, the camera operator — a woman in her thirties, orange beanie,
  black utility vest. Crowds: grown adults in their 30s–60s ONLY, stated
  explicitly in every crowd vehicle. NO children, NO teenagers, nobody who
  could read as under 25, no family stagings.
- WORLD — ONE COHERENT ELSEWHERE: the network's own broadcast city at
  night. A rooftop antenna tower above neon streets; a warehouse studio, a
  control room, a backlot. The HOST works the night shift at the tower; the
  verses are the grind, the choruses are the city chanting his name back,
  the final chorus is the whole skyline answering. Continuity carriers:
  the HOST + the gold microphone + teal-and-gold palette.
- LOOK: photoreal night-city pop, shot on 35mm film, visible fine grain.
  Palette: deep teal + broadcast gold, bone and ink; accent amber.
  Section colour map: intro/choruses warm gold-teal saturated; verses
  cooler blue desk-light (5000K+); pre-choruses warming; breakdown deep
  teal; final chorus full warm saturation peak; outro dawn amber.
- FORBIDDEN: legible text anywhere (all billboards, screens, signs and
  papers BLANK or pure light), logos, children, frozen stills, slow
  openings, ramp-from-rest starts. Every take is already moving at frame 0.

## Recurring motif (declared, escalating — motif law)

THE ANSWERING CITY: every chorus, more of the city answers the chant —
street crowd (Ch1) → crane + rooftops (Ch2) → balcony relay (Ch3) →
breakdown circle → whole skyline (Final Chorus) → dawn wave-back (Outro).
Each slot's MOTION MOTIF is distinct (see table); the escalation is the
city's answering scale, never a re-run staging.
