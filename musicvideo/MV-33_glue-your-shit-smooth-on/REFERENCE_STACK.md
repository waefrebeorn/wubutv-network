## 2. THE CANON — rococo Von (full affirmation block; identity source of truth)

*Version-locked for MV-33. Pasted in full into close/medium vehicles; the
compact paragraph (§3) is the default vehicle load per the compact-recipe
rule, with this block as the identity source of truth in the prepro.*

**Medium / style.** Photorealistic live action with warm golden light and a
gentle film grain; bright major-key palace light, never dim, never gritty.
(The forehead hero scene S-hook-2 is the one named exception: an
18th-century painted-illustration finish, declared per-scene.)

**The man.** An adult man in his mid-forties. He is an American man of
European descent, from the American South, and it shows in the visible
facts: fair skin with a warm undertone and light sun colour on the cheeks
and nose, light green-hazel eyes, a broad face with a strong square jaw, a
straight nose, and a wide mouth that opens into a broad grin showing even
upper teeth. His skin has real texture — pores, fine lines at the eyes,
light stubble shadow on the cheeks — never airbrushed smooth.

**Facial hair — signature detail.** He wears exactly one small dark-brown
goatee, centred on the chin below the lower lip, neatly trimmed, about an
inch and a half long. He wears a dark-brown mustache, kept subtle. His
cheeks and jawline are otherwise clean. The goatee is always there, in
every shot, at every distance — it is the face's signature.

**Hair / the wig.** His own hair is dark brown, worn as a mullet — shorter
on top, long past the ears down the back of the neck — and in this video it
is always covered by the wig. The wig is a tall white powdered rococo wig,
never grey, never blonde, never silver: dense uniform curls in stacked
rows over the crown, full curled masses at both sides of the face, and
long loose curls falling well past both shoulders, front and back. At the
back the wig is tied low with exactly one small black ribbon bow. The wig
sits high and full; it is never flat, never thin, never a judge's wig.

**Wardrobe — top to bottom, always the same.** A deep crimson-red velvet
frock coat — the red is a rich wine red, not scarlet, not burgundy-brown —
with heavy raised gold floral embroidery running along both front edges,
the lapels, the deep cuffs and the pocket flaps. Under it, a cream silk
waistcoat with fine gold embroidery down its front. At the throat, a full
white lace jabot, layered and frothy, tied at the neck over a white stock.
At both wrists, deep white lace cuffs fall over the hands' backs. For
full-length shots: white silk knee breeches, white stockings, and black
court shoes with plain gold buckles — the same court dress, completed.
The coat is always velvet, the embroidery always gold, the lace always
white.

**Signature prop.** A small white glue tube with a plain blue band and a
white cap — the wig glue. It is small enough to hide in one fist. It
appears only in the ritual beats; it is never oversized, never labelled
with readable text.

**Build (BUFF PASS, msg 1001).** The face stays exactly as the references
have it — all exaggeration lives in the body. In the mine-work beats he
carries a generous, powerful courtier-miner build under the coat: broad
chest, thick arms, and a grand round seat that the coat tails frame; the
coat is never slimmed, never tailored down.

**Keep / avoid.** Wig: always powdered white, always full curls, black
ribbon always at the back. Coat: always deep red velvet, always gold
embroidery. Goatee: always present, always centred. Default expression:
a graceful half-smile at court; deadpan — mouth closed, completely
serious — in the mine-work and booty beats; he never explains himself.
Banned words for this canon: "young", "clean-shaven", "blonde wig",
"grey hair", "silver wig", "plain coat", "skinny", "slim".

**Bookend line (closing re-affirmation for vehicles):** "the same man —
powdered white curls, small dark chin goatee, deep-red velvet and gold —
throughout."
