# MV_PREPRO MV-33 — "Glue Your Shit Smooth On, It Is Not Over"

**Plan shape: THE FULL RESTAGE.** The coal-mine song restaged in plot order
onto the baroque court-mine story: a courtier-miner is ordered to pack and
leave the Gilded Seam; instead he presses the tube to his forehead and glues
his wig smooth on; the court — and finally the mine itself — summons him
back, because this coal shall not mine itself. Choruses are the SMOOTHON
forehead ritual; Verse 2 is the fries shift in the baroque canteen; the
breakdown is the wig ritual at the harpsichord; the outro lands the
heart-hands.
