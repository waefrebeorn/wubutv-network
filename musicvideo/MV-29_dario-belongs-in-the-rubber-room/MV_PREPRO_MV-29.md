## Concept — THE PADDING SPREADS

One location per section, invaded panel by panel by a white padded
cell. Intro + breakdown + final chorus + outro live inside the room;
verses/choruses play in an AI-lab office, a keynote stage, a Nasdaq-
style bell floor, a boardroom, a war room and a chip-fab corridor —
each visit, more of the set is padded than the last. Frame 0 is
already inside the room, mid-hook. Final frame rhymes with frame 0.

## Style lock (every vehicle, verbatim)

"Shot on 35mm film, visible fine grain, slight falloff toward the
corners. Palette: warm cream-white padding + olive green + warm office
amber + ink. One continuous shot, no cuts. No text, letters or logos
anywhere — all screens, badges, banners and papers are blank. No
on-screen text, no subtitles, no captions, no morphing, no duplicate
props, no camera shake. Every person is a grown adult."

Camera law: exactly one primary camera move per take, named in the
vehicle; all other motion happens inside the frame. Cut points land on
frames already in motion. No take opens or closes on a held pose.

## Cast (cosplay; identity macros expanded into every vehicle)

- DARIO (THE singer, near every scene):
  "a grown man in his late thirties with curly dark brown hair and
  round black-rimmed glasses, wearing an olive-green hoodie, with an
  earnest wide-eyed expression" — verses may swap the hoodie for
  "a navy cardigan over a white t-shirt" (interview look, same man).
- HUANG (the departing foil; near-wordless — mouth closed in every
  appearance except the breakdown window-pass, where he is a
  silhouette): "a grown man in his sixties with swept-back silver-grey
  hair and thin glasses, wearing a black leather jacket".
- Supporting figures, all named grown adults in the vehicles:
  orderlies in white scrubs, office staff, traders in suits, board
  members, journalists. No crowd is ever anonymous.

Line ownership: every sung line belongs to DARIO. Huang never sings.
Breakdown + outro + gap slots are wordless covers by design (captions
carry the sheet lines; the breakdown's soft timings never carry a
mouth).
