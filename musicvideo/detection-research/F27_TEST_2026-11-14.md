status was UNTESTED. Test as banked: per-segment residual on the MV-8 v2
(rejected) vs v3 (accepted) masters, cut pairs excluded. Pass bar: v2's

## Method (as run)

- `c11_flow` built from source (~/workspace/wubumv/tools/c11_flow, make,
  zero warnings); its `flow` mode emits the dense Farneback field per
  pair — no C changes needed for this form of the test.
- Frames decoded 160x90 gray @ 12fps (house TSM/M6 convention, same as
  flow_curve.py). Backward warp warped(x,y) = f1(x-u, y-v), bilinear, in
  numpy (detection-research venv, numpy 2.5.4); residual per pair =
  mean |warped - f2| / 255 over in-frame pixels.
- Cuts: ffmpeg scene detection gt(scene, 0.4); pairs straddling a cut
  (+/- 1 pair) excluded from kept stats and reported separately.
- Full masters: 2,247 frames / 2,246 pairs each. Runner:
  f27_warping_error.py (this directory); raw series: f27_mv8_v2.json,
  f27_mv8_v3.json.
- NOT implemented vs the banked spec: the forward-backward occlusion
  mask (the bank calls it "not optional" for the production form) and
  residual maps for eye-check localisation. Spike verdicts below are
  therefore pair-time evidence only, no eye check has been run.

## Verdict: pass bar NOT met — and the pair is the problem

- Bar 1 (v2 above v3) fails inverted: the accepted v3 reads ~1.6x the
  rejected v2 on every aggregate. Bar 2 (v3 mostly under 0.1) holds on
  the median (0.056) but 16.7% of v3 pairs sit over the band.
- The machinery is not the failure: cut pairs score ~3x kept pairs in
  both masters (0.119 / 0.136 vs 0.040 / 0.064), so the residual does
  measure motion-unexplained change, and cut exclusion works.
- Why the separation inverts: (a) v2's rejection was for a singing
  character at its centre, reused footage, and timing off the song —
  not for morph/melt instability; the banked "what it would have
  caught in MV-8 v2" paragraph overstated v2's instability content, so
  this pair never was a clean positive control. (b) v3 is by design
  the faster, denser build; residual rises with legitimate fast motion
  and disocclusion (the bank's own stated limit), and without the
  forward-backward occlusion mask nothing in the current form
  separates that from incoherence.
- Status recommendation: F-27 stays UNPROVEN, not adopted, not killed.
  The mean-residual form is energy-confounded across edits; the
  spike-localisation form is untested because the eye check has not
  been run.
