Written 2026-10-21 by the CEO office for the Producer (Muse).
Target: 120 BPM, 48 bars = 96.000 s exactly. Every bar = 2.000 s.
Delivery spec lives in README.md — the gate reads everything below the last
separator as lyrics, so nothing but boxes may follow the lyrics block.

===============================================================
STYLE FIELD
===============================================================
90s boom-bap funk, 120 bpm half-time stomp with a dry backbeat, deadpan spoken verses snapping into a shouted gang chorus, gruff male lead close-mic and flat, quiet verse lifting into a hard brass chorus, clipped brass stabs and dry punchy drums over a walking electric bass

===============================================================
EXCLUDE STYLES
===============================================================
autotune, glossy pop vocals, edm drop, reverb wash, sung vibrato lead, trap hi-hats, orchestral swell, female backing diva runs
