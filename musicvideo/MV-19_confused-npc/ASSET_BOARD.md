# MV-19 — ASSET BOARD + PRODUCTION BIBLE

## Concept — LOOP/RESET WORLD (intake design family)
One small apartment, held all video. The room is a game level and he
is its NPC: he spawns at the doorway with a purpose, takes three
steps, and the purpose despawns. Every section the room quietly
RESETS — furniture back on its floor marks, door shut, light restored
— and he runs the loop again, worse. Escalation: V1 the loop is
innocent → C1 the reset is visible → V2 he searches the level's
containers → C2 the world starts correcting itself around him and a
second self flickers → BR the loop at dusk, the roommate tries to
wake him → C3 the level spawns MORE of him → OUT he stops fighting
it: two NPCs standing, mugs in hand, at peace. The joke never leaves
the room; the room is the joke.

## World / look lock
ONE apartment held all video (living room + kitchenette + hall
nook) — events change inside it; no world-snap spine (ledger
warning). Photoreal live action. LOOK base: Shot on 35mm film,
visible fine grain, slight falloff to the corners. Key: warm
practical lamp at frame right, ~3:1 comedy contrast, both eyes
readable. Palette: cool teal + bone + ink shadow; accent rust
orange (KIT's sweater) ≤10% pop per frame plus the fridge's cool
white glow as the one cold note.
Section colour map: verses cool-neutral, each RESET drifts the room
slightly cooler/more saturated, bridge = dusk blue texture break,
final chorus + outro warmest (acceptance). Grade in post; refs
carry full colour.
THE MARKS: small coloured tape X's on the floor = furniture "spawn
points" and his standing spot centre-room. Props return to marks on
resets. The centre X is his: every freeze happens standing on it.

## The "?" motif (POST layer only — never generated in-clip)
A small hand-drawn question-mark graphic (post PNG, kinetic ASS/
overlay layer) pops above his head at each freeze: C1 one small,
C2 two, BR it hovers and follows, C3 one above every self, OUT one
above BOTH heads, shared. Motif returns are escalations, staged
differently each time — not event repeats.
