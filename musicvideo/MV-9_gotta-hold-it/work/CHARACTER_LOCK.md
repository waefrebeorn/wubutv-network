# CHARACTER LOCK — THE MAN (shared across MV-9 + MV-10)

ONE character, two ages. This lock is IDENTICAL in both projects
except the age markers. A different face in the two videos breaks
the whole concurrent design. Character identity is the generator's
strongest carrier — restate this lock verbatim in every vehicle,
and condition singing takes on the anchor still
(`work/stills/anchor_young_man_1248x704.png` for MV-9).

## THE MAN — S1 (sole singer, both videos)
- Lean, restless build. Sharp nose. Dark messy hair. One eyebrow
  always raised. Long face.
- **Signature props (NEVER change):** paper festival wristband,
  CADMIUM YELLOW, on his LEFT wrist — he never took it off, in
  either era. Black phone, cracked corner, glowing screen, in his
  RIGHT hand. The phone is a memory object.
- **YOUNG (MV-9):** late 20s, sunburned nose and shoulders, damp
  grey t-shirt, cargo shorts, sneakers.
- **OLD (MV-10):** late 60s/70s, grey at the temples, deeper lines,
  slower movements, navy cardigan, slippers, reading glasses on a
  chain around his neck. Same face, same wristband, same phone.
- Face: stubbled jaw (young) / grey stubble (old).

## STYLE LOCK (both videos — MV-8 v3 family)
- **FLAT BOLD 2D CARTOON** — cel-shaded animated film, thick ink
  outlines, flat colour fields, squash-and-stretch energy.
- MV-9 palette: sun-bleached sky blue, grass green, porta-potty
  blue, CADMIUM YELLOW (wristband + QR glow), vermilion stage glow,
  bone white.
- MV-10 palette: deep night blue, bone white, warm lamp gold,
  CADMIUM YELLOW (QR glow — the SAME glow as MV-9), vermilion accent.
- Forbidden in every vehicle: no text, no logos, no children,
  no photoreal, no licensed material, no extra limbs, no morphing.

## SHARED MOTIF PROPS (must visually rhyme across both videos)
1. THE QR CODE — glowing on the phone screen.
2. FOUR SQUARES OF TP — four white squares, held up, counted.
3. SOAP AND WATER — the wash station by the fence.
4. THE FENCE — the line runs along it.
5. THE PORTA POTTY — the blue box.
Plus: ADDICTS STANDING NEAR — background figures, hazy, never in focus.
