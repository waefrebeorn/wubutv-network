## ONE CHARACTER, TWO AGES
- MV-9 "Gotta hold it": the man YOUNG (late 20s), festival, the porta-potty line.
- MV-10 "Aging tropes": the SAME man OLD (late 60s/70s), nighttime peeing,
  prostate checks.
- Character identity lock must match across both videos: same face, same
  build, same mannerisms — age is the only difference. The identity text
  in the vehicles is IDENTICAL except age markers. Character identity is
  the generator's strongest carrier; a different face in the two videos
  breaks the whole design.
- Draft lock (scene-plan coordinator finalizes): THE MAN — lean, restless,
  dark hair, festival wristband. Young: sunburned, damp t-shirt, cargo
  shorts, sneakers, phone in hand. Old: grey at the temples, lines,
  slower, cardigan, slippers, reading glasses on a chain, the same phone.
  The phone is a memory object: it is in his hand in both eras.

## THE SHARED MOTIFS (must visually rhyme across both videos)
These five objects appear in BOTH videos. In MV-9 they are present-tense
festival objects; in MV-10 they are flashback/memory objects — same
framing, older, dimmer, smeared.
1. THE QR CODE — glowing on the phone screen.
2. FOUR SQUARES OF TP — held up, counted, four white squares.
3. SOAP AND WATER — the wash station by the fence.
4. THE FENCE — the line runs along it.
5. THE PORTA POTTY — the blue box.
Plus: ADDICTS STANDING NEAR (background figures, hazy, never in focus).

## THE BRIDGE EVENT (MV-9) — hazy, insinuated, NEVER explicit
- What the song says: drug trip at the soap-and-water station; something
  happened in the line; he doesn't remember; his butt hurt after.
- What the screen shows: HAZE ONLY. Blur, smeared colors, the line
  doubling, the fence tilting, the soap station glowing wrong, time
  skipping, the four squares floating. The audience feels "something
  happened and he can't remember it" — the same way the song does.
- HARD LINE: no sexual content on screen, no depiction of assault, no
  graphic anything. Insinuation lives in the haze and the cut, not in
  the image. Adults only in every frame (standing rule).

## THE BRIDGE MEMORY (MV-10)
- During the prostate check, the old man hazily remembers the QR code
  and the four squares of TP. Same objects, same framing as MV-9's
  bridge, but older, dimmer, smeared — the memory rhymes with the
  young video's bridge shots. The two bridges are shot as a matched
  pair: same angles, same props, different age, different light.

## THE PLY LAYERS (user's term — 4–5 layers of jokes)
1. The porta-potty hold-it gag (the surface joke).
2. The drug-trip haze (the line goes wrong).
3. The insinuated thing in the line (never shown, only felt).
4. Aging / prostate (the same body, later, same need).
5. The QR code + four squares as the memory bridge that ties the two
   songs — the joke that only lands if you have seen both videos.
