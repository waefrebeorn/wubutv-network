## Notes for the redo cut

- No line start is worse than ±2.5s, so no line is flagged approximate
  under the ±5s rule — but bracket starts are estimates, not frame-exact
  vocal onsets; the bold highlight can lag the vocal by ~1s.
- Short lines to watch in the redo: Verse 1 line 6 (NTSB, ~3s,
  30.5–33.5s), Bridge line 31 (dollar and a dime, ~2s, 165.5–167.5s),
  Chorus 1 line 11 (~2s, 51.5–53.5s). Do not stretch these to a full
  10s scene; they are pickups inside longer sections.
- Long holds: Chorus 2 line 30 ("…they just keep coming", ~12.5s,
  153–165.5s) is held over the run into the bridge; Outro line 48 is
  held/fading ~15s (253–268.01s). The outro is NOT two short repeats —
  it is one soft warning (238.5–248s) plus a long fading mantra tail.
- Where v1's uniform 10s grid drifted most: Bridge actually starts
  165.5s (v1 put bridge material at 150–160s); the extended chorus
  actually starts 190s and runs 48.5s (v1 spread it 180–230s); the Outro
  actually starts 238.5s (v1 started outro scenes at 230s) — which is
  why at 245s v1 was already on the mantra repeat while the song was
  still singing "It ain't worth the crash" (243–248s).
- Frames used are scratch only in /tmp/mv2_frames (5s sweep + 1s
  refinement crops); no project file other than this timeline was
  touched, no video generated.
