# POSE CARDS — MV-2 "Toll Roads Are Dumb" (THE VOICE)

Character: THE VOICE (see ASSET_BOARD.md character lock — identity and
outfit blocks are verbatim in every vehicle; only the pose block changes).
Palette INK + BONE + CADMIUM accent. Motif object: toll ticket, visible or
in hand at every scene open. Viewpoints and layering per card.

## Signature poses

### P-LEAN — three-quarter, spoken aside
- Feet planted, weight slightly forward; torso leaned toward camera.
- Left hand on mic stand; right hand raised, palm open, making the point.
- Chin slightly down, eyes to camera, knowing half-smile.
- NEAR: mic stand. MID: VOICE. FAR: foggy plaza.
- Motion: lean-in → settle.

### P-BOOTH — three-quarter, at the glass
- Feet planted at booth window; torso angled to glass.
- Right hand taps ticket flat against the glass; left hand on hip.
- Head turned to glass, then to camera on the punchline.
- NEAR: glass + ticket. MID: VOICE. FAR: empty booth interior.
- Motion: tap → turn to camera.

### P-WINCE — front, step-back
- Feet staggered, weight back; shoulders up; torso recoiled.
- Both hands up, palms out, warding; ticket pinched between two fingers.
- Eyes wide, wincing, mouth a flat line.
- NEAR: braking car tail-lights (prop). MID: VOICE. FAR: plaza.
- Motion: planted → step-back wince.

### P-SPLIT — front, center of the split
- Feet planted wide; torso square to camera; arms slightly out, palms down.
- Head turns left, then right, tracking the two lanes.
- NEAR: lane markers. MID: VOICE. FAR: fast blur one side, crawl other side.
- Motion: still → head turn left → head turn right.

### P-WEAVE — three-quarter, walking weave
- Walking between lane markers, torso swaying side to side, easy gait.
- One hand holds ticket aloft; other hand swings loose.
- Chin up, eyes ahead, deadpan.
- NEAR: lane markers passing. MID: VOICE. FAR: plaza + fog.
- Motion: weave left → weave right.

### P-CROON — front, big-band stance
- Feet planted shoulder-width; torso tall; chin up; eyes to camera.
- One hand on mic; other hand open at waist, then rising with the line.
- NEAR: mic stand. MID: VOICE. FAR: plaza, headlights.
- Motion: still → hand rise on the lyric hit.

### P-BATON — front, conducting
- Feet planted; torso tall; mic stand held like a baton in one hand.
- Conducting arm sweeps once across the frame on the beat.
- Chin up, eyes following the sweep, deadpan.
- NEAR: mic stand. MID: VOICE. FAR: lanes + fog.
- Motion: stand lowered → one sweep.

### P-COUNT — front, finger count
- Feet planted; torso still; one hand up, counting on fingers, slow.
- Other hand holds ticket at waist.
- Eyes on the counting hand, then deadpan to camera.
- NEAR: counting hand. MID: VOICE. FAR: plaza.
- Motion: count 1-2-3 → deadpan to camera.

### P-MARCH — side, marching the lane line
- Marching along the lane line, upright, steady pace, three-quarter to side.
- Ticket visible in breast pocket; arms swinging in time.
- Chin up, eyes ahead.
- NEAR: lane line. MID: VOICE. FAR: fog, headlights.
- Motion: march in → march on.

### P-DIME — close-up hands, then wide
- Fingers flip a dime; hand fills frame at open, then camera widens to
  full VOICE at the plaza.
- VOICE catches the dime, deadpan to camera.
- NEAR: dime in fingers. MID: VOICE. FAR: full plaza wide.
- Motion: close-up flip → wide catch.

### P-WATCH — front, watch check
- One wrist raised to face height, eyes on watch; other hand holds ticket.
- Then wrist drops, eyes to camera, wincing.
- NEAR: wrist + watch. MID: VOICE. FAR: gantry shadow.
- Motion: watch check → wince to camera.

### P-ECHO — front, hand to ear
- Feet planted; one hand cupped to ear, head tilted toward it.
- Other hand holds ticket at waist; deadpan, waiting for the echo.
- NEAR: cupped hand. MID: VOICE. FAR: fog.
- Motion: hand to ear → hold.

### P-SILHOUETTE — back, backlit in fog
- Back to camera, still, silhouette against approaching headlights.
- Arms at sides; ticket on the mic stand beside him (visible in profile).
- NEAR: mic stand + ticket. MID: silhouette. FAR: headlights in fog.
- Motion: still → hold (no move; the fog moves).

### P-TEAR — front, tearing the ticket
- Both hands tear the ticket slowly; pieces held up a beat, then released.
- Soft smile to camera as the pieces blow away.
- NEAR: ticket pieces in hands. MID: VOICE. FAR: fog, empty plaza.
- Motion: tear → release → smile.
