## THE NEW AXIS — THE BALL PARK
One giant cartoon carnival/fairground complex where every ball world
lives side by side: a bowling alley, a basketball court, a disco hall,
a pinball arcade, a golf pond, a billiards room, a ball pit, a pool.
The camera travels through it; every scene turns into a new attraction.
MONTAGE-FIRST: multiple gags / cuts / worlds per scene, never one held
vantage. The joke lives in the PUNS — every scene is a different kind
of ball, and the water is always nearby (fountain, pond, pool, gutter
channel) so the song's splash lands somewhere real.
TASTE: no toilet humor on screen, nothing crude, nothing explicit.
The lyrics stay in the AUDIO; the picture is balls + water + motion.
BASED / X-TIMELINE-PILLED: fast cuts, shitpost energy, a visual gag
every few seconds, the timeline's own language — strike, swish, spike,
multiball, water hazard, 8-ball, bouncy castle logic.

## Method (carried from the Nov 10 rebuild proof — it worked, 23/23 KEEP)
1. Two-image journey calls: [1] framing text + style lock + "Wide 16:9
   video, exactly 10 seconds" + "the picture is already moving at
   frame 0" + first-image instruction; [2] IMAGE start ref; [3] TEXT
   transition naming image 2 + full-10s motion plan keyed to song
   beats/words + forbiddens; [4] IMAGE end ref; [5] TEXT arrival/end-state.
2. NEW per-project IMAGE-REFERENCE STACK (stack2): one start/end pair
   per scene, built from THIS build's own material (style lock, Ball
   Park world) — never a Nov 10 stack frame (duck/toilet retired).
   Stack lives in work/stills/stack2/ + work/REFERENCE_STACK2.md,
   written before volley 1. Every stack image is read back (eye +
   ffprobe 1248x704) before use. A drifted ref is re-generated, never
   used as-is (ref poisoning).
3. Duration: request exactly 10 seconds on every call (10s requests
   always returned 10.000s). Assembly trims to the slot.
4. "AT NOT OF" (user standing rule): every vehicle prompt leads with
   'at <place>' — the framing text places the camera at the place,
   then describes what happens there.
5. Fresh first frames where the cut TURNS (new angle, new setting, new
   scene). A fresh first frame must still line up with the outgoing
   frame's WORLD (same Ball Park, same lighting, same palette) so the
   joint doesn't jump. CHAIN (last-frame → first-frame) where the
   scene continues in the same world (S22→S23; any same-world pair in
   later volleys). Never chain from a rejected take.
6. Sequential firing within a volley only where a chain dependency
   exists; volley 1 has none (all fresh), so scenes may fire in any
   order, but the volley still proceeds S01 → S06 in song order.

  take = REJECT (wrong axis), reroll on the WORLD axis.

  its 10s — a single held vantage = REJECT (montage-first law).

  take N+1 first frame (frame-diff + eye read: world, lighting,
  palette). A joint that jumps = FAIL, reroll the downstream take on
  the JOINT axis.
